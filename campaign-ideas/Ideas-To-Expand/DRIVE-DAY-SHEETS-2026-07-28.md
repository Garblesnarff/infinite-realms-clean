# Day-of-Week Idea Sheets — Triage

**Source:** seven Google Sheets, one per day, each in a themed folder
(`Monday_Macabre`, `Tuesday_Twisted`, `Wednesday_Weird`, `Thursday_Thriller`,
`Friday_Fantasy`, `Saturday_SciFi`, `Sunday_Supernatural`).
**Read in full:** Saturday, Thursday, Wednesday. **Not read:** Monday, Tuesday,
Friday, Sunday — see the reasoning at the end.

Format is consistent across all of them:
`Title | Pitch | Comparative Titles | Synopsis | Setting | Themes`.

---

## The framing problem

These are **story pitches for a horror channel, not campaign frameworks.** A pitch
has one protagonist and a fixed ending. A campaign needs player agency, factions to
push against, and no predetermined hero. Converting one is close to writing fresh —
the reusable part is the premise, not the document.

So the question for each is only: *is the premise worth stealing?*

---

## Saturday (Sci-Fi) — worth taking

The only sheet that earns its keep. About 40 pitches. Roughly a dozen map onto the
genres with no shippable campaign.

### Post-Apocalyptic

**Blood Code** — a scavenger survives on symbiotic blood-borne nanotech, then finds
hers carries a dormant extinction code. Hunted and revered at once.

**Garden of Synthetic Eden** — a geneticist reverses desertification and creates
hyper-evolving predatory plant life that escapes containment.

### Urban

**The Skinjackers** — black-market tech lets people rent other bodies. A courier's
client refuses to vacate, and he is stranded through a chain of unwilling hosts.

**Hivemind** — everyone is neurologically linked until the governing AI fragments,
and a city's private thoughts spill into the street.

**The Soulcatcher** — a black-market memory dealer ends up holding a dead man's
memories, and something is looking back through them.

### Intrigue

**The Archivist** — memories are stored externally and monitored. A clerk finds a
suppressed memory of a joyful past and starts pulling the thread.

### Genre-flexible, strongest premises

| Idea | Why it stands out |
|---|---|
| **The Orpheus Implant** | An implant restoring memory to dementia patients unlocks ancestral memory; patients enact centuries-old vendettas |
| **The Chronovore** | A creature that feeds on stolen years, stalking a crew through a temporal distortion |
| **The Thoughtworm** | A neural implant that writes thought into text starts steering the thoughts, trapping a writer inside a narrative |
| **The Machinist's Symphony** | An android composer's music turns out to be a memetic weapon |

Handle with care: **The Skin Weaver** and **Children of the Swarm** are strong but
lean hard into body horror. Fine for a horror channel, a different call for a
subscription product with a broad trial audience.

## Thursday (Thriller) — skip

Contemporary slasher and stalker material: masked killers, home invasion, cabins in
the woods, crisis-hotline callers, social-media challenges. Almost none of it
converts to a tabletop fantasy campaign without total reinvention, and a lot of it
is violence against a lone woman in a modern suburb — a different product from a
solo AI adventure.

It is also the most derivative sheet. The comparative-titles column names Halloween,
Friday the 13th, The Strangers, Us, Oculus, Hush, Don't Breathe, and the synopses
follow those films closely.

One flag: **Skinwalker** uses a Navajo cultural and religious concept as a monster.
Worth avoiding regardless of genre fit.

## Wednesday (Weird) — mostly skip

Cosmic horror, and it has your library's exact disease. Counting the sheet's own
entries:

- **Seven** near-identical "astronomer detects a signal and goes mad" pitches:
  *The Murmurs of Unseen Stars*, *The Whisper Beyond the Veil*, *The Resonance of
  Forlorn Stars*, *The Resonance Beneath*, *The Murmuring Stars*, *The Whispering
  Void*, *The Whispering Void (variation)*. Several even reuse the same protagonist
  name, Dr. Eleanor Vance.
- **Four** "deep-sea crew wakes an ancient thing": *Echoes of the Void*, *Echoes of
  the Abyss*, *The Children of the Abyss*, *The Whisperer in the Depths*.
- **Seven** "mathematician or physicist finds a reality-breaking equation":
  the *Entropy* cluster plus *The Geometry of Madness*, *The Fractal Gods*,
  *The Fractured Mind*.

That is the same generated-variation pattern that produced your 114 identical
bestiaries. Same approach, same result, different output type — which is decent
evidence the generation spec was the right fix.

Salvageable: **Beneath Pleasantville** (a cosmic entity enforcing 1950s suburban
conformity) and **Echoes of the Unwritten** (an author trapped in his own unfinished
novel). Both are distinct from everything else in the sheet.

Note *The Whisperer in Darkness* is labelled a Lovecraft reimagining. Lovecraft is
public domain, so that is legally clear — just derivative.

## Recommendation

Take **Saturday only**, and take the premises, not the documents. Ten to twelve
concepts, aimed squarely at Sci-Fi, Post-Apocalyptic and Urban.

Against the 195 concepts already imported from the campaign-idea files, this is a
supplement rather than a main source — those were written as campaigns and arrive
with a locus, sensory anchor and expansion hook. These arrive as film pitches. Use
them where the campaign-idea pool is thin, which is exactly Sci-Fi and
Post-Apocalyptic.
