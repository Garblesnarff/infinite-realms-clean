# 1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY
`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`

### The Origin Myth: The Draft That Refused Deletion
Before the Gods, there were the Architects—meta-cosmic entities who drafted planes of existence like sketches on parchment. The world of *Colossus Ascent* (The Forbidden Lands) was a failed draft, a reality deemed "mathematically insolvent" due to the paradox of Dormin, an entity of perfect duality. The Architects attempted to delete this plane, to recycle its arcane matter. However, the inhabitants—the First Mortals—refused to unmake themselves. They forged sixteen "Nails of Reality" (the Colossi) to pin the fabric of their world down, preventing it from dissolving into the void. Dormin was not a demon invading, but the sentient operating system of this world, shattered and stuffed into the Nails to keep the physics engine running. To kill a Colossus is to pull a nail; to pull them all is to let the world be crumpled up and thrown away.

### The Timeline of Echoes
1.  ** The Zero Point:** The Architects issue the Decree of Nullification. The sky turns into static.
2.  **The Anchor Ritual:** Sixteen Heroes volunteer to become living geometries, trapping the fragments of Dormin.
3.  **The Year of Solid Rain:** Following the ritual, rain fell as glass shards for 300 days, crystallizing the landscape.
4.  **The Silence of the Birds:** All avian life in the Forbidden Lands lost its voice simultaneously; they now communicate via bioluminescence.
5.  **The First Breach:** A thief named Kael attempted to drill into Valus’s heel to mine "God-Marrow." He was fused into the stone, becoming a permanent pimple on the giant.
6.  **The Night of Inverted Gravity:** A minor seal fracture caused the western plateau to float into the stratosphere (now the Sky Cliffs).
7.  **The Moss Plague:** A sentient fungus evolved on the back of Phaedra, consuming three nomadic tribes before becoming dormant.
8.  **The Arrival of the Null-Walkers:** Beings from the void outside the draft began leaking in, appearing as 2D shadows in a 3D world.
9.  **The Treaty of Granite:** The surviving mortals agreed to leave the Forbidden Lands, sealing the gates to protect the Nails.
10. **The Current Awakening:** The protective brands appear on the Players, marking the start of the final integrity check of the reality.

### The Metaphysical "Why"
The conflict exists because the world is **Stagnant by Design**. The Colossi enforce a localized time-stop to prevent the "Rot of Progress" which attracted the Architects' deletion protocols. The Players believe they are heroes breaking a curse; in reality, they are viruses accelerating the simulation so it crashes, potentially rebooting the world into a new, stable version—or erasing it forever.

# 2. FACTIONS
`[TAG: FACTION_DATA]`

### [The Dust-Drinkers]
*   **Type:** Survivalist Scavengers / Cult
*   **Leader:** **Mother Loam** (A woman whose skin has turned to sandstone).
*   **Asset:** **The Divining Rods:** Tools made from Colossus bone that can locate water and weak points in reality.
*   **Rivals:** The Architects' Erasers.
*   **Philosophy:** Consume the land to become the land. They eat soil to gain stone-skin (natural armor).

### [The Architects' Erasers]
*   **Type:** Eldritch Paladins / Cosmic Janitors
*   **Leader:** **Unit 734** (A warforged-like construct made of white marble and void-stuff).
*   **Asset:** **Null-Blades:** Weapons that don't cut flesh, but delete the space the flesh occupies.
*   **Rivals:** The Marked (The Players), The Dust-Drinkers.
*   **Philosophy:** "The draft must be discarded." They want the Colossi dead to finish the deletion.

### [The Society of the Vertical Horizon]
*   **Type:** Extreme Mountaineers / Cartographers
*   **Leader:** **High-Altitude Hanno** (Missing an eye, missing 3 fingers, lung capacity of a whale).
*   **Asset:** **Gryphon-Hair Ropes:** Unbreakable climbing gear.
*   **Rivals:** The Moss-Chewers.
*   **Philosophy:** Enlightenment is found only above 500 feet. They map the backs of the Colossi without killing them.

### [The Moss-Chewers]
*   **Type:** Drug-Addled Mystics
*   **Leader:** **Spore-Lord Vix**.
*   **Asset:** **Psychoactive Lichen:** Harvested from Colossi. Allows short-term telepathy.
*   **Rivals:** The Society of the Vertical Horizon.
*   **Philosophy:** The Colossi are dreaming, and by eating their parasites, we join the dream.

### [The Echo-Smiths]
*   **Type:** Sonic Weaponsmiths
*   **Leader:** **Silence** (A bard who cut out their own tongue to hear better).
*   **Asset:** **Resonance Hammers:** Weapons that strike a bell to shatter armor via frequency.
*   **Rivals:** The Dust-Drinkers.
*   **Philosophy:** The world began with a sound; it will end with a silence.

### [The Order of the Empty Scabbard]
*   **Type:** Pacifist Monks
*   **Leader:** **Abbot Stone-Hand**.
*   **Asset:** **The Sanctuary of Stasis:** A zone where violence is physically impossible due to magic.
*   **Rivals:** Any who hunt Colossi.
*   **Philosophy:** To draw a weapon is to admit spiritual defeat. They guard the Colossi from hunters.

### [The Parasite Kingdoms]
*   **Type:** Micro-Civilizations
*   **Leader:** **King Louse the 40th**.
*   **Asset:** **Mobile Fortresses:** They live *on* the Colossi (specifically Phalanx and Malus) in huts made of skin-flakes.
*   **Rivals:** The Players.
*   **Philosophy:** "Don't kill the ground you walk on."

### [The Ink-Blots]
*   **Type:** Corrupted Scholars
*   **Leader:** **Professor Noctis**.
*   **Asset:** **Liquid Shadow:** A substance that can be molded into tools but drains sanity.
*   **Rivals:** The Architects' Erasers.
*   **Philosophy:** Study the shadow corruption to control it. They capture Shadow Wraiths for experiments.

### [The Broken Sigil]
*   **Type:** Anarchist Mages
*   **Leader:** **Rift-Walker Tara**.
*   **Asset:** **Portable Portals:** Unstable blink-magic.
*   **Rivals:** The Order of the Empty Scabbard.
*   **Philosophy:** Break the seals to see what happens. Pure curiosity driving apocalypse.

### [The Last Orchestra]
*   **Type:** Doomsday Bards
*   **Leader:** **Conductor Vrail**.
*   **Asset:** **The Great Horn:** A massive instrument carved from a canyon that can wake dormant Colossi.
*   **Rivals:** Everyone.
*   **Philosophy:** If the world is ending, it needs a soundtrack.

# 4. LOCATIONS
`[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]`

### Zone 1: The Weeping Plains (Valus/Quadratus)
*   **Concept:** Grassy steppe where the morning dew is salt water (tears of the land).
*   **Loc 1: The Hallow-Bridge:** A bridge made of a single colossal rib bone. **Sensory:** Smells of marrow and rain.
*   **Loc 2: The Whispering Grass:** Grass blades are razor sharp. **Sensory:** Sound of thousands of swords unsheathing when wind blows.
*   **Loc 3: The Stone-Herd:** Statues that move when you blink. **Sensory:** Smell of ozone.
*   **Loc 4: The Sinking Shrine:** A temple slowly being swallowed by mud. **Sensory:** Wet suction sounds.
*   **Loc 5: The Cloud-Anchor:** A chain reaching into the sky. **Sensory:** Cold metallic taste in air.

### Zone 2: The Calcified Forest (Kuromori)
*   **Concept:** Trees turned to stone instantly.
*   **Loc 1: The Branch-Library:** Books carved into stone leaves. **Sensory:** Dust everywhere.
*   **Loc 2: The Petrified Zoo:** Animals turned to stone mid-run. **Sensory:** Eerie silence.
*   **Loc 3: The Shadow-Well:** A hole with no bottom. **Sensory:** Vertigo, smell of cold air.
*   **Loc 4: The Resin-Falls:** Amber flowing like water. **Sensory:** Sweet, cloying scent.
*   **Loc 5: The Root-Cage:** Roots forming a prison cell. **Sensory:** Claustrophobic darkness.

### Zone 3: The Inverted Canyon (Avion)
*   **Concept:** A canyon where gravity pulls sideways.
*   **Loc 1: The Horizontal Waterfall:** Water flows from left to right. **Sensory:** Disorienting roaring sound.
*   **Loc 2: The Sky-Bottom:** Looking down reveals the sky. **Sensory:** Nausea.
*   **Loc 3: The Wind-Tunnel:** Constant hurricane force winds. **Sensory:** Ears popping.
*   **Loc 4: The Roost of Needles:** Spiky rock formations. **Sensory:** Sharp, scratching sounds.
*   **Loc 5: The Cloud-Coral:** Clouds that are solid to touch. **Sensory:** Wet, spongy texture.

### Zone 4: The Rust-Dunes (Phalanx)
*   **Concept:** Desert made of iron filings, not sand.
*   **Loc 1: The Magnetic North:** Pulls all metal items. **Sensory:** Hum of magnetic tension.
*   **Loc 2: The Oxidized Lake:** Red water, tastes like blood. **Sensory:** Metallic stench.
*   **Loc 3: The Gear-Graveyard:** Ancient massive cogs buried. **Sensory:** Creaking metal.
*   **Loc 4: The Mirror-Sand:** Glass patches formed by lightning. **Sensory:** Blinding reflection.
*   **Loc 5: The Buried Titan:** A hand reaching out of the rust. **Sensory:** Heat radiating from the metal.

### Zone 5: The Void-Edge (Malus)
*   **Concept:** The edge of the map where reality glitches.
*   **Loc 1: The Pixelated Shore:** Matter breaks into cubes. **Sensory:** Static noise.
*   **Loc 2: The Colorless Zone:** Black and white only. **Sensory:** Visual flattening.
*   **Loc 3: The Echo-Chamber:** Thoughts become audible. **Sensory:** Cacophony of whispers.
*   **Loc 4: The Wire-Frame Mountains:** Transparent mountains. **Sensory:** Electric buzzing.
*   **Loc 5: The Final Gate:** A door floating in nothingness. **Sensory:** Absolute zero temperature.

### The Grip Gauge
Instead of simple Athletics checks, players have "Grip Points" (GP) equal to **Str Score + Proficiency**.
*   Climbing costs 1 GP per round.
*   Colossus Shaking costs 1d4 GP.
*   Jumps cost 2 GP.
*   **Zero GP:** Player falls. Must find a flat surface to recharge.

### Corruption & Sanity
Every Colossus death adds **1 Corruption Point**.
*   **1-4:** Cosmetic changes (Black veins).
*   **5-8:** Mechanical drawback (Disadvantage on Wis Saves).
*   **9-12:** Hallucinations (DM gives false info).
*   **13-16:** Shadow Form (Gain powers, lose max HP).
*   **17:** **Total Subsumption.** Character becomes an NPC vessel for Dormin.

### The Physics of Scale
*   **Massive Damage:** Attacks from Colossi don't just deal HP damage; they force **Str Saves** to avoid being thrown.
*   **The "Ant" Rule:** Players are so small that some Colossus attacks *miss* them simply because the players are in the texture of the "skin" (moss/cracks), granting Full Cover.
