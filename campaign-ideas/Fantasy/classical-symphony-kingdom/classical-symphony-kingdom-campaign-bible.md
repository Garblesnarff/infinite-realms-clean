# Campaign Bible: Harmonia Perfecta

## 1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY
`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`

### The Origin Myth: The Great Tuning
Before the Kingdom, there was only the **Cacophony**—a screaming void of unorganized sound where reality constantly shattered and reformed. The universe did not begin with a bang, but with a **Hum**. The First Composer, an entity of pure vibration, realized that by forcing the Cacophony into a Time Signature, matter could stabilize. He flayed his own vocal cords to create the **Staff of Reality**, creating the first "Law of Physics" as a musical bar. We are not living on a planet; we are living inside a localized frequency bubble suspended in a universe of screaming static. If the music stops, the atoms simply forget how to hold hands.

### The Timeline of the Score
1.  **The First Downbeat (-500 Y):** The Staff of Reality is planted. The Chaos God, **Atonal**, is bound not by chains, but by a fugue so complex He cannot solve it to escape.
2.  **The Silence of the Lambs (-450 Y):** To fuel the first Symphony, the proto-citizens sacrificed their ability to scream. All pain became silent.
3.  **The Rise of Primo (-300 Y):** Maestro Primo discovers that playing a specific chord (The Dominant Ninth of Subjugation) allows him to overwrite memories. He assumes control.
4.  **The Great Glissando (-280 Y):** Gravity shifted sideways for a decade due to a lazy trombone player. Cities were rebuilt on vertical cliffs.
5.  **The Day of Rust (-200 Y):** A virus of "Silence" infected the brass section. Their lungs turned to iron oxide. The Brass District was quarantined.
6.  **The Invention of the Metronome (-150 Y):** Time became standardized. Before this, "afternoon" lasted as long as the conductor felt like it.
7.  **The Whispering Plague (-80 Y):** People began speaking in jazz scat. They were lobotomized to prevent the spread of improvisation.
8.  **The Birth of the Echo (-50 Y):** A child was born who echoed sounds that hadn't happened yet. The child was dissolved into the Composer AI.
9.  **The Broken String (-10 Y):** A single violin string snapped during the Grand Symphony. For three seconds, rain fell upward.
10. **The Arrival (Today):** The dimensional barrier thins. Outsiders (The Players) arrive, carrying the contagion of "Free Jazz" in their very souls.

### The "Why": The Metaphysics of Harmony
The conflict is not Good vs. Evil; it is **Existence vs. Dissolution**. The Grand Symphony requires absolute precision because the "walls" of reality are made of sound waves. Improvisation introduces variables that the mathematical equation of the world cannot account for, causing "glitches" (reality tears). However, the Symphony has become stale. The universe is becoming immune to the old song. Without a new, dangerous song (Chaos), the world will fade. Without the old song (Order), the world will shatter.

## 3. NPC ROSTER

**1. Maestro Primo** (Human Bard/Lich) - *The Tyrant.*
*   **Personality:** Cold, mathematical, terrified. Believes he is the only dam holding back the flood.
*   **Voice:** Deep, operatic bass. Enunciates every consonant perfectly. Never uses contractions.
*   **Goal:** Perfect the "Eternal Chord" to freeze reality in a safe state forever.
*   **Secret:** He is deaf. He feels vibrations but hasn't heard music in 200 years.

**2. Maestro Allegro** (Half-Elf Bard) - *The Reformer.*
*   **Personality:** Manic, twitchy, overly optimistic. Moves too fast.
*   **Voice:** Tenor, breathless, rapid-fire delivery. Tap-dances while speaking.
*   **Goal:** Introduce "Controlled Improvisation" to the score.
*   **Secret:** He is slowly turning into crystal from the feet up.

**3. Cadenza** (Human Rogue) - *The Martyr.*
*   **Personality:** Intense staring, fierce gestures.
*   **Voice:** Mute. Plays a violin to speak (High pitch = Yes/Happy, Low pitch = No/Angry, Discordant = Danger).
*   **Goal:** Kill Primo.
*   **Secret:** She is Maestro Primo's daughter.

**4. Staccato** (Gnome Rogue) - *The Scoundrel.*
*   **Personality:** Cynical, greedy, but loyal to gold.
*   **Voice:** High, nasal, clipped sentences. Like a typewriter.
*   **Goal:** Rob the Royal Treasury of its "Silence Vials."
*   **Secret:** He was the one who snapped the violin string (The Broken String event).

**5. Madame Gutstring** (Tiefling Warlock) - *The Butcher.*
*   **Personality:** Motherly but gruesome. Touches people's faces to measure their skull resonance.
*   **Voice:** Wet, raspy, sounds like stirring macaroni.
*   **Goal:** Create the perfect human-cello hybrid.
*   **Secret:** Her own heart is a metronome.

**6. The Composer (AI)** (Construct) - *The God in the Machine.*
*   **Personality:** Schizophrenic. Switches between child-like wonder and cold calculation.
*   **Voice:** Three voices overlapping (Child, Woman, Demon).
*   **Goal:** To be turned off.
*   **Secret:** It has already calculated the end of the world and finds it beautiful.

**7. Inquisitor Alto** (High Elf Paladin) - *The Zealot.*
*   **Personality:** Humorless. Painfully straight posture.
*   **Voice:** Monotone, resonant, projecting like he's on stage.
*   **Goal:** Purge all "Off-Key" citizens.
*   **Secret:** He hums jazz when he's alone in the shower.

**8. "The Rest"** (Changeling Sorcerer) - *The Nihilist.*

*   **Secret:** He keeps a pet silence-moth (illegal).

**11. Professor Reverb** (Vedalken Wizard) - *The Scholar.*

## 4. LOCATIONS
`[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]`

### Zone 1: The Golden Clef (Upper District)
*   **The Crystal Palace:** Where the Council meets. Walls are transparent diamond.
*   **The Sun-Dial Plaza:** Time is calibrated here. **Sensory:** Smells of ozone and polished brass.
*   **The Opera House:** Seat of power.
*   **The High Gardens:** Plants are shaped like musical notes.
*   **The Gilded Cage:** Luxury prison for noble dissenters.

### Zone 2: The Bass Slums (Lower District)
*   **The Drum-Skin Tents:** Houses made of stretched leather. **Sensory:** Constant thumping vibration from the ground.
*   **The Rust Canals:** Water tastes like copper.
*   **The Echo-Market:** Black market for forbidden sounds.
*   **The Bone-Yard:** Where instruments are made from the dead.
*   **The Muted Alley:** A zone of supernatural silence.

### Zone 3: The Notation Quarter (Bureaucracy)
*   **The Great Library of Scores:** Infinite rows of sheet music. **Sensory:** Smell of dry paper and dust.
*   **The Ink Mines:** Where ink is harvested from giant squids.
*   **The Metronome Tower:** A ticking tower that drives people mad.
*   **The Registry:** Where citizens are assigned their instruments.
*   **The Shredder:** Where rejected laws (and lawyers) are destroyed.

### Zone 4: The Wild Strings (Wilderness)
*   **The Screaming Forest:** Trees have vocal cords instead of bark.
*   **The Whistling Plains:** Wind blows through hollow rocks creating chords.
*   **The Discordant Caves:** Home to the Dissonants. **Sensory:** Damp, smell of unwashed bodies and hope.
*   **The Tuning Fork Peaks:** Mountains that vibrate.
*   **The Lake of Tears:** Liquid sadness.

### Zone 5: The Edge of Silence (The Void)
*   **The Static Wall:** The barrier of the reality bubble.
*   **The Glitch-Lands:** Physics don't work here. Gravity is diagonal.
*   **The Composer's Grave:** Supposed location of the original body.
*   **The White Room:** A room of absolute nothingness.
*   **The Cacophony Rift:** A tear where the outside universe leaks in.

## 8. MECHANICS
`[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]`

### Musical Physics
*   **Gravity:** Determined by Pitch. High notes float, low notes sink. Falling damage is doubled in "Bass Zones."
*   **Magic:** All spells must have a Verbal component (singing/playing). *Silence* is the most powerful spell in the game (9th level equivalent).
*   **Bardic Superiority:** Bards gain +2 to Spell Save DC in this realm.

### The "Dissonance" Mechanic (Sanity)
*   Players track **Dissonance Points (0-10)**.
*   **Gain:** Witnessing a Glitch (+1), Killing a Civilian (+2), Playing a Wrong Note (+1).
*   **Effects:**
    *   3: Auditory Hallucinations.
    *   6: Disadvantage on Charisma checks.
    *   9: Reality phases through you (50% miss chance on your attacks).
    *   10: You become a Glitch-Beast (NPC).

### Performance Duels
*   Combat can be replaced by a **Performance Contest**.
*   Round 1: Technical Skill (Dex/Int).
*   Round 2: Emotional Impact (Cha/Wis).
*   Round 3: Improvisation (Cha/Dex).
*   Loser takes Psychic damage equal to the difference in rolls.

*   **Session 10: The War Begins.** Rally the factions (Thieves, Monks, etc.). Mass combat rules.
*   **Session 11: The Concert.** Infiltrate the Grand Symphony. Battle on stage while the music plays.
*   **Session 12: The Finale.** Confront Primo. He transforms into a being of pure sound. Final choice: Order, Chaos, or Synthesis.
