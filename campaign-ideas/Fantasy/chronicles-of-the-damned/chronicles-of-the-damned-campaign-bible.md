## [TAG: LORE_CORE] Section 1: Deep Lore & History

### Origin Myth: The Stasis and the Rot
In the beginning, the cosmos was a chaotic, screaming cancer of creation. To save existence, the **Dominator** did not seek to rule, but to *embalm*. He sought to freeze the universe in a state of eternal, perfect crystal—a "Living Death" where nothing changes, nothing dies, and nothing grows. The forces of "Good" (The White Rose) are technically fighting for **The Rot**—the natural cycle of decay, rebirth, and entropy. The Company finds itself caught between the **Ice of Tyranny** and the **Rot of Freedom**. The Lady is the middle path: She wants Order without Stasis, but her methods are painted in blood.

### The Timeline of Shadows (10 Events)
1.  **The First Embalming:** The Dominator perfects the "Stillness Spell," turning the entire civilization of Khatovar into living obsidian statues.
2.  **The Betrayal of the Ten:** The Lady, originally the Dominator's wife, seduces the Ten Generals (The Taken) to help her bind him, not to save the world, but because she refused to be a statue.
3.  **The Age of Silent Kings:** For 200 years, the Taken rule independent city-states where speech was illegal to prevent counter-spells.
4.  **The Company's Shame:** The Black Company is formed from the Dominator's elite guard who fled instead of dying with him. They erased this from their own history.
5.  **The Rain of Teeth:** A magical catastrophe in the Northern Empire causes it to rain human molars for three years, cementing the soil into a calcium-rich wasteland.
6.  **The Resurrection of the White Rose:** A peasant girl eats a glowing tuber in the forest, inheriting the soul of the ancient rebel general.
7.  **The Siege of Dejagore:** The Company resorts to cannibalism to survive; the Annalist at the time writes it off as "unconventional logistical solutions."
8.  **The Lady's Awakening:** The Lady breaks the Fourth Seal on the Dominator’s tomb to siphon his power, accidentally waking his consciousness.
9.  **The Night of Long Knives:** The Taken General "Soulcatcher" murders the entire Guild of Assassins, not for politics, but because they charged too much.
10. **The Current Contract:** The Company signs with the Soulcatcher to suppress the rebellion in the North.

### The Metaphysical "Why"
The conflict is not Good vs. Evil. It is **Memory vs. Oblivion**. The Taken are immortal because they have forgotten how to die. The Company Annals are magic because they *force* the universe to remember. If the Company is wiped out and the Annals destroyed, their deeds didn't just "not matter"—they literally vanish from the timeline. You fight to prove you existed.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA] Section 2: Factions

### [The Black Company]
**Type:** The Protagonists / Mercenary Brotherhood
**Leader:** The Captain (Stoic, Pragmatic)
**Asset:** **The Annals.** A chronicle that grants tactical foresight based on past battles.
**Rivals:** The Circle, The Taken (eventually).

### [The Lady's Empire]
**Type:** Sorcerous Hegemony
**Leader:** The Lady (The Tyrant-Sorceress)
**Asset:** **The Taken.** Eleven demigods bound by true-name magic.
**Rivals:** The Dominator, The Circle.

### [The Circle of Eighteen]
**Type:** Rebel Alliance / Terrorist Cell
**Leader:** The White Rose (Reincarnated Hero)
**Asset:** **Null-Aura.** They can negate magic in small pockets.
**Rivals:** The Lady, The Black Company.

### [The Ministry of Post-Mortem Labor]
**Type:** Necro-Bureaucracy
**Leader:** Auditor-General Bones (A Lich obsessed with tax code)
**Asset:** **Infinite Manpower.** They raise the dead to farm and build, collapsing the local economy.
**Rivals:** Priests of the Sun, Labor Unions.

### [The Architects-Errant]
**Type:** Siege Engineer Cult
**Leader:** Grand Mason Dorus
**Asset:** **Geometry Magic.** Can weaken castle walls by finding "mathematical insults" in their architecture.
**Rivals:** Anyone who builds ugly buildings.

### [The Society of Weeping Spoons]
**Type:** Spy Network / Beggar Guild
**Leader:** "Soup" (An unassuming child)
**Asset:** **Auditory Network.** They communicate through the clatter of silverware in taverns worldwide.
**Rivals:** The Lady’s Secret Police.

### [The Order of the Empty Scabbard]
**Type:** Pacifist Swordmasters
**Leader:** Blademaster Null
**Asset:** **Intimidation.** They are so skilled they never draw weapons; enemies surrender from the *implication* of violence.
**Rivals:** Mercenaries who actually fight.

### [The Mycelial Clergy]
**Type:** Druidic Eco-Terrorists
**Leader:** The Spore-Mother
**Asset:** **Fungal Surveillance.** They hear everything spoken near mold.
**Rivals:** The Architects-Errant (who use stone).

### [The Dominator’s Remnant]
**Type:** Cult of the Deep Deep
**Leader:** The Buried Voice
**Asset:** **Whispers.** Can drive enemies insane via dreams.
**Rivals:** The Lady.

### [The Coin-Eaters]
**Type:** Anti-Mercenary Zealots
**Leader:** Pauper King Hral
**Asset:** **Curse of Poverty.** Gold turns to lead in their presence.
**Rivals:** The Black Company.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1] Section 3: NPC Roster (Major)

**1. Croaker** (Human Cleric/Bard) - Cynical, romantic, weary. **Voice:** Gravelly, noir-narrator style, often sighs. **Goal:** Finish the Annals before dying. **Secret:** He is writing a romance novel about the Lady in the back pages.

**2. The Captain** (Human Fighter) - Stoic, father figure. **Voice:** Deep, slow, authoritative, commands respect. **Goal:** Keep the Company solvent. **Secret:** He has lost the contract scroll and is bluffing about the terms.

**3. The Lady** (Ascended Human Warlock) - Terrifyingly beautiful, cold. **Voice:** Echoing, multi-tonal, smooth as glass. **Goal:** Break her bond to the Dominator. **Secret:** She fears the dark.

**4. Soulcatcher** (Taken Sorcerer) - Manic, shifts personalities. **Voice:** Changes every sentence (child, old man, woman). **Goal:** Overthrow the Lady. **Secret:** She is the Lady's sister.

**5. The Limper** (Taken Sorcerer) - Sadistic, resilient. **Voice:** Wet, rasping, like dragging meat on gravel. **Goal:** Kill the Black Company. **Secret:** He cannot be killed by violence, only by kindness (which burns him).

**6. One-Eye** (Human Wizard) - Short, black, ugly, creates illusions. **Voice:** Cackling, high-pitched, mocking. **Goal:** Prank Goblin. **Secret:** His hat creates a pocket dimension where he hides booze.

**7. Goblin** (Human Wizard) - Small, frog-like, creates blasting magic. **Voice:** Croaky, fast, defensive. **Goal:** Prank One-Eye. **Secret:** He actually respects One-Eye.

**8. The White Rose** (Human Rogue/Paladin) - Young, burdened, fierce. **Voice:** Soft but unyielding, clear distinct diction. **Goal:** Destroy the Empire. **Secret:** She hates being a "chosen one."

**9. Silent** (Human Monk) - Tall, albino, mute. **Voice:** N/A (Uses intense staring and hand signs). **Goal:** Protect the Darling. **Secret:** He isn't mute; he swore a vow that his next word would be the command to end the world.

**10. Raven** (Human Ranger) - Edgy, noble, deadly. **Voice:** Whispery, clipped, Clint Eastwood-esque. **Goal:** Protect the White Rose. **Secret:** He killed his own family to save them from the Taken.

**11. Elmo** (Human Fighter) - Sergeant, dependable, big. **Voice:** Booming, warm, laughs often. **Goal:** Keep the men in line. **Secret:** Deeply depressed.

**12. Shapeshifter** (Taken Druid) - Giant, feral. **Voice:** Growls, animal noises mixed with words. **Goal:** Destroy civilization. **Secret:** Wants to be a house cat.

**13. Feather** (Halfling Rogue) - Light as air, kleptomaniac. **Voice:** Chirpy, fast, enthusiastic. **Goal:** Steal a Taken's ring. **Secret:** Has actually stolen the Lady's hairbrush.

**14. Case** (Human Warlock) - Haunted, possessed by a demon he controls. **Voice:** Dual-toned, vibrates. **Goal:** Exorcise himself. **Secret:** The demon is the one in control; Case is the passenger.

**15. Sleepy** (Human Tactician) - Young, brilliant, narcoleptic. **Voice:** Yawning, monotone, sharp intellect. **Goal:** Perfect the drill. **Secret:** She is a girl pretending to be a boy (Standard trope, but executed well).

**16. The Hanged Man** (Taken Undead) - Neck bent at wrong angle. **Voice:** Gurgling, choked. **Goal:** Make everyone suffer. **Secret:** He regrets his treason.

**17. Toadkiller Dog** (Demon Hound) - Massive mastiff, intelligent. **Voice:** Telepathic, deep bass. **Goal:** Serve the Lady. **Secret:** Loves belly rubs.

**18. Bomanz** (Human Wizard) - Short, fat, tragic. **Voice:** Wheezing, scholarly, stuttering. **Goal:** Open the barrow. **Secret:** He knows he will die doing it.

**19. Mercy** (Tiefling Medic) - Butcher-surgeon. **Voice:** Clinical, cold, unbothered by gore. **Goal:** Anatomy research. **Secret:** Is stitching soldiers together to make a "Super Soldier."

**20. The Dominator** (Eldritch Entity) - The Shadow in the ground. **Voice:** Heard in the mind only, overwhelming static. **Goal:** Eternal Stasis. **Secret:** He is afraid of the Company.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN] Section 4: Locations

### Zone 1: The Northern Marches
*   **The Opal City:** A fortress made of iridescent stone. **Sensory:** Smells of ozone and unwashed bodies.
*   **The Whispering Woods:** Trees that repeat the last words spoken near them. **Sensory:** Constant low-level murmuring.
*   **Camp Vengeance:** The Company’s muddy, fortified base. **Sensory:** Smoke, roasting meat, latrines.
*   **The Rust Plains:** A battlefield where blood has rusted the iron-rich soil. **Sensory:** Taste of copper in the air.
*   **The Hanging Gardens of Beryl:** Beautiful gardens watered with poison. **Sensory:** Sweet, sickly floral scent that induces nausea.

### Zone 2: The Barrowland
*   **The Dominator’s Tomb:** A massive black pyramid. **Sensory:** Absolute silence; sound dies here.
*   **The Sea of Dust:** Grey ash dunes. **Sensory:** Dry throat, grit in eyes.
*   **The Obsidian Forest:** Trees turned to glass. **Sensory:** Chimes when the wind blows.
*   **The Screaming Caves:** Wind tunnels that sound like wailing. **Sensory:** Ear-popping pressure changes.
*   **Fortress of the Stake:** An ancient outpost surrounded by impaled skeletons. **Sensory:** Rattle of bones.

### Zone 3: The Southern Coast
*   **Jewel City:** Wealthy, corrupt trade hub. **Sensory:** Spices, fish, sewage.
*   **The Mangrove Maze:** Swamp filled with sorrow-spiders. **Sensory:** Wet rot, humidity.
*   **The Lighthouse of the Blind:** A tower that projects darkness. **Sensory:** Cold, damp stone.
*   **The Salt Flats:** Endless white expanse. **Sensory:** Blinding light, salt on lips.
*   **The Ship graveyard:** Hundreds of wrecks. **Sensory:** Creaking wood, crying gulls.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN] Section 5: Quest Architecture

### The Main Campaign (The Path of the Damned)
1.  **Contract Signed:** Enforce order in a rebellious town. (Moral test: Brutality vs. leniency).
2.  **The First Battle:** Defeat a rebel cell. Discover they are just starving peasants.
3.  **The Taken Arrives:** Soulcatcher demands the Company retrieve an artifact.
4.  **The Artifact:** It’s a child. (Protect or Deliver?)
5.  **The Betrayal:** The Empire refuses to pay.
6.  **The Switch:** The Company considers the White Rose’s offer.
7.  **The Journey South:** Escort the Rose to the Barrows.
8.  **The Siege:** Defend the Rose from the Limper.
9.  **The Revelation:** The Lady appears; she isn't trying to stop the Rose, she's helping her.
10. **The Barrow Opens:** Enter the tomb.
11. **The Horror:** Fight the Dominator’s guardians.
12. **The Sacrifice:** Someone must stay behind to hold the door.
13. **The True Name:** Find the Dominator’s name to bind him.
14. **The Choice:** Bind him (save the world, serve the Lady) or Kill him (chaos ensues).
15. **The Final Entry:** The Annalist records the end.

## [TAG: RULES_CUSTOM] Section 8: Mechanics

### The Annals Mechanic
At the end of each session, one player (The Annalist) writes a summary.
*   **Accurate/Gritty:** The Company gains +1 "Luck Points" next session.
*   **Lazy/Missed:** The Company takes -1 to all Charisma checks next session.

### The Dread System
Instead of simple HP, players have **Dread**.
*   When witnessing Taken magic or horrors, gain Dread.
*   At 10 Dread, gain a **Phobia** (e.g., Fear of Clouds).
*   Remove Dread through **Gallows Humor** (Roleplay banter during combat).

### Company Reputation
*   **Trust:** 1-10 Scale. High Trust = Better prices, more recruits.
*   **Infamy:** 1-10 Scale. High Infamy = Enemies fear you, but towns bar their gates.

## [TAG: DM_GUIDE] Section 9: Session Guide

*   **Sessions 1-3:** "The Grunt Work." Establish the tone. Dirty, paid work. Introduction of the Lady.
*   **Sessions 4-6:** "The Doubt." The rebels make good points. The horrors of the Taken increase.
*   **Sessions 7-9:** "The Turn." The Company must choose a side. Escape the Empire.
*   **Sessions 10-12:** "The Long March." Survival horror. Running from the Taken.
*   **Sessions 13-15:** "The End Game." The Barrowland. The metaphysical choice (Stasis vs. Rot).
