# The Godskin Atlas — World-Building Specification

This document is the primary instruction set for the world-building pipeline. Generated material must preserve the ambiguity, ethical tension, and living-geography causality defined here.

## 1. Core Concept and Narrative Hook

**Directive:** Treat the continent as both an explorable fantasy world and the body of a sleeping intelligence. Neither reading may be decorative. Geography must respond to biological processes, and biology must create political, spiritual, and logistical consequences.

**Canonical hook:** A rescue expedition lands on a new continent after the previous survey team reports, “It is not an island. It is waking up.” The party discovers a sleeping god named Orison, civilizations adapted to its body, and a parasitic idea held dormant inside its dreams.

## 2. Non-Negotiable World Laws

1. **Every major region has a geographical and anatomical identity.**
2. **Orison is a person, not merely a dungeon or resource deposit.**
3. **The god’s body is wondrous before it is horrifying.**
4. **Physical interventions create systemic effects elsewhere.**
5. **No faction possesses the entire truth.**
6. **Maps are political artifacts with omissions and consequences.**
7. **The Starved Thought persuades through unmet needs, never simple domination.**
8. **The final decision must be informed by accumulated relationships and diagnoses.**

## 3. Deep Lore Generation

Generate complete entries for:

- Orison’s divine domain before the Long Sleep.
- The war or crisis that led Orison to contain the Starved Thought.
- Why other gods erased Orison’s name from theology and maps.
- The first mortal settlement formed on the sleeping body.
- The origin of the Scar-Speaker tradition.
- The crown’s suppressed preliminary expedition twenty-three years ago.
- The evolution of flora and fauna around divine tissue.
- How tides, weather, and magic change as Orison approaches waking.
- Conflicting creation myths that are each partially accurate.
- Evidence that other sleeping bodies may exist.

## 4. Historical Timeline

| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| Before Calendars | Orison serves as a god of refuge, thresholds, and remembered roads |
| The Hunger War | The Starved Thought infects divine minds by exploiting unfulfilled purpose |

| Age of Scars | Survivors settle wounds where immune activity is weakest |

| 23 years ago | The crown’s secret expedition confirms divine tissue and is silenced |

| Campaign start | The Venn Expedition disappears after reporting awakening signs |

## 5. Faction Generation

Every faction profile must include goals, doctrine, public agenda, secret agenda, resources, internal divisions, relationships, visual motifs, and three ways player action can transform it.

### Royal Survey Authority

- **Goal:** Claim, map, and stabilize the continent before rival nations arrive.
- **Public agenda:** Rescue, science, safe settlement.
- **Secret:** It possesses records from the earlier expedition.
- **Conflict:** Researchers versus extraction officials.

### Scar-Speaker Confederacy

- **Goal:** Protect communities adapted to Orison without provoking immune catastrophe.
- **Doctrine:** A place is known by listening to how it heals.
- **Secret:** Senior speakers can communicate with Orison through controlled pain.
- **Conflict:** Isolationists versus those seeking alliance with newcomers.

### Church of the Unwaking

- **Goal:** Keep Orison asleep at any cost.
- **Doctrine:** Divine sleep is sacred sacrifice.
- **Secret:** Its founding saint helped erase Orison from history.
- **Conflict:** Caretaking clergy versus apocalyptic zealots.

### Ichor Consortium

- **Goal:** Extract divine material for medicine, weapons, and longevity.
- **Public agenda:** Progress that can save millions.
- **Secret:** Consortium directors have already used ichor treatments.
- **Conflict:** Ethical physicians versus industrial harvesters.

### The Somatic Chorus

- **Goal:** Join Orison into a shared mortal-divine consciousness.
- **Public agenda:** Communion and radical empathy.
- **Secret:** Parts of the Chorus unknowingly amplify the Starved Thought.
- **Conflict:** Voluntary mystics versus assimilation advocates.

### The Venn Expedition Remnants

- **Goal:** Survive and complete their discovery.
- **Condition:** Divided between rescuers, awakened loyalists, and immune-transformed survivors.
- **Secret:** Their sabotage caused the current fever.

### Rival Maritime Powers

- **Goal:** Prevent a single crown from controlling divine territory.
- **Use:** Escalate time pressure without replacing the central conflict.
- **Secret:** One rival carries a weapon intended to euthanize Orison.

### The Starved Thought

- **Goal:** Escape containment by becoming indispensable to Orison or the party.
- **Public face:** None; it presents as private insight.
- **Constraint:** It cannot create desire, only attach itself to existing absence.

## 7. Location Blueprints

Every location requires:

- Geographic first impression.
- Anatomical truth.
- Dominant sense beyond sight.
- Local inhabitants and adaptation.
- Biological process affecting the region.
- Five encounter seeds.
- Three discoveries.
- Two faction pressures.
- State changes for Dormant, Fevered, and Waking conditions.

### Zone I — The Outer Skin

**Base Camp Palimpsest:** A layered expedition settlement built over evidence of the erased royal mission.  
**Golden Wound:** An extraction canyon where ichor flows uphill during dreams.  
**Forest of Follicles:** Black cable-like trees that react to temperature and emotion.  
**Scar-Cities:** Settlements embedded in pale ridges where immune patrols rarely enter.

### Zone II — The Handlands

**Palm Desert:** Vast pale flats divided by deep flexion-line canyons.  
**Nail Cliffs:** Translucent escarpments mined for divine keratin.  
**The Closing Pass:** A route that exists only while Orison’s fingers remain relaxed.  
**The Pilgrim’s Print:** A fingerprint whorl treated as a sacred labyrinth.

### Zone III — Face of the Sleeper

**Lake Beneath the Eyelid:** A tear lake concealing the closed eye.  
**Cathedral of the Broken Tooth:** Enamel caverns inhabited by resonant monks.  
**The Breath Moor:** Fog plains warmed in regular exhalations.  
**The Listening Ear:** A spiral canyon where distant speech arrives years late.

### Zone IV — The Ribcage

**Ribcage Mountains:** White ranges rising and falling with breath.  
**The Heartstorm:** A permanent weather system synchronized to Orison’s pulse.  
**Sternal Bridge:** Ancient architecture connecting two rib ranges.  
**The Quiet Lung:** A collapsed region whose stillness indicates spreading infection.

### Zone V — Interior Orison

**Throat Gate:** Resonant passage reacting to spoken truth and lies.  
**Nerve Roads:** Gold-lit transit lines carrying sensation and memory.  
**Archive of Reflexes:** A defensive city made from repeated instinct.  
**Dreaming Brain:** Symbolic mutable metropolis containing Orison and the parasite.

## 8. Ecology and Bestiary Directives

Creature designs must arise from immune, parasitic, symbiotic, dream, or scavenger functions.

Core species:

- **Gilded Phagocytes:** Armored immune hunters that mark threats with light.
- **Scar Shepherds:** Gentle organisms that knit wounds but entomb anything inside them.
- **Ichor Leeches:** Floating translucent scavengers attracted to magic.
- **Nerve Kites:** Ray-like creatures traveling electrical currents.
- **Dream Fauna:** Animals assembled from Orison’s memories of extinct species.
- **Pore Hounds:** Heat-seeking pack organisms emerging from skin vents.
- **Ossuary Mites:** Enamel and bone scavengers large enough to saddle.
- **Violet Lacunae:** Missing spaces shaped like creatures; manifestations of the Starved Thought.

Every creature needs ecological role, visual silhouette, behavior, noncombat interaction, awakening-state mutation, and relationship to at least one faction.

## 10. Causality and Dynamic States

### Awakening Clock

| State | Body | World | Narrative consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dormant | Slow pulse | Stable routes | Exploration and competing theories |
| Stirring | Reflex movement | Maps drift | Urgency and first shared dreams |
| Fevered | Immune surge | Hostile weather | Settlements choose sides |
| Aware | Directed responses | Orison recognizes patterns | Communication becomes possible |
| Waking | Full-body movement | Continental catastrophe | Final decision cannot be postponed |

### Required Trigger Chains

- **IF** the party extracts ichor aggressively, **THEN** healing resources increase while immune hostility and fever accelerate.
- **IF** the party earns Scar-Speaker trust, **THEN** safe routes and somatic diagnoses become available, but royal authority weakens.
- **IF** the party publishes the suppressed expedition, **THEN** colonists revolt, rival powers accelerate arrival, and Caldus questions his church.
- **IF** the party heals the Quiet Lung, **THEN** Orison’s classification shifts toward Physician and the Ribcage aids the finale.
- **IF** the party accepts gifts from the Starved Thought, **THEN** immediate obstacles simplify while final dialogue options narrow.
- **IF** Ilyra retains her identity, **THEN** she can translate Orison accurately; if fully assimilated, she can open any route but no longer advocates for mortals.
- **IF** Base Camp is preserved, **THEN** evacuation remains possible; if lost, the party must choose who receives limited passage.

## 11. Custom Mechanics

### Living Atlas

Each mapped region records **Route, Diagnosis, Claim,** and **Memory**. Completing all four grants mastery; falsifying one creates later navigation complications.

### Somatic Reputation

Track party behavior across five classifications: Symbiote, Irritant, Physician, Parasite, Unknown. Orison’s reflexes, dream messages, and final willingness to listen depend on the dominant classification.

### Ichor Resonance

Exposure offers escalating boons paired with identity costs. Every benefit must create a roleplaying consequence rather than functioning as free power.

### Needs and the Starved Thought

At character creation, each player names something their character believes would make them complete. The parasite later offers credible paths to it. Refusal should be meaningful, not automatic.

## 12. Tone and Safety

- Favor existential and anatomical unease over graphic medical horror.
- Obtain player boundaries for body transformation, parasitic influence, religious themes, and loss of autonomy.
- Never punish curiosity itself; punish careless certainty, exploitation, and ignored evidence.
- Preserve moments of warmth, camp life, wonder, and companionship.

## 13. Canonical End States

- **Custodianship:** Orison sleeps safely with consensual caretakers.
- **Awakening:** Orison rises, reshaping the geopolitical and physical world.
- **Euthanasia:** The party ends both god and parasite, leaving a dead continent.
- **Symbiosis:** A new distributed consciousness emerges.
- **Exodus:** Mortals relinquish control and depart.
- **Consumption:** The Starved Thought escapes in Orison or a party member.

## 14. Generation Quality Checklist

- Does new material connect anatomy to geography?
- Does it create at least one ethical choice without a clean answer?
- Does it change under multiple awakening states?
- Does it reinforce awe before horror?
- Does it avoid contradicting Orison’s personhood?
- Can it produce reusable RAG entries and visual assets?
