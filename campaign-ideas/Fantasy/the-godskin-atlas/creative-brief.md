# Creative Brief: The Godskin Atlas

This document is the canonical style reference for visual, audio, interface, and promotional assets. When another prompt conflicts with this brief, this brief wins unless explicitly revised.

## Creative North Star

**An illuminated anatomical expedition manuscript painted with the monumental light and scale of a Romantic oil painting.**

The work should feel created by explorers inside the world: sacred, observational, weathered, and increasingly disturbed by what it records. Images must initially read as magnificent terrain; anatomical truth should emerge through careful viewing.

## Primary Visual Style

### Medium

- Hand-painted fantasy illustration on warm vellum.
- Precise ink contours and cartographic hatching.
- Selective gold-leaf illumination for divine ichor, nerves, and sacred annotations.
- Romantic landscape lighting: vast skies, atmospheric depth, sublime scale.
- Anatomical marginalia, measurement marks, specimen notations, and restrained ornamental borders.

### Visual Hierarchy

1. **Wonder:** The environment must be beautiful enough to justify exploration.
2. **Scale:** Human figures are frequently tiny against divine anatomy.
3. **Recognition:** Anatomy appears through pattern rather than immediate gore.
4. **Unease:** Something in the image contradicts its first interpretation.
5. **Documentation:** The work feels catalogued, witnessed, and preserved.

## Style Rules

### Always

- Preserve a legible focal subject.
- Use terrain/anatomy dual readings whenever relevant.
- Include evidence of expedition scale: tents, ropes, boats, survey poles, or figures.
- Treat divine tissue as ancient, mineral, botanical, or celestial rather than wet gore.
- Use gold only for divinity, ichor, nerves, or authoritative annotations.
- Favor asymmetric organic composition contained by orderly manuscript framing.
- Show wear appropriate to a field document: foxing, rubbed edges, repaired tears, faint stains.

### Never

- Generic red meat caverns or excessive exposed viscera.
- Modern laboratory aesthetics, plastic equipment, or contemporary tactical gear.
- Neon cyberpunk palettes.
- Clean white digital backgrounds.
- Cute chibi anatomy or broad comedy.
- Ornamental borders so dense that the subject becomes secondary.
- Random pseudo-text as a dominant feature. Use symbols, numbering, and short approved labels.
- Open divine eyes in ordinary assets; an open eye is reserved for major narrative milestones.

**Palette ratio:** 45% ivory/vellum, 25% charcoal/teal, 15% environmental natural colors, 10% gold, no more than 5% crimson/violet accents.

## Line, Texture, and Form

- Fine black-brown ink rather than pure digital black.
- Crosshatching follows geological contours that secretly track musculature.
- Gold leaf may break line work but should never flood the image.
- Human equipment is angular, measured, and practical.
- Divine forms are sweeping, anatomical, and too large to comprehend at once.
- Biological textures should resemble weathered stone, bark, coral, parchment, or nacre.

## Composition by Asset Type

### Campaign Key Art

- 16:9 landscape.
- One expedition figure or small group in the lower third.
- A single impossible anatomical landmark dominates the middle distance.
- Clear negative space for title treatment.
- No manuscript border on cinematic store/hero versions; use faint corner illuminations instead.

### Locations

- 16:9 or 3:2 environmental plate.
- Wide establishing view with scale references.
- Optional slim illuminated frame and plate number.
- One anatomical clue must be visible but not necessarily explained.

### Characters

- 4:5 portrait or 2:3 full figure.
- Neutral vellum or environment-derived background.
- Botanical/anatomical border motifs tied to faction.
- Include one occupational prop and one sign of contact with Orison.

### Monsters

- 4:5 bestiary plate.
- Full silhouette visible.
- Side annotations may show detail studies, tracks, or internal structure.
- Present as observed organism, not a videogame splash screen.

### Items

- Square or 4:5 artifact study.
- Object centered with two or three detail callouts.
- Measurement marks, material notes, and provenance symbol.
- Soft cast shadow on vellum is acceptable; no modern product photography.

### Scenes

- 16:9 narrative illustration.
- Fewer decorative elements; emotion and action lead.
- Manuscript language survives through edge annotations and selective gold.

### Maps

- 3:2 or 4:3.
- Antique expedition cartography with anatomical correspondences.
- Multiple truth layers: public geography, expedition diagnosis, Scar-Speaker interpretation.
- Avoid perfect anatomical diagram clarity until late-campaign maps.

## Typography and Graphic Design

- Display titles: engraved Roman serif or restrained blackletter hybrid.
- Body copy: highly readable old-style serif.
- Field notes: humanist italic, never illegibly ornate.
- Labels: small caps with plate numbers.
- Gold rules and red rubrication may separate sections.
- Approved campaign mark: a closed eye whose lower lid becomes a coastline and whose tear becomes a compass needle.

## Canonical Image Prompt Skeleton

> A field plate from **The Godskin Atlas**, rendered as an illuminated anatomical expedition manuscript with the monumental atmospheric scale of a Romantic oil painting. Fine sepia ink, warm vellum, restrained gold-leaf ichor, divine ivory terrain, abyssal teal distance, storm-charcoal expedition figures, rare bruised-violet and arterial-crimson accents. Beautiful terrain that reveals anatomical structure only on closer inspection. Precise scale cues, scientific marginalia, weathered field-document texture. Awe before horror. No modern technology, no graphic gore, no neon, no watermark, no illegible blocks of text.

## Audio Direction

### Score

- Low strings, bass clarinet, frame drums, bowed metal, distant choral breath.
- Expedition themes use measured plucked strings and hand percussion.
- Orison’s motif is a slow five-note phrase synchronized to a heartbeat.
- Gold/ichor moments add glass harmonica and overtone singing.
- The Starved Thought removes expected notes rather than adding dissonant noise.

### Sound World

- Distant pulse mistaken for weather.
- Wind moving through follicle forests like bowed cables.
- Subsonic bone resonance.
- Cartographic tools, scratching pen nibs, canvas, rope, and boots.
- Silence immediately before major bodily movement.

## Voice Direction

**Narrator:** Educated expedition chronicler, measured and observant, gradually losing confidence in categorical language. Warm mid-register voice; reverence without melodrama.  
**Orison:** Layered voices at different ages and distances, calm rather than monstrous.  
**Starved Thought:** Intimate, dry, and nearly indistinguishable from the listener’s internal voice.

## Cinematic Milestones

1. The coastline bleeding beneath a survey hammer.
2. The Golden Wound lighting an entire valley.
3. Palm Desert folding while caravans race across its lines.
4. A fleet floating over the closed eye beneath the tear lake.
5. Ribcage Mountains rising during the first deep breath.
6. The party walking luminous nerve roads through darkness.
7. Orison’s eye opening for the first time.

## Approval Checklist

- Does the image read as beautiful terrain before anatomy?
- Is scale unmistakable?
- Is gold reserved for the divine?
- Does the asset feel documented inside the world?
- Is the subject usable at app-card size?
- Are anatomy and horror suggestive rather than gratuitous?
- Does it match the canonical palette and faction language?
