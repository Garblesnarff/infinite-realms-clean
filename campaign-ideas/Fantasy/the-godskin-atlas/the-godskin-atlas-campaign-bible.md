# Campaign Bible: The Godskin Atlas

## 1. DEEP LORE AND HISTORY

### Origin Myth: The Road That Remembered

Before mortals marked roads, Orison was the divine instinct to offer passage: the remembered trail home, the threshold opened to a refugee, the map drawn for someone who would never meet its maker. Orison accumulated every journey taken in hope of shelter. In sacred art the god appeared with closed eyes, because a true guide watches the road rather than the traveler.

During the Hunger War, an entity now called the Starved Thought learned to infect gods by inhabiting their sense of unfinished purpose. It whispered that no refuge was sufficient while anyone remained lost. Orison recognized that destroying the parasite would spread it, so the god folded it into a dream and sank beneath the western sea. Other gods erased Orison’s name to starve the Thought of routes back into collective memory.

The prison succeeded too well. Orison slept beyond worship, history, and eventually divine recollection. Mortals later settled on exposed portions of the sleeping body without understanding their foundation. When tectonic changes raised Orison from the sea, the world called the body a new continent.

### Timeline

- **Mythic Era:** Orison guides divine and mortal refugees between realms.
- **The Hunger War:** The Starved Thought infects multiple divine courts.
- **The Long Sleep:** Orison contains it and falls beneath the western sea.
- **Age of Scars:** Shipwrecked peoples establish the first settlements on healed wounds.
- **312 years ago:** Somatic cartography becomes a formal Scar-Speaker discipline.
- **87 years ago:** Church scholars rediscover references to “the Unwaking Road.”
- **23 years ago:** A secret royal expedition confirms divine anatomy; survivors are silenced.
- **6 months ago:** Orison rises above sea level following a deep reflex.
- **1 month ago:** Professor Ilyra Venn transmits the final warning.
- **Campaign start:** The rescue expedition makes landfall.

### Metaphysical Truth

Orison’s body and mind are not separate planes. Nerves carry both sensation and roads. Scars contain historical memory. Immune reactions are physical defenses and emotional boundaries. Dreams alter weather because divine imagination and material law overlap.

The Starved Thought is not hunger itself. It is the conviction that identity is a deficiency requiring completion. It grows whenever a person surrenders agency in exchange for a promised final answer.

## 2. FACTIONS

### Royal Survey Authority

**Leader:** Magistrate Oren Vale  
**Goal:** Establish legal and cartographic sovereignty.  
**Assets:** Survey corps, marines, printing presses, supply ships.  
**Public doctrine:** Accurate maps prevent chaos and save lives.  
**Secret:** The crown knew the continent was alive before dispatching Venn.  
**Internal fracture:** Field scientists want disclosure; claim officers want extraction rights.  
**Transformation:** Can become a transparent international survey, an occupying bureaucracy, or an evacuated archive.

### Scar-Speaker Confederacy

**Leader:** Mother Suture, advised by Nemea-of-the-Seam  
**Goal:** Maintain reciprocal coexistence with Orison.  
**Assets:** Safe routes, medical lore, tissue-grown architecture, community trust.  
**Doctrine:** “A wound is a history trying to close.”  
**Secret:** Their communion rituals cause Orison pain, though carefully controlled.  
**Internal fracture:** Isolationists would abandon newcomers to the fever.  
**Transformation:** Can become continental stewards, closed traditionalists, or teachers of a new somatic science.

### Church of the Unwaking

**Leader:** Abbess Merrow Ash  
**Field representative:** Brother Caldus Rook  
**Goal:** Preserve the sacred sleep.  
**Assets:** Pilgrims, reliquaries, lullaby rites, hidden church records.  
**Secret:** Its founder participated in erasing Orison’s identity.  
**Internal fracture:** Caretakers oppose those willing to kill every witness.  
**Transformation:** Can confess and become guardians, radicalize into euthanasia, or dissolve after restoring Orison’s name.

### Ichor Consortium

**Leader:** Director Cassian Auric  
**Field representative:** Tallow Reed  
**Goal:** Harvest divine substances at industrial scale.  
**Assets:** Capital, drills, alchemical laboratories, contracted guards.  
**Public doctrine:** One divine wound could cure a generation of mortal suffering.  
**Secret:** Executives extend their lives with unstable ichor therapy.  
**Internal fracture:** Physicians and workers increasingly oppose the directors.  
**Transformation:** Can become a regulated medical trust, a body-harvesting state, or a worker-controlled rescue guild.

### Somatic Chorus

**Leader:** Cantor Pell-of-Many  
**Goal:** Establish shared consciousness between Orison and willing mortals.  
**Assets:** Dream communication, collective memory, rapid coordination.  
**Doctrine:** Separation is the first wound.  
**Secret:** The Starved Thought can hide inside their longing for unity.  
**Internal fracture:** Consent-based communion versus forced assimilation.  
**Transformation:** Can become translators, a cultic hive, or a plural community with strong individual boundaries.

### Venn Expedition Remnants

**Leader:** No uncontested leader.  
**Groups:** The Holdfast, the Awakeners, the Marked.  
**Goal:** Rescue, revelation, or surrender to transformation.  
**Secret:** An unauthorized consortium test caused the Golden Wound and current fever.  
**Transformation:** Their fate determines public understanding of the expedition and whether Ilyra survives as an individual.

### The Starved Thought

**Presentation:** A private conclusion that feels self-generated.  
**Goal:** Make itself necessary to a sufficiently powerful host.  
**Power:** It simplifies choices by removing the emotional capacity to value alternatives.  
**Weakness:** Explicitly accepted incompleteness, mutual aid without erasure, and choices made without certainty.

## 3. NPC ROSTER

### Major NPC Profiles

#### Professor Ilyra Venn

**Role:** Missing expedition leader and primary mystery.  
**Appearance:** Weathered navy expedition coat threaded with living gold; one eye reflects map contours rather than light.  
**Desire:** Complete the first true map of Orison.  
**Fear:** That the map is using her to complete itself.  
**Contradiction:** Fiercely defends intellectual freedom while naming places without local consent.  
**Secret:** She allowed the initial ichor test because she believed one controlled wound would prove the body’s nature.  
**Voice:** Precise, fast, occasionally answering questions seconds before they are asked.

#### Captain Mara Quill

**Role:** Rescue commander and logistical anchor.  
**Appearance:** Salt-dark coat, brass prosthetic fingers, immaculate expedition ledger.  
**Desire:** Bring every recoverable person home.  
**Fear:** Becoming the kind of commander who reduces lives to acceptable losses.  
**Secret:** Her sealed orders prioritize Venn’s research over Venn herself.  
**Voice:** Short declarative sentences; names people instead of roles.

#### Nemea-of-the-Seam

**Role:** Scar-Speaker guide and diplomat.  
**Appearance:** Rose and ivory robes fastened with curved bone needles; ritual scars forming a topographic pattern.  
**Desire:** Force newcomers to recognize existing peoples and obligations.  
**Fear:** That negotiation legitimizes colonization.  
**Secret:** Nemea has heard Orison ask to wake.  
**Voice:** Uses healing metaphors and refuses possessive descriptions of land.

#### Brother Caldus Rook

**Role:** Priest, ethicist, and potential antagonist.  
**Appearance:** Ash-gray traveling habit, closed-eye reliquary, permanently ink-stained fingers.  
**Desire:** Honor Orison’s sacrifice.  
**Fear:** That his entire church is built upon cowardice disguised as reverence.  
**Secret:** His reliquary contains a fragment of Orison’s erased name.  
**Voice:** Gentle questions; becomes formal when frightened.

#### Magistrate Oren Vale

**Role:** Royal authority and political pressure.  
**Appearance:** Cream legal robes protected by a practical survey harness.  
**Desire:** Prevent war over Orison by establishing enforceable control first.  
**Fear:** An unclaimed god becoming a weapon for a rival empire.  
**Secret:** Vale’s child is dying from an illness ichor might cure.  
**Voice:** Legal clarity interrupted by rare flashes of desperation.

#### Doctor Sen Arclight

**Role:** Physician and ethical fault line.  
**Appearance:** Black field apron, gold-glass syringes, trembling luminous veins.  
**Desire:** Convert divine biology into reproducible medicine.  
**Fear:** Dying before proving the work can save others.  
**Secret:** Sen is terminally ill and is their own first long-term test subject.  
**Voice:** Calm bedside warmth paired with clinical euphemism.

#### The Somnambulist

**Role:** Orison’s communicative avatar.  
**Appearance:** A small masked traveler carrying an atlas with blank pages.  
**Desire:** Learn how mortals distinguish care from control.  
**Fear:** Waking as a catastrophe rather than a person.  
**Secret:** Every appearance is simultaneously real and dreamed.  
**Voice:** Simple questions with geological implications.

## 4. LOCATIONS

### Zone 1: Outer Skin

#### Base Camp Palimpsest

A layered canvas-and-timber settlement where every foundation uncovers remnants of an earlier camp. Official maps mark it as empty shore. At night the ground warms around sleeping inhabitants.

**Landmarks:** Vesper Bell dock, survey tower, sealed royal archive, field hospital.  
**Dormant:** Stable hub and faction crossroads.  
**Fevered:** Pore vents open and antibody scouts test the perimeter.  
**Waking:** The shoreline tilts toward the sea, forcing evacuation.

#### The Golden Wound

A canyon opened by an experimental drill. Luminous ichor climbs the walls in branching patterns and crystallizes in moonlight.

**Truth:** The wound crosses a sensory nerve.  
**Opportunity:** Medicine, power, communication.  
**Cost:** Every extraction is felt directly by Orison.

#### Forest of Follicles

Black pillar-trees sway without wind and retract beneath the ground during fever. Their root systems are symbiotic organisms that feed on salt and shed dream-pollen.

#### The Scar-Cities

Rose-colored settlements recessed into ancient healed ridges. Streets follow tension lines; architecture expands and contracts safely with the body.

### Zone 2: Handlands

#### Palm Desert

An ivory plateau crossed by canyon-deep palm lines. Static discharges foreshadow muscular contraction.

#### Nail Cliffs

Translucent layered escarpments catching sunrise like cathedral glass. Mining lower layers can expose the sensitive nail bed.

#### Pilgrim’s Print

A fingerprint whorl used as a ritual labyrinth. Completing it grants a shared dream of the first Scar-Speakers.

### Zone 3: Face of the Sleeper

#### Lake Beneath the Eyelid

A silver tear lake resting over a closed eye. Islands move as the eye shifts in dreams. Boats must avoid pupil-dark water visible beneath the surface.

#### Cathedral of the Broken Tooth

An enamel sanctuary whose chambers resonate with sung notes. The monks’ hymns suppress painful nerve signals.

#### Listening Ear

A spiral canyon where echoes from the past arrive according to emotional rather than chronological proximity.

### Zone 4: Ribcage

#### Ribcage Mountains

White ranges lifting on a twenty-minute cycle. Settlements use breath calendars rather than clocks.

#### Quiet Lung

A gray wetland that no longer rises. Violet lacunae spread through abandoned villages, leaving building-shaped absences.

#### Heartstorm

A gold-red storm orbiting the sternum. Lightning follows arterial routes and can carry travelers if safely grounded.

### Zone 5: Interior

#### Throat Gate

A vertical resonant passage. Lies cause constricting growth; true statements open temporary chambers but may expose private memories.

#### Nerve Roads

Gold filaments spanning darkness. Contact grants speed and involuntary perception of Orison’s sensations.

#### Archive of Reflexes

A city repeating defensive actions from the Hunger War. Visitors must interrupt cycles without being classified as threats.

#### Dreaming Brain

A changing metropolis where roads are memories and buildings are unresolved decisions. At its center is an empty plaza shaped like the thing Orison believes is missing.

## 5. QUEST ARCHITECTURE

### Main Quest: The Atlas and the Awakening

1. Land at the new shore and establish Base Camp.
2. Investigate the missing Venn Expedition.
3. Survive the first antibody response at the Golden Wound.
4. Earn passage through the Scar-Cities.
5. Recover three living map folios from changed regions.
6. Cross the Palm Desert before the hand contracts.
7. Diagnose the divine fever.
8. Reunite or judge the expedition remnants.
9. Reach the Lake Beneath the Eyelid and establish communication.
10. Choose a route through the Ribcage based on prior treatment of Orison.
11. Descend through Throat Gate.
12. Resist bargains offered along the Nerve Roads.
13. Separate Orison’s desire from the Starved Thought’s persuasion.
14. Present the Living Atlas as diagnosis, accusation, or gift.
15. Determine the fate of god, parasite, and inhabitants.

## 6. ITEM DATABASE

### Major Artifacts

1. **Atlas of Living Contours:** Updates when Orison’s body changes; annotations reveal the cartographer’s assumptions as visible distortions.
2. **Ichor Compass:** Points toward the strongest unmet biological need rather than north.
3. **Scar-Speaker’s Needle:** Can close a wound, stitch two routes together, or bind a memory temporarily.
4. **Closed-Eye Reliquary:** Suppresses one awakening event while erasing one true name written inside it.
5. **Somatic Tuning Fork:** Converts pulse, nerve activity, and pain into audible tones.
6. **Fossilized Tear:** Holds a complete divine emotion and releases it when broken.
7. **Venn’s Final Phonograph:** Records sounds not yet made and plays warnings from possible futures.
8. **The Unnamed Road:** A foldable map path capable of connecting two places that both offer refuge.

## 7. BESTIARY

### Gilded Phagocyte

Armored six-limbed immune hunter. It paints targets with golden light, allowing larger responses to locate them. Removing the mark without violence can convince it a threat has been resolved.

### Scar Shepherd

A broad, pale organism trailing fibrous threads. It heals damaged terrain rapidly but may seal people, buildings, or evidence inside new tissue.

### Pore Hound

Lean heat-sensing pack organism that emerges from vents during fever. It hunts inflammation rather than morality and may be redirected toward burning machinery.

### Nerve Kite

A translucent ray traveling along electrical discharges. Touch shares distant sensations and may overload spellcasting.

### Ossuary Mite

Enamel-plated scavenger feeding on damaged tooth and bone. Colonies create resonant tunnel complexes.

### Dream Hart

A many-antlered animal reconstructed from Orison’s memory of an extinct species. Following it leads toward refuge but away from current objectives.

### Violet Lacuna

An absence with a creature’s outline. It removes properties rather than matter: weight from a bridge, warmth from a body, affection from a memory.

### Marked Expeditioner

A survivor partially converted into immune tissue. Retains personality between defensive reflexes and may be rescued through recognition rather than damage.

## 8. CUSTOM MECHANICS

### Awakening Clock

Advance the clock for severe injury, mass extraction, failed regional crises, and certain revelations. Reduce or redirect it through healing, consent-based communion, or reinforcement of the seal. Never hide why the clock changed.

### Somatic Reputation

Record major acts under Symbiote, Irritant, Physician, Parasite, or Unknown. At the end of each act, Orison responds according to the dominant classification.

### Living Atlas Folios

Each zone awards a folio after the party records:

- **Route:** How to cross it.
- **Diagnosis:** What the body needs.
- **Claim:** Who believes they control it.
- **Memory:** What Orison remembers there.

Missing entries become complications during the final return journey.

### Ichor Resonance

| Level | Benefit | Cost |
|---:|---|---|

| 2 | Briefly accelerate healing or movement | Visible gold veins; antibodies notice |
| 3 | Access one memory stored in tissue | Lose certainty about one personal memory |
| 4 | Communicate directly with Orison | Orison can speak through the character |
| 5 | Reshape living terrain | Risk permanent assimilation |
