# Cartel Wars: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Land of Coffee and Gold was once a paradise, a place where the mountains touched the sky and the rivers ran clear. But then came the 'White Gold'—cocaine. It didn't arrive as a curse; it arrived as a promise of prosperity for a people tired of poverty. The Cartels didn't just build empires of dust; they built a new reality. They became the shadow-government, the Robin Hoods of the slums, and the monsters of the jungle. The 'Wars' aren't just about territory; they're about the soul of a nation. It's a world where a man's word is his bond, but his life is worth less than a kilo of powder. The only gods here are Plata o Plomo—Silver or Lead.

### Timeline
1.  **The Coffee Boom (1950s):** The nation grows fat on legal exports, creating a class of wealthy landowners and a desperate underclass.
2.  **The Rise of the Patron (1975):** Pablo, a small-time smuggler, realizes the potential of the US market for cocaine. He forms the Medellín Cartel.
3.  **The Robin Hood Era (1980):** Pablo begins building schools and housing for the poor, buying their loyalty and creating a 'Slum Army.'
4.  **The Arrival of the DEA (1982):** American agents arrive, bringing high-tech surveillance and a deep cynicism to the jungle.
5.  **The Palace of Justice Siege (1985):** A guerrilla group, funded by the Medellín Cartel, seizes the Supreme Court to destroy evidence. The ensuing battle leaves hundreds dead.
6.  **The Rise of Cali (1988):** The Cali Cartel, more businesslike and discreet, begins to challenge Medellín's dominance. 'Gentlemen prefer Cali' becomes their motto.
7.  **The Narco-Terrorism War (1989-1991):** A period of intense bombings and assassinations as Pablo declares war on the state.
8.  **The Prison of Gold (1992):** Pablo 'surrenders' and is allowed to build his own luxury prison, 'La Catedral,' from which he continues to run his empire.
9.  **The Present Day:** Pablo has escaped. The DEA, the Cali Cartel, and a group of vigilantes known as 'Los Pepes' are all hunting him. The final showdown on the rooftops is imminent.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the corrupting nature of power and the illusion of morality in a world of absolute greed. It is a battle between three versions of 'Order': the brutal populism of Pablo, the cold-blooded capitalism of Cali, and the cynical interventionism of the DEA. The players are the ones caught in the middle—agents who have lost their way, sicarios who have found their conscience, or civilians just trying to survive. The 'Why' is the question: 'How much blood are you willing to step over to get what you want?' It explores the beauty of the landscape versus the horror of the violence and the tragedy of a nation being torn apart by its own success.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Medellín Cartel (The Kings of Chaos)
**Type:** Populist Criminal Empire
**Leader:** Pablo (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Infinite wealth, a loyal army of sicarios, and the support of the slums.
**Rivals:** The Cali Cartel, The DEA, The National Police.

### The Cali Cartel (The Gentlemen)
**Type:** Corporate Criminal Syndicate
**Leader:** The Rodriguez Brothers (Tier 1 NPCs).
**Asset:** Political influence, high-tech surveillance, and a 'clean' business image.
**Rivals:** The Medellín Cartel, The DEA (unofficially).

### The DEA (The Outsiders)
**Type:** Foreign Law Enforcement (Cynical)
**Leader:** Agent Murphy and Agent Peña (Tier 1 NPCs).
**Asset:** Advanced surveillance technology, US funding, and a willingness to break the rules to get the 'Big Fish.'
**Rivals:** Both Cartels, the corrupt elements of the National Police.

### The National Police (The Compromised)
**Type:** Local Law Enforcement (Divided)
**Leader:** Colonel Carrillo (A man of incorruptible violence).
**Asset:** Legal authority, military-grade hardware, and a handful of officers who haven't been bought.
**Rivals:** The Cartels, internal traitors.

### Los Pepes (The Vigilantes)
**Type:** Shadow-Assassin Group
**Leader:** Unknown (secretly funded by Cali and the DEA).
**Asset:** Secrecy and a brutal focus on destroying everything Pablo loves.
**Rivals:** The Medellín Cartel.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Pablo** (Criminal - Charismatic Leader) - The Patron of Medellín. **Voice:** Charming, terrifying, and deeply manipulative. **Goal:** To be recognized as the legitimate leader of his people. **Secret:** He is terrified of extradition and will destroy the entire country to prevent it.
**Agent Murphy** (DEA - Cynical Investigator) - The American outsider. **Voice:** Increasingly hardened and world-weary. **Goal:** To capture Pablo and go home with his soul intact. **Secret:** He has begun to use the same brutal methods as the people he's hunting.
**Gilberto Rodriguez** (Criminal - Corporate Boss) - The leader of Cali. **Voice:** Businesslike, cold, and calculated. **Goal:** To turn the drug trade into a legitimate global corporation. **Secret:** He views Pablo as an 'embarrassment' to the profession.
**Colonel Carrillo** (Law Enforcement - Brutal Soldier) - The only honest man in the police. **Voice:** Intense, disciplined, and utterly without mercy. **Goal:** To kill every sicario in the country. **Secret:** He knows he will never survive this war and has accepted his own death.
**Teresa** (Independent - Family Matriarch) - Pablo's wife. **Voice:** Soft, devoted, and blind to the horror. **Goal:** To keep her family safe and together. **Secret:** She knows exactly where the bodies are buried but chooses to see only the gold.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Medellín Slums
A colorful, overcrowded labyrinth of brick houses and loyalty.
*   **The Barrio Pablo Escobar:** A housing project built by the Patron. **Smell:** Charcoal smoke and hope. **Sound:** Children playing and the constant buzz of radios.
*   **The Overlook:** A hill where Pablo sits to watch 'his' city. **Visual:** Thousands of lights looking like a field of gold.

### The Jungle Lab
A hidden, high-tech factory in the heart of the rainforest.
*   **The Processing Shack:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the 'White Gold' is made. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.
*   **The Perimeter:** A deadly maze of traps and sicario patrols. **Visual:** Jungle green hiding military khaki.

### The Cali Social Club
A monument to corporate criminal luxury.
*   **The Ballroom:** Crystal chandeliers, expensive salsa bands, and 'Gentlemen' making deals. **Smell:** High-end cologne and Caribbean blue sea air.
*   **The Surveillance Room:** A hidden chamber where Cali listens to every phone in the country.

### The Palace of Justice
A grand, art deco symbol of the law that became a tomb.
*   **The Great Hall:** Site of the final standoff during the siege. **Visual:** Bullet holes in the marble and the smell of cordite and old paper.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **Silver or Lead:** Your first day on the job. You are approached by a Medellín sicario with a bribe. Do you take the silver or risk the lead?
2.  **The Jungle Heist:** Infiltrate a Medellín jungle lab to retrieve a ledger of political bribes. The lab is guarded by 'Popeye.'
3.  **A Night in Cali:** Attend a Cali Social Club gala undercover. You must identify the 'Gentlemen's' connection to the Ministry of Justice.
4.  **The Palace Siege:** You are caught inside the Palace of Justice when the guerrillas attack. You must destroy the evidence against the Cartel before the army arrives.
5.  **The Prison Break:** Pablo has 'escaped' from La Catedral. You must track him through the mountains before he reaches the safety of the slums.
6.  **The Los Pepes Gamble:** You are approached by a mysterious group offering to help you kill Pablo. Do you join the vigilantes or maintain your 'DEA' principles?
7.  **The Rooftop Finale:** The final hunt for Pablo in Medellín. A chaotic, three-way battle between the DEA, the National Police, and the Cartel remnants on the rain-slicked rooftops.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. Pablo's Golden Luger
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Pistol)
**Description:** A custom-made, gold-plated pistol with 'El Patron' engraved on the handle.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks and deals an extra 2d6 'Legendary' damage to any government official.

### 2. The Cali Wiretap
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A suitcase-sized device that can intercept any radio or telephone signal in the city.
**Mechanics:** Once per day, the user can ask the DM a question about a target's current location or their most recent conversation.

### 3. Carrillo's Beret
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A blood-stained green beret that represents the incorruptible will of the National Police.
**Mechanics:** Makes the wearer immune to being charmed or frightened and grants a +2 bonus to AC in urban environments.
