# Central City Noir: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
Central City wasn't built; it was spawned. It is a concrete labyrinth where the American Dream went to die and was reborn as a beautiful, rain-slicked nightmare. There are no ancient gods here, only the twin deities of Greed and Survival. The city's 'soul' is a collection of every whispered secret, every back-alley deal, and every drop of rain that has ever hit the pavement. It is a place where the architecture is as sharp as a switchblade and the shadows are deep enough to hide an army of ghosts. Heroism in Central City isn't about saving the world; it's about holding back the dark for one more night, knowing that the dawn will only reveal a new set of tragedies.

### Timeline
1.  **The Founding (1890s):** Central City is established as a rail hub, built on the promise of industry. The first art deco skyscrapers begin to rise, symbols of an ambition that would soon turn sour.
2.  **The Great Fog (1912):** A month-long smog blankets the city, during which the first major criminal syndicates consolidate power under the cover of darkness.
3.  **The Volstead Era (1920s):** Prohibition turns the city's alleyways into blood-soaked boardrooms. The underworld becomes more organized than the police.
4.  **The Great Crash (1929):** The economy collapses, but crime pays better than ever. The disparity between the corrupt opulence of the elite and the squalor of the working class becomes a chasm.
5.  **The Rise of the Spirit (5 Years Ago):** A young detective, believed killed in the line of duty, 'returns' as the Spirit—a masked vigilante who operates from the shadows of Wildwood Cemetery.
6.  **The Election of 1942:** Commissioner Dolan takes office, vowing to clean up the force, but finds himself increasingly reliant on the city's masked protector.
7.  **The Night of the Long Knives (3 Years Ago):** A massive gang war erupts, leaving several crime bosses dead and creating a power vacuum that a new breed of 'elegant' mobsters quickly fills.
8.  **The Whispering Rain (1 Year Ago):** A local legend begins about the rain itself—that it only falls when the city is about to witness a great sin.
9.  **The Present Day:** The rain hasn't stopped in three days. The crime bosses are planning something big, and the Spirit has seen a shadow moving in the cemetery that shouldn't be there.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for the city's identity. Is Central City a lost cause, a monument to human corruption, or is it a place worth saving? The 'Why' is the question of why anyone bothers to be good in a city that rewards the wicked. It explores the blurred line between justice and vengeance, and the weight of wearing a mask in a world where everyone is hiding behind a facade of respectability. The players are the few who still believe in the city, or at least, the few who hate the alternative more.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Central City Police Department (CCPD)
**Type:** Law Enforcement (Corrupt)
**Leader:** Commissioner Dolan (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** The law, a fleet of rumpled squad cars, and a handful of detectives who haven't been bought yet.
**Rivals:** The Underworld Syndicates, The Spirit (officially).

### The Spirit's Network
**Type:** Vigilante Support Group
**Leader:** The Spirit (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Secrecy, Wildwood Cemetery hideout, and a network of street-level informants (Adepts).
**Rivals:** Every criminal in the city, the CCPD (unofficially).

### The Silk Tie Syndicate
**Type:** Elegant/High-Class Organized Crime
**Leader:** 'Elegant' Eddie, a man who views murder as a fine art.
**Asset:** Financial control of the city's clubs, 'clean' business fronts, and hired femme fatales.
**Rivals:** The Spirit, The Waterfront Gang.

### The Waterfront Gang
**Type:** Street-Level/Brutal Organized Crime
**Leader:** 'Iron' Mike, a former dockworker with a personal grudge against the Spirit.
**Asset:** Control of the docks, smuggling routes, and a small army of street thugs.
**Rivals:** The Silk Tie Syndicate, The Spirit.

### The Wildwood Cemetery Hermits
**Type:** Information Brokers/Outcasts
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Knowledge of the city's secret history and the ability to disappear into the cemetery's gothic labyrinth.
**Rivals:** None (they are too small to care about).

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**The Spirit** (Vigilante - Fighter/Detective) - Masked hero in a blue suit and red tie. **Voice:** Hard-boiled, cynical but deeply moral. **Goal:** To protect Central City from the shadows. **Secret:** He is technically legally dead, and his base of operations is a tomb in Wildwood Cemetery.
**Ellen Dolan** (Independent - Journalist/Socialite) - Daughter of the Commissioner. **Voice:** Smart, practical, and fearless. **Goal:** To expose the city's corruption through her writing. **Secret:** She knows the Spirit's true identity and is his most trusted ally.
**Commissioner Dolan** (Law Enforcement - Veteran Detective) - A man tired of the city's filth. **Voice:** Gruff, weary, and prone to coughing fits from too many cheap cigars. **Goal:** To clean up the CCPD, even if it kills him. **Secret:** He secretly leaves files for the Spirit on his desk, knowing they'll be 'stolen.'
**Femme Fatale (P'Gell)** (Criminal - Socialite/Infiltrator) - Dangerous, beautiful, and utterly ruthless. **Voice:** Seductive, intelligent, and wary. **Goal:** To manipulate the city's power players for her own gain. **Secret:** She actually loves the Spirit, but her ambition is stronger than her heart.
**'Elegant' Eddie** (Criminal - Mob Boss) - A man who never gets his hands dirty. **Voice:** Soft, cultured, and terrifyingly polite. **Goal:** To become the 'shadow mayor' of Central City. **Secret:** He has a crippling phobia of the rain, which is why he's rarely seen outside.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Wildwood Cemetery
A gothic, atmospheric labyrinth of crumbling mausoleums and weeping statues.
*   **The Spirit's Tomb:** A hidden base of operations beneath a family crypt. **Smell:** Damp earth and old stone. **Sound:** The constant drip of water.
*   **The Gatekeeper's Hut:** A small, ramshackle building near the entrance. **Visual:** Always surrounded by a thick, unnatural fog.

### Central City Police Headquarters
A grim, art deco fortress of bureaucracy and cigarette smoke.
*   **Commissioner Dolan's Office:** A cluttered room filled with files and the smell of stale coffee. **Visual:** A single window overlooking the rain-slicked streets.
*   **The Bullpen:** A chaotic room of ringing phones and shouting detectives. **Smell:** Ink and sweat.

### The Blue Note Jazz Club
A smoky, high-end club where the city's elite and its criminals mingle.
*   **The VIP Lounge:** A dark, velvet-lined room where deals are made. **Sound:** Smoky saxophone music and the clink of glasses.
*   **The Back Alley:** A dangerous, rain-slicked shortcut where many a secret has been buried.

### Wildwood Rooftops
The soaring, art deco heights where the Spirit and his rivals play a deadly game of cat and mouse.
*   **The Elevated Train Tracks:** A dangerous, high-speed path through the city's heart. **Sound:** The rhythmic roar of the train.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **A Night in the Rain:** Investigate a series of murders in Wildwood Cemetery. Discover a clue that points to a new criminal syndicate.
2.  **The Commissioner's Dilemma:** Dolan is being blackmailed by 'Elegant' Eddie. The Spirit must retrieve the evidence before the Commissioner is forced to resign.
3.  **The Femme Fatale's Gambit:** P'Gell 'asks' for the Spirit's help in escaping a hit squad sent by a rival boss. Is it a trap, or is she actually in danger?
4.  **The Dockside Heist:** A shipment of illegal weapons is arriving at the docks. The Spirit must stop the transfer and find out who the buyer is.
5.  **The Secret of the Tomb:** A group of tomb raiders breaks into Wildwood Cemetery, threatening the Spirit's secret base.
6.  **The Mayor's Mask:** Expose the corruption at City Hall during a high-society masquerade ball.
7.  **The Final Confrontation:** 'Elegant' Eddie and 'Iron' Mike have formed an uneasy alliance to take down the Spirit once and for all. The battle culminates on the rain-slicked rooftops during the storm of the century.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Spirit's Mask
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A simple, blue domino mask that seems to meld with the wearer's face.
**Mechanics:** Grants advantage on Stealth checks and makes the wearer immune to being recognized by anyone who doesn't already know their true identity.

### 2. Dolan's Lucky Cigar Case
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A rumpled, silver case that has seen better days.
**Mechanics:** Once per day, the wearer can re-roll a failed Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check.

### 3. P'Gell's Perfume
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A small, elegant vial of a scent that is both dangerous and alluring.
**Mechanics:** The wearer can use an action to release the scent. All creatures within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom save or be charmed for 1 minute.
