# Can You Dig It?: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The City is a beast that feeds on its own. It's a concrete jungle where the only law is territory and the only currency is respect. Long ago, it's said the gangs were just tribes, protecting their blocks from the outside world. But the City changed—it became a labyrinth of flickering fluorescent lights, screeching subway trains, and shadows that breathe. The 'Great Summit' wasn't just a meeting; it was a religious gathering, an attempt to unify the tribes into one unstoppable army. But when the prophet Cyrus fell, the City's soul fractured. Now, the journey home isn't just a walk through hostile turf; it's a trial by fire, a mythic quest where every alleyway is a gauntlet and every train ride is a gamble with fate.

### Timeline
1.  **The Tribal Era (1960s):** Small, neighborhood-based gangs protect their blocks. The 'Colors' are born as symbols of identity.
2.  **The Subway Expansion (1972):** The iron veins of the City are completed, allowing gangs to travel and clash in new territories. The 'Colors' become recognized city-wide.
3.  **The Rise of Cyrus (1975):** A visionary leader from the Gramercy Riffs begins to speak of 'One City, One Army.' He gains a cult-like following.
4.  **The Day of the Truce (1978):** A city-wide truce is called for the first time. The violence drops by 80% as everyone waits for Cyrus's next move.
5.  **The Great Summit (The Present - Nightfall):** 100 gangs meet at Van Cortlandt Park. Cyrus is assassinated. The 'Warriors' are framed for the murder.
6.  **The Gauntlet Begins (The Present - Midnight):** Every gang in the City is given the order to hunt down the Warriors. The subway lines are sabotaged.
7.  **The Riverside Clash (The Present - 2 AM):** The Warriors face the Baseball Furies in their own turf. The 'Colors' of the Warriors are stained but not broken.
8.  **The Union Square Standoff (The Present - 4 AM):** A three-way confrontation between the Warriors, the Punks, and the Lizzies. The iron veins of the City become a blood-soaked arena.
9.  **The Dawn at Coney (The Present - 6 AM):** The Warriors reach their home turf. The truth is revealed, and the City witnesses the birth of a new urban legend.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for survival and the preservation of brotherhood in a world that has turned against you. It is about the mythic journey of the 'Outcast'—can you make it back to where you belong when everyone wants you dead? The 'Why' is the question: 'What defines a Warrior when their back is against the wall?' It explores the theatricality of urban warfare, the weight of a false accusation, and the power of a legend being born through one impossible night. The players are the few who still believe in their Colors, and they'll do anything to keep them flying.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Warriors (The Outcasts)
**Type:** Street-Level Survivalist Gang
**Leader:** Cleon (initially), then Swan (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Brown leather vests, red trim, mastery of urban navigation, and unbreakable brotherhood.
**Rivals:** Every other gang in the City.

### The Gramercy Riffs (The Enforcers)
**Type:** Elite/Disciplined Urban Army
**Leader:** Cyrus (initially), then Masai.
**Asset:** Numbers, discipline, and total control of the City's communication network.
**Rivals:** The Rogues (the true killers).

### The Rogues (The Killers)
**Type:** Psychotic/Professional Chaos Gang
**Leader:** Luther (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Black leather, an addiction to violence, and the 'clinking bottles' taunt.
**Rivals:** The Warriors, The Riffs.

### The Baseball Furies (The Theatricals)
**Type:** Coordinated/Silent Combat Gang
**Leader:** None (they operate as a hive-mind).
**Asset:** Baseball uniforms, face paint, wooden bats, and terrifying silence.
**Rivals:** Anyone who enters Riverside Park.

### The Lizzies (The Seductives)
**Type:** All-Female Infiltration/Ambush Gang
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Seduction, hidden weapons, and a turf that is impossible to navigate without their permission.
**Rivals:** The Warriors.

### The Punks (The Chaotics)
**Type:** Mobile/Roller-Skating Chaos Gang
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Roller skates, hockey sticks, and the ability to strike and disappear in seconds.
**Rivals:** Anyone in the Subway tunnels.

### The Orphans (The Wannabes)
**Type:** Low-Tier/Desperate Neighborhood Gang
**Leader:** Sully.
**Asset:** Numbers and a deep-seated need for recognition.
**Rivals:** The Warriors (who they see as 'easy prey').

### The Turnbull ACs (The Brutals)
**Type:** Heavy-Duty/Skinhead Gang
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** A customized bus and a total disregard for human life.
**Rivals:** Anyone on the streets of the Bronx.

### The Boppers (The Stylists)
**Type:** High-Fashion/Smooth-Talking Gang
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Satin vests, fedoras, and a network of information brokers.
**Rivals:** The Warriors.

### The Hi-Hats (The Mimes)
**Type:** Silent/Theatrical Fear Gang
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Top hats, mime makeup, and the ability to move through the City without making a sound.
**Rivals:** None (they are too weird to attack).

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Swan** (Warrior - Fighter/Leader) - The reluctant war chief of the Warriors. **Voice:** Cool, analytical, and street-smart. **Goal:** To get his brothers back to Coney Island in one piece. **Secret:** He hates the 'Colors' and wants to leave the gang life behind after tonight.
**Luther** (Rogue - Psychotic/Killer) - The man who killed Cyrus. **Voice:** High-pitched, taunting, and utterly insane. **Goal:** To watch the City burn and watch the Warriors take the fall. **Secret:** He works for a high-ranking official who wants the gangs to destroy each other.
**The DJ** (Independent - Media Personality) - The omniscient voice of the City. **Voice:** Smooth, calm, and mysterious. **Goal:** To narrate the night's events and give the 'boppers' the latest bulletins. **Secret:** She is actually a high-level Adept who can see everything that happens through the City's shadows.
**Cyrus** (Riff - Visionary Leader) - The prophet of the gangs. **Voice:** Booming, charismatic, and filled with a grand vision. **Goal:** To unite the gangs into one unstoppable force. **Secret:** He was already dying of a terminal illness and viewed the Summit as his final performance.
**Mercy** (Independent - Survivor) - A woman caught in the crossfire. **Voice:** Tough, sarcastic, and world-weary. **Goal:** To find someone who can get her out of this dump. **Secret:** She was once a member of the Lizzies but left after seeing too much blood.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Van Cortlandt Park
The site of the Great Summit and the birth of the nightmare.
*   **The Podium:** A massive, natural rock formation where Cyrus gave his final speech. **Smell:** Night air and woodsmoke. **Sound:** The rhythmic chanting of 1,000 gang members.
*   **The Clearing:** A wide-open field that became a chaotic battlefield after the shooting. **Visual:** Discarded 'Colors' and broken bottles.

### The Subway Iron Veins
The dangerous, graffiti-covered lifelines of the City.
*   **The Moving Train:** A cramped, flickering arena of close-quarters combat. **Sound:** The screech of metal on metal and the heartbeat of the tracks.
*   **Union Square Station:** A massive, multi-level labyrinth of tunnels and platforms. **Visual:** Shadows that move faster than the trains.

### Riverside Park
The dark, wooded turf of the Baseball Furies.
*   **The Promenade:** A long, narrow path where the Furies hunt in formation. **Smell:** Damp grass and sweat. **Sound:** The 'clack' of wooden bats on the pavement.
*   **The Overlook:** A dark, quiet spot where the Warriors had their first moment of rest.

### Coney Island
The home of the Warriors and the end of the journey.
*   **The Boardwalk:** A long, wooden path that leads to the ocean at dawn. **Smell:** Salt air and fried dough. **Sound:** The roar of the ocean and the distant sound of a carousel.
*   **The Beach:** A wide-open expanse of sand where the final truth is revealed. **Visual:** The sun rising over the water, turning the sky orange and pink.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Summit:** Attend the Great Summit. Survive the chaos after Cyrus is shot and make your escape from Van Cortlandt Park.
2.  **The Bronx Gauntlet:** Cross through the Turnbull AC turf. Use the subway or your wits to avoid their customized bus.
3.  **The Orphan's Pride:** Deal with the Orphans. Choose whether to fight them or negotiate your way through their pathetic turf.
4.  **The Iron Vein Duel:** Survive a fight against the Punks on a moving subway train. The tracks are sabotaged, so you must get out before the train crashes.
5.  **The Riverside Shadows:** Face the Baseball Furies in Riverside Park. Survive their coordinated attack and make it to the nearest station.
6.  **The Lizzie's Trap:** Escape an ambush by the Lizzies in their private club. The club is a maze of velvet and death.
7.  **The Dawn at Coney:** Reach Coney Island at dawn. Face the Rogues and Luther in a final standoff on the beach. Reveal the truth to the Gramercy Riffs.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Warriors' Colors
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Vest)
**Description:** A brown leather vest with red trim and the 'Warriors' logo embroidered on the back.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer advantage on all Charisma (Persuasion) checks with fellow Warriors and makes them immune to being frightened as long as they are within 10 feet of an ally.

### 2. Luther's Clinking Bottles
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** Three empty glass bottles that make a chilling 'clink' when struck together.
**Mechanics:** The user can use an action to strike the bottles. All enemies within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom save or be frightened for 1 minute.

### 3. The DJ's Microphone
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A large, silver microphone from a dimly lit radio studio.
**Mechanics:** The user can use an action to speak into the microphone. Their voice is heard by everyone in the City who is currently tuned into a radio or near a shadow.
