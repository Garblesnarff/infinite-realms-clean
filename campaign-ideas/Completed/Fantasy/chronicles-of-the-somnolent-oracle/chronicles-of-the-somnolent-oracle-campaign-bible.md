**System:** 5e Compatible / Custom Narrative Systems

**Tone:** *Minority Report* meets *Salvador Dali* inside a lucid nightmare.

## 1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY
`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`

### The Origin: The Parasite of Linearity
In the Beginning, there was only the **Eternal Now**. Existence was a singular, perfect diamond of simultaneous reality where birth and death were the same facet. Then came the **Tick**. A cosmic parasite that introduced the concept of "Sequence." It forced the universe to stretch into a line, rotting the Now into the Past and pushing the fresh potential into the Future. The **Somnolent Oracles** are not merely seers; they are the immune system of the universe, fever-dreaming in an attempt to reject the infection of Linear Time and return to the Eternal Now. The conflict is not just about saving lives; it is about whether Time itself is a natural law or a terminal disease.

### The Timeline of Scars
1.  **The First Tock (Year 0):** The universe fractured. The first sun rose, and for the first time, entities felt the horror of "waiting."
2.  **The War of the Second-Hand (Year 300):** Mages attempted to reverse the flow. The backlash caused the **Great Stutter**, where everyone in the world repeated the same sentence for three years.
3.  **The Birth of Cassandra Prime (Year 890):** The first Oracle slept for a decade. When she woke, she screamed for six days, creating the **Canyon of Echoes** through sonic force alone.
4.  **The Year of the Backward Rain (Year 1002):** Precipitation fell upward from the earth to the clouds. Crops drowned in the soil; birds evolved gills.
5.  **The Silence of the Canaries (Year 1150):** Every bird in the kingdom spoke a prophecy of a specific citizen's death at noon, then spontaneously combusted at 12:01.
6.  **The Treaty of Tomorrow (Year 1200):** A peace treaty was signed by three kings who had not yet been born. Their signatures appeared on the parchment 40 years before their conception.
7.  **The Invention of the Forget-Me-Knot (Year 1350):** A rogue Chronomancer created a knot that, when tied, erased a memory from the collective unconscious. He tied himself out of existence.
8.  **The Day the Shadows Detached (Year 1402):** For 24 hours, shadows acted out the observers' future crimes. The crime rate dropped to zero for a decade out of sheer terror.
9.  **The Inevitability’s First Hunt (Year 1499):** An entire city state, *Ouroboros*, vanished. Maps now show a blank spot that causes nosebleeds when looked at directly.
10. **The Present Day (Year 1500):** The waking world feels "thin." Déjà vu is becoming a plague. The Oracles are dreaming louder.

### The Metaphysical Truth
The "Inevitability" is not a guardian. It is the calcification of the timeline. If it wins, the universe becomes a script with no improvisation—a dead, crystalline history book. If the Oracles change too much, the universe dissolves into chaotic noise. The party walks the razor's edge between **Stagnation** and **Entropy**.

## 2. FACTIONS
`[TAG: FACTION_DATA]`

### [The Circle of Seers]
**Type:** Prophetic Mutual Aid Network
**Leader:** Grandmother Oracle (A construct made of porcelain and written regrets).
**Asset:** *The Sleep-Tanks* – Sensory deprivation chambers that amplify visions.
**Rivals:** The Inevitability, The Architects of Inertia.

### [The Inevitability]
**Type:** Cosmic Antibodies / Temporal Enforcers
**Leader:** The First Paradox (Identity Unknown).
**Asset:** *Causality Anchors* – Pylons that prevent teleportation or time manipulation in a 10-mile radius.
**Rivals:** All Oracles, The Paradox Zone inhabitants.

### [The Architects of Inertia]
**Type:** Nihilistic Bureaucracy
**Leader:** High Auditor Kaelen (A man who moves so slowly he appears invisible to the naked eye).
**Asset:** *Stasis Ink* – Anything written with this cannot be changed or destroyed.
**Rivals:** The Circle of Seers.
**Goal:** To stop all change. To make today last forever.

### [The Society of Weeping Spoons]
**Type:** Absurdist Rebels
**Leader:** The Jester of Yesterday.
**Asset:** *Improbable Luck* – They worship the chaos of the timeline. Their headquarters moves location every time someone sneezes.
**Rivals:** The Inevitability.
**Goal:** Disrupt fixed points through sheer nonsense to prove free will exists.

### [The Mnemosyne Cartel]
**Type:** Memory Traffickers
**Leader:** "The Siphon" (A vampire who drinks nostalgia instead of blood).
**Asset:** *Bottled Moments* – Vials containing first kisses, last breaths, and forgotten passwords.
**Rivals:** The Circle of Seers.
**Goal:** Harvest the memories of erased timelines to sell to the rich.

### [The Anachronistic Botanists]
**Type:** Eco-Terrorist Druids
**Leader:** Spore-Mother Vyla.
**Asset:** *Retro-Flora* – Plants that bloom in the past when you water them in the present.
**Rivals:** Industrialists.
**Goal:** To overgrow the cities with prehistoric jungles by planting seeds yesterday.

### [The Echo-Eaters]
**Type:** Cult of Entropy
**Leader:** The Mouthless One.
**Asset:** *Sonic Voids* – Areas of absolute silence where prophecy cannot be heard.
**Rivals:** Anyone who speaks.
**Goal:** To consume the "noise" of the future so only the silence of the void remains.

### [The Déjà Vu Duelists]
**Type:** Assassin Guild
**Leader:** Madame Twice.
**Asset:** *Pre-Cognitive Parry* – A fighting style that blocks attacks before the enemy decides to strike.
**Rivals:** The City Guard.
**Goal:** Perfect combat through temporal cheating.

### [The Archivists of Never-Was]
**Type:** Sad Historians
**Leader:** The Curator (A ghost who haunts a library that doesn't exist yet).
**Asset:** *The Blank Books* – Tomes that record timelines the party destroys.
**Rivals:** None (they are too pity-inducing).
**Goal:** To remember the people erased by the Oracles' choices.

### [The Order of the Palindrome]
**Type:** Religious Zealots
**Leader:** Hannah (Named because it reads the same backward).
**Asset:** *Reversal Rites* – Rituals that heal wounds by rewinding the injury.
**Rivals:** The Inevitability (philosophical differences).
**Goal:** To live life backward from death to birth, believing it is the only way to be pure.

## 3. NPC ROSTER
`[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]`

1.  **Grandmother Oracle** (Construct Warlock) - A sentient porcelain doll inhabited by the spirits of dead seers.
    *   **Voice:** Multiple voices overlapping, creating a chord. Resonant, metallic.
    *   **Goal:** Preserve the lineage of Oracles.
    *   **Secret:** She is the vessel for the party’s future souls if they fail.
2.  **Cassandra Prime** (Human Bard) - The original seer, kept alive by spite. Blindfolded with bandages soaked in golden blood.
    *   **Voice:** Raspy, chain-smoker, cynical, frequently interrupts herself.
    *   **Goal:** To die permanently.
    *   **Secret:** She invented the Inevitability to stop herself from dreaming.
3.  **Paradox the Chronomancer** (Elf Wizard) - Wears robes made of clocks. Constantly vibrating.
    *   **Voice:** Fast-paced, manic, breathless, like an auctioneer on caffeine.
    *   **Goal:** To date his past self.
    *   **Secret:** He is already dead; he’s just a time-echo refusing to fade.
4.  **The Inevitability (Avatar)** (Celestial Paladin) - A faceless knight in armor that reflects the viewer's corpse.
    *   **Voice:** Recorded backwards and played forwards. Slurring, unnatural intonation.
    *   **Goal:** Enforce the Fixed Points.
    *   **Secret:** It acts out of love, trying to save the universe from heat death.
5.  **Chronos** (Deity/NPC) - Appears as a child, adult, and elder shifting every blink.
    *   **Voice:** Childlike sweetness mixed with ancient weariness. Monotone.
    *   **Goal:** To find a successor.
    *   **Secret:** He has forgotten his real name.
6.  **The Butterfly** (Halfling Commoner) - A small child with a limp.
    *   **Voice:** Tiny, shy, high-pitched, frequent sniffles.
    *   **Goal:** To catch a frog.
    *   **Secret:** Their heartbeat sets the tempo for the Prime Timeline.
7.  **High Auditor Kaelen** (Human Rogue) - Bureaucrat of the Architects.
    *   **Voice:** Nasal, slow, deliberate, incredibly boring. Drones on.
    *   **Goal:** File the paperwork to end the week.
    *   **Secret:** He secretly writes chaotic poetry.
8.  **"The Siphon"** (Vampire) - Elegant, Victorian dressing.
    *   **Voice:** Velvety, seductive, barely a whisper, intimate.
    *   **Goal:** Taste the memory of the party's victory.
    *   **Secret:** He is addicted to the taste of heartbreak.
9.  **Spore-Mother Vyla** (Firbolg Druid) - Covered in moss. Smell of rain.
    *   **Voice:** Earthy, croaking, long pauses as if listening to plants.
    *   **Goal:** Reclaim the city for nature.
    *   **Secret:** She is a plant piloting a corpse.
10. **Madame Twice** (Tiefling Fighter) - Two scars over her eyes. Blind fighting master.
    *   **Voice:** Sharp, staccato, commanding, military precision.
    *   **Goal:** To kill the person who scarred her (herself from the future).
    *   **Secret:** She can only see 2 seconds into the future, nothing else.
11. **Grave-Keeper Silas** (Dwarf Cleric) - Tends the graves of those who haven't died yet.
    *   **Voice:** Gravelly, grumpy, Scottish accent, clears throat constantly.
    *   **Goal:** Keep the pre-dug graves clean.
    *   **Secret:** He buries empty coffins to trick death.
12. **Echo** (Changeling Bard) - Cannot form their own face, only mirrors others.
    *   **Voice:** Mimics the player they are talking to perfectly.
    *   **Goal:** To find a face of their own.
    *   **Secret:** They are a living reflection that escaped a mirror.
13. **The Curator** (Ghost Expert) - Translucent, blue, melancholic.
    *   **Voice:** Echoing, faint, sounds like wind in a tunnel.
    *   **Goal:** Catalog the lost books.
    *   **Secret:** He is the author of the campaign setting guide.
14. **General Tock** (Warforged Barbarian) - A clockwork soldier with a broken mainspring.
    *   **Voice:** Mechanical grinding, ticking background noise, robotic.
    *   **Goal:** To stop the war.
    *   **Secret:** His ticker is a bomb set to go off in Year 0.

## 4. LOCATIONS
`[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]`

### Zone 1: The Chronos Citadel (Hub)
*   **The Spire of Seconds:** A tower made of frozen lightning.
*   **The Market of Moments:** Stalls selling "Last Thursdays" and "Next Tuesdays."
*   **The Silent Barracks:** Where the Inevitability sleeps standing up.
*   **The Whispering Well:** A pit that narrates your death if you toss a coin in.
*   **The Plaza of Statues:** Statues of people who haven't been born yet.
    *   **Sensory:** Smells of ozone and old paper. Sound of a billion ticking clocks, syncing and desyncing.

### Zone 2: The Temporal Dreamscape
*   **The Library of Tomorrows:** Infinite books, shelves that move like snakes.
*   **The Ocean of Moments:** Liquid time. Viscous, silver fluid.
*   **The Branching Reef:** Coral made of crystalized choices.
*   **The Paradox Void:** A hole in the floor where gravity reverses.
*   **The Mirror Cliffs:** Cliffs made of mirrors showing alternate selves.
    *   **Sensory:** Tastes like metal. Silence is "loud." Colors are colors that don't exist (Octarine).

### Zone 3: The Bleeding Edge (Warzone)
*   **The Crater of Tomorrow:** A bomb hit here 50 years in the future; the crater exists now.
*   **The Trenches of Loop:** Soldiers running the same charge forever.
*   **The Rust Fields:** Metal ages instantly here.
*   **The Ghost-Forest:** Trees that only exist at night.
*   **The Bunker of the Unborn:** A shelter for children not yet conceived.
    *   **Sensory:** Smell of rot and fresh baby powder. Sound of screams played backward.

### Zone 4: The Static City (Stasis)
*   **The Frozen Fountain:** Water hanging in mid-air droplets.
*   **The Statue Crowd:** People frozen in mid-stride (alive, just slow).
*   **The Echo Hall:** Footsteps echo for hours.
*   **The Monochrome District:** All color has drained away.
*   **The Still-Fire:** Flames that are hard as stone.
    *   **Sensory:** Absolute stillness. The air is thick and hard to breathe, like syrup.

### Zone 5: The Paradox Zone
*   **The House of Stairs:** Stairs go everywhere, Escher-style.
*   **The Room of Rain:** It rains indoors, dry outside.
*   **The Ceiling Floor:** Furniture is bolted to the ceiling.
*   **The Door to Yesterday:** Opens into the room you were in 5 minutes ago.
*   **The Melting Clock:** Dali-esque landscape.
    *   **Sensory:** Nausea. Vertigo. Smell of burning hair.

## 8. MECHANICS
`[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]`

### The Sanity of Seconds (Chronal Dysphoria)
Whenever a player changes a Fixed Point or enters the Dreamscape, they gain **Dysphoria Points (DP)**.
*   **1-3 DP:** Visual hallucinations (glowing edges on objects).
*   **4-6 DP:** Auditory hallucinations (ticking, whispers). Disadvantage on Perception.
*   **7-9 DP:** Temporal drifting. 50% chance to blink out of existence for a round in combat.
*   **10 DP:** "Unstuck." The character becomes an NPC under DM control, lost in time.

### Prophetic Dice
At the start of a session, each player rolls a d20. Record the number. **Once** during the session, they can replace any roll (theirs or enemy's) with this "Prophesied Number." Narrative flavor: "I dreamt this would happen."

### Timeline Fragility
The DM tracks a hidden **Chaos Score**.
*   Killing a random NPC: +1 Chaos.
*   Killing a Major NPC: +5 Chaos.
*   Changing a Fixed Point: +10 Chaos.
*   **At 50 Chaos:** The Time Storm begins.
*   **At 100 Chaos:** Reality collapses (Game Over).

## 9. SESSION GUIDE
`[TAG: DM_GUIDE]`

**Session 1-4: The Awakening**
*   **Focus:** Horror, Confusion.
*   **Beat:** Establish the "Dream" mechanic. Save The Butterfly.

*   **Focus:** Combat, High Stakes.
*   **Beat:** The Inevitability actively hunts them. Dysphoria mechanics ramp up.
*   **Boss:** Future-Self Duel (PvP or PvNPC).

**Session 19-24: The Collapse**
*   **Focus:** Survival, Weirdness.
*   **Beat:** The Time Storm hits. Zones merge. Past and Future bleed.
*   **Boss:** The Clockwork Dragon.

**Session 25-28: The Finale**
*   **Focus:** Philosophical Choice.
*   **Beat:** Ascend the Spire. Confront the First Paradox.
*   **Boss:** The Inevitability (God Form) OR The Entropy of the Universe.
