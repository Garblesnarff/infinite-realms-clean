# The Betrayed Hero: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a shield that's being held by an invisible guardian. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Melromarc' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Shield Hero'—an ancient, celestial consciousness who believes that the only true life is 'Protective Resilience.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Shield' realized that 'Defense' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Shields' and 'Demi-Human Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Shield-Energy'—a shimmering force found in green glowing runes and practical leather armor, making every 'Counter-Attack' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Hero or a Sword is to be a ghost in your own city, knowing that 'The Betrayal' is not a failure—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Guard (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Guardian creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced block.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Four Cardinal Heroes' to monitor the protective energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Shield Hero Era (Last Age):** A period of dark fantasy with heroic undertones occurs, establishing the Michael Whelan beauty (equivalent) and the Code of the Shield.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Summoned' as the Shield Hero, only to witness their first 'Betrayal' reveal.
5.  **The Slum-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Church Incursion'—a team of beautiful but calculating shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Shield Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the defense, you develop 'Sound-Sensitivity' and protective 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Naofumi Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of quiet determined voice and suppressive anger offers you a 'Heroic Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Wave Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of dark greens and reddish oranges. The shields are vibrating, and the 'Resilient-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Duality of Perception.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can be a hero even when the world calls you a villain. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single курса in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the dark fantasy, the pride in mastering a protective shield, and the quiet, resonant voice of a hero who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the heroes and swords who must navigate the social bureaucracies and the intensity of a Wave battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

### The Shield Guardians (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Heroic Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Naofumi (equivalent - the heart).
**Asset:** Ancient shields, demi-human allies, and the 'Bond' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Church Syndicate, The Stagnant-Spawn.

### The Church Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Extraction Group
**Leader:** Malty (equivalent).
**Asset:** Gilded architecture, 'Status' magic, and absolute control of the city's supply lines.
**Rivals:** The Shield Guardians.

### The 'Raphtalia' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Sibling Sages
**Leader:** Raphtalia (Tier 1 NPC - the sword).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tier weaponry, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Demi-Human' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Slaver Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Shield history, 'Scrappy-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be weighed.
**Rivals:** The Church Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** The Waves (The Calamity).
**Asset:** Reality-tearing plot holes, dark shadows, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings with hearts.

**Raphtalia** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A sword who voice is determined, loyal, and maternal (reimagined). **Voice:** White moonlight glow, sad but kind eyes, melodic. **Goal:** To find her lost partner and stop the church-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the shield noise.
**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless operation-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Malty's logic that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient student in practical regional gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the shield ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Melromarc (The Hub)
A grand urban monument to architecture, kingdoms, and dark light.
*   **The Hero Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and floor wax. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Shield Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Slums (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each district has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Shield-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Wave Spire:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Church Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and distribution.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Church Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Naofumi Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Naofumi. You must identify the 'Shield-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Shield Heist:** Infiltrate the Church HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Bond-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The City Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated Wave fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Resilient Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Malty face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Balance (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Sacred Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Shield Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the shield seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Shield-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Church' magic.
