# Steel Meets Sorcery: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a bridge that's being held by an invisible builder. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Tokyo' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Gate'—an ancient, celestial portal that exists between the modern world and a high fantasy realm. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Crossing' realized that 'Diplomacy' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Modern Military' and 'Fantasy Kingdoms' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Chakra-Energy' (equivalent) —a shimmering force found in tactical gear and glowing spells, making every 'First Contact' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Soldier or a Mage is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Invasion' is not a war—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Verse (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Performer creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced spirit wave.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Special Region' to monitor the spiritual energy of the provinces.
3.  **The GATE Era (Last Age):** A period of modern military meets high fantasy occurs, establishing the character-driven martial arts and the Code of the Soldier.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Deployed' through the gate, only to witness their first 'Dragon Attack.'
5.  **The FOB-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Imperial Incursion'—a team of plate-armor shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Diplomacy Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the language, you develop 'Culture-Sensitivity' and tactical 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Itami Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of spiky hair and modern uniform offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Imperial Palace. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of military greens and royal purples. The gates are vibrating, and the 'Bridge-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Culture Shock.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can have a rifle and still have a heart. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single курса in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful urban fantasy, the pride in mastering a fantasy language, and the professional, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the soldiers and mages who must navigate the bureaucratic oppressions and the intensity of a dragon battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A researcher whose body is a map of countless operation-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Molt's logic that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient student in practical regional gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the bridge ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Tokyo (The Hub)
A grand urban monument to architecture, streets, and school uniforms.
*   **The Gate Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and floor wax. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Bridge Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Special Region (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where water acts as a thick medium with visible currents (equivalent).
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Bridge-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Imperial Palace:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Dragon Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and dragon fire.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Bridge Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a school cell. You must survive your first 'Imperial Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Itami Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Itami. You must identify the 'Bridge-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Artifact Heist:** Infiltrate the Imperial HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Bridge-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Tokyo Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated fantasy fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Diplomacy Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Emperor Molt face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Bridge (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Bridge Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Gate Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the gate seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Bridge-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Imperial' magic.
