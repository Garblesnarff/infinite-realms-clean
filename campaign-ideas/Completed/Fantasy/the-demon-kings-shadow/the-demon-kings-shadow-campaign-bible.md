# The Demon King's Shadow: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a bridge that's being built by an invisible army. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Lanka' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of 'The Demon King'—an ancient, celestial consciousness who believe that the only true life is 'Unified Power.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Dharma' realized that 'Sacrifice' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Arrows' and 'Forest Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Divine-Energy'—a shimmering force found in saffron robes and golden arrows, making every 'Agni Pariksha' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Hero or a Rakshasa is to be a ghost in your own legend, knowing that 'The Final Arrow' is not a win—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Verse (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Performer creates the world with a single, perfectly pitched note (equivalent).
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Eight Provinces' to monitor the divine energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Ramayana Era (Last Age):** A period of Hindu epic meets high fantasy occur, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Dharma.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the forest, only to witness their first 'Demon Incursion' reveal.
5.  **The Bridge-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Rakshasa Incursion'—a team of ten-headed shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Dharma Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the rituals, you develop 'Spirit-Sensitivity' and sacred 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Rama Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of dignified divine voice and golden armor offers you a 'Heroic Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Lanka Throne. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of saffron golds and deep green forests. The bridges are vibrating, and the 'Dharma-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Choice.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that wholeness requires balance. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kursi in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the Hindu epic, the pride in mastering a divine arrow, and the multiple, resonant voice of a storyteller who understands that every moment is a prayer. The players are the heroes and rakshasas who must navigate the mythological politics and the intensity of a divine judgment to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Archetypa (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, bridges, and divine light.
*   **The Council Circle:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and incense. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Dharma Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Symbolic Landscapes (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each region has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Divine-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Forest of Sita:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Lanka Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and information overload.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Rakshasa Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Rama Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Rama. You must identify the 'Divine-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Arrow Heist:** Infiltrate the Lanka HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Dharma-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Bridge Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated shadow fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Agni Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Ravana face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Balance (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Divine Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Arrow Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the arrow seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Divine-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Chaos' magic.
