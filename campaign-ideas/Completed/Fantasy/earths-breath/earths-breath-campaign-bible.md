# Earth's Breath: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a lung that's breathing through the crust. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Hekla-Zone' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the return of the 'Frost-That-Hungers'—an ancient, cryogenic intelligence who believes that the only true life is 'Absolute Stasis.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Deep' realized that 'Heat' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Earthheart'—a sentient magma core that regulates the global temperature through geothermal cycles. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Geothermal-Energy'—a shimmering force found in volcanic red and ice blue, making every 'Eruption' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Magma Shepherd or a Stone-Speaker is to be a guardian of the 'Breath,' knowing that 'The Freeze' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Eruption (c. -Infinity):** The Earthheart wakes up, creating the planetary crust with its first breath.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Grandmother Hekla' lineage to monitor the steam energy of the vents.
3.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** Frost-That-Hungers arrives on a comet, releasing the mechanical 'Aethel' into the global atmosphere.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient shepherd (the player) arrives at the rift, only to witness a geyser erupting in a pattern of warning.
5.  **The Lava-River Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Ice-Crawl'—a creature of absolute cold—in a dark, steam-lit cavern (equivalent).
6.  **The Earthheart Descent (Ongoing):** As you descend through the Dante-like layers, you develop 'Heat-Resistance' and geological 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Grandmother Hekla Revelation (Ongoing):** An ancient woman of steam and stone offers you a 'Prophetic Path.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Magma Chamber. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of volcanic reds and obsidian blacks. The geysers are rhythmic, and the 'Earth-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Stasis.' It is about the 'Primordial Resilience' of the human spirit—the realization that you are part of a living system. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the very ground beneath you is trying to speak its own name through fire and ice?' It explores the visceral beauty of the Icelandic geothermal features, the pride in maintaining the balance, and the deep, rumbling voice of a planet that sounds like its own breath. The players are the shepherds and speakers who must navigate the stone-groans and the intensity of a volcanic eruption to find a truth that stays solid in the heat.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Magma Chamber (The Hub)
A grand subterranean monument to architectural grandeur and planetary fire.
*   **The Heart-Seat:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh geothermal rituals. **Smell:** Sulfur, vanilla, and old wood. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Steam Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Icelandic Tundra (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of ice blues, volcanic reds, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Geyser Field:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Earth-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Obsidian Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Frost Citadel (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to absolute stasis and frozen stone.
*   **The Stasis Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Geothermal Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a geothermal vent. You must survive your first 'Frost Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Hekla Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Grandmother Hekla. You must identify the 'Earth-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Steam Heist:** Infiltrate the Frost Citadel to retrieve a stolen 'Magma-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Eruption Defense:** Your chamber is under attack by a coordinated frost fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Earthheart Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Frost-That-Hungers face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
