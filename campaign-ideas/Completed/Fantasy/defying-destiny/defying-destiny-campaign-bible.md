# Defying Destiny: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a script that's being rewritten by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century Oz (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the return of the 'Wizard'—an ancient, mechanical intelligence who believes that the only true magic is 'Smoke and Mirrors.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Balance' realized that the 'Talking Animals' were the planet's original custodians. They granted them the 'Gospel of Hospitality' and built the 'Emerald City' as a sanctuary of green light and art deco elegance. But the Wizard has emerged, a man of carnival barker charm who has initiated the 'Animal Silencing' protocol to eliminate the dissenters. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Sparkle-Energy'—a shimmering force found in academic robes and pink gowns, making every 'Popular' act a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Witch or a Prince is to be a guardian of the 'Animal Song,' knowing that 'Defying Gravity' is not a spell—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The Wizard's ship strikes Earth, releasing the mechanical 'Aethel' into the global atmosphere.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Shiz Academy' to monitor the magical energy of the cities.
3.  **The Elphaba Awakening (Present Day - Day 1):** A green-skinned student (the player) arrives at Shiz Academy, only to witness her first 'Animal Silencing' event.
4.  **The First Bloom (Week 1):** You meet Glinda, a woman of pink and white perfection who offers you a 'Perpetual Summer' (equivalent).
5.  **The Smoke and Mirrors Reveal (Ongoing):** The Wizard's throne room is revealed as a site of 'Mechanical Magic' and reality distortion.
6.  **The Defying Gravity Ascension (Ongoing):** The player learns to fly/levitate with magic, appearing almost weightless against the morning mist (equivalent).
7.  **The Animal Prison Siege (Upcoming):** The rebels must lead a charge of talking animals in a full-scale assault on the sterile horror of the prison.
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the central Emerald City spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant emerald and midnight blue. The flower petals are drifting, and the 'True Love' pulse is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Theatrical Performance.' It is about the 'Romantic Idealism' of the human spirit—the realization that being yourself is the ultimate form of magic. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the world itself is trying to delete your soul for being green?' It explores the elegant linework of the shoujo manga (equivalent), the visceral horror of the animal silencing, and the fierce, determined voice of a narrator who understands that fairy tales teach real lessons. The players are the villainesses and heroes who must navigate the subculture politics and the intensity of a choice-box entry to find a truth that stays fabulous in the light.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Shiz Academy (The Hub)
A grand European-inspired monument to architectural grandeur and high magic.
*   **The Main Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh artificial lighting. **Smell:** Vanilla, old wood, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Library:** A site of 'Visual Representation' and glowing script text.

### The Emerald City (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of neon greens, concrete grays, and black-purple waves.
*   **The Throne Room:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Cursed-Energy (equivalent). **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Alleyway:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Animal Prison (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and sterile horror.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Western Castle (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Gothic Refuge:** Where the 'Final Choice' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Ingestion:** Wake up in a Shiz cell. You must survive your first 'Wizard Ambush' while maintaining your 'Sanity' level.
2.  **The Bloom run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Glinda. You must identify the 'Respiration Seed' (equivalent) before the flicker.
3.  **The School Heist:** Infiltrate the Shiz Library to retrieve a stolen 'Script-Focus.' You must bypass Megumi's (equivalent) shadows.
4.  **The Shibuya Incursion:** The city is under attack by a reality-warping drone (equivalent). You must defend the station while the Wizard mounts his counter-strike.
5.  **The Domain Expansion:** Lead a team of sorcerers (equivalent) into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the non-euclidean geometry.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Wizard face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff at the top of the spires. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Choice (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A simple, jade-green sword that seems to hum with the energy of the first Master.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Cursed-Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Relationship Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Death Flag' magic.
