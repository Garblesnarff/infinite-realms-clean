# Motes in the Giant's Eye: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a carpet that's being vacuumed by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archetypa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of scale was shattered by the discovery of the 'Giants'—ancient, celestial consciousness who believe that the only true life is 'Macro-Existence.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Small' realized that 'Miniaturization' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Buttons' and 'Pencil Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Scale-Energy'—a shimmering force found in wood grain landscapes and fabric weaves, making every 'Creative Solution' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Mote or a Guardian is to be a ghost in your own home, knowing that 'The Giant's Eye' is not a gaze—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Squelch (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Giant creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced footprint.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Small Communities' to monitor the scale energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Borrowers Era (Last Age):** A period of miniature survival occurs, establishing the forced-perspective beauty and the Code of the Small.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Shrunk' into the carpet, only to witness their first 'Giant Footstep' earthquake.
5.  **The Pencil-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Spider Incursion'—a team of eight-legged nightmares—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Scale Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the environment, you develop 'Sound-Sensitivity' and textured 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Emmie Revelation (Ongoing):** A being of child's voice and innocent cruelty offers you a 'Possessive Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Giant's Eye. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of wood browns and fabric weaves. The clocks are vibrating, and the 'Scale-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Overwhelming Noise.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can be small and still be a hero. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternalkurs (equivalent)?' It explores the forced-perspective beauty of the miniature survival, the pride in mastering a button shield, and the bass-boosted, resonant voice of a giant who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the motes and guardians who must navigate the domestic oppressions and the intensity of a giant encounter to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

4.  **The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless survival-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the giant's geometry that he's too professional to share.
5.  **The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient student in practical miniature gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the shrink ritual.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Archetypa (The Hub)
**Type:** Hub
**Description:** A massive, high-contrast monument to architecture, scale, and household energy.
*   **The Reading Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh miniature rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Scale Tree:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Household Landscapes (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each space has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Scale-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Carpet Spire:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Giant's Chamber (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and macro-existence.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Spider Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Guardian Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find The High Guardian. You must identify the 'Scale-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Button Heist:** Infiltrate the Web-Master's HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Scale-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Carpet Siege:** Your community is under attack by a coordinated insect fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Macro Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Giants face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
