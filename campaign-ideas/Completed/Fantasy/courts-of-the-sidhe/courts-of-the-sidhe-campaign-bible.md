# Courts of the Sidhe: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a circle; it's a Knot that's being tied by an invisible queen. In the beginning, the 'Architects' (The Tuatha Dé Danann) spoke the first 'AUM' (equivalent), and the 'Grand Accord' of reality began to weave the 'Courts of the Sidhe.' Ancient Ireland is the 'Prime Harmony,' a land of misty hills and standing stones. But the knot has two loops: the Seelie Court (Eternal Summer) and the Unseelie Court (Eternal Twilight). Long ago, the 'First Bard' realized that 'Oaths' are the only threads that keep the tapestry of existence from being erased by the 'Void Season' (The Fomorian Blight). The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Glamour'—a shimmering force found in knotwork patterns and moonlight, making every bargain a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Warrior or a Fey-Touched is to be a guardian of the 'Prime Melody,' knowing that 'The Otherworld' is not a dream—it's the truth that mortal eyes struggle to process.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Bloom (c. -Infinity):** The Tuatha Dé Danann create the Otherworld and establish the Seelie/Unseelie cycle.
2.  **The Era of Sods (c. -10,000 years):** The Sidhe mounds are anchored to the mortal world, establishing the 'Threshold' gateways.
3.  **The Fomorian Incursion (-1,000 years):** A periodic reality-glitch occurs, resetting the physical world while the 'Soul-Records' persist.
4.  **The Rise of the Queen (Last Age):** The Queen of Air and Darkness leads a campaign of 'Winter Twilight' to reclaim the mortal hills.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A newly awakened champion (the player) finds themselves at a standing stone circle after a recent 'Karmic Reset.'
6.  **The Lady Verdantia Encounter (Week 1):** You meet the Seelie Queen, a being of body-horror beauty (equivalent) who offers you a 'Perpetual Summer.'
7.  **The Unseelie Siege (Ongoing):** The Unseelie Court leads an 'Ice Palace' assault against the mortal hill forts.
8.  **The Tír na nÓg Revelation (Upcoming):** The party discovers that the Otherworld is a 'Static Paradise' that's slowly deleting the individual.
9.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the central Sidhe mound. You must choose between immortality and freedom.
10. **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant golds and absolute blacks. The air is humid with spores (equivalent), and the 'Knot' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Performance.' It is about the 'Bardic Tragedy' of the human spirit—the realization that beauty is a mask for cruelty. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the gods themselves want to turn your life into a song?' It explores the symbolic density of the Celtic knotwork, the tragedy of the 'Mostly Dead' hero (equivalent), and the lyrical voice of a bard who knows that every bargain costs more than expected. The players are the warriors and fey-touched who must navigate the gopurams (equivalent) and sacred rivers to find a resolution that honors the memory of the Architects.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Seelie Court (The Eternal Summer)
**Type:** Theocratic/Growth-Based Superpower
**Leader:** Queen Titania (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Glass towers, flower-armor, and the 'Perpetual Bloom' magic.
**Rivals:** The Unseelie Court, The Fomorian Blight.

### The Unseelie Court (The Eternal Twilight)
**Type:** Elite/Geometric Ice Order
**Leader:** The Queen of Air and Darkness (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Ice palaces, shadow-markets, and a total disregard for 'Soft' biological life.
**Rivals:** The Seelie Court.

### The 'Dharma' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Irish Bards
**Leader:** Hosteen (The Elder - equivalent).
**Asset:** Knowledge of sacred laws, hogan sanctuaries (equivalent), and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Karma' Archivists
**Type:** Scientific/Bureaucratic Spiritual Branch
**Leader:** The Morrigan (The Goddess of Prophecy).
**Asset:** Access to the 'Soul-Records,' knowledge of past lives, and the ability to declare a 'Karmic Victory.'
**Rivals:** The Nihilists.

### The 'Fomorian' Remnants (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Pre-Era Force
**Leader:** Balor of the Evil Eye.
**Asset:** Sickly greens, bruised purples, and the ability to unravel reality.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Queen Titania** (Seelie - The Leader) - A woman of pre-Raphaelite beauty meets body horror. **Voice:** Mezzo-soprano, musical, and persuasive. **Goal:** To turn the entire world into a permanent 'Eternal Garden.' **Secret:** She is secretly an Adept of 'Glamour' who can 'persuade' a target's limbs to become plant-life through her touch.
**Queen of Air and Darkness** (Unseelie - The Villain) - A woman of crystal fractal patterns and cold clarity. **Voice:** Alto, crystalline, and emotionally flat. **Goal:** To stabilize the world through 'Absolute Zero' and stop the Fomorian Blight. **Secret:** She was the first monarch to 'Sync' with the anti-light, allowing her to become shadow.
**The Morrigan** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A being of black wings and stars. **Voice:** Whispered, like the sound of a dying sun. **Goal:** To witness the end of the song and record the final silence. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the battle cries.
**Balor of the Evil Eye** (Fomorian - The Mastermind) - A massive, multi-limbed creature with a hundred eyes. **Voice:** A high-pitched, screeching harmony of a thousand voices. **Goal:** To consume the Otherworld and become the new god of the gut (equivalent). **Secret:** He was once a human maiden who was 'Perfected' by the Pollution (equivalent).
**The Bard (Echo)** (Independent - The Narrator) - A man with a frame drum and a tagelharpa. **Voice:** Bardic storyteller channeling the Irish oral tradition. **Goal:** To witness the apocalypse and ensure the new world remembers the heroes. **Secret:** He is a direct descendant of the first World-Forger and his 'Song' is the only thing keeping the knot from collapsing.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Standing Stones (The Hub)
A high-contrast landscape of misty hills and ancient stone circles.
*   **The Threshold:** A site of 'Liminal Framing' and unstable key changes. **Smell:** Petrichor, ozone, and old earth. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'creak-creak' of rotating weather vents (equivalent).
*   **The Memory Walls:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Eternal Garden (Seelie HQ)
A site of 'Body Horror' beauty and Versailles-meets-Annihilation aesthetic.
*   **The Main Gate:** A site of 'Saturated Greens' and harsh morning sunlight. **Visual:** Plants that move when watched and rearranging paths. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of growing vines.
*   **The Mirror Gallery:** A site of 'Seductive Horror' and desaturated pastels.

### The Frost Throne (Unseelie HQ)
Brutal monumental architecture of Islamic tile patterns in ice.
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Savage Spectacle' and white-hot light. **Smell:** Sweat, charcoal smoke, and wet leather. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'roar-roar' of 50,000 voices.

### Tír na nÓg (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Soul-Core:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a Sidhe mound. You must escape a 'Fey Ambush' using only your iron axe (equivalent).
2.  **The Bloom run:** Navigate the islands of the acidic sea to find the Messenger. You must identify the 'Respiration Seed' (equivalent) before the tide rises.
3.  **The Pollution Infiltration:** Infiltrate the Fungal Mind's garden to find evidence of the 'Reverse-Ingestion' plan. You must bypass the 'Blighted Guard.'
4.  **The Heart-Beat Chase:** Lead a fleet of arterial-rafts through a circulation surge. You must cast 'Bile-Spells' while traveling at 100 mph.
5.  **The Brain-Chamber Revelation:** Enter the Cranial Cathedral undercover. You must find your own 'Soul-Memory' without alerting the Immune System.
6.  **The Final Digestion:** The Beast has begun its final 'Reset.' You must defend the Heart Chamber while the self-destruct activates.
7.  **The Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Queen Leech face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you serve the Beast or die for the individual?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Oaths
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Strike of Duty' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Glamour-Energy Capsule
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Fey' magic.

(CR 5). Monstrosity. HP 80, AC 15.
**Abilities:**
*   **Taboo Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet takes 1d10 acid damage at the start of their turn.
*   **Reality-Warping Pulse:** Ranged attack (Psychic). 2d10 damage + Target is confused for 1 round.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Queen (Proxy)
(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Malevolent Whisper Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the Mind's 'Compost Directive.'
*   **The Final Reset:** Once per combat, the Mind can force all creatures within 100 feet to re-roll their initiative.
