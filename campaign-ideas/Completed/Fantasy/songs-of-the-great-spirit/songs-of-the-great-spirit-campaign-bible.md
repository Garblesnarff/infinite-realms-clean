# Songs of the Great Spirit: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a song that's being sung by an invisible breath. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archetypa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Great Spirit'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirits of all beings. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Song' realized that 'Balance' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Rituals' and 'Sacred Landscapes' to serve as anchors for the world's spiritual stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Spirit-Energy'—a shimmering force found in ledger art line work and glowing animal eyes, making every 'Vision Quest' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Seeker or an Elder is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Trickster' is not a monster—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Verse (c. -Infinity):** The Great Spirit creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced breath through the void.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Spirit Sanctuaries' to monitor the lyrical energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Oral Era (Last Age):** A period of spiritual mysticism occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Song.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the sacred mountain, only to witness their first 'Coyote' transformation.
5.  **The Spirit-World Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Shadow-Incursion'—a team of dark smoke creatures—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Vision Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the rituals, you develop 'Spirit-Sensitivity' and ritual 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Coyote Revelation (Ongoing):** A being of dusty golds and scraggly trickster presence offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Spirit Lodge. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of earth tones and aurora shimmers. The landscapes are vibrating, and the 'Spirit-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Divine Bureaucracy (equivalent).' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the mischief of the trickster to appreciate the wisdom of the mind. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal scroll?' It explores the beautiful mysticism of the Native American tradition, the pride in mastering a ritual, and the rhythmic, resonant voice of a storyteller who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the seekers and elders who must navigate the celestial politics and the intensity of a vision quest to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Mythic Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Song history, 'Wild-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be weighed.
**Rivals:** The Shadow Syndicate.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** The Great Spirit (The First Presence).
**Asset:** Reality-tearing plot holes, dark shadows, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**The High Elder** (Order - The Lead) - A being whose voice is rhythmic, repetitive, and wise. **Voice:** Storyteller by the fire, measured pacing, rhythmic words. **Goal:** To protect the 'Spirit Balance' and guide the new seekers. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Spirit-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his campfire stories.
**The Devourer** (Shadow - The Villain) - A being whose voice is smooth, hierarchical, and measured. **Voice:** Beautiful but hollow, hiding a deep-seated terror. **Goal:** To consolidate all spiritual talent into a single 'Global Colony' under its control. **Secret:** It is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the original Silicon surge.
**Coyote** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A being whose voice is energetic, mischievous, and theatrical. **Voice:** Scraggy trickster, noble figure later, playful delivery. **Goal:** To teach through laughter and confusion, stopping the order-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the spirit clatter.
**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless vision-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Great Spirit's presence that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient student in practical regional gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the song ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Sacred Mountain (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, petroglyphs, and ritual.
*   **The Council Circle:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and sage. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Spirit Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Spirit Landscapes (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each region has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Spirit-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Desert Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Spirit Lodge (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and dreamlike architecture.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Spirit Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Shadow Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Elder Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find The High Elder. You must identify the 'Spirit-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Ritual Heist:** Infiltrate the Shadow HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Ritual-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Mountain Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated smoke fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Vision Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Great Spirit face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Balance (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Divine Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Cedar Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the cedar seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Spirit-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Shadow' magic.
