# Heavy Metal Hell: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a giant speaker that's being played at maximum volume. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Infernum Rex' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Lord Metallicus'—an ancient, musical consciousness who believes that the only true life is 'Absolute Silence.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Riff' realized that 'Heavy Metal' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Instruments' and 'Seven Territories' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Metal-Energy'—a shimmering force found in blood red skies and chrome silver armor, making every 'Power Chord' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Roadie or a Demon Lord is to be a ghost in your own concert, knowing that 'The Silence' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Riff (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Songwriter creates the world with a single, massive bass kick.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Eternal Stage' to monitor the musical energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Frank Frazetta Era (Last Age):** A period of brutal fantasy occurs, establishing the album-art beauty and the Code of the Riff.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) lands in a mosh pit, only to witness their first 'Silence Incursion.'
5.  **The Concert-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Thrash Wraith'—a creature of rust and chrome—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Metal Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the instruments, you develop 'BPM-Sensitivity' and musical 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Ozzy Revelation (Ongoing):** A mumbling prophet of Birmingham accent offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Eternal Concert. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of blood reds and obsidian blacks. The speakers are vibrating, and the 'Metal-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Constant Noise.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the silence to appreciate the riff. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself is trying to turn you into a white-hot spark in an eternal bird (equivalent)?' It explores the brutal beauty of the heavy metal, the pride in mastering a power chord, and the powerful, band-frontman voice of a demon lord who understands that every beat is a prayer. The players are the roadies and lords who must navigate the reality-breaks and the intensity of a musical drop to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Leader:** Lord Valorious (Tier 1 NPC).

**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Rex history, 'Subculture-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be silenced.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing reverse-metal, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

**Raven Darkheart** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A woman whose voice is operatic but with an edge. **Voice:** Urban, modern, and witty banter. **Goal:** To find her lost partner and stop the silence-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the metal clatter.
**Ozzy the Eternal** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless concert-scars. **Voice:** Mumbling prophet, incomprehensible then suddenly clear. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Metallicus's reversed-logic that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient roadie in practical leather and spikes gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the riff ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Infernum Rex (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, speakers, and red firelight.
*   **The Eternal Stage:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh musical rituals. **Smell:** Sulfur, vanilla, and old paper. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Riff Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Genre Territories (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of blood reds, chrome silvers, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Mosh Pit:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Metal-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Volcano Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Silence Chamber (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and reversal.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Song Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a mosh pit. You must survive your first 'Silence Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **Lord Valorious Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Valorious. You must identify the 'Metal-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Riff Heist:** Infiltrate the Silence HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Riff-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Stage Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated silent fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Solo Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Metallicus face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Riff (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Sound Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Solo Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the solo seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Metal-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Silence' magic.

(CR 2). Humanoid. HP 30, AC 13 (Silk patches).

*   **Ordered Muting:** The thrall has advantage on attacks if they have an ally within 10 feet.
*   **Neural Prod:** Melee attack. 1d8+3 damage + Target is paralyzed for 1 round.

(CR 5). Construct. HP 80, AC 16.
**Abilities:**
*   **Ominous Sound Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet takes 1d10 psychic damage at the start of their turn.
*   **Reality-Warping Pulse:** Ranged attack (Psychic). 2d10 damage + Target is confused for 1 round.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Metallicus (Proxy)
(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Order of Progress Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the leader's 'Archaic Script.'
*   **The Final Move:** Once per combat, Metallicus can force an enemy to take an action that serves his own glory.
