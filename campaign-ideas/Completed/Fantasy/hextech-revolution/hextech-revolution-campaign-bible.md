# Hextech Revolution: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a gear that's being played by an invisible inventor. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Piltover' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of 'Hextech'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the crystal. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Divide' realized that 'Innovation' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Hexgates' and 'Undercity' to serve as anchors for the world's progress. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Hextech-Energy'—a shimmering force found in blue-glowing crystals and golden brass, making every 'Invention' a focus for reality-warping power. To be an Enforcer or a Revolutionary is to be a ghost in your own city, knowing that 'The Shimmer' is not an enhancement—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Spark (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Inventor creates the first Hextech crystal, releasing the blue 'Aethel' into the global atmosphere.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Council of Piltover' to monitor the technological energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Arcane Era (Last Age):** A period of class warfare occurs, establishing the toxic beauty and the Code of the Inventor.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient enforcer (the player) arrives in Piltover, only to witness their first 'Shimmer Incursion' revealing the grit beyond.
5.  **The Zaun-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Firelight Attack'—a team of graffiti-masked rebels—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Revolution Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the hextech, you develop 'Crystal-Sensitivity' and mechanical 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Silco Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of scarred face and persuasive voice offers you an 'Industrial Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Hexgate. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of golden towers and green chemical smogs. The gears are vibrating, and the 'Progress-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Systemic Inequality.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that family is more than just blood. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal machine?' It explores the painterly beauty of the steampunk class divide, the pride in mastering a hextech device, and the tragic, emotional voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the enforcers and revolutionaries who must navigate the toxic beauties and the intensity of a sisterhood torn apart to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Piltover Council (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Bureaucratic Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Vi (equivalent - the enforcer).
**Asset:** Golden armor, hextech gauntlets, and the 'Unity' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Zaibach Syndicate, The Shimmer-Spawn.

### The Zaun Undercity (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Industrial Group
**Leader:** Silco (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Pipe architecture, 'Shimmer' magic, and absolute control of the city's resources.
**Rivals:** The Piltover Council.

### The 'Jinx' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Revolutionary Sages
**Leader:** Jinx (Tier 1 NPC - the bridge).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, graffiti-masked (scavenged) gear, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Firelight' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Rebel Communities
**Leader:** Ekko (The Inventor).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Hextech history, 'Time-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be repressed.
**Rivals:** The Zaun Undercity.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** The Shimmer (The Ultimate Corruption).
**Asset:** Reality-tearing purple voids, dark shadows, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings with hearts.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Piltover (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to Art Nouveau architecture and steampunk innovation.
*   **The Council Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh divine rituals. **Smell:** Incense, vanilla, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Hexgate:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Undercity (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of green chemical smogs, industrial pipes, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Hextech-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Last Drop:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Zaun Citadel (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and industrial grit.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Progress Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a Piltover jail. You must survive your first 'Firelight Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Vi Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Vi. You must identify the 'Hextech-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Crystal Heist:** Infiltrate the Zaun HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Hextech-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Piltover Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated revolutionary fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Arcane Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Silco face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Progress (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Hextech Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Crystal Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the crystal seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Hextech-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Shimmer' magic.
