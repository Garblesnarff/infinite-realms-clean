# Scale Shift Awakening: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a tabletop that's being painted by an invisible creator. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archetypa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Wargaming Table'—an ancient, celestial arena that exists within the human imagination. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Mini' realized that 'War' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Models' and 'Workshop Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Paint-Energy'—a shimmering force found in bold primaries and metallic highlights, making every 'Critical Hit' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Miniature or a Creator is to be a ghost in your own game, knowing that 'The Awakening' is not a victory—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Prime (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Painter creates the world with a single, perfectly applied base coat.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Wargaming Table' to monitor the strategic energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Miniature Era (Last Age):** A period of grimdark wargaming occurs, establishing the heroic proportions and the Code of the Model.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Awakened' on the table, only to witness their first 'Scale-Shift' reveal.
5.  **The Workshop-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Paint-Wraith'—a creature of liquid acrylic—in a dark, desk-lamp-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Growth Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the environment, you develop 'Sound-Sensitivity' and painted 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Ferrus Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of heroic booming voice and heroic proportions offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Workshop Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of bold primaries and muted natural tones. The tables are vibrating, and the 'Strategic-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Predetermined Moves.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you are more than just a piece of plastic. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal game?' It explores the grimdark beauty of the wargaming, the pride in mastering a tactical maneuver, and the heroic, resonant voice of a miniature who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the miniatures and creators who must navigate the domestic oppressions and the intensity of a tabletop battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Wargaming Table (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, terrain, and desk-lamp light.
*   **The Command Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and acrylic paint. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Game Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Household Landscapes (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each space has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Paint-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Workshop Spire:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Paint Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and solvent.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Eraser Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Ferrus Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Captain Ferrus. You must identify the 'Paint-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Model Heist:** Infiltrate the Eraser HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Paint-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Table Siege:** Your academy is under attack by a coordinated logical fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Miniature Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Eraser face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Paint (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Painted Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the model seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Solvent' magic.
