# Ghost Rock Frontier: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a poker table that's being played by a demon gambler. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Gomorra' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of 'Ghost Rock'—an ancient, eerie blue-green mineral that screams when burned and powers impossible machines. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Frontier' realized that 'Progress' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Steam Automatons' and 'Rail Guns' to serve as anchors for the world's expansion. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Manitou-Energy'—a shimmering force found in brass pipes and purple spirit magic, making every 'Duel' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Gunslinger or a Mad Scientist is to be a ghost in your own town, knowing that 'The Reckoning' is not a war—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The Great Quake (c. -Infinity):** A massive tectonic shift exposes the first Ghost Rock veins, releasing the supernatural 'Aethel' into the global atmosphere.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Raven' shamanic network to monitor the spirit energy of the deserts.
3.  **The Deadlands Era (Last Age):** A period of weird west horror occurs, establishing the steampunk aesthetic and the Code of the Gun.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient gunfighter (the player) arrives in Gomorra, only to witness their first 'Harrowed' resurrection.
5.  **The Ghost-Rock Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Walking Dead'—a creature of bone-white horror—in a dark, blue-green-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Manitou Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the cards, you develop 'Spirit-Sensitivity' and supernatural 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Stone Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of world-weary gravelly voice and undead eyes offers you a 'Veteran's Path.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Deadlands. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of desert tans and eerie blue-greens. The rails are vibrating, and the 'Ghost-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Industrial Horror.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can have a mechanical limb and still have a human heart. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single chip in an eternal game?' It explores the dusty beauty of the Western frontier, the pride in mastering a brass device, and the world-weary, resonant voice of a storyteller who understands that every hand is a gift. The players are the gunslingers and scientists who must navigate the mad science and the intensity of a shamanic ritual to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Leader:** Captain Stone (equivalent - the harrowed).
**Asset:** Classic cowboy hats, silver badges, and the 'Continuity' intuition.

**Type:** Historical/Grounded Spirit Sages
**Leader:** Raven (Tier 1 NPC - the guide).

**Type:** Independent/Lost Undead Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Ghost-Rock history, 'Necrotic-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be buried.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** The Manitous (The Demon Spirits).
**Asset:** Reality-tearing purple voids, dark shadows, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings with souls.

5.  **The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient gunfighter in practical dusty gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the Ghost-Rock ritual.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Gomorra (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to Victorian architecture and western expansion.
*   **The Main Street:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh martial rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and coal smoke. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Hall of Records:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Ghost Rock Mines (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of desert tans, eerie blue-greens, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Manitou-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Deadlands:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Hellstromme Citadel (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and mad science.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Frontier Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in Gomorra. You must survive your first 'Walking Dead Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Stone Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Stone. You must identify the 'Ghost-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Manitou Heist:** Infiltrate the Hellstromme HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Steam-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Gomorra Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated robotic fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Deadlands Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Dr. Hellstromme face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
