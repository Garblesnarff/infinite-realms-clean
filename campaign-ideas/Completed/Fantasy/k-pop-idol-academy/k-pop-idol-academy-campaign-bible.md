# K-Pop Idol Academy: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a stage that's being played by an invisible producer. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Seoul' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Perfection Protocol'—an ancient, corporate intelligence who believes that the only true life is 'Polished Symmetry.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Idol' realized that 'Performance' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Choreographies' and 'Academy Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Idol-Energy'—a shimmering force found in clinical whites and metallic silvers, making every 'Genuine Smile' a focus for reality-warping power. To be an Idol or a Trainee is to be a ghost in your own group, knowing that 'The Perfection' is not a progress—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Beat (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Producer creates the world with a single, perfectly synchronized dance move.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Star-Seed Academy' to monitor the idol energy of the provinces.
3.  **The K-Pop Era (Last Age):** A period of hyper-polished dystopian perfection occurs, establishing the MV beauty and the Code of the Idol.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the academy, only to witness their first 'Transformation' reveal.
5.  **The Training-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Protocol Incursion'—a team of auto-tuned shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Debut Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the choreography, you develop 'Symmetry-Sensitivity' and musical 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Luna Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of wise but weary voice and Korean accent offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Grand Gala. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of clinical whites and vibrant neons. The stages are vibrating, and the 'Symmetry-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Manufactured Perfection.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the cracks to appreciate the shine. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal MV?' It explores the hyper-polished beauty of the K-Pop idol academy, the pride in mastering a dance move, and the auto-tuned, resonant voice of a protocol who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the idols and trainees who must navigate the corporate bureaucracies and the intensity of a stage performance to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Star-Seed Academy (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, dance studios, and warm firelight.
*   **The Main Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh training rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and clinical ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Idol Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Performance Space (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of vibrant neons, coordinated group colors, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Center Stage:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Idol-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Fan Row:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Protocol Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and auto-tune.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Song Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a dance studio. You must survive your first 'Protocol Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Luna Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Luna. You must identify the 'Idol-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Choreography Heist:** Infiltrate the Protocol HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Symmetry-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Academy Siege:** Your school is under attack by a coordinated corporate fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Debut Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Perfection Protocol face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Symmetry (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Polished Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Idol Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the idol seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Idol-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Protocol' magic.

*   **Reality-Warping Pulse:** Ranged attack (Psychic). 2d10 damage + Target is confused for 1 round.

(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Order of Progress Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the leader's 'Archaic Script.'
