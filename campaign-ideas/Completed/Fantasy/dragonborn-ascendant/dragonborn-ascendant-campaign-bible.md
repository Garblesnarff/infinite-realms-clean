# Dragonborn Ascendant: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a dragon that's waking up from a long sleep. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Aethelgard' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the return of the 'World-Eater'—an ancient, draconic intelligence who believes that the only true reality is the 'Final Devouring.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Thu'um' realized that 'Speech' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Word Walls' and 'Dragon-Shouts' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Dragon-Energy'—a shimmering force found in dragon bone and ancient stone, making every 'Shout' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Dragonborn or a Greybeard is to be a guardian of the 'Prophecy,' knowing that 'The Voice' is not a spell—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Shout (c. -Infinity):** Akatosh (equivalent) creates the world with a single word.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Throat of the World' monastery to monitor the dragon energy.
3.  **The Dragon War (-1,000 years):** A massive rebellion occurs, establishing the Nordic ruins and the Code of the Voice.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient warrior (the player) is 'Captured' at the border, only to witness their first dragon attack.
5.  **The Word Wall Revelation (Week 1):** You face your first 'Dragon-Soul' absorption—a shimmering force entering your body—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Throat of the World Ascension (Ongoing):** As you climb the 7,000 steps, you develop 'Thu'um Mastery' and draconic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Paarthurnax Revelation (Ongoing):** An ancient dragon of frozen wisdom offers you a 'Prophetic Destiny.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of Sovngarde. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of snow whites and dragon-fire oranges. The sky is splitting, and the 'Dragon-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Destiny.' It is about the 'Heroic Defiance' of the human spirit—the realization that you are the one who chooses the words of the world. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when your soul is that of a dragon, destined to consume the very world you love?' It explores the rugged beauty of the northern landscape, the pride in mastering the Thu'um, and the earth-shaking, resonant voice of a hero who understands that every shout is a prayer. The players are the dragonborn and warriors who must navigate the civil war and the intensity of a dragon-soul absorption to find a truth that stays solid in the cold.

## Factions

### The Greybeards (The Order)
**Type:** Theocratic/Martial Arts Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Arngeir (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Stone monasteries, Thu'um scrolls, and the 'Voice' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Blades, The Dragon-Eaters.

**Type:** Elite/Ancient Shadow Collective
**Leader:** Delphine (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Hidden temples, dragon-slaying katanas (equivalent), and absolute indifference to the Greybeards' philosophy.
**Rivals:** The Greybeards, The World-Eater's Guard.

### The 'Paarthurnax' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Dragon Sages
**Leader:** Paarthurnax (Tier 1 NPC - the mentor).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tier weaponry, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Storm-Cloak' Rebels (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Nordic Communities
**Leader:** Ulfric (equivalent).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Imperial history, 'Cold-Energy' magic, and a refusal to bow to the Empire.
**Rivals:** The Imperial Legion.

### The 'World-Eater' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Draconic Force
**Leader:** Alduin (The World-Eater).
**Asset:** Black scales, reality-tearing fire, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Whiterun (The Hub)
A grand timber and stone monument to architectural grandeur and dragon lore.
*   **The Dragonsreach:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh artificial lighting. **Smell:** Vanilla, old wood, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Cloud District:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Throat of the World (The Battlefield)
The impossible mountain axis of snowy peaks and dangerous shadows.
*   **The 7,000 Steps:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and high-tier trials. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Dragon-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Monastery:** Where the 'Harmonic Convergence' first occurs.

### The Nordic Ruins (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and ancient stone.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### Sovngarde (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Hall of Valor:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Ingestion:** Wake up in a border cell. You must survive your first 'Dragon Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Greybeard Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Arngeir. You must identify the 'Dragon-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Word Wall Heist:** Infiltrate a rival ruin to retrieve a stolen 'Shout-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Civil War Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated rebel fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Dragon-Soul Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Alduin face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of the Dragon (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Dragon-Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Mandala Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Dragon-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Shout' magic.
