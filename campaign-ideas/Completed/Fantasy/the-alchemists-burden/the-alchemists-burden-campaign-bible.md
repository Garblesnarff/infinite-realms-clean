# The Alchemist's Burden: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a circle that's being drawn by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Amestris' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of 'Alchemy'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of equivalent exchange. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Circle' realized that 'Innovation' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Transmutation Circles' and 'State Military Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Alchemical-Energy'—a shimmering force found in golden lightning and glowing circuitry, making every 'Transmutation' a focus for reality-warping power. To be an Alchemist or a Soldier is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Gate' is not a portal—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Circle (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Alchemist creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced transmutation.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Central Command' to monitor the alchemical energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Fullmetal Era (Last Age):** A period of steampunk military fantasy occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Exchange.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) performs a forbidden ritual, only to witness their first 'Gate' reveal.
5.  **The State-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Homunculus Incursion'—a team of regenerative shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Alchemy Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the circles, you develop 'Exchange-Sensitivity' and calligraphic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Edward Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of automail limbs and golden hair offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Truth Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of navy blues and golden lightnings. The machinery is vibrating, and the 'Alchemical-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Equivalent Exchange.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can't gain something without giving something of equal value. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single курса in an eternal book?' It explores the steampunk beauty of the alchemical fantasy, the pride in mastering a transmutation, and the determined, resonant voice of an alchemist who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the alchemists and soldiers who must navigate the military oppressions and the intensity of a homunculus battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The State Alchemists (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Sacred Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Roy Mustang (equivalent - the mentor).
**Asset:** Military uniforms, transmutation gloves, and the 'Continuity' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Homunculus Syndicate, The Rebel Underground.

### The Homunculus Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Extraction Group
**Leader:** Father (equivalent).
**Asset:** Crystalline architecture, 'Regeneration' magic, and absolute control of the city's supply lines.
**Rivals:** The State Alchemists.

### The 'Edward' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Alchemical Sages
**Leader:** Edward (Tier 1 NPC - the alchemist).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, automail limbs, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Armor' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Soul Communities
**Leader:** Alphonse (equivalent).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Alchemy history, 'Armor-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be weighed.
**Rivals:** The Homunculus Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** Truth (The Ultimate Witness).
**Asset:** Reality-tearing plot holes, dark shadows, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

**Edward** (Order - The Lead) - An alchemist whose voice is determined, weighted with guilt, and resonant. **Voice:** Guilt-weighted, technical when discussing alchemy, emotional when discussing lost. **Goal:** To protect the 'Humanity' of his brother and guide the new alchemists. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Alchemical-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his hand claps.

**Roy Mustang** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A military officer whose voice is sharp, quick, and street-smart (reimagined). **Voice:** Urban, modern, and witty banter. **Goal:** To find the 'destination' and stop the homunculus-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the alchemical noise.
**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless operation-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Truth's logic that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient student in practical regional gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the circle ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Central Command (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, military, and golden light.
*   **The War Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and machine oil. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Circle Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Transmutation Regions (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where matter glows and reconfigures.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Alchemical-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Gate of Truth:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Homunculus Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and regeneration.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Exchange Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Homunculus Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Edward Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Edward. You must identify the 'Alchemical-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Circle Heist:** Infiltrate the Homunculus HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Circle-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Central Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated regenerative fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Truth Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Father face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Exchange (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Alchemical Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Circle Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the circle seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Alchemical-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Regenerative' magic.
