# Oaths of the Radiant: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a storm that's being played by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Roshar' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Highstorms'—ancient, celestial discharges that exist within the spirit of the world. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Oaths' realized that 'Honor' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Shardblades' and 'Urithiru Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Stormlight-Energy'—a shimmering force found in electric blue wisps and glowing gemstones, making every 'Ideal' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Radiant or a Parshman is to be a ghost in your own desolation, knowing that 'The Highstorm' is not a weather—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Storm (c. -Infinity):** The Honor God (equivalent) creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced highstorm.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Knights Radiant' to monitor the stormlight energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Stormlight Era (Last Age):** A period of storm-swept epic fantasy occurs, establishing the Michael Whelan beauty and the Code of the Oath.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the Shattered Plains, only to witness their first 'Highstorm' reveal.
5.  **The Plateau-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Chasmfiend'—a creature of massive crustacean plate—in a dark, storm-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Radiant Ascension (Ongoing):** As you swear oaths, you develop 'Stormlight-Sensitivity' and glowing 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Dalinar Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of resonant weathered voice and Shardplate offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Urithiru Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of storm grays and electric blues. The plates are vibrating, and the 'Honor-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Divine Predetermination.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can be a radiant and still be human. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal storm?' It explores the epic beauty of the stormlight archive, the pride in mastering a shardblade, and the resonant, weathered voice of a chronicler who understands that every oath is a lesson. The players are the radiants and parshmen who must navigate the cryptic prophecies and the intensity of a highstorm to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Knights Radiant (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Heroic Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Dalinar (equivalent - the bondsmith).
**Asset:** Shardplate armor, shardblades, and the 'Honor' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Voidbringer Syndicate, The Ghostbloods.

### The Voidbringer Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Divine Group
**Leader:** Odium (equivalent).
**Asset:** Crystalline architecture, 'Void' magic, and absolute control of the city's desolations.
**Rivals:** The Knights Radiant.

### The 'Kaladin' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Storm Sages
**Leader:** Kaladin (Tier 1 NPC - the windrunner).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tier weaponry, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Lightweaver' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Creative Communities
**Leader:** Shallan (The Lightweaver).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Storm history, 'Creative-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be ordered.
**Rivals:** The Voidbringer Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing highstorms, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **Dalinar** (Order - The Lead) - A man whose voice is weathered, resonant, and formal. **Voice:** Resonant, determined, slight weathered quality. **Goal:** To protect the 'Humanity' of Roshar and guide the new radiants. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Stormlight-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his noble presence.
2.  **Odium** (Shadow - The Villain) - A being whose voice is smooth, hierarchical, and measured. **Voice:** Beautiful but hollow, hiding a deep-seated terror. **Goal:** To consolidate all global talent into a single 'Global Desolation' under his control. **Secret:** He is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the original Silicon surge.
3.  **Shallan** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A woman whose voice is sharp, quick, and street-smart (reimagined). **Voice:** Urban, modern, and witty banter. **Goal:** To find her lost partner and stop the storm-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the stormlight clatter.
4.  **The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless desolation-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Odium's logic that he's too professional to share.
5.  **The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient radiant in practical worn gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the oath ritual.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Urithiru (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, spires, and glowing gemstones.
*   **The Bondsmith Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and clinical ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Oath Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Shattered Plains (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of rocky plateaus, deep chasms, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Stormlight-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Honor Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Void Zones (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and highstorms.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Honor Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a chasm. You must survive your first 'Void Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Dalinar Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Dalinar. You must identify the 'Stormlight-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Shardblade Heist:** Infiltrate the Void HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Oath-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Urithiru Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated divine fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Grace Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Odium face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Honor (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Radiant Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Oath Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the oath seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Stormlight-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Apocalypse' magic.
