# CAMPAIGN BIBLE: CHILDREN OF THE STARS

## 1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY

`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`
**Origin Myth: The Great Tuning and the Static**
Before the Zodiac, there was only the *Cacophony*—a screaming, chaotic void where physics and magic warred. The twelve "First Architects" (now worshipped as the Zodiac Lords) did not *create* the world; they *sang* it into a cage. They built the **Cosmic Staff** (the firmament) and pinned the screaming stars into specific constellations to lock the Cacophony away. The "Golden Age" was actually a prison sentence for the universe, forcing it into rigid archetypes (The Warrior, The Healer, The Judge). Now, the song is fading. The silence between the notes (The Void) is leaking through, and the stars are rattling their bars. The Children of the Stars are not saviors; they are living tuning forks designed to reset the frequency—or shatter the instrument entirely.

`[TAG: LORE_TIMELINE]`
**Timeline of the Spheres**

1.  **-3000 AE (After Era):** The **Era of Screaming Colors**. Reality is fluid; gravity is a suggestion. The First Architects enact the *Rite of the Grid*, forcing the universe into 12 distinct metaphysical zones.
2.  **-2500 AE:** The **Exile of Ophiuchus**. A 13th Architect refuses to sing the binding song. They are erased from history, their realm folded into the "Space Between Seconds."
3.  **-1000 AE:** **The First Grand Alignment.** The first twelve Children are vat-grown. They discover the prison-nature of the world. Instead of resetting the cage, they attempt to unmake gravity.
4.  **-999 AE:** **The Day of Falling Ash.** The Zodiac Lords euthanize the First Twelve. The resulting magical backlash turns the center of the world into the **Glass Desert**.
5.  **-800 AE:** **The Great Amnesia.** To prevent rebellion, the Lords modify the atmosphere. Civilians forget the First Twelve; history books are rewritten to call them "The Failed Heroes."
6.  **-500 AE:** **The Clockwork Rebellion.** The Virgo Realm attempts to replace magic with steam and gears. The Leo Realm burns their cities with orbital sun-strikes.
7.  **-200 AE:** **The Weeping Plague.** A virus that makes victims cry until they dehydrate spreads through the Pisces Realm. It is cured only by the tears of a Phoenix (Scorpio).
8.  **-50 AE:** **The Whisper in the Well.** Miners in the Capricorn Realm dig too deep and find a massive, sleeping eyelid. They bury it and execute the miners.
9.  **-21 AE:** **The Second Grand Alignment.** The current PCs are "born" (decanted) in the Star Temple. The sky turns violet for one hour.
10. **Present Day:** The stars in the night sky have begun to drift out of position. Astrologers who notice this are disappearing.

`[TAG: LORE_METAPHYSICS]`
**The Metaphysics of Truth**
The conflict exists because **Order (The Zodiac)** is stagnation, but **Chaos (The Void)** is annihilation. The universe is currently dying of boredom. The souls of mortals are recycled fuel for the Zodiac Barriers. The "Third Path" isn't balance; it is **Synthesis**—breaking the cage to let the stars dance without burning the house down.

## 2. FACTIONS

`[TAG: FACTION_DATA]`

### [The Zodiac Council]
**Type:** Theocratic Oligarchy / Divine Wardens
**Leader:** The High Speaker (A being whose face changes every hour to a different sign).
**Asset:** **The Star-Charts:** Can rewrite local weather or gravity by adjusting a map.
**Rivals:** The Void Cult, The Unfated.

### [The Void Cult (The Silence)]
**Type:** Nihilistic Terrorists
**Leader:** "Zero" (A humanoid shape made of television static).
**Asset:** **Null-Iron:** Metal that passes through magical shields as if they weren't there.
**Rivals:** The Zodiac Council, The Children.

### [The Unfated]
**Type:** Anarcho-Fatalists
**Leader:** Tana the Eraser (A woman who has surgically removed her fingerprints and birthmark).
**Asset:** **Blank Scrolls:** Paper that, when burned, deletes a prophecy from existence.
**Rivals:** The Star Watchers.

### [The Architects of Ophiuchus]
**Type:** Underground Masons / Secret Society
**Leader:** The Serpent Bearer (Unknown identity).
**Asset:** **The 13th Door:** Technology that allows travel between realms without crossing borders.
**Rivals:** All traditional Zodiac Lords.

### [The Chorus of Weeping Spoons]
**Type:** Surrealist Bards / Information Brokers
**Leader:** Maestro Vivaldi (A skeleton conducting an invisible orchestra).
**Asset:** **Echo-Network:** They hear everything said near a silver spoon, anywhere in the world.
**Rivals:** The Leo Aristocracy.

### [The Gear-Heart Collective]
**Type:** Transhumanist Artificers (Virgo extremists)
**Leader:** Prime Integer (A brain in a jar on spider legs).
**Asset:** **Logic-Bombs:** Devices that suppress emotional magic in a 1-mile radius.
**Rivals:** Pisces Dreamers, Cancer Healers.

### [The Red Ledger]
**Type:** Infernal Accountants (Libra/Taurus mix)
**Leader:** The Auditor (A devil in a pinstripe suit).
**Asset:** **Debt Magic:** They can literally buy your "luck" or "years of life" for gold.
**Rivals:** Thieves Guilds, Anarchists.

### [The Garden of Flesh]
**Type:** Bio-mancers (Scorpio/Cancer twisted)
**Leader:** Mother Bloom.
**Asset:** **Grafting:** Can attach monster limbs to human soldiers.
**Rivals:** The Gear-Heart Collective.

### [The Horizon Walkers]
**Type:** Explorers of the impossible (Sagittarius elite)
**Leader:** Captain Noland.
**Asset:** **Maps of What Isn't There:** Navigation tools for the Void.
**Rivals:** Isolationist border guards.

### [The Glass Menagerie]
**Type:** Collectors of Prophecy
**Leader:** The Curator.
**Asset:** **Frozen Moments:** They have trapped specific seconds of time in crystal spheres.
**Rivals:** Anyone trying to change the past.

## 8. MECHANICS

`[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]`
**Zodiac Resonance**
*   **The Bond:** PCs gain +1 to rolls for every other PC within 30ft.
*   **Discord:** If PCs argue (DM discretion), they lose the bonus and take -1.
*   **Compound Magic:** Two PCs can spend an Action to fuse abilities (e.g., Fireball + Heal = Cauterizing Flame).

**Sanity: Cosmic Drift**
*   When witnessing the Void or high-level magic, roll Wisdom Save.
*   **Failure:** Gain 1 point of "Drift."
*   **Effect:** At 5 Drift, you start phasing out of reality (50% miss chance on *your* attacks). At 10 Drift, you become an NPC Void Beast.

**Reputation: The Star-Chart**
*   Track: **Order (Zodiac)** vs. **Chaos (Void)** vs. **Innovation (Third Path)**.
*   Ending depends on which track is highest.

*   **Session 21-23 (The War):** The Anti-Stars invade. Mass combat.
*   **Session 24 (The Choice):** Final Boss (Zero or The Council). The Epilogue.
