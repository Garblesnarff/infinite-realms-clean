# Empire in Flames: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a meat-grinder that's being turned by dark gods. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Aethelgard' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the arrival of 'Chaos'—an ancient, entropic intelligence that believes the only true order is 'Total Corruption.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Empire' realized that 'Human Will' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Order of Sigmar' and 'Imperial Steel' to serve as anchors for the world's survival. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Warp-Energy'—a shimmering force found in sickly greens and purple-pinks, making every 'Mutation' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Witch Hunter or a Soldier is to be a guardian of the 'Wall,' knowing that 'The End' is not a future—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Incursion (c. -Infinity):** The Chaos Gods break through the dimensional rifts, releasing the entropic 'Aethel' into the global atmosphere.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Order of Sigmar' to monitor the warp energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The first 'Warpstone' meteor strikes, releasing the mechanical 'Aethel' into the global atmosphere.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient soldier (the player) wakes up in the mud of a battlefield, only to witness their comrade sprout tentacles.
5.  **The Witch-Hunter Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Chaos Spawn'—a creature of pure body horror—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Middenheim Siege (Ongoing):** As you defend the city, you develop 'Warp-Resistance' and physical 'Tattoos' (scars).
7.  **The Veteran Revelation (Ongoing):** A battle-weary man of grim determination offers you a 'Veteran's Path.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Chaos Wastes. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of steel grays and blood reds. The sky is splitting, and the 'Warp-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Corruption.' It is about the 'Heroic Defiance' of the human spirit—the realization that survival is the ultimate victory. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself is trying to turn you into a monster?' It explores the rugged beauty of the Renaissance warfare, the pride in holding a breach, and the battle-weary, resonant voice of a hero who understands that every day is a gift. The players are the hunters and soldiers who must navigate the reality-breaks and the intensity of a chaos mutation to find a truth that stays solid in the mud.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Order of Sigmar (The Order)
**Type:** Theocratic/Martial Arts Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** The Grand Theogonist (equivalent).
**Asset:** Black leather coats, holy symbols, and the 'Faith' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Chaos Wastes, The Skaven Underground.

### The Chaos Wastes (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/Ancient Shadow Collective
**Leader:** The Chaos Lord (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Spiked armor, daemon engines, and absolute indifference to human life.
**Rivals:** The Order of Sigmar.

### The 'Witch Hunter' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Dark Sages
**Leader:** Victor (equivalent - the mentor).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tier weaponry, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Skaven' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Rat-Folk Communities
**Leader:** Grey Seer (equivalent).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Empire history, 'Warpstone-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be exterminated.
**Rivals:** The Order of Sigmar.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing daemons, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Middenheim (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to medieval architecture and desperate defense.
*   **The High Temple:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh religious rituals. **Smell:** Incense, old stone, and damp wool. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Breach Wall:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Chaos Wastes (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of sickly greens, purple-pinks, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Warp Field:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Warp-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Mutation Pit (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and Chaos.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Empire Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up on a battlefield. You must survive your first 'Chaos Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Veteran Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find the Veteran. You must identify the 'Human-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Warpstone Heist:** Infiltrate a Chaos camp to retrieve a stolen 'Faith-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Middenheim Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated Chaos fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Martyr's Stand:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Chaos Lord face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Sigmar (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Faithful Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Mandala Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Warp-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Chaos' magic.
