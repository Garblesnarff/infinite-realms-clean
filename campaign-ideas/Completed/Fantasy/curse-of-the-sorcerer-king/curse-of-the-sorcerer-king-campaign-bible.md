# Curse of the Sorcerer King: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a wound that's leaking negative emotion. In the heart of the 21st-century Tokyo, the 'Grand Accord' between humanity and the spirit world was shattered by the return of the 'Sorcerer King'—an ancient super-intelligence of pure cursed energy. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Seal' realized that human fear, anger, and grief could manifest as 'Cursed Spirits'—grotesque monsters that haunt the shadows of modern urban life. They forged the 'Sorcerer Schools' to train agents capable of manipulating 'Cursed Energy'—a black-purple aura that flows through the veins of those with the 'Sorcerer Gene.' The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Negative-Energy'—a shimmering force found in traumatic memories and dark electricity, making every punch a focus for reality-warping body horror. To be a Sorcerer or a Vessel is to be a guardian of the 'Seal,' knowing that 'Domain Expansion' is not a spell—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The Sorcerer King is defeated and divided into twenty 'Cursed Fingers.'
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Sorcerer Schools' to monitor the Cursed Energy of the cities.
3.  **The Sukuna Awakening (Present Day - Day 1):** A high school student (the player) accidentally consumes one of the Sorcerer King's fingers, becoming his 'Vessel.'
4.  **The First Squelch (Week 1):** You face your first Grade 4 'Cursed Spirit' in a dark, neon-lit alleyway.
5.  **The Domain Strike Anomaly (Ongoing):** As the player consumes more fingers, they develop black tattoo patterns and 'Wrong Eye Reflections.'
6.  **The Shibuya Incident (Recent):** A massive reality-fracture in the heart of Tokyo reveals an infinite Japanese garden under moonlight.
7.  **The Special Grade Incursion (Upcoming):** A group of intelligent curses led by 'The Shadow' initiates a plan to release the Sorcerer King.
8.  **The Final Domain Expansion (Upcoming):** The Sorcerer King face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff.
9.  **The Present Day:** Tokyo is a high-contrast nightmare of urban grays and black-purple waves. The neon is flickering, and the 'Cursed-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Individual Identity' and 'Cursed Predestination.' It is about the 'Existential Dread' of the human spirit—the realization that 'Everyone's going to die eventually, but we protect people anyway.' The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when you are simultaneously the monster that wants to delete the world?' It explores the sharp geometry of the cityscapes, the visceral horror of the splitting skin, and the darkly humorous voice of a god that speaks in negative emotions. The players are the sorcerers and vessels who must navigate the subculture politics and the intensity of a domain expansion to find a truth that stays human in the light.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Tokyo Sorcerer School (The Order)
**Type:** Theocratic Martial Arts Academy
**Leader:** Satoru Gojo (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Cursed Energy intuition, high-tech (scavenged) tools, and the 'Seal' magic.
**Rivals:** The Cursed Spirit Syndicate, The Higher-Ups.

### The Cursed Spirit Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/Body-Horror Rebel Group
**Leader:** Mahito (A being of dark patterns and age-old cunning).
**Asset:** Cursed Energy manipulation, 'Domain Expansion' magic, and an army of mutated humans.
**Rivals:** The Tokyo Sorcerer School.

### The 'Sukuna' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Cursed Sages
**Leader:** Itadori Yuji (Tier 1 NPC - the vessel).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, physical strength, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The Higher-Ups (The Formalists)
**Type:** High-Society/Bureaucratic Political Entity
**Leader:** Various Elders.
**Asset:** Control of school funding, legal authority, and a practiced warmth that hides cowardice.
**Rivals:** Satoru Gojo.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Cursed Spirits
**Leader:** The Sorcerer King (Tier 1 NPC - figurehead).
**Asset:** Black-purple voids, reality-tearing tendrils, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Tokyo Jujutsu High (The Hub)
A gothic, institutional monument to cursed knowledge and old stone.
*   **The Main Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh artificial lighting. **Smell:** Incense, old stone, and machine oil. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Training Court:** Where the 'Cursed-Energy' is first received. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the blood of the first word.

### Shibuya District (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of neon lights, concrete grays, and black-purple waves.
*   **The Station:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Cursed-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon (equivalent).
*   **The Alleyway:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Cursed Prison (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and non-euclidean geometry.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Domain of the King (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Altar of Bones:** Where the 'Final Choice' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Ingestion:** Wake up in a Shibuya cell. You must survive your first 'Cursed Spirit Ambush' while maintaining your 'Sanity' level.
2.  **The Finger run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Satoru Gojo. You must identify the 'Cursed-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The School Heist:** Infiltrate the Tokyo Sorcerer School to retrieve a stolen 'Cursed-Tool.' You must bypass Megumi's shadows.
4.  **The Shibuya Incursion:** The city is under attack by a reality-warping Special Grade. You must defend the station while the Sorcerer King mounts his counter-strike.
5.  **The Domain Expansion:** Lead a team of sorcerers into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the non-euclidean geometry.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Sorcerer King face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of the Sorcerer
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A simple, black katana that seems to hum with the energy of the first Master.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Cursed-Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Cursed-Energy Capsule
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Domain' magic.
