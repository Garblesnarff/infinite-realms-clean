# Dance Magic Dance: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a puzzle that's being played by an invisible king. In the heart of the 1980s fantasy metropolis of Lexis (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of the city was shattered by the return of the 'Goblin King' (Jareth)—a glam-rock deity of pure performance and theatrical glamour. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Labyrinth' realized that meaning isn't found in a crown, but in the sound of a 'Magic Dance.' They forged the 'Crystal Balls'—metaphysical anchors of reality that turn the simple act of storytelling into a 'Spiritual Awakening.' The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Glamour-Energy'—a shimmering force found in glitter and velvet, making every performance a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Seeker or a Goblin is to be a ghost in a world of impossible geometry and dream-like transitions, knowing that 'The Labyrinth' is not a prison—it's the only way to grow up.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. 1980s):** Jareth takes control of the city's subcultures, establishing the Goblin King's reign.
2.  **The Age of the Labyrinth (c. -10,000 years):** The first 'Founders' establish the stone walls and the Code of Chivalry (equivalent).
3.  **The Sarah Awakening (Last Age):** A young girl wishes her brother away, triggering the first major 'Reality-Break' in the city.
4.  **The Move to the Castle (Present Day - Day 1):** You wake up in a dilapidated Victorian mansion (equivalent) after a recent 'Glamour Reset.'
5.  **The Hoggle Encounter (Week 1):** You meet a grumpy dwarf with warts and bristles who offers you a 'Perpetual Bloom' (equivalent).
6.  **The Escher Castle Reveal (Ongoing):** The leader's hidden base behind a rock face is revealed as a site of 'Impossible Geometry' and stairs everywhere.
7.  **The Masked Ball (Upcoming):** A grand party where the player is manipulated into wearing a costume that defines them.
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Labyrinth. You must choose between immortality and freedom.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of silver golds and midnight blues. The crystal is glowing, and the 'Glamour-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Romantic Idealism' and 'Emotional Maturity.' It is about the 'Theatrical Swashbuckler' of the human spirit—the realization that growing up doesn't mean giving up the magic. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain a part of the Labyrinth when your own nature wants you to run alone?' It explores the tactile realism of the puppets, the visceral horror of the intercision (equivalent), and the theatrical, knowing voice of a man who knows that wisdom is found in every word. The players are the seekers and goblins who must navigate the subculture politics and the intensity of a crystal-ball entry to find a truth that stays fabulous in the light.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Gentle Giants

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Labyrinth (The Hub)
An iconic monument to stone walls, decay, and graffiti masterpieces (reimagined).
*   **The Main Hall:** A site of 'Grand Strategy' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Dust, stale incense, and old wood. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'clack-clack' of wheels on tile.
*   **The Memorial Wall:** A site of 'Visual Representation' and layered history tags.

### The Bog of Eternal Stench (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of murky greens and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Bridge:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and high-speed boat chases. **Smell:** Decay, wet earth, and old gunpowder. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the water.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** A dark, silent place where the shadows move wrong.

### The Escher Castle (The Gauntlet)
A monumental fortress of marble ledges, glass, and steel.
*   **The Throne Room:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** Rows of crystal balls reflecting in the chrome walls. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'thump-thump' of heavy tires (equivalent).

### The Dream Garden (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Rock Formation:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' were first received. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in the Labyrinth. You must escape a 'Goblin Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Crystal run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Sarah. You must identify the 'Respiration Seed' (equivalent) before the flicker.
3.  **The King's Heist:** Infiltrate the Escher Castle to retrieve a stolen 'Glamour-Focus.' You must bypass Jareth's guards.
4.  **The Bog Siege:** Your home is under attack by a coordinated goblin fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Masked Ball:** Lead a team of rebels into the spirit world to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the non-euclidean geometry.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Jareth face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of the Labyrinth
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Magic-Dance' strike (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Glamour-Energy Capsule
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Glamour' magic.
