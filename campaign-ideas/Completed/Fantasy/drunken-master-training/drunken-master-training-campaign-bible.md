# Drunken Master Training: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a balance beam that's being played by a drunk god. In the heart of the 19th-century 'Guangdong' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of martial arts was shattered by the return of the 'Eight Drunkards'—ancient, celestial masters who believe that the only true path to power is through 'Fluidity and Imbalance.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Form' realized that a stiff tree breaks in a storm, but a swaying reed survives. They forged the 'Path of the Eight Drunkards'—a secret martial art that uses 'Drunken-Energy' to perform impossible physical feats. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Spirit-Energy'—a shimmering force found in wine jugs and filthy robes, making every 'Pratfall' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Disciple or a Master is to be a guardian of the 'Balance,' knowing that 'Swaying' is not a weakness—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Sip (c. -Infinity):** The Drunken Immortal creates the world while stumbling through a celestial tavern.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Temple of the Eight' to monitor the fluid energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Jackie Chan Era (1978):** A legendary master (equivalent) survives a 'Training Brutality' ritual, establishing the comedic kung fu code.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A prideful student (the player) arrives at Beggar So's hut, only to be hit with a wine jug.
5.  **The Ladder-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Assassination Attempt' using only a wooden ladder and your 'Comedic Timing' in a dark alleyway.
6.  **The Training Grind (Ongoing):** As you perform increasingly painful and absurd drills, you develop 'Drunken-Energy' and physical 'Tattoos' (bruises).
7.  **The Beggar So Revelation (Ongoing):** A filthy man of impossible feats offers you 'Drunken Wisdom' in a slurred baritone.
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Crimson School. You must choose between your pride and your fluidity.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of earthy browns and vibrant reds. The bottles are clinking, and the 'Drunken-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Rigid Formality.' It is about the 'Comedic Resilience' of the human spirit—the realization that you can be clumsy and still be a hero. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain a master when the world views your training as a joke?' It explores the physical comedy of the Jackie Chan era, the pride in mastering a difficult form, and the slurred, wise voice of a mentor who understands that true power is found in the sway. The players are the disciples and masters who must navigate the training beatings and the intensity of a final duel to find a truth that stays solid in the stumble.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Beggar So's Hut (The Hub)
A filthy, disheveled monument to discarded wine jugs and training equipment.
*   **The Courtyard:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh training rituals. **Smell:** Wine, stale sweat, and damp earth. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bonk' of a wooden pole.
*   **The Wine Cellar:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Guangdong Market (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of vibrant stalls, concrete grays, and black-purple waves.
*   **The Stall Row:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Faction-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Alleyway:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Crimson School (The Gauntlet)
A grand monument to architectural grandeur and rigid formality.
*   **The Training Floor:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented masters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Balance Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Sip:** Wake up in the market. You must survive your first 'Iron-Fist Ambush' using only a wooden bench and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Beggar Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Beggar So. You must identify the 'Drunken-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Jug Heist:** Infiltrate the Crimson School to retrieve a stolen 'Wine-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Training Defense:** Your hut is under attack by a coordinated Imperial fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Drunken Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Master Iron face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
