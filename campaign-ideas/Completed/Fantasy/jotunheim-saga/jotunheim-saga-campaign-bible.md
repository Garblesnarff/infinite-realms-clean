# Jotunheim Saga: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a branch that's being played by an ancient ash tree. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Midgard' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Jotunheim'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirits of the giants. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Runes' realized that 'Giants' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Clans' and 'Stone Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's survival. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Runic-Energy'—a shimmering force found in ice-like features and glowing heat, making every 'Prophecy' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Giant or a God is to be a ghost in your own myth, knowing that 'The Ragnarok' is not an ending—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Branch (c. -Infinity):** Yggdrasil (equivalent) creates the world with a single, massive groan of wood.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Giant Sanctuaries' to monitor the runic energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Eddaic Era (Last Age):** A period of mythic unrest occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Giant.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives in Midgard, only to witness their first 'Giant Incursion' revealing the ice beyond.
5.  **The Runic-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Frost-Wraith'—a creature of cold blue horror—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Clan Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the runes, you develop 'Mythic-Sensitivity' and runic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Þrymr Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of deep resonant voice and aged wisdom offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Ragnarok Field. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of cold blues and volcanic reds. The branches are vibrating, and the 'Mythic-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Predetermined Myths.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can change the story if you have the heart to fight the plot. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal book (equivalent)?' It explores the mythic beauty of the Norse saga, the pride in mastering a runic weapon, and the deep, resonant voice of a giant who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the giants and gods who must navigate the skaldic poetry and the intensity of a mythic choice to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

5.  **The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient giant in practical fur gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the runic ritual.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Midgard (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, community, and warm firelight.
*   **The High Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh mythic rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and woodsmoke. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Runic Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Jotunheim Frontier (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of spectacular vistas, ice blues, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Mythic-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Wall of Giants:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Ragnarok Field (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and cosmic scale.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Mythic Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a giant village. You must survive your first 'Aesir Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Þrymr Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Þrymr. You must identify the 'Mythic-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Rune Heist:** Infiltrate the Aesir HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Runic-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Midgard Siege:** Your village is under attack by a coordinated godly fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Eddaic Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Odin face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Myth (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Skaldic Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Rune Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the rune seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Mythic-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Apocalypse' magic.
