# Forged in Darkness: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a mouth that's being played by an ancient predator. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Great Labyrinth' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the return of the 'Liberators'—an ancient, biomechanical intelligence who believes that the only true life is 'Predatory Adaptation.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Deep' realized that 'Darkness' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Magic' and 'Black Azantium' to serve as anchors for the world's survival. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Abyssal-Energy'—a shimmering force found in bioluminescent moss and monster essence, making every 'Transformation' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Survivor or a Forger is to be a ghost in your own labyrinth, knowing that 'The Light' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Descent (c. -Infinity):** The Great Labyrinth is created to contain the unruly spirits of the primordial monsters.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Liberator' monastery to monitor the abyssal energy of the depths.
3.  **The Berserk Era (Last Age):** A period of dark fantasy horror occurs, establishing the visceral brutality and the Code of the Survivor.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) falls into the labyrinth, only to witness their first 'Behemoth' attack.
5.  **The Labyrinth-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Stalker'—a creature of chitinous plate—in a dark, bioluminescent alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Forging Ascension (Ongoing):** As you forge armor from monster parts, you develop 'Monster-Sensitivity' and physical 'Tattoos' (scars).
7.  **The Guts Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of deep gravelly voice and scarred face offers you a 'Veteran's Path.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Primordial Core. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of deep blacks and stone browns. The caverns are vibrating, and the 'Abyssal-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Hostility.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the darkness to appreciate the light. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself is trying to turn you into a predator in an eternal mouth?' It explores the visceral beauty of the dark fantasy, the pride in mastering a custom weapon, and the deep, resonant voice of a survivor who understands that every scar is a story. The players are the survivors and forgers who must navigate the reality-breaks and the intensity of an abyssal shift to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

### The Deep Survivors (The Order)
**Type:** Theocratic/Martial Arts Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Guts (equivalent - the survivor).
**Asset:** Black Azantium armor, bone weapons, and the 'Labyrinth' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Liberator Syndicate, The Behemoths.

### The Liberator Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Transformation Group
**Leader:** Griffith (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Crystalline architecture, 'Godhand' magic, and absolute control of the city's depths.
**Rivals:** The Deep Survivors.

### The 'Casca' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Labyrinth Sages
**Leader:** Casca (Tier 1 NPC - the guide).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tier weaponry, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Chitin' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Predator Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Labyrinth history, 'Scrappy-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be consumed.
**Rivals:** The Liberator Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing gravity-defying forms, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings with abyssal energy.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Great Labyrinth (The Hub)
A grand subterranean monument to ancient stone and impossible geometry.
*   **The Survivor Camp:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh transformation rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and damp earth. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Azantium Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Deep Caverns (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of deep blacks, bioluminescent greens, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Abyssal-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Magma Chamber:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Liberator Citadel (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to biomechanical horror and Godhand light.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Labyrinth Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in the labyrinth. You must survive your first 'Liberator Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Guts Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Guts. You must identify the 'Abyssal-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Azantium Heist:** Infiltrate the Liberator HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Azantium-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Labyrinth Siege:** Your camp is under attack by a coordinated monster fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Abyss Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Griffith face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Azantium (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Abyssal Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Labyrinth Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the labyrinth seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Abyssal-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Transformation' magic.
