# Hearts of the Dragon and Tiger: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a classroom that's being played by an invisible student. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Academy of the Star' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Seed'—a sentient, emotional consciousness that exists within the human heart. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Bond' realized that 'Emotion' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Relationships' and 'Academy Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Romantic-Energy'—a shimmering force found in golden afternoon light and cherry blossom petals, making every 'Genuine Moment' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Dragon or a Tiger is to be a ghost in your own school, knowing that 'The Confession' is not an ending—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Bloom (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Student creates the school with a single, perfectly written love letter.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Bond-Sanctuaries' to monitor the emotional energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Toradora Era (Last Age):** A period of warm romantic comedy occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Bond.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the academy, only to witness their first 'Tiger Outburst.'
5.  **The Apartment-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Social Incursion'—a team of beautiful but calculating rivals—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Bond Ascension (Ongoing):** As you develop relationships, you develop 'Emotional-Sensitivity' and romantic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Ryuji Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of sharp eyes and gentle voice offers you a 'Domestic Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Grand Gala. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of golden oranges and soft pinks. The classrooms are vibrating, and the 'Heart-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Predetermined Reputations.' It is about the 'Emotional Resilience' of the human spirit—the realization that you need the vulnerability of the heart to appreciate the strength of the fist. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single episode in an eternal romance?' It explores the warm beauty of the slice-of-life anime, the pride in mastering a relationship, and the fierce, resonant voice of a tiger who understands that every outburst is a prayer. The players are the dragons and tigers who must navigate the awkward domesticities and the intensity of a romantic confession to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Academy of the Star (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, community, and warm firelight.
*   **The Dining Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh social rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and woodsmoke. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Bond Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Messy Apartment (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of wealth and profound loneliness.
*   **The Low Table:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Romantic-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Rooftop:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Social Citadel (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and influence.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Bond Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in your classroom. You must survive your first 'Syndicate Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Ryuji Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Ryuji. You must identify the 'Romantic-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Star-Seed Heist:** Infiltrate the Syndicate HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Bond-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Academy Siege:** Your school is under attack by a coordinated social fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Heart Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Ami face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Heart (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Genuine Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Bond Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the bond seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Romantic-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Social' magic.
