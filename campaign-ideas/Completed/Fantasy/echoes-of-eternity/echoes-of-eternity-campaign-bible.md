# Echoes of Eternity: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a tapestry that's being rewoven by an invisible loom. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Chronopolis' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of reality was shattered by the discovery of the 'Chronograph'—a crystalline-clockwork device that allows a person to send messages to the past. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Sequence' realized that 'Time' is not a line, but a tree of infinite worldlines. They forged the 'Reading Steiner' ability—a metaphysical sensitivity that allows a person to retain memories across timeline shifts. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Temporal-Energy'—a shimmering force found in bronze gears and amber light, making every 'Message' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Time-Traveler or a Seeker is to be a ghost in your own history, knowing that 'The Convergence' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Divergence (c. -Infinity):** The Chronograph is activated for the first time, splitting the Alpha worldline from the original sequence.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Convergence Plaza' to monitor the temporal energy of the city.
3.  **The Reading Steiner Awakening (2010):** A team of researchers (equivalent) develops the first 'Divergence Meter' to track worldline shifts.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient architect (the player) wakes up in the Alpha worldline, only to witness a beautiful building flickering between versions.
5.  **The Reality-Rewrite Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Timeline Erasure'—a person fading to transparency—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Reading Steiner Ascension (Ongoing):** As you retain memories across shifts, you develop 'Temporal-Blur' and worldline 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Professor Thane Revelation (Ongoing):** An elderly man of refined academic wisdom offers you a 'Prophetic Path.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Convergence Point. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of amber golds and cyan silvers. The sky is splitting, and the 'Time-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Infinite Variables.' It is about the 'Heroic Responsibility' of the human spirit—the realization that your choices have consequences across infinite realities. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain yourself when the very memories of your existence are being rewritten every time you blink?' It explores the fractured beauty of the worldline shifts, the pride in mastering the Chronograph, and the refined, scholarly voice of a professor who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the travelers and seekers who must navigate the reality-ripples and the intensity of a reading steiner activation to find a truth that stays solid in the change.

## NPC Roster

1.  **Professor Aldric Thane** (Order - The Lead) - An elderly man whose voice is scholarly, energetic, and refined. **Voice:** Refined academic, slight European flavor. **Goal:** To protect the 'Sequence of Eternity' and guide the new travelers. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Temporal-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his technical jargon.

4.  **The Prophet (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose voice is tired, haunted, and desperate. **Voice:** Ages, same as PC, cryptic warnings. **Goal:** To warn the present about the mistakes of the future. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Convergence's logic that he's too professional to share.
5.  **The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient traveler in practical urban gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the chronograph ritual.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Convergence Plaza (The Hub)
A massive, high-contrast monument to architecture, clockwork, and temporal energy.
*   **The Chronograph Chamber:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh temporal rituals. **Smell:** Ozone, old paper, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Worldline Tree:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Chronopolis (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of amber golds, cyan silvers, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Time-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Reality Ripple:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Chronograph Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and clockwork.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Sequence Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Reality Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Thane Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Professor Thane. You must identify the 'Temporal-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Chronograph Heist:** Infiltrate the Convergence HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Chronograph-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Reading Steiner Defense:** Your plaza is under attack by a coordinated worldline fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Convergence Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Mobius and the Shadow face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Sequence (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Reading Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Chronograph Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the chronograph seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Temporal-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Temporal' magic.
