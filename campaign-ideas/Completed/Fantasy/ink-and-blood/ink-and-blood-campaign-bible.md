# Ink and Blood: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a page that's being written by an invisible author. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Scriptorium' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of reality was shattered by the discovery of the 'Wordweaving'—a sentient, ink-based consciousness that allows a person to summon fictional characters and objects into the real world. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Tome' realized that 'Stories' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Books' and 'Ink Reservoirs' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Narrative-Energy'—a shimmering force found in swirling black ink and golden light, making every 'Reading' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Wordweaver or a Reader is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Exchange' is not a price—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Word (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Author creates the world with a single, perfectly chosen noun.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Scriptorium' to monitor the narrative energy of the cities.
3.  **The Inkheart Era (Last Age):** A period of metafictional magic occurs, establishing the hyperreal beauty and the Code of the Reader.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the Scriptorium, only to witness their first 'Character Manifestation.'
5.  **The Ink-Sea Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Ink Corruption'—a team of liquid text-shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Wordweaving Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the reading, you develop 'Narrative-Sensitivity' and ink-stained 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Cordelia Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of mature British accent and academic wisdom offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Paper Palace. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of hyper-saturated colors and muted natural tones. The pages are vibrating, and the 'Story-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Endless Fictions.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that creation always has a cost. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal book?' It explores the hyperreal beauty of the fictional quarter, the pride in mastering a wordweaving spell, and the academic, resonant voice of a reader who understands that every word is a prayer. The players are the weavers and readers who must navigate the ink corruption and the intensity of a character summon to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless ink-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Crimson King's plot that he's too professional to share.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Scriptorium (The Hub)
A grand Victorian monument to architecture, libraries, and ink reservoirs.
*   **The Reading Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh narrative rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old paper, and machine oil. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Ink Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Fictional Quarter (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where castles sit next to spaceships and cottages.
*   **The Genre Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Narrative-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Paper Palace:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Grey Library (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and trapped souls.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Narrative Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in the Scriptorium. You must survive your first 'Ink Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Cordelia Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Cordelia Writhe. You must identify the 'Narrative-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Tome Heist:** Infiltrate the Crimson King's HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Wordweaving-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Guild Siege:** Your library is under attack by a coordinated fictional fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Meta Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Crimson King face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Word (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Narrative Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Tome Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the tome seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Narrative-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Fictional' magic.
