# The Broken Knight: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a pedestal that's being held by an invisible titan. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Gotham' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of justice was shattered by the discovery of 'The Shatterbane'—an ancient, entropic intelligence who believes that the only true life is 'Absolute Brokenness.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Watch' realized that 'Heroism' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Cowls' and 'Gothic Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Redemptive-Energy'—a shimmering force found in defiant golds and blood reds, making every 'Recovery' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Nightwatch or a Successor is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Break' is not an ending—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Shine (c. -Infinity):** The Alpha God creates the world with a single, perfectly rendered stroke.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Nightwatch Agency' to monitor the heroic energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Knightfall Era (Last Age):** A period of gothic noir occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Cowl.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the city, only to witness their first 'Shatterbane' break.
5.  **The Alleyway-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Successor Incursion'—a team of aggressive armor shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Redemption Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the recovery, you develop 'Trauma-Sensitivity' and ritual 'Tattoos' (scars).
7.  **The Nightwatch Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of gravelly wounded voice and spine brace offers you a 'Sacred Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Cathedral Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of deep blacks and defiant golds. The buildings are vibrating, and the 'Heroic-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Brokenness.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that wholeness requires darkness. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the gothic noir, the pride in mastering a redemptive choice, and the gravelly, resonant voice of a hero who understands that every moment is a prayer. The players are the nightwatch and successors who must navigate the bureaucratic oppressions and the intensity of a redemption shift to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

### The Nightwatch Order (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Sacred Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** The Nightwatch (broken/recovered).
**Asset:** Ancient cowls, gothic sanctuaries, and the 'Continuity' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Shatterbane Syndicate, The Successor Coalition.

### The Shatterbane Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Extraction Group
**Leader:** Shatterbane (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Spiked architecture, 'Breaking' magic, and absolute control of the city's quiet zones.
**Rivals:** The Nightwatch Order.

### The 'Jean-Paul' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Heroic Sages
**Leader:** Jean-Paul (Tier 1 NPC - the successor).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, aggressive armor, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Hidden' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Civilian Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Nightwatch history, 'Hidden-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be broken.
**Rivals:** The Shatterbane Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing plot holes, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**The Nightwatch** (Order - The Lead) - A being whose voice is gravelly, wounded, and resonant. **Voice:** Wounded determination, gravelly timbre, introspective tone. **Goal:** To protect the 'Heroic Balance' and guide the new nightwatch. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Redemptive-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his recovery speeches.

**Jean-Paul** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A being whose voice is intense, aggressive, and street-smart. **Voice:** Urban, modern, and witty banter. **Goal:** To find the 'destination' and stop the successor-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the city noise.
**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless recovery-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Shatterbane's logic that he's too professional to share.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Scriptorium (The Hub)
A grand urban monument to architecture, libraries, and candlelight.
*   **The Reading Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and floor wax. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Cowl Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Gothic Streets (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each space has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Redemptive-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Rainy Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Shatterbane Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and distribution.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Shatterbane Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Nightwatch Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find The Nightwatch. You must identify the 'Redemptive-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Cowl Heist:** Infiltrate the Shatterbane HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Cowl-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The City Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated aggressive fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Redemption Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Shatterbane face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Choice (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Sacred Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Cowl Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the cowl seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Redemptive-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Breaking' magic.
