# Death Flags & Destiny: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a script that's being written by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century Royal Academy, the 'Grand Accord' of reality was shattered by the discovery of 'The Tome of Fated Paths'—a magical artifact that contains the entire story of the 'Romance Game' we are all living in. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Story' realized that true power isn't found in magic, but in the 'Relationship Meters' of the heart. They forged the 'Death Flags'—metaphysical triggers of doom that occur whenever a character deviates from their 'Scripted Role.' The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Fame-Energy' (equivalent)—a shimmering force found in floating hearts and dialogue boxes, making every choice a focus for reality-bending power. To be a Villainess or a Prince is to be a ghost in a world of elaborate ball gowns and soft pastels, knowing that 'True Love' is the only way to rewrite your own ending.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The Tome of Fated Paths is created, establishing the original narrative structure.
2.  **The Age of the Story (-10,000 years):** The Academy is established to monitor the Relationship Meters of the elite.
3.  **The Villainess Awakening (Present Day - Day 1):** A high-society student (the player) regains memories of her 'Past Life' and realizes she is the villainess of a romance game.
4.  **The First Death Flag (Week 1):** You trigger a 'Red Outlined' warning after accidentally bumping into the pure-hearted protagonist.
5.  **The Prince Cedric Encounter (Ongoing):** The earnest and warm Prince Cedric offers a 'Safe Moment' and a +2 relationship increase.
6.  **The Lady Seraphina Reveal (Ongoing):** The intelligent friend reveals she can also see the 'Glowing Script' describing what should happen.
7.  **The Grand Ball Chaos (Upcoming):** All relationship meters become visible simultaneously as multiple death flags trigger in sequence.
8.  **The Destroying the Tome (Upcoming):** A final, reality-bending standoff against Headmaster Wordwright to shatter the script.
9.  **The Present Day:** The Academy is a high-contrast nightmare of soft lavenders and ominous reds. The flower petals are drifting, and the 'True Love' pulse is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Free Will' and 'Scripted Predestination.' It is about the 'Romantic Comedy' of the human spirit—the realization that you can earn a happy ending even if the story says you shouldn't. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you rewrite your own destiny when the world itself is trying to kill you for it?' It explores the elegant linework of the shoujo manga, the visceral horror of the death flag activation, and the theatrical, knowing voice of a narrator who understands that fairy tales teach real lessons. The players are the villainesses and heroes who must navigate the subculture politics and the intensity of a choice-box entry to find a truth that stays fabulous in the light.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Royal Academy (The Hub)
A grand European-inspired monument to architectural grandeur and high magic.
*   **The Main Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh artificial lighting. **Smell:** Vanilla, old wood, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Library:** A site of 'Visual Representation' and glowing script text.

### The Academy Garden (The Battlefield)
A hyper-real forest monument to weaponized nature and meticulously maintained roses.
*   **The Arbors:** A place where 'Relationship Level Ups' and sudden saturation occur. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the blood of the first word. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the butterflies.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** A dark, silent place where the shadows move wrong.

### The Grand Ballroom (The Gauntlet)
A royal gold and white marble monument to opulent arrogance and crystal chandeliers.
*   **The Dance Floor:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** Rows of mirrors reflecting in the character's cold eyes. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'thump-thump' of waltz music.

### The Narrative Space (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Center:** Where the 'Final Choice' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Reaping:** Wake up in the Academy. You must survive your first 'Death Flag Activation' while maintaining your 'Humanity' level.
2.  **The Romance run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Prince Cedric. You must identify the 'Heartland-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Tome Heist:** Infiltrate the School Library to retrieve a stolen 'Script-Focus.' You must bypass the Headmaster's guards.
4.  **The Grand Ball Siege:** The Academy is under attack by a reality-warping drone (equivalent). You must defend the Ballroom while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Choice-Box Revelation:** Enter the Narrative Space undercover. You must find your own 'Soul-Memory' without alerting the Tome.
6.  **The Final Reset:** The Academy's central crystal has begun its final 'Reset.' You must defend the Spires while the self-destruct activates.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Headmaster face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff at the top of the spires. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Choice
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Script-Break' strike (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Relationship Capsule
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Death Flag' magic.
