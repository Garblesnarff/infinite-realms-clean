# Empire of Endless Courses: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a menu that's being served by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Gourmet Gate' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of taste was shattered by the return of the 'Guild of the Iron Fork'—an ancient, corporate intelligence who believes that the only true culinary art is 'Standardized Profit.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Flavor' realized that 'Food' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Recipes' and 'Kitchen Ballet' to serve as anchors for the world's culture. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Culinary-Energy'—a shimmering force found in polished surfaces and imperial golds, making every 'Successful Service' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Chef or an Inspector is to be a guardian of the 'Empire,' knowing that 'The Bland' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Taste (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Chef creates the world with a single, perfectly seasoned dish.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Imperial Guild' to monitor the culinary energy of the cities.
3.  **The Rusty Spoon Era (Last Age):** A period of culinary decline occurs, establishing the worn-brown aesthetic and the Code of the Kitchen.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient chef (the player) arrives at the Rusty Spoon, only to witness a beautiful dish being 'Hostilely Taken' by a rival empire.
5.  **The Kitchen-Rush Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Corporate Sabotage'—a team of rival sous-chefs—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Plate Ascension (Ongoing):** As you earn your first 'Imperial Plate,' you develop 'Taste-Sensitivity' and culinary 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Margaux Revelation (Ongoing):** A cold corporate woman of Meryl Streep-like gravitas offers you a 'Corporate Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Grand Gala. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of polished golds and tarnished metals. The kitchens are vibrating, and the 'Flavor-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Corporate Branding.' It is about the 'Creative Resilience' of the human spirit—the realization that a meal is more than just a transaction. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you maintain your soul when the universe itself views your art as a 15% profit margin?' It explores the fast-paced beauty of the kitchen ballet, the pride in earning a Plate, and the cold, measured voice of a narrator who understands that every ingredient is a choice. The players are the chefs and inspectors who must navigate the hostile takeovers and the intensity of a final inspection to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Grand Gala (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to polished surfaces and imperial fine dining.
*   **The High Table:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh culinary rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Recipe Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Rusty Spoon (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of worn browns, tarnished metals, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Kitchen Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Culinary-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Alleyway:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Guild Citadel (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and standardized profit.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Empire Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in the Rusty Spoon. You must survive your first 'Guild Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Giovanni Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Chef Giovanni. You must identify the 'Culinary-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Recipe Heist:** Infiltrate the Guild HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Recipe-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Grand Service:** Your restaurant is under attack by a coordinated corporate fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Plate Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Margaux DuBois face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Flavor (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Seasoned Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Plate Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the plate seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Culinary-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Bland' magic.

(CR 2). Humanoid. HP 30, AC 13 (Silk patches).

(CR 5). Construct. HP 80, AC 16.
**Abilities:**
*   **Ominous Bland Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet takes 1d10 psychic damage at the start of their turn.
*   **Reality-Warping Pulse:** Ranged attack (Psychic). 2d10 damage + Target is confused for 1 round.

(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Order of Progress Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the leader's 'Archaic Script.'
*   **The Final Move:** Once per combat, Margaux can force an enemy to take an action that serves her own glory.
