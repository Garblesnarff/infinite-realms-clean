# Kingsmen of the Crystal Crown: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a suit that's being tailored by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'London' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of etiquette was shattered by the discovery of the 'Crystal Crown'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the bespoke. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Stitch' realized that 'Manners' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Umbrellas' and 'Savile Row Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Bespoke-Energy'—a shimmering force found in navy charcoals and burgundy golds, making every 'Pub Fight' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Kingsman or a Statesman is to be a ghost in your own tailor shop, knowing that 'The Brawl' is not a chaos—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Stitch (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Tailor creates the world with a single, perfectly executed seam.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Kingsman Agency' to monitor the refined energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Bespoke Era (Last Age):** A period of refined brutality occurs, establishing the fashion-photography beauty and the Code of the Gentleman.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the tailor shop, only to witness their first 'Manners Maketh Man' brawl.
5.  **The Church-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Valentine Incursion'—a team of high-tech chrome shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Kingsman Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the etiquette, you develop 'Bespoke-Sensitivity' and tailored 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Arthur Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of perfect received pronunciation and unflappable wisdom offers you a 'Gentleman's Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Worldwide Operations. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of rich navy charcoals and explosive contrast reds. The suits are vibrating, and the 'Bespoke-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Silicon Valley Enthusiasts.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that refinement is the ultimate form of brutality. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal MV (equivalent)?' It explores the bespoke beauty of the refined brutality, the pride in mastering a gentleman's duel, and the perfect, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the kingsmen and statesmen who must navigate the global conspiracies and the intensity of a single-take ultraviolence nightmare to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Savile Row (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, tailors, and warm firelight.
*   **The Fitting Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and expensive wool. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Stitch Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Worldwide Operations (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of global cities and explosive contrast.
*   **The Church Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Bespoke-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Pub Row:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Valentine Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and high-tech chrome.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Refinement Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a tailor shop. You must survive your first 'Valentine Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Arthur Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Lord Arthur. You must identify the 'Bespoke-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Umbrella Heist:** Infiltrate the Valentine HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Stitch-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Savile Row Siege:** Your shop is under attack by a coordinated robotic fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Gentleman Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Richmond Valentine face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Refinement (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Bespoke Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Stitch Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the stitch seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Bespoke-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Silicon' magic.
