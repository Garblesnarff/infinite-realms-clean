# Spirit Detective Chronicles: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a ring that's being played by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Tokyo' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Spirit-Energy'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the human spirit. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Form' realized that 'Spirit Combat' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Licenses' and 'Spirit Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's spiritual stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Spirit-Energy'—a shimmering force found in blue-white beams and pompadour hairstyles, making every 'Spirit Gun' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Detective or a Demon is to be a ghost in your own city, knowing that 'The Tournament' is not a win—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Squelch (c. -Infinity):** The Spirit King (equivalent) creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced spirit wave.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Spirit World' to monitor the spiritual energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Hakusho Era (Last Age):** A period of urban fantasy occurs, establishing the character-driven martial arts and the Code of the Detective.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Killed' in an accident, only to witness their first 'Spirit Investigation.'
5.  **The Arena-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Demon-Incursion'—a team of dark purple shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Detective Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the spirit energy, you develop 'Will-Sensitivity' and spiritual 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Yusuke Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of spiky hair and green uniform offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Demon World. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of urban grays and demonic reds. The arenas are vibrating, and the 'Spirit-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Divine Bureaucracy.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the strength of the fist to appreciate the wisdom of the heart. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single match in an eternal tournament?' It explores the beautiful urban fantasy, the pride in mastering a spirit weapon, and the street-smart, resonant voice of a detective who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the detectives and demons who must navigate the celestial politics and the intensity of a demon battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Living World (The Hub)
A grand urban monument to architecture, streets, and school uniforms.
*   **The School Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and floor wax. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Spirit Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Demon Realm (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where water acts as a thick medium with visible currents (equivalent).
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Spirit-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Valley of the End:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Dark Tournament (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and demon spectators.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Spirit Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a school cell. You must survive your first 'Demon Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Yusuke Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Yusuke. You must identify the 'Spirit-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Artifact Heist:** Infiltrate the Demon HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Spirit-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Tokyo Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated demonic fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Spirit Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Toguro face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Form (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Spirit Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The License Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the license seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Spirit-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Demonic' magic.
