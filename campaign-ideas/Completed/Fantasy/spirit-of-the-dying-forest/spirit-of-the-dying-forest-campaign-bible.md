# Spirit of the Dying Forest: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a seed that's being tended by an invisible gardener. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Nibel Forest' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Seed'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirits of the forest. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Deep' realized that 'Light' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Magic' and 'Spirit Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Luminescent-Energy'—a shimmering force found in white glowing spirits and golden-white lights, making every 'Restoration' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Spirit or a Mother is to be a ghost in your own home, knowing that 'The Decay' is not a poison—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Squelch (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Spirit creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced division in the dark.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Deep Sanctuaries' to monitor the luminescent energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Ori Era (Last Age):** A period of painterly fantasy occurs, establishing the Michael Whelan beauty (equivalent) and the Code of the Spirit.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Adopted' by a motherly creature, only to witness their first 'Decay' reveal.
5.  **The Ginso-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Kuro Incursion'—a team of black-feather shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Restoration Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the environment, you develop 'Light-Sensitivity' and cellular 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Ori Revelation (Ongoing):** A small white spirit of ethereal glow and cat-like ears offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Spirit Lodge. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of fresh greens and purple corruptions. The ruins are vibrating, and the 'Heart-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Decay.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the love of the family to appreciate the strength of the oath. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kursi in an eternal book?' It explores the warm beauty of the painterly fantasy, the pride in mastering a relationship, and the wordless, resonant voice of a mentor who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the spirits and mothers who must navigate the awkward domesticities and the intensity of a divine grace to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.

**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless operation-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Kuro's logic that he's too professional to share.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Nibel Forest (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, ruins, and hand-painted beauty.
*   **The Family Hollow:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and fresh water. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Spirit Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Ginso Tree (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each region has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Luminescent-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Decayed Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Stagnant Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and information overload.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a forest hollow. You must survive your first 'Stagnant Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Naru Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Naru. You must identify the 'Luminescent-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Bond Heist:** Infiltrate the Stagnant's HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Bond-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Forest Siege:** Your family is under attack by a coordinated stagnant fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Spirit Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Kuro face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Hearth (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Luminescent Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Bond Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the bond seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Luminescent-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Stagnation' magic.
