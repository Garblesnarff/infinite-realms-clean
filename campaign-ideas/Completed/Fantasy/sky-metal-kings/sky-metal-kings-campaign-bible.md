# Sky Metal Kings: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a battery that's being charged by a divine bolt. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Storm-Eye' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Storm King'—an ancient, celestial consciousness who believes that the only true life is 'Absolute Voltage.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Riff' realized that 'Aerial Motocross' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Storm-Metal Bikes' and 'Lightning Roads' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Electric-Energy'—a shimmering force found in glowing blue circuits and crackling cyan trails, making every 'Aerial Trick' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Rider or a Storm King is to be a ghost in your own island, knowing that 'The Final Trick' is not a win—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Rev (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Rider creates the world with a single, perfectly executed 1080 spin through the void.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Storm-Sanctuaries' to monitor the electric energy of the provinces.
3.  **The X Games Era (Last Age):** A period of adrenaline-fueled aerial freedom occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Rider.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) lands on a floating island, only to witness their first 'Lightning Road' formation.
5.  **The Circuit-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Dominion Incursion'—a team of industrial iron shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Metal Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the tricks, you develop 'BPM-Sensitivity' and electrical 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Magnus Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of gruff Norwegian accent and scarred bike offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Storm King Arena. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of storm grays and electricity purples. The islands are vibrating, and the 'Sky-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Industrial Oppression.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that freedom is worth more than gold. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal game?' It explores the beautiful freedom of the aerial motocross, the pride in mastering a storm-metal bike, and the energetic, resonant voice of a rider who understands that every beat is a prayer. The players are the riders and kings who must navigate the industrial oppressions and the intensity of a trick-for-trick battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Storm King Arena (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, storms, and ancient voltage.
*   **The Command Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Metal Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Floating Islands (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each island has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Electric-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Lightning Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Ferrum Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and industrial iron.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Flight Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Ferrum Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Magnus Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Magnus. You must identify the 'Electric-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Bike Heist:** Infiltrate the Ferrum HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Metal-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Arena Siege:** Your academy is under attack by a coordinated silent fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Storm King Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Storm King face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
