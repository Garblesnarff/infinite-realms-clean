# Fimbulwinter's Chains: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a snowflake that's being played by an ice queen. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Midgard' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the return of the 'Empress of Ending Seasons'—an ancient, cryogenic intelligence who believes that the only true life is 'Absolute Stasis.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Hearth' realized that 'Warmth' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Seasons' and 'Underground Cities' to serve as anchors for the world's survival. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Seasonal-Energy'—a shimmering force found in ice blues and white frosts, making every 'Spring Ghost' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Seeker or a Hearth-Guardian is to be a ghost in your own home, knowing that 'The Winter' is not a season—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Freeze (c. -Infinity):** The Empress creates the eternal winter to contain the unruly spirits of the spring and summer.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Hearth-Sanctuaries' to monitor the thermal energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Fimbulwinter Era (Last Age):** A period of frozen desolation occurs, establishing the crystalline beauty and the Code of the Hearth.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the rift, only to witness their first 'Season-Spirit' being pulled into the ice.
5.  **The Frost-Palace Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Ice-Crawl'—a creature of absolute cold—in a dark, aurora-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Seasonal Ascension (Ongoing):** As you collect spirits, you develop 'Thermal-Sensitivity' and crystalline 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Winter Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of inhuman perfection and ice-cracking voice offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Frost Palace. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of monochrome blues and whites. The towers are vibrating, and the 'Seasonal-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Stasis.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the decay of autumn to appreciate the life of spring. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself is trying to turn you into a statue of ice?' It explores the crystalline beauty of the frozen desolation, the pride in maintaining a fire, and the inhumanly beautiful, resonant voice of an empress who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the seekers and guardians who must navigate the reality-breaks and the intensity of a seasonal shift to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**The Empress of Ending Seasons** (Shadow - The Villain) - A woman whose voice is beautiful but inhumanly cold. **Voice:** Female, reverb, ice cracking sounds, ancient and weary. **Goal:** To maintain the 'Eternal Stasis' and prevent the spring from returning. **Secret:** She is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the original Silicon surge.

**Fury of Summer** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A man whose voice is crackling, aggressive, and hot. **Voice:** Male energy, crackling, rage barely contained. **Goal:** To destroy the ice queen and stop the seasonal-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the blizzard static.
**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless frost-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Empress's crown that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient researcher in practical winter gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the seasonal ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Underground City (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to warm reds, geothermal fire, and survival.
*   **The Hearth-Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh thermal rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and woodsmoke. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Heat Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Frozen Surface (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of ice blues, monochrome whites, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Blizzard Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Seasonal-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Obsidian Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Frost Palace (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and crystalline perfection.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Seasonal Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in an underground city. You must survive your first 'Frost Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Fire Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find the Fire-Sage. You must identify the 'Seasonal-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Spirit Heist:** Infiltrate the Frost Palace to retrieve a stolen 'Seasonal-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Surface Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated frost fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Seasonal Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Empress of Ending Seasons face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Hearth (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Seasonal Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Hearth Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the hearth seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Seasonal-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Frost' magic.

(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
