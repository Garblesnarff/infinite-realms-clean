# Magma Heart Rebellion: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a furnace that's being played by a primordial fire. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Great Labyrinth' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Seed'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the planetary magma. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Forge' realized that 'Heat' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Magic' and 'Volcanic Tunnels' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Magma-Energy'—a shimmering force found in living flames and glowing ember eyes, making every 'Rebellion' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Slave or a Revolutionary is to be a ghost in your own volcano, knowing that 'The Liberation' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Spark (c. -Infinity):** The Primordial Infernum creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced eruption.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Deep Sanctuaries' to monitor the luminescent energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Duergar Era (Last Age):** A period of industrial oppression occurs, establishing the volcanic hell beauty and the Code of the Slave.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) falls into the forges, only to witness their first 'Revolutionary' outburst.
5.  **The Forge-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Duergar Incursion'—a team of gray-skinned shadows—in a dark, lava-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Rebellion Ascension (Ongoing):** As you break your chains, you develop 'Fire-Sensitivity' and revolutionary 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Ember Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of passionate fire voice and white-gold flames offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Primordial Core. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of lava oranges and obsidian blacks. The tunnels are vibrating, and the 'Fire-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Industrial Slavery.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the destruction of the fire to appreciate the renewal of the ash. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal machine?' It explores the beautiful volcanic hell of the fire genasi, the pride in mastering a revolutionary anthem, and the passionate, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every spark is a prayer. The players are the slaves and revolutionaries who must navigate the chemical clouds and the intensity of a mass uprising to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Volcanic Tunnels (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, fire, and bio-luminescent beauty.
*   **The Slave Forges:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and damp earth. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Flow Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Great Labyrinth (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where water acts as a thick medium with visible currents.
*   **The Current Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Luminescent-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Surface Tension Ceiling:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Mining Zone (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to chemical clouds and industrial scarring.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Flow Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a forge. You must survive your first 'Duergar Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Nightbloom Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Archdruid Nightbloom. You must identify the 'Luminescent-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Current Heist:** Infiltrate the Mining HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Current-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Garden Siege:** Your droplet is under attack by a coordinated industrial fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Division Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Director Copper-Vein face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Flow (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Luminescent Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Current Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the current seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Aquatic-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Industrial' magic.
