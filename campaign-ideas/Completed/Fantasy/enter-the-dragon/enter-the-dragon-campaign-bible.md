# Enter the Dragon: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's an arena that's being played by a master hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Han's Island' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of martial arts was shattered by the discovery of the 'Dragon-Energy'—a sentient, celestial consciousness that exists within the human spirit. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Form' realized that 'Combat' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Techniques' and 'Island Fortress' to serve as anchors for the world's survival. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Spirit-Energy'—a shimmering force found in silk robes and iron gauntlets, making every 'Strike' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Martial Artist or a Shaolin Monk is to be a guardian of the 'Flow,' knowing that 'The Defeat' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Strike (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Master creates the world with a single, perfectly executed punch.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Shaolin Temple' to monitor the spirit energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Han's Island Era (Last Age):** A period of criminal decadence occurs, establishing the exotic danger and the Code of the Tournament.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient warrior (the player) arrives at Han's Island, only to witness their first tournament match being 'Dishonored.'
5.  **The Mirror-Maze Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Shadow-Fighter'—a creature of disorienting reflections—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Dragon Ascension (Ongoing):** As you win matches, you develop 'Spirit-Sensitivity' and martial 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Han Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of cold menace and refined silk robes offers you a 'Criminal Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Hall of Mirrors. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of crimson golds and azure tropical skies. The arenas are vibrating, and the 'Dragon-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Criminal Decadence.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the human spirit—the realization that you are the master of your own destiny. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single match in an eternal tournament?' It explores the athletic beauty of the kung fu cinema, the pride in mastering a technique, and the commanding, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every strike is a prayer. The players are the artists and monks who must navigate the sinister conspiracies and the intensity of a mirror-maze fight to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Han's Island (The Hub)
A grand tropical monument to pagoda architecture and criminal decadence.
*   **The Main Courtyard:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh martial rituals. **Smell:** Incense, vanilla, and salt spray. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Island Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Tournament Arena (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of crimson reds, gold banners, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Stone Platform:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Spirit-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Mirror-Maze:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Underground Complex (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and drug labs.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Flow Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up on the island docks. You must survive your first 'Han Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Lee Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Master Lee. You must identify the 'Spirit-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Gauntlet Heist:** Infiltrate the Underground Complex to retrieve a stolen 'Dragon-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Island Siege:** Your fortress is under attack by a coordinated syndicate fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Mirror Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Master Han face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Flow (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Fluid Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Dragon Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the dragon seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Spirit-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Drug' magic.
