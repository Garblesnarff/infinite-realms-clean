# The Crystal of Truth: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a shard that's being held by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Thra' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of 'The Crystal of Truth'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the planet. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Balance' realized that 'Harmony' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Shards' and 'Skeksis Castles' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Crystalline-Energy'—a shimmering force found in rainbow lights and earthly textures, making every 'Great Conjunction' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Gelfling or a Skeksis is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Darkening' is not a shadow—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Shine (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Scribe creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced sentence (equivalent).
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Crystal Spire' to monitor the crystalline energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Jim Henson Era (Last Age):** A period of puppet fantasy occurs, establishing the tactile beauty and the Code of the Balance.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Reaped' into Thra, only to witness their first 'Crystal Crack' reveal.
5.  **The Castle-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Skeksis Incursion'—a team of vulture-reptile shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Shard Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the environment, you develop 'Light-Sensitivity' and ritual 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Aughra Revelation (Ongoing):** A being of one-eyed wisdom and wild-haired Offers you a 'Sacred Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Great Conjunction. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant rainbows and oppressive blacks. The crystals are vibrating, and the 'True-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Choice.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that wholeness requires balance. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kursi in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the puppet fantasy, the pride in mastering a crystalline device, and the mythic, resonant voice of an elder who understands that every moment is a prayer. The players are the gelflings and skeksis who must navigate the mythological politics and the intensity of a crystal integration to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Scriptorium (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, libraries, and candlelight.
*   **The Reading Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and expensive ink. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Wash Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Spirit Town (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each space has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Spirit-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Flooded Plain:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Yubaba Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and contracts.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Yubaba Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Haku Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Haku. You must identify the 'Spirit-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Token Heist:** Infiltrate the Yubaba HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Token-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Bathhouse Siege:** Your sanctuary is under attack by a coordinated extraction fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Contract Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Yubaba face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Balance (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Sacred Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Token Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the token seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Spirit-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Industrial' magic.
