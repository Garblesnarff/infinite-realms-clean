# Saga of the Stone-Born: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a glacier that's being played by an ancient frost. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Midgard' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Stone-Born'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the ice. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Runes' realized that 'Resilience' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Spears' and 'Fur Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's survival. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Elemental-Energy'—a shimmering force found in ice-blue glows and mammoth furs, making every 'Survival Choice' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Hunter or an Elder is to be a ghost in your own myth, knowing that 'The Ice Age' is not a weather—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Freeze (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Frost creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced discharge through the void.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Stone-Born Sanctuaries' to monitor the elemental energy of the provinces.
3.  **The God of War Era (Last Age):** A period of gritty mythic realism occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Stone.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Adopted' by a stone-born tribe, only to witness their first 'Elemental Power' reveal.
5.  **The Glacier-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Sabertooth Incursion'—a team of eight-legged nightmares (equivalent)—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Survival Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the environment, you develop 'Sound-Sensitivity' and runic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Kratos Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of deep resonant voice and muscular presence offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Primordial Core. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of cold blues and white frosts. The glaciers are vibrating, and the 'Elemental-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Brutality.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the darkness to appreciate the light. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal kurs (equivalent)?' It explores the raw beauty of the ice age survival, the pride in mastering a functional weapon, and the deep, resonant voice of a hunter who understands that every cent is a gift. The players are the hunters and elders who must navigate the domestic oppressions and the intensity of a giant encounter to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Midgard (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, glaciers, and mythic realism.
*   **The Family Courtyard:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and fresh snow. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Stone Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Great Glacier (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each realm has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Elemental-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Ice Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Stagnant Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and information overload.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a chasm. You must survive your first 'Stagnant Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Kratos Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Kratos. You must identify the 'Elemental-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Spear Heist:** Infiltrate the Stagnant's HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Spear-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Midgard Siege:** Your village is under attack by a coordinated stagnant fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Survival Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Stagnatio face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Resilience (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Elemental Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Current Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the current seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Elemental-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Stagnation' magic.
