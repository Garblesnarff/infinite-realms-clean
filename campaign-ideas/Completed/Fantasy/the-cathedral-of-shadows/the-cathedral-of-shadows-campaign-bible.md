# The Cathedral of Shadows: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a cathedral that's being played by an invisible organist. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archetypa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Cathedral of Shadows'—an ancient, celestial architecture that exists within the spirits of the stone. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Arch' realized that 'Faith' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Gargoyles' and 'Stained Glass Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Sacred-Energy'—a shimmering force found in pointed arches and golden halos, making every 'Ritual' a focus for reality-warping power. To be an Architect or a Guardian is to be a ghost in your own sanctuary, knowing that 'The Shadow' is not a darkness—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Shine (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Architect creates the world with a single, perfectly rendered stroke through the void.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Cathedral Network' to monitor the sacred energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Notre-Dame Era (Last Age):** A period of gothic architecture occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Arch.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the cathedral, only to witness their first 'Gargoyle Awakening' reveal.
5.  **The Altar-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Demonic Incursion'—a team of grotesque shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Sacred Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the rituals, you develop 'Light-Sensitivity' and ritual 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Architect Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of every mentor voice Essence (equivalent) offers you a 'Sacred Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Knowledge Singularity (equivalent). You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant district colors and Monopoly money pastels (equivalent). The cathedrals are vibrating, and the 'Sacred-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Choice.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that wholeness requires darkness. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kursi in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the gothic architecture, the pride in mastering a ritual, and the multiple, resonant voice of a storyteller who understands that every moment is a prayer. The players are the architects and guardians who must navigate the mythological politics and the intensity of a shadow integration to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Archetypa (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, cathedrals, and divine light.
*   **The Council Circle:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Incense, old stone, and expensive ink. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Sacred Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Symbolic Landscapes (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each region has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Sacred-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Shadow Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Collective Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and information overload.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Shadow Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Architect Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find The Architect. You must identify the 'Sacred-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Ritual Heist:** Infiltrate the Shadow HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Ritual-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Collective Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated shadow fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Integration Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Shadow Priest face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
