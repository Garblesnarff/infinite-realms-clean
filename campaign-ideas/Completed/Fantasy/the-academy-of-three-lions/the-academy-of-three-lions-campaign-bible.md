# The Academy of Three Lions: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a map that's being played by an invisible tactician. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Fodlan' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Three Houses'—ancient, celestial lineages that exists within the spirits of the students. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Church' realized that 'Tactics' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Heroes' Relics' and 'Academy Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Crest-Energy'—a shimmering force found in house heraldry and glowing weapons, making every 'Tactical Choice' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Student or a Commander is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The War' is not a failure—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Turn (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Tactician creates the world with a single, perfectly calculated move.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Garreg Mach Monastery' to monitor the crest energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Three Houses Era (Last Age):** A period of tactical medieval fantasy occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Lion.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the monastery, only to witness their first 'Relic' awakening.
5.  **The Monastery-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Imperial Incursion'—a team of red-black shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Tactical Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the formations, you develop 'Crest-Sensitivity' and royal 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Edelgard Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of white hair and crimson armor offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Fodlan Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of royal blues and imperial crimsons. The monasteries are vibrating, and the 'Strategic-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Divine Predetermination.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that friendship is worth more than glory. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single курса in an eternal book?' It explores the tactical beauty of the medieval fantasy, the pride in mastering a formation, and the youthful then war-weary voice of a commander who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the students and commanders who must navigate the house rivalries and the intensity of an army battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Garreg Mach Monastery (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, spires, and warm firelight.
*   **The Cathedral Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Incense, old stone, and floor wax. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Monastery Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Battlefields (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each base has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with crest-energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Rain-Soaked Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Imperial Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and crimson metal.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Strategic Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in an academy cell. You must survive your first 'Imperial Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Dimitri Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Dimitri. You must identify the 'Strategic-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Relic Heist:** Infiltrate the Imperial HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Relic-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Monastery Siege:** Your school is under attack by a coordinated silent fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Crest Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Edelgard face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Honor (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Strategic Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Crest Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the crest seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Crest-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Imperial' magic.
