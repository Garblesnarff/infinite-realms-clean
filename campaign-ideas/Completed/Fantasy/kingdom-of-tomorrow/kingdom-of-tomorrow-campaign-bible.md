# Kingdom of Tomorrow: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a pedestal that's being held by an invisible titan. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Metropolis' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Gulag'—an ancient, celestial prison that exists within the human sense of justice. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Cape' realized that 'Heroism' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Symbols' and 'Sanctuaries of Solitude' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Mythic-Energy'—a shimmering force found in painted realism and golden halos, making every 'Choice' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Hero or a Witness is to be a ghost in your own legend, knowing that 'The Twilight' is not an ending—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Flight (c. -Infinity):** The Alpha God creates the world with a single, perfectly rendered stroke.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Heroic Sanctuaries' to monitor the mythic energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Alex Ross Era (Last Age):** A period of painted realism occurs, establishing the heroic beauty and the Code of the Cape.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives in Metropolis, only to witness their first 'New-Gen' brutality.
5.  **The Gulag-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Ideological Horror'—a team of darker, muted shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Legacy Ascension (Ongoing):** As you join the old guard, you develop 'Legend-Sensitivity' and mythic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Superman Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of aged weary voice and larger-than-life presence offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Armageddon Field. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of rich painted colors and apocalyptic stakes. The pedestals are vibrating, and the 'Mythic-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Modern Brutality.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the classic idealism to appreciate the modern weight. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal painting?' It explores the mythic beauty of the painted realism, the pride in mastering a symbol, and the aged, resonant voice of a witness who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the heroes and witnesses who must navigate the generational clashes and the intensity of an apocalyptic stake to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Martial/Heroic Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Superman (equivalent - the old hero).
**Asset:** Classic costumes, golden halos, and the 'Truth' intuition.
**Rivals:** The New Generation, The Shadow Cabinet.

**Type:** Elite/High-Society Brutality Group
**Leader:** Magog (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Darker architecture, 'Violence' magic, and absolute control of the city's resources.

**Type:** Historical/Grounded Strategy Sages
**Leader:** Batman (Tier 1 NPC - the strategist).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tier weaponry, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Human' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Civilian Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Hero history, 'Mundane-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be collateral damage.
**Rivals:** The New Generation.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing nuclear explosions, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Metropolis (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, heroism, and golden light.
*   **The Daily Planet:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh mythic rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old paper, and city ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Hall of Justice:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Armageddon Field (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of destroyed cities and gathered armies.
*   **The Front Line:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Mythic-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Gulag Ruins:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Shadow Cabinet (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and political intrigue.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Legend Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a destroyed city. You must survive your first 'New-Gen Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Old Hero Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Superman. You must identify the 'Mythic-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Symbol Heist:** Infiltrate the Shadow Cabinet HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Cape-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Metropolis Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated brutal fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Legacy Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Magog face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Truth (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Classic Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Symbol Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the symbol seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Mythic-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Brutality' magic.
