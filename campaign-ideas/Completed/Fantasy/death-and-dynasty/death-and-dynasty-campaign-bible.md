# Death and Dynasty: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a blade that's being held by an invisible hand. In the heart of the medieval metropolis of Lexis (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of humanity was shattered by the return of the 'Order of Blades'—an ancient, tactical guild of professional killers who operate by a code as ancient as the stone itself. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Dynasty' realized that chaos is bad for business, and that even murderers need a place to call home. They forged the 'Lexis Accord'—a treaty that established neutral sanctuaries where no blood can be spilled. The 'High Table' (equivalent) was formed to govern the underworld through a lens of 'Dark Fantasy Realism' and 'Tactical Power.' In this world, a gold coin is more than currency; it's a social contract. To be an assassin is to be an artist of the 'Deadly Ballet,' knowing that every action you take is a debt that will eventually be paid in full.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon Treaty (c. -500 years):** The High Table is established to regulate the disparate assassin guilds of the world.
2.  **The Founding of the New York Continental (equivalent - 1920):** The first modern sanctuary is opened, a masterpiece of art deco elegance and marble.
3.  **The Great Excommunicado (1975):** A legendary assassin breaks the Accord by killing a High Table member on Continental grounds.
4.  **The Rise of the Ghost (10 Years Ago):** A young noble assassin (the player) completes an 'Impossible Task' to buy his way out of the life.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A newly minted assassin checks into the Lexis Sanctuary for their first official 'Job.'
6.  **The Seduction at the Gala (Day 3):** An undercover operation to retrieve a 'Marker' from a rival boss. You meet the enigmatic Concierge.
7.  **The Shadow Breach (Ongoing):** Evidence surfaces that a High Table member is selling 'Excommunicado' lists to the highest bidder.
8.  **The Sanctuary Siege (Upcoming):** The High Table declares the Lexis Sanctuary 'Deconsecrated.' The sanctuary is no longer safe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of stone grays and blood red. The rules are being broken, and every coin is a countdown to the final confrontation.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Personal Honor' and 'Survival.' It is about the 'Deadly Grace' of being an elite operative—the realization that your reputation is your only true shield. The 'Why' is the question: 'What is the value of a code in a world that rewards betrayal?' It explores the opulent environments of the luxury hotel, the gritty reality of the training hall, and the balletic violence of the professional kill. The players are the assassins who must navigate the subtext of a polite conversation and the intensity of a hall of mirrors ambush to find a truth that doesn't belong to the High Table.

**Type:** Elite/Multicultural Assassin Order
**Leader:** Winston (Equivalent - The Manager).

**Leader:** Ezio (Tier 1 NPC - the bridge).

**Type:** High-Tier/Emotionless Enforcers
**Leader:** The High Adjudicator.
**Asset:** Absolute authority from the High Table and a refusal to be swayed by reputation or gold.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Debt-Based Group
**Leader:** Various.
**Asset:** Blood Markers (unbreakable debts) and a commitment to the 'Deadly Ballet.'
**Rivals:** Each Other.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Lexis Sanctuary (The Hub)
An art deco masterpiece of black marble floors, gold columns, and warm chandelier light.
*   **The Lobby:** A site of 'Polite Formality' and high-society business. **Smell:** Bourbon, expensive tobacco, and old stone. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Weapon Hangar:** A site of 'Tactical Elegance' and organized chaos.

### The High Table Vault (Rome)
A royal gold and deep crimson monument to ancient power and sophisticated menace.
*   **The Hall of Markers:** A room filled with blood-stained medals and golden maps. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the blood of the first debt. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the High Table's broadcast.

### The 'Ghost' Safehouse (The Underworld)
A chaotic, crumbling maze of tunnels, rooftop pigeon lofts, and secret passages.
*   **The Observation Room:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** Rows of mirrors reflecting in the chrome walls. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'thump-thump' of heavy tires.

### The Mirror Hall (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Center:** Where the 'Final Choice' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **Checking In:** Arrive at the Sanctuary. You must check in with the Concierge and receive your first gold coin while maintaining your 'Aristocratic Cool.'
2.  **The Marker Retrieval:** Infiltrate a High Table gala to retrieve a Blood Marker stolen by a rival boss. You must navigate the 'Hall of Mirrors' ambush.
3.  **The Sommelier's Tasting:** Meet the Sommelier to select your mission equipment. You must complete a 'Safety Demonstration' that involves disarming three guards.
4.  **The Ghost Signal:** Receive a message from Ezio. You must retrieve a 'Truth-Seed' from a hidden vault in the High Table's Rome HQ.
5.  **Excommunicado:** You are declared 'Excommunicado' by the High Table. You must escape the city while being hunted by every assassin in the Accord.
6.  **The Siege of the Sanctuary:** Lead a defense of the hotel against a High Table 'Adjustment' squad. You must protect Winston while the self-destruct activates.
7.  **The Final Consequence:** Face the Elder face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you serve the Table or die for the Code?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of the Accord
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Deadly Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Gold Coin of Choice
**Type:** Artifact (Currency)
**Description:** A heavy, gold coin with the High Table seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Excommunicado' magic.
