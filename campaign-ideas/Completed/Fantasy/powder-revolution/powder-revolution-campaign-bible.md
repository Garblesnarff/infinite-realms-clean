# Powder Revolution: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a mountain that's being shredded by an invisible boarder. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Mount Fuji' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Yuki-Onna'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the powder. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Shred' realized that 'Snow Sports' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Boards' and 'Backcountry Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's winter. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Powder-Energy'—a shimmering force found in ice patterns and aurora shimmers, making every 'Perfect Landing' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Rider or a Shredder is to be a ghost in your own resort, knowing that 'The Avalanche' is not a weather—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Run (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Rider creates the world with a single, perfectly executed 1080 spin through the void.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Hana-Sanctuaries' to monitor the seasonal energy of the peaks.
3.  **The SSX Era (Last Age):** A period of joyful rebellion occurs, establishing the extreme sports beauty and the Code of the Shred.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the resort, only to witness their first 'Yuki-Onna' vision.
5.  **The Trick-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Corporate Incursion'—a team of artificial snow shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Sacred Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the tricks, you develop 'Powder-Sensitivity' and crystalline 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Hana Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of cryptic wisdom and gentle firm voice offers you a 'Winter Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Final Run. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of pure whites and chemical blues. The peaks are vibrating, and the 'Powder-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Corporate Greed.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the respect of the mountain to appreciate the life of the shred. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal game?' It explores the joyful beauty of the extreme sports, the pride in mastering a perfect run, and the wind-whispered, resonant voice of a winter spirit who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the riders and shredders who must navigate the industrial oppressions and the intensity of a sacred descent to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Mount Fuji (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, peaks, and warm firelight.
*   **The Hana Lodge:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and woodsmoke. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Shred Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Sacred Descents (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each descent has its own visual style.
*   **The Trick Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Powder-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Backcountry Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Corporate Resort (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and artificial snow.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Winter Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a resort cell. You must survive your first 'Corporate Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Hana Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Elder Hana. You must identify the 'Powder-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Board Heist:** Infiltrate the Corporate HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Shred-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Lodge Siege:** Your sanctuary is under attack by a coordinated robotic fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Sacred Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Yuki-Onna face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Shred (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Powder Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Snowflake Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the snowflake seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Powder-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Chemical' magic.
