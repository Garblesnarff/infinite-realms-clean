# Stones of Eternity: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a board that's being played by two invisible titans. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archetypa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Goban'—an ancient, celestial grid that contains the spirits of all things. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Form' realized that 'Placement' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Stones' and 'Star Points' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Qi-Energy'—a shimmering force found in obsidian black and alabaster white, making every 'Two Eyes' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Stone or a Player is to be a ghost in your own game, knowing that 'The Capture' is not a death—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Move (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Master creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced stone placement at the Tengen.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Goban' to monitor the strategic energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Zen Era (Last Age):** A period of minimalist cosmic vastness occurs, establishing the sumi-e beauty and the Code of the Stone.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Placed' on the board, only to witness their first 'Atari' warning.
5.  **The Ko-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Obsidian Incursion'—a team of polished spheres—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Influence Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the intersections, you develop 'Qi-Sensitivity' and calligraphic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Seki Master Revelation (Ongoing):** A being of androgynous Zen voice and perfect balance offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Empty Board. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of absolute blacks and pure whites. The grids are vibrating, and the 'Qi-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Predetermined Moves.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the emptiness to appreciate the possession. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single intersection in an eternal game?' It explores the Zen-like beauty of the Go board, the pride in mastering a two-eyed formation, and the nuanced, resonant voice of a master who understands that every 'pak' is a prayer. The players are the stones and pathfinders who must navigate the influence storms and the intensity of a ko fight to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force

**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Goban (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, grids, and golden light.
*   **The Tengen:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old wood, and slate. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Qi Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Influence Fields (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each region has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Qi-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Star Point Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Void Beyond (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and absolute nothingness.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Obsidian Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Nullstone Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Harmonia. You must identify the 'Qi-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Eye Heist:** Infiltrate the Obsidian HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Eye-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Board Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated white fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Influence Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Void Beyond face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Balance (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Divine Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Stone Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the stone seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Qi-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Chaos' magic.

### 1. Obsidian Thrall (Standard)
(CR 2). Humanoid. HP 30, AC 13 (Silk patches).

(CR 5). Construct. HP 80, AC 16.
**Abilities:**
*   **Ominous Style Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet takes 1d10 psychic damage at the start of their turn.
*   **Reality-Warping Pulse:** Ranged attack (Psychic). 2d10 damage + Target is confused for 1 round.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Harmonia (Proxy)
(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Order of Play Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the leader's 'Archaic Script.'
*   **The Final Move:** Once per combat, Harmonia can force an enemy to take an action that serves his own glory.
