# CAMPAIGN BIBLE: CLASH OF OLYMPUS

## 1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY
`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`

### The Origin of the Fracture
Before the Golden Age, there was the **Scream of Ouranos**. When Kronos castrated his father, the sky did not merely bleed; it shattered. The Olympians are not just rulers; they are living bandages holding the reality of the Sky (Olympus) away from the hunger of the Earth (Gaia). The "Ichor" flowing through divine veins is actually liquid calcified time, stolen from the Titans. The current conflict arises not because the Titans are simply "evil," but because the bandages are rotting. The Olympians have become septic with Hubris, and the wound of the world is trying to reopen to heal itself through total chaos.

### The Timeline of Echoes
1.  **The Castration of the Sky:** Kronos creates the concept of "Linear Time" by killing the concept of "Eternal Now."
2.  **The Titanomachy:** Zeus wins not by strength, but by bargaining with the Hecatoncheires (the Hundred-Handed Ones) to hold the concept of "Defeat" physically in their many hands.
3.  **The Binding of Prometheus:** Fire was not stolen; it was leaked. Prometheus is currently chained to the *idea* of a rock, which hurts more than physical stone.
4.  **The Birth of Athena:** She burst from Zeus’s head, an event that actually lobotomized the King of Gods, removing his capacity for pure empathy in exchange for pure strategy.
5.  **The Day the Sun Blinked:** **(Weird/Rare)** For one hour in history, Apollo fell asleep at the chariot wheel. The sun stopped, and shadows across Greece became tangible, eating three city-states.
6.  **The Sinking of Atlantis:** Not a natural disaster, but a divine foreclosure. Poseidon liquidated the assets when they stopped paying tribute in "Awe."
7.  **The Weeping of Niobe:** Her grief was so potent it created a new river in the Underworld, the *Cocytus*, which now threatens to flood the Elysian Fields.
8.  **The Invention of Lying:** **(Weird/Rare)** Hermes sold the first Lie to a mortal king for a pair of sandals. Before this, deception was physically impossible.
9.  **The Revolt of the Tools:** **(Weird/Rare)** Hephaestus’s automatons achieved sentience 50 years ago and went on strike. They now inhabit a sovereign island of clockwork logic called "Antikythera."
10. **The First Crack:** Last month, a crack appeared in the sky above Mount Olympus. It bleeds black smoke. The gods claim it is a cloud; the Oracles know it is the breath of Tartarus.

### The Metaphysical "Why"
The Universe runs on **Theos-Dynamics**. Gods require mortal *Awe* (worship/terror) to maintain their physical forms. Without it, they dissolve into conceptual radiation. The Titans do not need Awe; they run on **Primordial Mass**. The Olympians are starving because mortals are becoming cynical (Philosophers are killing the gods by over-analyzing them). The Conspiracy aims to release the Titans just enough to terrify mortals back into blind worship, but they underestimate the hunger of the Old Kings.

*   **Type:** Theocratic Hegemony / Dysfunctional Family
*   **Leader:** Zeus (The Paranoid King)
*   **Asset:** **Ambrosia** (Substance that grants temporary immortality/super-sanity).
*   **Rivals:** The Titans, The Philosophers.

*   **Type:** Cosmic Nihilists / Entropy Cult
*   **Leader:** Kronos (The Devourer)
*   **Asset:** **Primordial Truth** (Knowledge that drives mortals insane but grants power).
*   **Rivals:** All Olympians.

*   **Type:** Military Coup
*   **Leader:** Ares (God of War)
*   **Asset:** **The Spartoi Seeds** (Dragon teeth that grow into instant armies).
*   **Rivals:** Athena, Zeus.

*   **Leader:** Euclid the Eye-Gouged
*   **Asset:** **The Null-Equation** (A mathematical proof that can delete minor magical effects).
*   **Rivals:** The Oracle of Delphi, The Fates.

*   **Type:** Spiritual Terrorists
*   **Leader:** Niobe the Stone
*   **Asset:** **Bottled Grief** (Grenades that cause instant, crippling depression).

### [The Architects of Daedalus]
*   **Type:** Trap-Makers / Dungeon Designers
*   **Leader:** Icarus (Survived, now wears mechanical wings).
*   **Asset:** **Labyrinthine Blueprints** (Can rewrite the layout of a room).

## 3. NPC ROSTER

**1. Zeus (God)**
*   **Role:** King of Olympus.
*   **Voice:** Booming, thunderous bass, but with a crackle of static anxiety. Constant background hum of electricity.
*   **Goal:** Maintain the status quo at any cost.
*   **Secret:** He has lost his lightning bolt; he is using a fake forged by Hephaestus to maintain appearances.

**2. Kronos (Titan)**
*   **Role:** The Prisoner.
*   **Voice:** Multiple voices layered: a crying child, a dying old man, and the grinding of tectonic plates. Very slow.
*   **Goal:** Consume time itself to revert the universe to the Golden Age.
*   **Secret:** He doesn't want to rule; he wants to die, but he is immortal, so he must unmake reality to sleep.

**3. Hades (God)**
*   **Role:** The Jailer.
*   **Voice:** Dry, whispery, like dead leaves skittering on stone. Polite but exhausted.
*   **Goal:** Keep the dead inside; keep the living outside.
*   **Secret:** He is the only god who actually loves his job; he views it as a mercy to the dead.

**4. Athena (Goddess)**
*   **Role:** The Strategist.
*   **Voice:** Crisp, icy, rapid-fire. Sounds like a stern university professor. No emotional inflection.
*   **Goal:** Calculate the path of least resistance to survival.
*   **Secret:** She knows the prophecy of the "Twilight of Gods" is true and is preparing an escape ark for wisdom, leaving the gods to die.

## 4. LOCATIONS
`[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]`

### 1. Mount Olympus: The Gilded Cage
*   **Description:** A floating archipelago of marble islands connected by bridges of solid light. The physics here are "Narrative"—if you feel small, you physically shrink.
*   **Locations:**
    1.  **The Throne Room of Thunder:** Ceiling is a live storm. Floor is glass looking down at the mortal world. **Smell:** Ozone and fear.
    2.  **Hera’s Gardens of Spite:** Plants here eat other plants. **Sound:** Constant soft whispering of gossip.
    3.  **The Forge of Hephaestus:** A factory of clockwork horrors. **Smell:** Burning oil and sulfur.
    4.  **The Hall of Nectar:** Where gods drink. The fountains flow with gold liquid.
    5.  **The Edge:** Where the mountain ends and drops into the mortal realm.

### 2. The Underworld: The Gray Bureaucracy
*   **Description:** Not hell, but a waiting room. The sky is rock, the air is stagnant.
*   **Locations:**
    1.  **The River Styx:** Black water that screams. **Sound:** The voices of the drowning.
    2.  **The Fields of Asphodel:** Endless gray wheat fields where shades wander. **Smell:** Dust and old paper.
    3.  **The Palace of Hades:** Made of black diamond. It absorbs light.
    4.  **Tartarus Gate:** A massive bronze door that radiates heat. **Sound:** Deep, rhythmic pounding from the other side.
    5.  **Lethe’s Bank:** The river of forgetfulness. It looks like vodka.

### 3. The Labyrinth: The Living Concrete
*   **Description:** A maze that shifts. Walls move when you aren't looking.
*   **Locations:**
    1.  **The Moving Corridor:** The floor is a treadmill.
    2.  **The Mirror Room:** Reflections step out to attack you.
    3.  **The Center:** A calm garden where the Minotaur sleeps. **Smell:** Blood and roses.
    4.  **Daedalus’s Workshop:** Abandoned blueprints cover the walls.
    5.  **The Oubliette:** A pit with no bottom.

### 4. Delphi: The Gas-Choked Temple
*   **Description:** Nestled in mountains, covered in green hallucinogenic mist.
*   **Locations:**
    1.  **The Tripod:** Where the Oracle sits.
    2.  **The Treasury of Gifts:** Filled with bribes from kings.
    3.  **The Omphalos:** The "Navel of the World" stone. It hums. **Sound:** Low frequency vibration.
    4.  **The Python’s Corpse:** The rotting remains of the old guardian. **Smell:** Decay and incense.
    5.  **The Whispering Caves:** Echoes here answer questions you haven't asked yet.

### 5. The Battlefield of Troy (Ruins)
*   **Description:** A haunted wasteland where the Trojan War never really ended for the ghosts.
*   **Locations:**
    1.  **The Scaean Gate:** Still burning with ghostly fire.
    2.  **Achilles’ Tent:** Preserved in a time bubble.
    3.  **The Wooden Horse Skeleton:** Rotting lumber ribs.
    4.  **The River Scamander:** Choked with rusty armor. **Smell:** Rust and dried blood.
    5.  **The Wall of Skulls:** A barricade made of... well, skulls.

## 5. QUEST ARCHITECTURE
`[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]`

### Act I: The Blood (Sessions 1-4)
1.  **Awakening:** Survive the initial monster ambush (Harpies).
2.  **Revelation:** Discover Divine Parentage via a sign (e.g., getting struck by lightning and enjoying it).
3.  **The Summons:** Climb the invisible stairs to Olympus.
4.  **The Test:** Defeat the Nemean Lion (who is actually a polymorphed Olympian spy).

### Act II: The Labors (Sessions 5-9)
5.  **The Birds:** Clear the Stymphalian Birds using noise (Sonic warfare).
6.  **The Bull:** Capture the Cretan Bull without weapons (Grapple check hell).
7.  **The Descent:** Enter the Labyrinth to find the Titan Key.
8.  **The Betrayal:** Ares interferes; boss fight against his avatar.
9.  **The Accusation:** Framed for the murder of a minor god. Flee Olympus.

### Act III: The Underworld (Sessions 10-14)
10. **The Crossing:** Bribe Charon (He wants a memory, not money).
11. **The Judge:** Convince Minos you are alive.
12. **The Bargain:** Deal with Hades—retrieve the Helm of Darkness from Tartarus.
13. **The Pit:** Stealth mission through Tartarus. Witness Kronos.
14. **The Escape:** Outrun the Furies up the vertical shaft of the world.

### Act IV: The War (Sessions 15-22)
15. **The Proof:** Present evidence to the Council.
16. **The Break:** Kronos escapes. The sky cracks.
17. **The Rally:** Recruit armies (Amazons, Centaurs, Dead).
18. **The Siege:** Defend Olympus from Climbing Titans.
19. **The Deep Magic:** Visit the Fates to cut Kronos's thread.
20. **The Titan King:** Final Boss Fight: Kronos (Mechanic: He rewinds combat turns).
21. **The Choice:** Save Zeus, Kill Zeus, or Destroy the Throne.
22. **Epilogue:** Apotheosis or Humanity.

## 8. MECHANICS
`[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]`

### The Hubris Meter
*   **Concept:** The cooler you are, the more the gods hate you.
*   **Mechanic:** Rolling a Natural 20 adds +1 Hubris.
*   **Effect:**
    *   Hubris 1-5: Mortals recognize you. Advantage on Social.
    *   Hubris 6-10: Monsters hunt you specifically.
    *   Hubris 11+: A God sends a personalized curse (Disadvantage on a stat).
    *   **Reset:** Sacrifice something valuable at a temple.

### Divine Parentage
*   **Zeus:** 1/day Re-roll thunder damage or Lightning Resistance.
*   **Poseidon:** Breathe water, swim speed.
*   **Hades:** Invisible to Undead until you attack.
*   **Ares:** +1 to hit with a specific weapon type.
*   **Athena:** Proficiency in History and Insight.
*   **Apollo:** 1/day Heal 1d8+Level.
*   **Hermes:** +5ft movement speed.

### Sanity: "The Gaze"
*   Seeing a Titan or Primordial form requires a Wisdom Save. Failure results in "Fracture"—a permanent quirk (e.g., "You can hear color").

## 9. SESSION GUIDE
`[TAG: DM_GUIDE]`

*   **Sessions 1-2:** **The Awakening.** Combat with Harpies. Meet the Parents. Establish the "Hubris" mechanic.
*   **Sessions 3-4:** **The Investigation.** Delphi. The prophecy is "The Sky Will Eat The Earth." Dungeon: The Nemean Lion's Cave.
*   **Sessions 5-6:** **The Labors.** High adventure. Flying combat with Birds. Puzzle combat with the Bull.
*   **Sessions 7-8:** **The Labyrinth.** Dungeon Crawl. Non-Euclidean geometry. Boss: Minotaur. Reveal: Ares is the traitor.
*   **Sessions 9-10:** **The Fall.** Accused of treason. Chase scene escaping Olympus. Descent into the Underworld.
*   **Sessions 11-12:** **The Dead.** Navigate the Styx. Social encounter with Hades. Heist mission to steal the Key from Tartarus.
*   **Sessions 13-14:** **The Pit.** Tartarus exploration. Horror vibes. Encounter Kronos (Social/Stealth). Escape.
*   **Sessions 15-16:** **The Return.** Back to the surface. The world is changing (sun is dim). Rally allies.
*   **Sessions 17-18:** **War.** Mass combat mechanics. Titans climb Olympus. Siege warfare.
*   **Sessions 19-20:** **Titanomachy.** Breach the Throne Room. Boss Rush (Ares, then Typhon).
*   **Sessions 21-22:** **The End.** Kronos fight. The player with the highest Hubris must make the final sacrifice.
