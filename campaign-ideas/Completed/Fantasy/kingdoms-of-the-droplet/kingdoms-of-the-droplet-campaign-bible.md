# Kingdoms of the Droplet: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a droplet that's being played by an invisible current. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Micro-World' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Seed'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the water's surface tension. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Flow' realized that 'Micro-Organisms' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Current' and 'Algae Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's biological stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Aquatic-Energy'—a shimmering force found in crystal blues and translucent membranes, making every 'Division' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Paramecium or an Amoeba is to be a ghost in your own droplet, knowing that 'The Pollution' is not a poison—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Ripple (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Current creates the droplet with a single, perfectly balanced vibration.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Micro-Sanctuaries' to monitor the aquatic energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Droplet Era (Last Age):** A period of microscopic civilization occurs, establishing the translucent beauty and the Code of the Flow.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Reaped' into the droplet, only to witness their first 'Pollution Incursion' revealing the grit beyond.
5.  **The Algae-Forest Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Chem-Wraith'—a creature of dark brown smog—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Division Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the current, you develop 'Aquatic-Sensitivity' and cellular 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Cilia Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of professional diplomatic voice and thousands of tiny cilia offers you a 'Biological Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Surface Tension Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of fresh greens and pollution blacks. The currents are vibrating, and the 'Aquatic-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Industrial Decay.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the purity of the water to appreciate the complexity of the cell. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal machine (equivalent)?' It explores the translucent beauty of the microscopic world, the pride in mastering a current, and the aquatic, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the paramecia and amoebas who must navigate the chemical clouds and the intensity of a cellular division to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Kingdoms of the Droplet (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, currents, and translucent beauty.
*   **The High Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and fresh water. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Flow Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Algae Forest (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where water acts as a thick medium with visible currents.
*   **The Current Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Aquatic-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Surface Tension Ceiling:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Pollution Zone (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to chemical clouds and dead cells.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Flow Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a droplet. You must survive your first 'Pollution Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Cilia Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Governor Cilia. You must identify the 'Aquatic-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Current Heist:** Infiltrate the Pollution HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Current-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Forest Siege:** Your droplet is under attack by a coordinated chemical fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Division Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Emperor Pseudopod face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Flow (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Aquatic Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Current Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the current seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Aquatic-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Chemical' magic.
