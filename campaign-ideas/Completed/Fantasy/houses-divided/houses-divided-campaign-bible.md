# Houses Divided: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a zodiac that's being played by twelve siblings. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archetypa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Thirteenth House'—an ancient, celestial consciousness who believe that the only true life is 'Unified Harmony.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Divide' realized that 'Philosophy' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Twelve Territories'—ideological kingdoms that regulate the world's mental energy. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Zodiac-Energy'—a shimmering force found in hierarchical towers and communal circles, making every 'Negotiation' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a House-Walker or a Mediator is to be a ghost in your own kingdom, knowing that 'The War' is not a failure—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Thought (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Strategist creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced idea.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Thirteenth Ruins' to monitor the zodiac energy of the continents.
3.  **The Divided Era (Last Age):** A period of ideological warfare occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the House.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the ruins, only to witness their first 'House Incursion' revealing the grit beyond.
5.  **The No-Man's-Land Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Ideological Horror'—a creature of clashing architectures—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Zodiac Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the philosophies, you develop 'Style-Sensitivity' and royal 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Mediator Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of weary authority and cosmic burden offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Thirteenth House. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of golden wealth and gothic death imagery. The territories are vibrating, and the 'Ideological-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Certainty.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the other to appreciate the self. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal machine (equivalent)?' It explores the sweeping beauty of the twelve territories, the pride in mastering a negotiation, and the passionate, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the walkers and mediators who must navigate the political thrillers and the intensity of a military campaign to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Thirteenth Ruins (The Hub)
**Type:** Hub
**Description:** A massive, high-contrast monument to architecture, ruins, and zodiac energy.
*   **The Script Chamber:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh narrative rituals. **Smell:** Ozone, old paper, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Zodiac Tree:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Twelve Territories (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of golden wealth and gothic death imagery.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Zodiac-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Tenth Tower:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The No-Man's-Land (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and devastation.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Ideology Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first ' Purist Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Walker Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find the Walker. You must identify the 'Zodiac-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Status Heist:** Infiltrate the Tenth Tower to retrieve a stolen 'Status-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Territory Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated purist fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Mediator Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Lord of the Tenth face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
