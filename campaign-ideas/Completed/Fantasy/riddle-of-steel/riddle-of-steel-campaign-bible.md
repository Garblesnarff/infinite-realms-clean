# Riddle of Steel: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's an anvil that's being hammered by an invisible smith. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Hyboria' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Riddle of Steel'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the strength of the will. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Forge' realized that 'Suffering' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Swords' and 'Wheel of Pain' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Will-Energy'—a shimmering force found in blood red fires and scarred skin, making every 'Choice' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Barbarian or a Chronicler is to be a ghost in your own legend, knowing that 'The Steel' is not a metal—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Strike (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Smith creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced hammer blow.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Tower of Serpents' to monitor the will energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Frazetta Era (Last Age):** A period of raw muscular sword and sorcery occurs, establishing the visceral beauty and the Code of the Steel.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Captured' as a slave, only to witness their first 'Wheel of Pain' rotation.
5.  **The Serpent-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Cult Incursion'—a team of snake-scaled shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Steel Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the blade, you develop 'Will-Sensitivity' and mythic 'Tattoos' (scars).
7.  **The Barbarian Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of primal philosophical voice and muscular presence offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Serpent Temple. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of earthy browns and blood reds. The anvils are vibrating, and the 'Will-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Brutality.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that strength is earned through suffering. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal kurs (equivalent)?' It explores the raw beauty of the sword and sorcery, the pride in mastering a functional weapon, and the primal, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every cent is a gift. The players are the barbarians and chroniclers who must navigate the industrial oppressions and the intensity of a serpent ritual to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Wheel of Pain (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, slavery, and strength.
*   **The Hub Table:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and sweat. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Will Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Desert Wastelands (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each space has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Will-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Tower of Serpents:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Serpent Temple (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and sacrificial altars.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up at the Wheel of Pain. You must survive your first 'Cult Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Barbarian Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Conan. You must identify the 'Will-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Steel Heist:** Infiltrate the Serpent HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Steel-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Temple Siege:** Your sanctuary is under attack by a coordinated snake fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Steel Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Thulsa Doom face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Will (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Steel Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Anvil Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the anvil seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Will-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Serpent' magic.
