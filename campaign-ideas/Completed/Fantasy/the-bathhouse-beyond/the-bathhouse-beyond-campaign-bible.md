# The Bathhouse Beyond: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a bathhouse that's being cleaned by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Spirit Town' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of 'The Bathhouse'—an ancient, celestial sanctuary that exists within the spirit of the water. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Wash' realized that 'Hospitality' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Tokens' and 'Lantern-Lit Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's spiritual stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Spirit-Energy'—a shimmering force found in golden lantern light and amber bathhouse glows, making every 'Bath' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Visitor or a Worker is to be a ghost in your own town, knowing that 'The Contract' is not a job—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Verse (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Spirit creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced splash in the dark.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Spirit Town' to monitor the lyrical energy of the islands.
3.  **The Ghibli Era (Last Age):** A period of magical realism occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Wash.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Reaped' into the spirit world, only to witness their first 'Bathhouse' reveal.
5.  **The Spirit-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Stagnant Incursion'—a team of grotesque shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Work Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the chores, you develop 'Spirit-Sensitivity' and ritual 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Haku Revelation (Ongoing):** A boy of blue hair and dragon form offers you a 'Friendly Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Yubaba Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of indigo purples and lantern golds. The bathhouses are vibrating, and the 'Harmony-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Choice.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that magic is real if you're with your friends. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kursi in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the spirit world, the pride in mastering a hospitality feat, and the gentle, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the visitors and workers who must navigate the celestial politics and the intensity of a spirit train sequence to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Bathhouse (The Hub)
A grand wooden monument to architecture, baths, and lantern light.
*   **The Reading Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old wood, and expensive tea. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Wash Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Spirit Town (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each space has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Spirit-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Flooded Plain:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Yubaba Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and contracts.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Yubaba Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Haku Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Haku. You must identify the 'Spirit-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Token Heist:** Infiltrate the Yubaba HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Token-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Bathhouse Siege:** Your sanctuary is under attack by a coordinated extraction fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Contract Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Yubaba face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Balance (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Sacred Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Token Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the token seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Spirit-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Industrial' magic.
