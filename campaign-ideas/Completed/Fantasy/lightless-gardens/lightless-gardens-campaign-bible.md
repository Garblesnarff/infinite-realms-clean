# Lightless Gardens: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a seed that's being tended by an invisible gardener in the dark. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Great Labyrinth' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Seed'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the darkness-adapted ecology. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Deep' realized that 'Darkness' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Magic' and 'Bio-Luminescent Gardens' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Luminescent-Energy'—a shimmering force found in cold blues and green glows, making every 'Sprout' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Druid or a Miner is to be a ghost in your own garden, knowing that 'The Light' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Squelch (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Gardener creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced division in the dark.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Deep Sanctuaries' to monitor the luminescent energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Lightless Era (Last Age):** A period of dark ecology occurs, establishing the alien beauty and the Code of the Deep.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) falls into the gardens, only to witness their first 'Light Incursion' revealing the grit beyond.
5.  **The Mining-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Chem-Wraith'—a creature of industrial yellow—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Garden Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the darkness, you develop 'Light-Sensitivity' and cellular 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Nightbloom Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of weary passion and eyeless wisdom offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Surface Tension Spire (equivalent). You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of fresh greens and pollution blacks. The currents are vibrating, and the 'Aquatic-Energy' (equivalent) is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Industrial Invasion.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the darkness to appreciate the light. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal machine?' It explores the translucent beauty of the microscopic world (equivalent), the pride in mastering a current, and the quiet, minimal voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the druids and miners who must navigate the chemical clouds and the intensity of a cellular division to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing plot holes, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Lightless Gardens (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, darkness, and bio-luminescent beauty.
*   **The High Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and damp earth. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Flow Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Great Labyrinth (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where water acts as a thick medium with visible currents.
*   **The Current Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Luminescent-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Surface Tension Ceiling:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Mining Zone (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to chemical clouds and industrial scarring.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Flow Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a garden. You must survive your first 'Mining Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Nightbloom Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Archdruid Nightbloom. You must identify the 'Luminescent-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Current Heist:** Infiltrate the Mining HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Current-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Garden Siege:** Your droplet is under attack by a coordinated industrial fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Division Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Director Copper-Vein face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Flow (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Luminescent Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Current Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the current seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Aquatic-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Industrial' magic.
