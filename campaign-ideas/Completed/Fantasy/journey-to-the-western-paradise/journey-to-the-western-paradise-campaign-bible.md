# Journey to the Western Paradise: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a stage that's being played by an invisible troop. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Gourmet Frontier' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Western Paradise'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the mountain. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Opera' realized that 'Performance' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Sutras' and 'Celestial Banners' to serve as anchors for the world's spiritual stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Opera-Energy'—a shimmering force found in ink paintings and vibrant silks, making every '72 Transformation' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Pilgrim or a Sage is to be a ghost in your own legend, knowing that 'The Wire-Fu' is not a movement—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Verse (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Performer creates the world with a single, perfectly pitched note.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Jade Court' to monitor the opera energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Peking Era (Last Age):** A period of fantasy opera occurs, establishing the wire-fu action and the Code of the Pilgrim.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the court, only to witness their first 'Staff-Extension' revealing the power beyond.
5.  **The Cloud-Palace Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Demon-Troop'—a team of poisonous green shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Sutra Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the chants, you develop 'Spiritual-Sensitivity' and calligraphic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Wukong Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of orange-gold fur and crimson cape offers you a 'Theatrical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Western Paradise. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant golds and ink blacks. The stages are vibrating, and the 'Opera-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Theatrical Performance.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the mischief of the heart to appreciate the wisdom of the mind. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single course in an eternal opera?' It explores the operatic beauty of the Chinese fantasy, the pride in mastering a transformation, and the cocky, resonant voice of a monkey king who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the pilgrims and sages who must navigate the celestial politics and the intensity of a demon battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Flower-Fruit Mountain (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to dynamic brushwork, waterfalls, and warm firelight.
*   **The Monkey Throne:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh spiritual rituals. **Smell:** Peach, vanilla, and old paper. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Sutra Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Demon Territories (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of poisonous greens, blood reds, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Opera-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The White-Bone Ledge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Jade Court (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and celestial bureaucracy.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Scroll Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up at the mountain. You must survive your first 'Jade Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Wukong Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Sun Wukong. You must identify the 'Opera-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Sutra Heist:** Infiltrate the Jade Court HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Ancient-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Pilgrim Siege:** Your mountain is under attack by a coordinated bureaucratic fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Western Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Jade Emperor face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of West (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Theatrical Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Sutra Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the sutra seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Opera-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Bureaucratic' magic.
