# Monopolis Rising: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a board that's being played by a cosmic banker. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archetypa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Items'—ancient, magical currency that determine the ownership of every building. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Board' realized that 'Capitalism' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Properties' and 'Board Walk' to serve as anchors for the world's economy. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Monopoly-Energy'—a shimmering force found in property deeds and pastel currency, making every 'Transaction' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Landlord or a Vagrant is to be a ghost in your own city, knowing that 'The Bankruptcy' is not a failure—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Turn (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Banker creates the world with a single, perfectly calculated investment.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Board Walk' to monitor the monetary energy of the provinces.
3.  **The 1930s Era (Last Age):** A period of Art Deco dystopian capitalism occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Board.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the start line, only to witness their first 'Bankruptcy' reveal.
5.  **The Jail-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Warden Incursion'—a team of harsh shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Property Ascension (Ongoing):** As you acquire deeds, you develop 'Monetary-Sensitivity' and fiscal 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Baron Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of aristocratic British accent and dripping condescension offers you a 'Luxury Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Board Walk Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant district colors and Monopoly money pastels. The buildings are vibrating, and the 'Fiscal-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Wealth.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the poverty of the vagrant to appreciate the wealth of the baron. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal game?' It explores the Art Deco beauty of the dystopian capitalism, the pride in mastering a property negotiation, and the cold, resonant voice of a banker who understands that every cent is a gift. The players are the landlords and vagrants who must navigate the industrial oppressions and the intensity of a mass uprising to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Board Walk (The Hub)
A grand Art Deco monument to architecture, wealth, and golden light.
*   **The Luxury Hotel:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and expensive champagne. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Deed Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Color Districts (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each district has its own visual style.
*   **The Transaction Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Monopoly-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Mediterranean Lane:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Jail Dimension (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and iron gray stone.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Capital Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a brown district. You must survive your first 'Warden Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Banker Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find The Banker. You must identify the 'Fiscal-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Deed Heist:** Infiltrate the Baron's HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Property-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The City Siege:** Your hotel is under attack by a coordinated railroad fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Fiscal Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Jail Warden face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Property (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Monetary Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Die Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the die seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Fiscal-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Economic' magic.
