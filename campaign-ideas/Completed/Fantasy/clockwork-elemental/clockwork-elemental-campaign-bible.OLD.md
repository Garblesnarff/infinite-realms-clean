## [TAG: LORE_CORE] DEEP LORE & HISTORY

### The Origin Myth: The Great Weaver and the Iron Loom
In the beginning, there was only the **Great Weaver**, a spirit of pure flow who spun the threads of reality from the Ether. For eons, the Weaver spun the Spirit World, a place where thought defined form. But the Weaver grew lonely and desired something that would persist without observation. It built the **Iron Loom**, the first machine, to weave the Physical World—a realm of rigid laws, gravity, and mortality. The friction between the Weaver’s fluid threads and the Loom’s rigid gears created **Chi**, the spark of life that allows the two worlds to touch. Now, the Loom is turning too fast, stripping the gears, and the heat of industry is burning the Weaver’s threads.

### The Timeline of Ten Eras
1.  **The Age of Stillness:** Humans live in lion-turtle shells; spirits roam freely. No bending, only prayer.
2.  **The Severing:** The first Avatar separates the worlds. Spirits are banished to the Ether; physics takes hold of the Material Plane.
3.  **The First Flame:** Firebending is discovered, not from dragons, but from observing the combustion of spirit-wood.
4.  **The Black Snow:** **(Weird/Rare)** A volcanic eruption in the Fire Nation rains ash for a decade; during this time, children are born with grey skin and the ability to bend smoke, not fire. They are purged.
5.  **The Hundred Year War:** The classic conflict (referencing input).
6.  **The Discovery of the Pulse:** A miner in the Earth Kingdom discovers that Spirit Vines pulse with a rhythm identical to a human heart.
7.  **The Day of Rust:** **(Weird/Rare)** All iron in the Earth Kingdom capital turned to rust instantly due to a spirit’s curse. This necessitated the invention of Platinum refining.
8.  **The Founding of Republic City:** The diplomatic hub is built on a "Thin Place" where the veil between worlds is permeable.
9.  **The Whispering Plague:** **(Weird/Rare)** A mania where citizens began speaking in binary code, later discovered to be the language of a machine-spirit trying to be born.
10. **The Equalist Manifesto:** Amon publishes his pamphlets, written in ink mixed with the blood of a debended master.

### The Metaphysical Conflict
The universe seeks **Homeostasis**. Bending is the release of pressure between the Spirit and Physical worlds. Industrialization acts as a "dam" on this flow, hoarding energy (Chi) in batteries and engines. This pressure build-up is causing "Spiritual Leaks"—mutations, madness, and the materialization of angry spirits in downtown alleys.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN] LOCATIONS

### 1. Republic City: The Steam-Soaked Jewel
*   **Sensory Details:** Smell of ozone and roasting duck. Sound of jazz mixing with pile drivers. Haze of purple smog at sunset.

*   **Dragon Flats Tenements:** Cramped, laundry hanging on lines, smell of boiled cabbage, sound of arguing neighbors.

### 2. Zaofu: The Metal Lotus
*   **Sensory Details:** Everything is smooth and cool to the touch. Sound of ringing bells. Reflective surfaces everywhere.
*   **The Meteorite Garden:** Glowing space rocks, humming vibration, smell of metallic dust.

## [TAG: RULES_CUSTOM] MECHANICS & PHYSICS

### The Chi-Stress System
Using bending or high-tech gear generates **Chi-Stress**.
*   **Limit:** Constitution Modifier + Proficiency.
*   **Effect:** If you exceed your limit, roll on the **Burnout Table** (1: Cannot bend for 1 day, 20: Explode for 4d10 force damage).
*   **Clearing:** Meditation or Maintenance (for Tech) clears 1 point per hour.

### Spirit Vine Volatility
Spirit Vines are highly reactive to emotion.
*   **Angry:** Vines explode (4d6 Force).
*   **Calm:** Vines glow and heal (2d8 HP).
*   **Fear:** Vines constrict (Grapple DC 15).

### Tech vs. Spirit Rock-Paper-Scissors
*   **Platinum** blocks **Bending**.
*   **Bending** disrupts **Electronics** (Lightning/Water).
*   **Spirit Energy** melts **Platinum**.
