# Clockwork Elemental: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a circle; it's a four-part harmony that's being played by a thousand arms. In the beginning, the 'Architect' (The Avatar) spoke the first 'Chord of Order,' and the 'Grand Accord' of reality began to harmonize the 'Elemental Balance.' Republic City is a masterpiece of Art Deco architecture and Asian-inspired industrialization, where bending is a dance-like martial art. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Balance' realized that the four elements—Water, Earth, Fire, and Air—were the planet's ultimate defense system. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Spirit-Energy'—a shimmering force found in the spirit world and high-tech mecha-suits, making every movement a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Bender or an Equalist is to be a guardian of the 'Prime Melody,' knowing that 'The Wrong Note' is not a mistake—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Bloom (c. -Infinity):** The Avatar creates the universe and establishes the Elemental Balance.
2.  **The Era of Nations (c. -10,000 years):** The four nations establish their respective cultures and the Code of Chivalry (equivalent).
3.  **The Hundred Year War (-100 years):** A periodic reality-glitch (The Fire Nation) disrupts the balance, seeking to monopolize the 'Spirit-Energy.'
4.  **The Rise of Republic City (70 Years Ago):** Avatar Aang and Fire Lord Zuko establish a neutral hub for all cultures and industrialization.
5.  **The Amon Rebellion (Present Day - Day 1):** A calm revolutionary (Amon) begins a campaign to 'Equalize' the city by removing bending.
6.  **The Mecha-Suit Reveal (Week 1):** You face your first high-tech mecha-suit in a dark, neon-lit alleyway.
7.  **The Harmonic Convergence (Upcoming):** A celestial alignment that triggers a mass airbender awakening and a reality-melt into the spirit world.
8.  **The Colossus Laser Standoff (Upcoming):** You must lead a team of benders and equalists in a final, mandala-like battle to stop Kuvira's mecha-kaiju.
9.  **The Present Day:** Republic City is a high-contrast nightmare of Jazz Age gold and industrial gray. The neon is flickering, and the 'Spirit-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Technological Equality' and 'Spiritual Privilege.' It is about the 'Martial Arts Fluidity' of the human soul—the realization that being yourself is the ultimate form of magic. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can logic and technology coexist with a world of spiritual wonder, or is the future a zero-sum game?' It explores the geometric precision of the mecha-suit, the visceral horror of power loss, and the manic, entrepreneurial voice of a man who views the spirit world as a market. The players are the benders and equalists who must navigate the subculture politics and the intensity of a spirit-world entry to find a truth that stays human in the light.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The United Republic (The Order)
**Type:** Theocratic Strategic Alliance/Hegemony
**Leader:** Tenzin (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Art Deco architecture, airbending mastery, and the 'Dharma-Energy' (equivalent) intuition.
**Rivals:** The Equalist Syndicate, The Earth Empire.

### The Equalist Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Meritocratic/Individualist Rebel Group
**Leader:** Amon (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** High-tech goggles, 'Null-Magic' chi-blocking, and a refusal to bow to the Bender's order.
**Rivals:** The United Republic.

### The 'Spirit' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Elemental Sages
**Leader:** The Avatar (Tier 1 NPC - the bridge).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, third eyes (equivalent), and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Mecha' Engineers (The Experts)
**Type:** Scientific/Bureaucratic Technological Branch
**Leader:** Varrick (A man of manic entrepreneurship).
**Asset:** Platinum armor, electricity blue magic, and the ability to declare a 'Technological Victory.'
**Rivals:** The Earth Empire (who they view as 'Monstrous').

### The 'Void' Spirits (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Pre-Era Force
**Leader:** Vaatu (The Spirit of Chaos).
**Asset:** Dark purple energy, reality-tearing tendrils, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **Avatar Korra** (Hero - The Lead) - A woman in elemental blues with a determined expression and martial arts grace. **Voice:** Sincere, precise, and emotionally resonant. **Goal:** To restore the elemental balance and find her own worth. **Secret:** She is secretly an Adept of 'Spirit-Energy' who can 'persuade' a spirit to return to its home through her dance steps.
2.  **Amon** (Equalist - The Villain) - A man in a simple white mask with a calm revolutionary rhetoric. **Voice:** Whispered, slow, and terrifyingly reasonable. **Goal:** To 'Equalize' the city and eliminate the Bender's privilege. **Secret:** He is secretly a high-level Waterbender who uses 'Ichor' magic to rewrite a person's chi.
3.  **Kuvira** (Earth Empire - The Inquisitor) - A woman in military precision gear with an authoritarian stance. **Voice:** Clear, confident, and aerodynamic. **Goal:** To unify the world through efficiency and the elimination of all variable threats. **Secret:** She was the first Bender to 'Sync' with the Platinum armor, allowing her to become shadow (equivalent).
4.  **Varrick** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A man in a vintage flannel (equivalent) who smells of roasted beans (equivalent). **Voice:** Manic, entrepreneurial, and fast-paced. **Goal:** To monetize the spirit world and create the 'Perfect Product.' **Secret:** He is a direct descendant of the first Architect and his 'Mecha-Technology' is the only thing keeping the city's power grid from collapsing.
5.  **Tenzin** (Republic - The Mentor) - A man in air nomad robes with J.K. Simmons' patient wisdom. **Voice:** Authoritative, measured, and British (equivalent). **Goal:** To preserve the ancient traditions and guide the new airbenders. **Secret:** He suffers from constant bouts of 'Modern Frustration' when his intellectual limits are pushed.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Republic City (The Hub)
An Art Deco masterpiece of Jazz Age gold and industrial gray.
*   **The Pro-Bending Arena:** A site of 'Savage Spectacle' and harsh neon lighting. **Smell:** Ozone, machine oil, and expensive cologne. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'roar-roar' of 50,000 voices.
*   **The Central Plaza:** Where the Pyramids and Colosseum (reimagined) dominate the landscape. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the marble of the first word.

### The Spirit World (The Battlefield)
A hyper-real watercolor dreamscape of ethereal purples and cosmic blues.
*   **The World Tree (Banyan-Grove):** A place where reality is shimmering and ancient runes glow. **Visual:** Vibrant cyans and greens in harmony. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the spirits.
*   **The Portal:** Where the 'Harmonic Convergence' first occurs.

### The Earth Empire Camp (The Gauntlet)
A militaristic, sharp-angled monument to hunter's grace and platinum armor.
*   **The Mecha-Factory:** A site of 'Engineering Marvels' and electricity blue light. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Mecha-Energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Avatar Sanctuary (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Memory Vault:** Where the 'Truth-Seeds' are assembly from pieces of the past. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Bending:** Wake up in Republic City. You must survive an 'Equalist Ambush' using only your martial arts intuition.
2.  **The Pro-Bending Match:** Win your first official match in the arena. You must identify the 'Spirit-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Mecha Heist:** Infiltrate a Future Industries lab to retrieve a stolen 'Engine-Focus.' You must bypass Asami's security.
4.  **The Spirit Incursion:** The city is under attack by a reality-warping spirit. You must defend the central plaza while the Avatar mounts her counter-strike.
5.  **The Harmonic Convergence:** Lead a team of benders into the spirit world to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the non-euclidean geometry.
6.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Amon and the Equalists face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?
7.  **The Final Coronation:** Face the Warlord face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Balance:**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Strike of Duty' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Mandala Signet:**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Spirit-Energy Capsule:**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Spirit' magic.
