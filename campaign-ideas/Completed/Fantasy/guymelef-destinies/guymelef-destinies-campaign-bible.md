# Guymelef Destinies: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a card that's being played by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Gaea' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Escaflowne'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the dragon. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Guymelef' realized that 'Mechanical Knights' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Mecha' and 'Tarot Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Destiny-Energy'—a shimmering force found in flowing capes and ornate armor, making every 'Vision' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Pilot or a Visionary is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Fate' is not a path—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Dragon (c. -Infinity):** The Dragon God creates the world with a single, massive roar.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Fanelia' dragon-kingdom to monitor the destiny energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Escaflowne Era (Last Age):** A period of romantic fantasy occurs, establishing the elegant mecha and the Code of the Knight.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Reaped' into Gaea, only to witness their first Guymelef attack.
5.  **The Tarot-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Zaibach Incursion'—a team of black-red shadows—in a dark, stained-glass alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Guymelef Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master your mecha, you develop 'Visionary-Sensitivity' and royal 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Van Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of short dark hair and dragon-scale patterns offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Fanelia Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of warm golds and royal blues. The gears are vibrating, and the 'Destiny-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Predetermined Paths.' It is about the 'Romantic Resilience' of the human spirit—the realization that you can change your fate for the sake of your love. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single card in an eternal deck?' It explores the elegant beauty of the mechanical knights, the pride in mastering a soaring mecha, and the passionate, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the pilots and visionaries who must navigate the mystical prophecies and the intensity of a mecha combat to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Factions

### The Dragon Knights (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Noble Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Van (equivalent - the dragon king).
**Asset:** White armored Guymelefs, dragon motifs, and the 'Flow' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Zaibach Syndicate, The Fate-Thieves.

### The Zaibach Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Industrial Group
**Leader:** Emperor Isaac (equivalent).
**Asset:** Angular architecture, 'Science' magic, and absolute control of the city's archives.
**Rivals:** The Dragon Knights.

### The 'Hitomi' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Visionary Sages
**Leader:** Hitomi (Tier 1 NPC - the bridge).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, tarot cards, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Asturia' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Royal Communities
**Leader:** Allen (The Knight).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Zaibach history, 'Honor-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be industrialised.
**Rivals:** The Zaibach Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Tragic Force
**Leader:** Folken (The Tragic Brother).
**Asset:** Reality-tearing black-red voids, missing prosthetic arms, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Fanelia (The Hub)
A grand dragon-kingdom monument to warm golds, reds, and ancient stone.
*   **The Dragon Throne:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh knightly rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and dragon-fire ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Tarot Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Gaea Frontier (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of spectacular vistas, royal blues, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Destiny-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Zaibach Border:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Zaibach Citadel (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to industrial logic and angular metal.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Destiny Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a dragon village. You must survive your first 'Zaibach Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Van Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Van. You must identify the 'Destiny-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Tarot Heist:** Infiltrate the Zaibach HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Escaflowne-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Fanelia Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated industrial fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Visionary Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Emperor Isaac face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Destiny (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Visionary Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Tarot Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the tarot seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Destiny-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Science' magic.
