# The Alliance-Horde Pact: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a shield that's being held by an invisible titan. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Azeroth' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Burning Legion'—an ancient, entropic intelligence who believes that the only true life is 'Total Consumption.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Pact' realized that 'Unity' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Artifacts' and 'Faction Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Chakra-Energy' (equivalent) —a shimmering force found in blue shields and red beasts, making every 'Honor-Bound Choice' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Hero or a Demon is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Pact' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Turn (c. -Infinity):** The Alpha God (equivalent) creates the world with a single, perfectly rendered stroke.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Eight Provinces' to monitor the lyrical energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Warcraft Era (Last Age):** A period of high fantasy epic realism occurs, establishing the Michael Whelan beauty (equivalent) and the Code of the Pact.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the capital, only to witness their first 'Legion Portal' reveal.
5.  **The Stormhaven-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Demon-Incursion'—a team of fel-green shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Pact Ascension (Ongoing):** As you unite the factions, you develop 'Unity-Sensitivity' and ritual 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Thrall Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of deep resonant voice and spiky hair offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Burning Core. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of royal blues and blood reds. The plates are vibrating, and the 'Pact-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Factional Hatred.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that we are stronger together. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single курса in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the Warcraft inspired world, the pride in mastering a faction armor, and the powerful, resonant voice of a commander who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the heroes and commanders who must navigate the industrial oppressions and the intensity of an army battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Alliance (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Heroic Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** High King Varian (equivalent - the leader).
**Asset:** Blue banners, plate armor, and the 'Honor' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Horde, The Burning Legion.

### The Horde (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Extraction Group
**Leader:** Warchief Thrall (equivalent).
**Asset:** Red architecture, 'Savage' magic, and absolute control of the city's supply lines.
**Rivals:** The Alliance.

### The 'Sylvanas' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Spirit Sages
**Leader:** Sylvanas (Tier 1 NPC - the guide).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tier weaponry, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Legion' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Demon Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Pact history, 'Fel-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be weighed.
**Rivals:** The Horde.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** Sargeras (The Legion Master).
**Asset:** Reality-tearing plot holes, dark shadows, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**High King Varian** (Order - The Lead) - A man whose voice is powerful, resonant, and heroic. **Voice:** Heroic quality, North American accent, commanding. **Goal:** To protect the 'Humanity' of Azeroth and guide the new soldiers. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Unity-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his noble presence.
**Sargeras** (Shadow - The Villain) - A being whose voice is smooth, hierarchical, and measured. **Voice:** Beautiful but hollow, hiding a deep-seated terror. **Goal:** To consolidate all global talent into a single 'Global Fire' under his control. **Secret:** He is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the original Silicon surge.
**Warchief Thrall** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A being whose voice is deep, aggressive, and measured. **Voice:** Urban, modern, and witty banter. **Goal:** To find the 'destination' and stop the legion-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the army noise.
**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A researcher whose body is a map of countless operation-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Sargeras's logic that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient student in practical regional gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the Pact ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Stormhaven (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, spires, and golden light.
*   **The War Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and expensive ink. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Pact Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Faction Regions (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each base has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Unity-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Fel Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Burning Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and fel-green fire.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Unity Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Legion Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Thrall Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Thrall. You must identify the 'Pact-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Artifact Heist:** Infiltrate the Legion HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Artifact-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The City Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated demon fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Unity Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Sargeras face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Honor (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Sacred Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Shield Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the shield seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Unity-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Fel' magic.

(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Order of Progress Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the leader's 'Archaic Script.'
