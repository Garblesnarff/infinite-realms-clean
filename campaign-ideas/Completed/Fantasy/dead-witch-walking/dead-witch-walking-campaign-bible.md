# Dead Witch Walking: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a grid that's being powered by the earth's own heartbeat. In the heart of the Midwestern metropolis of Cincinnati, the 'Grand Accord' of humanity was shattered by the return of the 'Ley Lines'—veins of green-gold earth energy that flow through the pavement like luminous roots. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Hollows' realized that true power isn't found in a laboratory, but in the 'Inderlander Gene'—the ability to channel the planet's primal magic. They established 'Cincinnati' as a sanctuary of species diversity and bounty hunter professionalism. But the 'Demons' have emerged from the 'Ever-After,' a twisted dimension where every deal has a 'Smut'—a visible mark of corruption on the soul. To be a Witch or a Living Vampire is to be a guardian of the 'Found Family,' knowing that 'Power' is a debt that must be paid in blood or smut.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The Ley Lines are released during a prehistoric reality-glitch.
2.  **The Era of Mounds (c. -10,000 years):** The first 'Hogan Sanctuaries' (equivalent) are anchored to the earth nodes.
3.  **The Great Separation (-1,000 years):** The Inderlanders and Humans establish their 'Territorial Accord.'
4.  **The Rise of the IS (Last Age):** The Inderland Security agency is formed to regulate the species diversity.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A newly awakened witch bounty hunter (the player) finds herself in a converted stone church after a recent 'Shifter Reset.'
6.  **The Vampire Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Living Vampire' in a dark, neon-lit alleyway.
7.  **The Demon Deal Anomaly (Ongoing):** As you make deals with demons, you develop 'Dark Smudges' on your soul and 'Wrong Eye Reflections.'
8.  **The Ever-After Revelation (Upcoming):** The party discovers that the demon dimension is a 'Twisted Reflection' of Cincinnati.
9.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the central Ley Line node. You must choose between your own soul and the lives of your friends.
10. **The Present Day:** Cincinnati is a high-contrast nightmare of green-gold energy and red-black corruption. The church bells are ringing, and the 'Pulse' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Personal Integrity' in a world of 'Species Politics.' It is about the 'Sarcastic Heroism' of the human spirit—the realization that found family is the only thing worth the cost of power. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when you are simultaneously the monster that makes deals with demons?' It explores the realistic texture of the weathered leather, the visceral horror of the soul-mark, and the determined, sarcastic voice of a woman who knows that wisdom is found in every word. The players are the witches, vampires, and pixies who must navigate the subculture politics and the intensity of a demon-negotiation to find a truth that stays human in the light.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Hollows Agency (The Heroes)
**Type:** Elite/Multicultural Bounty Hunter Order
**Leader:** Rachel Morgan (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** The Church base, ley line nodes, and the 'Found Family' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Inderland Security (IS), The Vampire Syndicate.

### The Vampire Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** High-Society/Seductive Criminal Branch
**Leader:** Ivy Tamwood (A woman of pale beauty and controlled bloodlust).
**Asset:** Mind-link magic, wealth, and total control of the city's underbelly.
**Rivals:** The Hollows Agency.

### The 'Jenks' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Pixie Sages
**Leader:** Jenks (Tier 1 NPC - the bridge).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, dragonfly wings, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The IS Enforcers (The Formalists)
**Type:** Theocratic/Totalitarian Species Regulation Branch
**Leader:** Various Officers.
**Asset:** Concrete quarantine zones (equivalent), legal authority, and a practiced warmth that hides cowardice.
**Rivals:** The Hollows Agency.

### The 'Ever-After' Remnants (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Demon Collectives
**Leader:** Algaliarept (The Demon - Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Reality-warping smut, twisted geometry, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings with souls.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Rachel Morgan** (Hero - The Lead) - A red-haired witch in practical leather and denim. **Voice:** Determined, sarcastic, and action-oriented. **Goal:** To protect her friends and find a way to stop the demons. **Secret:** She is secretly an Adept of 'Ley-Energy' who can 'persuade' a demon to return to its home through her spell-patterns.
**Ivy Tamwood** (Vampire - The Partner) - A pale, beautiful woman in business attire. **Voice:** Seductive, intelligent, and controlled. **Goal:** To control her bloodlust and build a legitimate life with Rachel. **Secret:** she is already part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the Great Separation.
**Jenks** (Pixie - The Catalyst) - A four-inch pixie warrior with dragonfly wings. **Voice:** Squeaky, high-pitched, and fierce. **Goal:** To support his 'Big' friends and find a place where pixies are respected. **Secret:** he is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the ley line's hum.
**Algaliarept** (Demon - The Mentor) - A being who appears as a normal man but has 'Dark Smudges' on his skin. **Voice:** Gruff, slow, and wood-toned. **Goal:** To reclaim his own power and rule through the Ever-After. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the ley line nodes that he's too professional to share.
**The Master (Shadow)** (Demon equivalent - Shifting form) - A massive, intelligent shadow that stands on its hind legs (equivalent). **Voice:** None (human-like intelligence in movements). **Goal:** To stalk the player and wait for the moment of 'Taboo Violation.' **Secret:** It is actually the player character's 'Shadow-Self,' a manifestation of their own repressed desire for demon power.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Converted Church (The Hub)
An old stone church, graveyard, sitting on a ley line node.
*   **The Living Space:** A site of 'Found Family' and warm amber light. **Smell:** Incense, old stone, and expensive port. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Work Bay:** A site of 'Visual Representation' and spell-patterns.

### The Cincinnati Hollows (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of Midwestern urban lighting and green-gold energy.
*   **The Alleyway:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and high-speed chases. **Smell:** Diesel, wet earth, and old gunpowder. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the city's heartbeat.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** A dark, silent place where the shadows move wrong.

### The Ever-After (The Gauntlet)
A twisted, reality-distorting dimension of red-black corruption.
*   **The Demon Citadel:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** Rows of smut-marks reflecting in the chrome walls. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'thump-thump' of heavy tires.

### The Ley Line Node (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Source:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' were first received. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Sighting:** Wake up in your church. You must survive your first 'Vampire Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Ley Line Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Rachel. You must identify the 'Ley-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The IS Heist:** Infiltrate the Inderland Security HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Cursed-Tool.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Demon Siege:** Your church is under attack by a coordinated demon fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Ever-After Crossing:** Lead a team of rebels into the spirit world to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the non-euclidean geometry.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Algaliarept and the IS face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Ibelin (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Life-Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Ley-Energy Capsule
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Demon' magic.

## [TAG: ENEMY_STATBLOCK]
### 1. Vampire Enforcer (Standard)
(CR 2). Undead. HP 30, AC 13 (Predatory stillness).
**Abilities:**
*   **Seductive Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet has disadvantage on saving throws against being charmed.
*   **Vicious Bite:** Melee attack. 1d8+3 damage + Target is infected.

### 2. Demon Guardian (Elite)
(CR 5). Fiend. HP 80, AC 15.
**Abilities:**
*   **Taboo Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet takes 1d10 acid damage at the start of their turn.
*   **Reality-Warping Pulse:** Ranged attack (Psychic). 2d10 damage + Target is confused for 1 round.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Algaliarept (Proxy)
(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Ancient Pact Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the Master's 'Archaic Script.'
*   **The Final Move:** Once per combat, Algaliarept can force an enemy to take an action that serves his own glory.
