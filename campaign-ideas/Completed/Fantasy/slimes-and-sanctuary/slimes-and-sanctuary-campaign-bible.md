# Slimes and Sanctuary: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a garden that's being tended by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Thornwatch' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Seed'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirits of the slimes. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Hearth' realized that 'Kindness' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Bonds' and 'Sanctuary of Small Things' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Pastoral-Energy'—a shimmering force found in translucent gelatinous bodies and warm golden sunlight, making every 'Genuine Smile' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Tamer or a Slime is to be a ghost in your own home, knowing that 'The Sanctuary' is not a prison—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Bloom (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Gardener creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced division.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Bond-Sanctuaries' to monitor the emotional energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Wholesome Era (Last Age):** A period of wholesome fantasy occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Hearth.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Adopted' by a slime family, only to witness their first 'Taming Bond' reveal.
5.  **The Road-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Undead Incursion'—a team of beautiful but decaying rivals (equivalent)—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Sanctuary Ascension (Ongoing):** As you develop relationships, you develop 'Divine-Sensitivity' and sacred 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Tamer Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of gentle determined voice and compassionate presence offers you a 'Gentle Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Stagnant-Spire (equivalent). You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of golden oranges and soft greens. The ruins are vibrating, and the 'Heart-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Power.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the love of the family to appreciate the strength of the oath. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single episode in an eternal romance?' It explores the warm beauty of the wholesome fantasy, the pride in mastering a relationship, and the gentle, resonant voice of a mentor who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the tamers and slimes who must navigate the awkward domesticities and the intensity of a divine grace to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

### The Thornwatch Family (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Sacred Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** The Tamer (equivalent - the heart).
**Asset:** Slime storage, ancient bonds, and the 'Bond' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Shadow Syndicate, The Stagnant-Spawn.

### The Shadow Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Extraction Group
**Leader:** Baron Valorin (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Crystalline architecture, 'Stagnation' magic, and absolute control of the city's quiet zones.
**Rivals:** The Thornwatch Family.

### The 'Willow' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Divine Sages
**Leader:** Grandmother Willow (Tier 1 NPC - the guide).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, translucent robes, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Slime' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Cultural Communities
**Leader:** Glorp (The Slime).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Sanctuary history, 'Gelatinous-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be stagnant.
**Rivals:** The Shadow Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing plot holes, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**The Tamer** (Order - The Lead) - A being whose voice is gentle, determined, and resonant. **Voice:** Gentle, determined, natural kindness, passionate defense. **Goal:** To protect the 'Humanity' of his children and guide the new tamers. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Pastoral-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his encouraging words.
**Baron Valorin** (Shadow - The Villain) - A being whose voice is deep, initially pompous, and measured. **Voice:** Pompous baritone, hiding insecurity, commanding to sympathetic later. **Goal:** To consolidate all global talent into a single 'Global Order' under his control. **Secret:** He is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the original Silicon surge.
**Grandmother Willow** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A being whose voice is elderly, wise, and maternal. **Voice:** Wise alto, grandmotherly warmth, slow pacing. **Goal:** To find the 'destination' and stop the stagnant-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the sanctuary noise.
**Glorp (The Mentor)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A slime whose body is a map of countless operation-scars. **Voice:** Adorable blob sounds, squelching, expressive without words. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Valorin's logic that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient student in practical real-world gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the sanctuary ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Thornwatch Fortress (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, ruins, and translucent beauty.
*   **The Family Courtyard:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and wildflowers. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Hearth Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Pastoral Countryside (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of green meadows, winding dirt paths, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Current Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Pastoral-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Grove of Small Things:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Stagnant Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and information overload.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a ruined city. You must survive your first 'Stagnant Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Tamer Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find The Tamer. You must identify the 'Pastoral-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Bond Heist:** Infiltrate the Stagnant's HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Bond-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Fortress Siege:** Your family is under attack by a coordinated stagnant fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Sanctuary Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Baron Valorin face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Hearth (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Pastoral Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Bond Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the bond seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Pastoral-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Stagnation' magic.
