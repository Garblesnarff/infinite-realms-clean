# Dice of Dominion: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a board that's being played by invisible titans. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Global War Room,' the 'Grand Accord' of nations was shattered by the discovery of the 'Dice of Dominion'—ancient, cosmic cubes that determine the fate of entire continents. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Strategy' realized that reality is governed by 'Probability-Energy.' They forged the 'Territory Pieces' and 'Troop Counters' to serve as anchors for this power. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Risk-Energy'—a shimmering force found in colored territory overlays and 1950s military propaganda, making every 'Attack' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a General or a Dice Prophet is to be a piece in the 'Eternal Game,' knowing that 'Victory' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The first 'Die' falls from the stars, releasing the mathematical 'Aethel' into the global atmosphere.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Six Factions' to monitor the strategic energy of the continents.
3.  **The Great Deployment (1950):** A massive mobilization occurs, establishing the 'War Room' aesthetic and the Code of Strategy.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient general (the player) wakes up in the 'Start Zone,' only to witness a beautiful city being highlighted in 'Enemy Red.'
5.  **The Dice-Battle Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Massive Dice Roll'—hundreds of cubes tumbling across the battlefield—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Continental Conquest (Ongoing):** As you capture territories, you develop 'Probability-Blur' and strategic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Dice Prophet Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of nervous mathematics offers you a '73% Chance of Victory.'
8.  **The Total Domination (Upcoming):** The final enemy territory falls. The world map transforms to a single color, and reality begins to fracture.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of faction colors and ivory pips. The map-lines are vibrating, and the 'Strategic-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Pure Probability.' It is about the 'Strategic Brilliance' of the human spirit—the realization that you are more than just a roll of the dice. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you maintain your humanity when the universe itself views your life as a 27% casualty rate?' It explores the elegant linework of the war map, the pride in holding a continent, and the omniscient, rhythmic voice of a Game Master who understands that every move is part of the script. The players are the generals and prophets who must navigate the betrayal and the intensity of a dice roll to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The War Room (The Hub)
A massive, high-contrast monument to maps, strategy, and glowing territory overlays.
*   **The War Table:** A site of 'Grand Strategy' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Ozone, charcoal smoke, and old paper. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Card Archive:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The World Map (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of faction colors, infantry counters, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Front Line:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and massive dice-rolls. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Faction-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Dice Chamber (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to probability and ivory cubes.
*   **The Rolling Floor:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Massive dice tumbling across the landscape. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Probability Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Deployment:** Wake up in a starting territory. You must survive your first 'Dice Ambush' while maintaining your 'Deployment' status.
2.  **The Strategy Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find the Dice Prophet. You must identify the 'Strategic-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Card Heist:** Infiltrate the Red Legion HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Reinforcement-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Continental Siege:** Your territory is under attack by a coordinated Blue fleet. You must defend the border while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Martyr's Stand:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Game Master and the Shadow face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
