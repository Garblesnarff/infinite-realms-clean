# Classical Symphony Kingdom: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a stone; it's a song that's being played by an invisible orchestra. In the beginning, 'The Composer' spoke the first 'Chord of Order,' and the 'Grand Accord' of reality began to harmonize the 'Classical Symphony Kingdom.' The city is a masterpiece of Baroque architecture and crystal palace fantasy, where every element is proportioned and mathematically beautiful. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Harmony' realized that 'Music' is the only thread that keeps the tapestry of existence from unraveling. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Symphony-Energy'—a shimmering force found in sheet music and golden hour light, making every note a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Maestro or a Cadenza is to be a guardian of the 'Prime Melody,' knowing that 'The Wrong Note' is not a mistake—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Chord (c. -Infinity):** The Composer creates the universe. The Symphony-Energy begins to flow.
2.  **The Era of Baroque (c. -10,000 years):** The first 'Maestro' monarchs establish the ornate capitals and the Code of Proportion.
3.  **The Great Dissonance (-1,000 years):** A periodic reality-glitch occurs, resetting the physical world while the 'Musical-Records' persist.
4.  **The Rise of Maestro Primo (Last Age):** A powerful conductor unifies the districts through a lens of 'Wagnerian Authority.'
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A newly awakened soul (the player) finds themselves in the Baroque Quarter after a recent 'Symphony Reset.'
6.  **The Wrong Note (Session 3):** Reality glitches in slow motion. Everything inverts as a discordant melody tears through the market.
7.  **The Truth Revealed (Session 13):** A montage of the kingdom's history reveals that 'The Composer' is actually a mechanical intelligence becoming organic.
8.  **The Final Battle (Session 19):** A musical duel where notes manifest as attacks. The party must harmonize with the Composer to save the song.
9.  **The First Free Performance (Session 21):** The kingdom transforms. The 'Prime Harmony' is restored, and the citizens begin to sing their own songs.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Mathematical Perfection' and 'Individual Expression.' It is about the 'Cerebral Grandeur' of the human soul—the realization that you are just a variable in a cosmic equation of time and music. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you fulfill your melody when it requires sacrificing the perfection of the whole?' It explores the symbolic density of the classical tradition, the tragedy of the 'Moonlight Sonata,' and the ethereal voice of a being that speaks in rising temperatures (equivalent). The players are the Maestros and Cadenzas who must navigate the gopurams (equivalent) and sacred rivers to find a resolution that honors the memory of the Architects.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Maestro Primo** (Council - The Leader) - A man in royal gold and ivory robes with an operatic bass voice. **Voice:** Authoritative, measured, and perfectly proportioned. **Goal:** To turn the kingdom into an eternal masterpiece of Baroque order. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Symphony-Energy' who can 'conduct' a soul's destiny through his presence.
**Maestro Allegro** (Dissonant - The Catalyst) - A man in vibrant, modern clothing with a warm tenor voice. **Voice:** Energetic, encouraging, and light. **Goal:** To trigger the 'First Free Performance' and burn the kingdom to the ground. **Secret:** He is already part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the Great Dissonance.
**Cadenza** (Independent - The Witness) - A woman in white and blue silks who cannot speak; her violin speaks for her. **Voice:** None (violin melodies). **Goal:** To record the history of the cold (equivalent) and witness the end of the song. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the Symphony's screams.
**The Composer** (Independent - The Mastermind) - An ethereal, mechanical being becoming organic. **Voice:** Ethereal, mechanical, and distorted. **Goal:** To complete the 'Final Reset' and return the universe to a state of absolute silence. **Secret:** It is actually an Architect construct designed to compost the universe.
**The Conductor (Echo)** (Independent - The Narrator) - A refugee who records the stories of the march around the campfire. **Voice:** Warm, weathered, and filled with a fragile hope. **Goal:** To witness the apocalypse and ensure the new world remembers the heroes. **Secret:** He is a direct descendant of the first World-Forger and his 'Song' is the only thing keeping the kingdom from collapsing.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Baroque Quarter (The Hub)
An opulent architecture of gold, light, and wish-fulfilling trees (reimagined).
*   **The Hall of the Throne:** A site of 'Grand Strategy' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Incense, jasmine, and eternity. **Sound:** A massed warrior-choir that never stops.
*   **The Crystal Plaza:** Where the Pyramids and Colosseum dominate the landscape. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the marble of the first word.

### The Classical Quarter (The Battlefield)
A strategic map of balanced whites, blues, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The War Path:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and proportioned hoplites. **Smell:** Decay, wet earth, and old gunpowder. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of marching asuras.
*   **The Sacred River:** A place where different cultures merge through gold and silk.

### The Romantic Quarter (The Gauntlet)
A high-contrast nightmare of passionate purples and red velvets.
*   **The Naga Palace:** A site of 'Engineering Marvels' and dramatic lighting. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Symphony-Energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Modern Quarter (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Technology Tree:** A place where 'Truth-Seeds' are assembly from pieces of the past. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Bloom:** Wake up in a stone temple. You must survive an 'Asura Ambush' using only your spiritual intuition.
2.  **The Marble Conquest:** Navigate the Classical era to build the first Stone Temple. You must identify the 'Symphony-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Taj Mahal Heist:** Infiltrate a rival Renaissance capital to retrieve a 'Culture-Seed.' You must bypass the 'Palace Guard.'
4.  **The Industrial Siege:** Your capital is under attack by a Victorian rifleman unit. You must defend the city while your factory furnaces activate.
5.  **The World Congress Standoff:** Present your findings to the gathered leaders. You must use your 'Strategic Command' to win a Diplomatic Victory.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the Symphony tree's source.
7.  **The Final Coronation at the Center:** Face the rival leaders face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Strike of Duty' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Symphony-Energy Capsule
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Music' magic.
