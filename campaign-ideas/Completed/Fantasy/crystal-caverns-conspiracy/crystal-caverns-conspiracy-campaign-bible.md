# Crystal Caverns Conspiracy: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Earth is not a silent rock; it's a living geode that's being mined for its soul. In the heart of the subterranean metropolis of 'Gemhaven,' the 'Grand Accord' between humanity and the earth was shattered by the discovery of 'Primal Crystals'—organic, growing minerals of immense magical power. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Deep' realized that these crystals were the planet's secondary nervous system. They established 'Gemhaven' as a sanctuary of crystalline beauty and industrial progress. But the 'Cartels' have emerged, a group of wealthy merchants who view the crystals as mere industrial commodities. The 'Conspiracy' isn't just a labor dispute; it's a 'Biological Corruption' where the very air is thick with crystal dust, turning the workers into 'Crystal-Corrupted' beings of rainbow-veined horror.

### Timeline
1.  **The Ingestion (c. -10,000 years):** The first Primal Crystals are discovered in the deepest caverns.
2.  **The Age of Gemhaven (c. -5,000 years):** The city is established as a neutral hub for all crystal research and trade.
3.  **The Great Strike (-1,000 years):** A periodic reality-glitch occurs, resetting the physical world while the 'Karmic-Records' persist.
4.  **The Rise of the Radiant Crown (Last Age):** Magistrate Cornelius Brightgem consolidates the cartels into a single, oppressive hegemony.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** An exhausted dwarf miner (the player) finds themselves in the Lower Gemhaven tenements after a recent 'Crystalline Reset.'
6.  **The Squelch in the Deep (Week 1):** You face your first multi-limbed 'Crystal-Corrupted' parasite in a dark, humid mining shaft.
7.  **The Kara Stoneheart Revelation (Ongoing):** A revolutionary leader (Tier 1 NPC) shares forbidden knowledge about the 'Crystal Wyrm' and the Cartels' true origin.
8.  **The Siege of Upper Gemhaven (Upcoming):** The miners lead a full-scale assault on the opulent merchant quarter to stop the 'Reverse-Rebirth' plan.
9.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff with the Crystal Wyrm (Prisma'keth) in the center of the geode.
10. **The Present Day:** The ocean (equivalent) is a high-contrast nightmare of abyssal blacks and bioluminescent cyans. The air is humid with spores, and the 'Lumen-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Economic Justice' and 'Crystalline Transcendence.' It is about the 'Industrial Exploitation' of the human spirit—the realization that natural beauty is often a mask for human ugliness. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can humanity survive the siren call of a perfect, alien evolution, or is the apocalypse just justice?' It explores the sharp geometry of the crystal formations, the visceral horror of the splitting skin, and the dreamy, synesthetic voice of a being that speaks in colors and sensations. The players are the miners and revolutionaries who must navigate the gurgling chambers and pressure-cooker politics to find a truth that stays human in the dark.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Radiant Crown (The Order)
**Type:** Theocratic/Industrial Bureaucracy
**Leader:** Magistrate Cornelius Brightgem (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Polished crystal architecture, brilliant lighting, and the legal authority of the 'Grand Descent.'
**Rivals:** The Fracture Syndicate, The Miners' Union.

### The Fracture Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Meritocratic/Individualist Criminal Group
**Leader:** The Faceless Broker (Tier 1 NPC - distorted/multi-voiced).
**Asset:** Jazz-like criminal energy, reality-warping 'Taboo' magic, and a refusal to bow to the Crown's order.
**Rivals:** The Radiant Crown.

### The 'Stoneheart' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Mining Sages
**Leader:** Kara Stoneheart (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tech (scavenged) goggles, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Deep Vein' Company (The Experts)
**Type:** Scientific/Bureaucratic Military Branch
**Leader:** Ironhand Magnus (A man of rough, damaged orders).
**Asset:** Industrial mining equipment, heavy armor, and the ability to declare a 'Technological Victory.'
**Rivals:** The Miners' Union (who they view as 'Monstrous').

### The 'Prisma'keth' Remnants (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Pre-Era Force
**Leader:** The Crystal Wyrm (Tier 1 NPC - ethereal/mechanical).
**Asset:** Living constellations, light-language, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beers.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **Magistrate Cornelius Brightgem** (Order - The Leader) - A man in opulent robes with refined features and crystal jewelry. **Voice:** Refined, upper-class British, smooth and persuasive. **Goal:** To build a 'New Gemhaven' on the graves of the old. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Karma-Energy' who can 'persuade' a target's limbs to become plant-life through his touch.
2.  **The Faceless Broker** (Dissonant - The Catalyst) - A being whose voice is distorted by a crystal mask, multiple voices out of sync. **Voice:** Distorted, processed, and multi-voiced. **Goal:** To control the city's underbelly and trade 'The List' for his own profit. **Secret:** He is already part-Apocalypse, having stayed in the 'Void Quarter' too long to maintain his physical form.
3.  **Kara Stoneheart** (Independent - The Witness) - A woman in ragged, dust-covered work clothes with a determined expression. **Voice:** Strong, clear, rallying quality, Scottish/Irish accent. **Goal:** To save her community and find a way to stop the Great Decay. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the crystal's screams.
4.  **Ironhand Magnus** (Deep Vein - The Inquisitor) - A man whose body is a map of countless mining scars. **Voice:** Deep, rough, and damaged. **Goal:** To maintain his bloodline and rule the Wasteland through the control of water. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Crystal Wyrm's armor that he's too professional to share.
5.  **The Crystal Wyrm (Echo)** (Ocean - Mythic Leader) - A massive, queen-like being of biomechanical beauty. **Voice:** Thousands of voices speaking in perfect harmony. **Goal:** To complete the 'Final Evolution' and merge all souls into its own decay. **Secret:** It is actually an Architect construct designed to compost the universe.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Upper Gemhaven (The Hub)
An organic, Art Nouveau monument to symmetry and stained-glass effects.
*   **The Harbor Docks:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harbor sounds. **Smell:** Brine, expensive coffee, and machine oil. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant bell.
*   **The Harvester Bank:** Where the 'Harvested Light' is stored in glass vats. **Visual:** Sickly yellows and warning oranges reflecting in the brass.

### Lower Gemhaven (The Battlefield)
A chaotic, crumbling maze of laundry lines and improvised repairs.
*   **The Antique Shop:** A dark, nostalgic sanctuary where pre-Party items are hidden. **Smell:** Dust, old paper, and a faint hint of lavender. **Sound:** The rhythmic ticking of a clock that still has a second hand.
*   **The Courtyard:** Where the Proles (equivalent) sing and the Cartel's influence is thinnest.

### The Mining Depths (The Gauntlet)
A dark, narrow labyrinth of wooden support beams and organic chambers.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the crawler eggs are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Crystal Geode (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Soul-Core:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a narrow, wet tunnel. You must escape a 'Crawler Ambush' using only your improvised bone-cleaver (equivalent).
2.  **The Crystal Run:** Navigate the islands of the acidic sea to find Kara. You must identify the 'Respiration Seed' (equivalent) before the flicker.
3.  **The Cartel Infiltration:** Infiltrate the Radiant Crown's garden to find evidence of their 'Reverse-Ingestion' plan. You must bypass the 'Blighted Guard.'
4.  **The Heart-Beat Chase:** Lead a fleet of arterial-rafts through a circulation surge. You must cast 'Bile-Spells' while traveling at 100 mph.
5.  **The Brain-Chamber Revelation:** Enter the Cranial Cathedral undercover. You must find your own 'Soul-Memory' without alerting the Immune System.
6.  **The Final Digestion:** The Beast has begun its final 'Reset.' You must defend the Heart Chamber while the self-destruct activates.
7.  **The Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Queen Leech face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you serve the Beast or die for the individual?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Ibelin (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Life-Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Mandala Signet:**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Crystal-Energy Capsule:**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Crystal' magic.
