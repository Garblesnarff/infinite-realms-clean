# The Coral Kingdom's Fall: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a reef that's being cleaned by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Micro-World' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Seed'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirits of the coral. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Flow' realized that 'Reefs' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Currents' and 'Coral Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's biological stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Aquatic-Energy'—a shimmering force found in vibrant rainbows and bleached whites, making every 'Restoration' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Coraline or a Shimmer (equivalent) is to be a ghost in your own reef, knowing that 'The Bleaching' is not a weather—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Ripple (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Current creates the reef with a single, perfectly balanced vibration.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Eight Provinces' to monitor the luminescent energy of the oceans.
3.  **The Mononoke Era (Last Age):** A period of environmental tragedy occurs, establishing the fading beauty and the Code of the Flow.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Reaped' into the reef, only to witness their first 'Bleaching' reveal.
5.  **The Reef-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Pollution Incursion'—a team of gray-white shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Restoration Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the environment, you develop 'Light-Sensitivity' and ritual 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Polypa Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of melodic symbiotic voice and whale song harmonics offers you a 'Sacred Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Knowledge Singularity (equivalent). You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant rainbows and bleached whites. The corals are vibrating, and the 'Reef-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Choice.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that magic is real if you're with your friends. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kurs in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the environmental tragedy, the pride in mastering a restoration pulse, and the melodic, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the coralines and shimmers who must navigate the industrial oppressions and the intensity of a mass uprising to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Coraline Cities (The Hub)
A grand urban monument to architecture, coral, and glowing light.
*   **The Reading Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and expensive ink. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Flow Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Glowing Reef (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where every surface glows with bioluminescence.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Luminescent-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Bleaching Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Void Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and absolute black silhouettes.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Bleaching Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Polypa Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Queen Polypa. You must identify the 'Luminescent-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Pattern Heist:** Infiltrate the Bleaching HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Sync-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Reef Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated gray fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Harmony Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Eraser face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?
