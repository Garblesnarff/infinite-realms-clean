# Retrograde Catastrophe: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a mirror that's being played by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archetypa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Reversal'—an ancient, celestial consciousness who believes that the only true life is 'Unified Inversion.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Zodiac' realized that 'Reality' is governed by 12 archetypal kingdoms. They forged the 'Zodiac Anchors'—metaphysical devices that keep the world's styles separate. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Retrograde-Energy'—a shimmering force found in backward-moving stars and wrong-saturation hues, making every 'Shadow-Integration' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Variable or a Lord is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Catastrophe' is not an ending—it's just a change of perspective.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Draft (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Face creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced expression.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Celestial Undercroft' to monitor the retrograde energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Jungian Era (Last Age):** A period of surreal psychological horror occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Mirror.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the undercroft, only to witness their first 'Retrograde Shift' revealing the shadows beyond.
5.  **The Inverted-Kingdom Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Shadow Incursion'—a team of twelve speaking as one—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Integration Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the shadows, you develop 'Shadow-Sensitivity' and inverse 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Reversal Revelation (Ongoing):** A being of twelve voices and sad but not menacing offered you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Retrograde Core. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant reds and desaturated grays. The kingdoms are vibrating, and the 'Mirror-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Endless Archetypes.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that wholeness requires darkness. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kursi in an eternal collapse (equivalent)?' It explores the surreal beauty of the inverted fairy tale, the pride in mastering a shadow integration, and the multiple, resonant voice of a reversal who understands that every moment is a prayer. The players are the variables and lords who must navigate the psychological thrillers and the intensity of a retrograde shift to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Archetypa (The Hub)
**Type:** Hub
**Description:** A massive, high-contrast monument to architecture, mirrors, and zodiac energy.
*   **The Mirror Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh narrative rituals. **Smell:** Ozone, old paper, and expensive perfume. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Zodiac Tree:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Inverted Kingdoms (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each kingdom has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Mirror-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Shadow Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Reversal Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and shadow masks.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Lords Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Valorian Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Valorian. You must identify the 'Mirror-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Anchor Heist:** Infiltrate the Reversal HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Zodiac-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Kingdom Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated shadow fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Integration Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Reversal face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

### 1. The Blade of Mirror (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Shadow Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Zodiac Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the zodiac seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Mirror-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Inversion' magic.
