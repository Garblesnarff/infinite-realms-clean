# Sky Dominion: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a floating island that's being played by an invisible current. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Laputa' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Aetherium'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the sky. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Flight' realized that 'Floating Cities' are the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Robots' and 'Sky-Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Sky-Energy'—a shimmering force found in blue-glowing crystals and golden sunlight, making every 'Vision' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Pilot or a Visionary is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Orbital Strike' is not a weapon—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Flight (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Aviator creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced levitation move.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Laputa' sky-city to monitor the aetherium energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Ghibli Era (Last Age):** A period of steampunk wonder occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Flight.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives at the sky-city, only to witness their first 'Robot Awakening.'
5.  **The Cloud-Palace Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Goliath Incursion'—a team of military shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Sky Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the airships, you develop 'Aether-Sensitivity' and royal 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Sheeta Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of young soprano voice and steel core offers you a 'Visionary Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Laputa Gardens. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of sky blues and industrial slates. The islands are vibrating, and the 'Aether-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Authoritarian Threat.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can have power and still be human. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single курса in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful wonder of the Studio Ghibli inspired world, the pride in mastering a floating island, and the young, resonant voice of a visionary who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the pilots and visionaries who must navigate the industrial oppressions and the intensity of a sky battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

### The Sky Pilots (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Heroic Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Dola (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Airships, ancient pendants, and the 'Sync' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Goliath Syndicate, The Stagnant-Spawn.

### The Goliath Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Military Group
**Leader:** Colonel Muska (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Industrial architecture, 'Orbital' magic, and absolute control of the city's supply lines.
**Rivals:** The Sky Pilots.

### The 'Sheeta' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Aether Sages
**Leader:** Sheeta (Tier 1 NPC - the visionary).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, robotic guardians, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

**Type:** Independent/Lost Sky Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Laputa history, 'Freedom-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be ordered.
**Rivals:** The Goliath Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing destruction spells, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Laputa (The Hub)
A grand sky monument to architecture, gardens, and ancient aether.
*   **The Crystal Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and expensive paper. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Aether Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Floating Islands (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each island has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Aether-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Sky Spire:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Goliath Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and military grey.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Flight Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Goliath Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Sheeta Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Sheeta. You must identify the 'Aether-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Pendent Heist:** Infiltrate the Goliath HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Aether-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Sky Siege:** Your academy is under attack by a coordinated silent fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Aether Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Colonel Muska face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Flight (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Aether Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Pendent Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the pendent seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Aether-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Military' magic.
