# Daemon Revolution: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Soul is not an island; it's a companion that walks beside you. In the heart of the Victorian Gothic metropolis of Jordan College (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' between humanity and the spirit world was shattered by the discovery of 'Dust'—elementary particles of pure consciousness that are attracted to adults and repelled by children. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Bond' realized that every human was born with a 'Daemon'—a photorealistic animal manifestation of their inner truth. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Dust-Energy'—a shimmering force found in the aurora lights and alethiometer mechanisms, making every truth-seeking action a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Seeker or a Rebel is to be a guardian of the 'Bond,' knowing that 'Intercision' is not a surgery—it's the surgical removal of the soul.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** Dust descends from the heavens, triggering the original daemon bond.
2.  **The Era of Dogma (c. -10,000 years):** The Magisterium establishes the 'Order of Progress' and the Code of Authority.
3.  **The Great Separation (-1,000 years):** A periodic reality-glitch occurs, resetting the physical world while the 'Musical-Records' (equivalent) persist.
4.  **The Rise of the Bolvangar (Last Age):** Mrs. Coulter leads a campaign of 'Intercision Research' to eliminate the Dust-influence in children.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A newly awakened seeker (the player) finds themselves in Jordan College after a recent 'Daemon Reset.'
6.  **The Alethiometer Reveal (Week 1):** You receive an ancient truth-telling device that hums with the key of the first Strategist.
7.  **The Iorek Duel (Ongoing):** An armored bear fight in the Arctic wastes where notes (equivalent) manifest as attacks.
8.  **The Aurora Bridge Opening (Upcoming):** The final reality-tear in the northern lights where all worlds occupy the same space.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of aurora greens and Magisterium grays. The zeppelins are circling, and the 'Dust-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Spiritual Freedom' and 'Institutional Authority.' It is about the 'Visceral Horror' of the human condition—the realization that true power comes from truth, not dogma. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain yourself when the system itself wants to delete your soul?' It explores the naturalistic magic of the animal daemons, the tragedy of the 'surgical soul removal,' and the cold elegance of a woman who knows that wisdom is found in every word. The players are the seekers and rebels who must navigate the subculture politics and the intensity of a reality-tear to find a truth that stays fabulous in the light.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Magisterium (The Order)
**Type:** Theocratic/Totalitarian Global Superpower
**Leader:** Mrs. Coulter (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Authority black architecture, 'Intercision' magic, and total control of the narrative.
**Rivals:** The Asriel Rebels, The Witches.

### The Asriel Rebels (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Meritocratic/Individualist Rebel Group
**Leader:** Lord Asriel (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Stolen alethiometers, knowledge of the aurora gaps, and a refusal to bow to the Authority.
**Rivals:** The Magisterium.

### The 'Dust' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Scientific Sages
**Leader:** Lyra Belacqua (Tier 1 NPC - the bridge).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, truth-blue magic, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The Panserbjørn (The Experts)
**Type:** Militaristic/Martial Arts Bear Race
**Leader:** Iorek Byrnison (A bear of deep bass and absolute certainty).
**Asset:** Sky-iron armor, knowledge of the Arctic wastes, and the ability to declare a 'Martial Victory.'
**Rivals:** The Magisterium (who they view as 'Monstrous').

### The 'Spectre' Remnants (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Daemon-Eaters
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Shadow-purple voids, reality-tearing tendrils, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings with Daemons.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Mrs. Coulter** (Order - The Leader) - A woman in elegant furs with a golden monkey daemon. **Voice:** Nicole Kidman's cold elegance, maternal warmth hiding cruelty. **Goal:** To control the Dust and protect the Magisterium's order. **Secret:** She is secretly an Adept of 'Dust-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's memory through her touch.
**Lord Asriel** (Rebel - The Villain) - A man in a tailored long coat with a snow leopard daemon. **Voice:** Daniel Craig's intensity, aristocratic rebel. **Goal:** To overthrow the Authority and build a 'New Republic' of heaven. **Secret:** He is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the aurora bridge.
**Iorek Byrnison** (Panserbjørn - The Expert) - A massive polar bear in sky-iron armor. **Voice:** Deep bass, slow speech, absolute certainty. **Goal:** To reclaim his throne and protect Lyra. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Magisterium's armor that he's too professional to share.
**Lee Scoresby** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A man in a vintage flannel (equivalent) with a hare daemon. **Voice:** Sam Elliott's Texan drawl, humor masking heroism. **Goal:** To provide aerial support and find a 'Safe Moment' for the seekers. **Secret:** He is a direct descendant of the first Architect and his 'Zeppelin-Technology' is the only thing keeping the seekers airborne.
**Lyra Belacqua (The Link)** (Independent - The Lead) - A young girl in practical Arctic gear with an ever-shifting daemon. **Voice:** Sincere, sarcastic, and emotionally resonant. **Goal:** To find her friend Roger and outrun her own ghosts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the alethiometer's clicks.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Jordan College (The Hub)
A Victorian-meets-Arctic monument to ancient knowledge and old stone.
*   **The Master's Study:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Ink, old paper, and expensive port. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Crypt:** Where the 'Truth-Seeds' were first received. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the marble of the first word.

### The Arctic Wastes (The Battlefield)
A strategic map of ice blues, aurora greens, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Bear Fortress (Svalbard):** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and armored bear duels. **Smell:** Sulfur, charcoal smoke, and wet fur. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the wind through the ice.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** A dark, silent place where the shadows move wrong.

### The Magisterium HQ (The Gauntlet)
A monumental fortress of authority black and dogma gray.
*   **The Intercision Lab (Bolvangar):** A site of 'Engineering Marvels' and electricity blue light. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Dust-Energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Aurora Bridge (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Worldspine:** Where the 'Final Choice' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Sealing:** Wake up in Jordan College. You must survive your first 'Magisterium Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Alethiometer Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Lyra. You must identify the 'Dust-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Bear Heist:** Infiltrate the Svalbard fortress to retrieve a stolen 'Armor-Focus.' You must bypass Iorek's guards.
4.  **The Intercision Rescue:** The city is under attack by a reality-warping drone. You must defend the laboratory while the Asriel Rebels mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Aurora Crossing:** Lead a team of seekers into the spirit world to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the non-euclidean geometry.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Mrs. Coulter and the Authority face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Subtle Knife
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A simple, double-edged knife that seems to hum with the energy of the first Architect.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Reality-Cut' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Alethiometer
**Type:** Artifact (Focus)
**Description:** A brass truth-telling device that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'See' any communication within 1 mile. Once per day, the user can 'Lock' a target's memory for 1 hour.

### 3. The Dust Capsule
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of your own identity.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Spectre' magic.

(CR 2). Humanoid. HP 30, AC 13 (Dogma patches).
**Abilities:**
*   **Ordered Discipline:** The agent has advantage on attacks if they have an ally within 10 feet.
*   **Neural Prod:** Melee attack. 1d8+3 damage + Target is paralyzed for 1 round.

### 2. Armored Bear (Elite)
(CR 8). Monstrosity. HP 150, AC 18 (Sky-iron).
**Abilities:**
*   **Martial Majesty Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet has disadvantage on Charisma checks.
*   **Seismic Slam:** Melee attack. 4d10 damage + Target is knocked prone.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Mrs. Coulter (Proxy)
(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Cold Elegance Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the leader's 'Grand Design.'
*   **The Final Choice:** Once per combat, Mrs. Coulter can force an enemy to choose between saving their daemon and achieving the mission objective.
