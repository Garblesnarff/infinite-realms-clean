# Death Descent: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a mountain that's waiting to be descended. In the heart of the Pacific Northwest, the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of 'Titan-Energy'—a primal force that grants gravity-defying speed to those with the 'Rider Gene.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Descent' realized that the mountains were not just stone; they were the fossilized bones of the 'First Titans.' They forged the 'Thirteen Blood Runs'—metaphysical anchors of reality that turn the simple act of downhill biking into a 'Spiritual Awakening.' The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Speed-Energy'—a shimmering force found in tire tracks and mountain mist, making every jump a prayer and every descent a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Rider or a Ghost is to be a guardian of the 'Flow,' knowing that 'Gravity' is the only rule that really matters.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The first Titans fall and their bones become the scaffolds for the mountains.
2.  **The Age of the Descent (c. -10,000 years):** The first 'Founders' establish the Blood Runs and the Code of Chivalry (equivalent).
3.  **The Silicon Shift (2001):** Summit Industries (equivalent) attempts to monopolize the 'Titan-Energy' through industrial drilling.
4.  **The Axel Awakening (2010):** A young, cocky rider discovers the 'Speed-Energy' in the Spine run, establishing the first crews.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient rider (the player) arrives at Kahale Village, only to witness stone flowing like water after a trick.
6.  **The Heart Chamber Discovery (Week 1):** You perform the 'Foundations' (equivalent) grind in the Heart Chamber, reclaiming a geological node.
7.  **The Summit Inquisitor Uprising (Ongoing):** Dr. Victoria Summit initiates 'Operation Ground Zero' (equivalent)—a plan to level the mountain.
8.  **The Thirteenth Run Standoff (Upcoming):** The multiple crews must perform all thirteen runs simultaneously to trigger the 'Awakening.'
9.  **The Present Day:** The mountain is a high-contrast nightmare of stone grays and vibrant prismatic fractals. The board never lies (equivalent), and the 'Speed-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Corporate Performance.' It is about the 'Found Family' of the human spirit—the realization that your greatest weapon is your bike, not your reputation. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can the mountain remember what it was before it was drilled, or is the future a zero-sum game of glass and steel?' It explores the realistic texture of the weathered stone, the visceral horror of the snapped frame, and the deep, weathered voice of a man who has seen too many legends vanish into the shadows. The players are the riders and artists who must navigate the crew politics and the intensity of a high-speed chase to find a truth that stays human in the light.

**Leader:** Crash (The Ghost Rider - Tier 1 NPC).

**Type:** Independent/Lost Ancient Voices
**Leader:** The Titan Dreamer (A being of layered, ancient voices).

**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Kahale Village (The Hub)
A traditional wooden monument to ancient carvings and modern repair facilities.
*   **The Main Bay:** A site of 'Grand Strategy' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Pine wood, machine oil, and coffee. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'clank-clank' of a bike lift.
*   **The Storytelling Circle:** A site of 'Visual Representation' and layered history tags.

### The Blood Runs (The Battlefield)
A strategic map of bone-white stone, snow, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Spine:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and high-speed chases. **Smell:** Rust, machine oil, and ozone. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of tire-on-bone screeches.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** A dark, silent place where the shadows move wrong.

### Summit Resort (The Gauntlet)
A pristine corporate monument to glass towers, marble, and steel.
*   **The Executive Terrace:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh artificial lighting. **Visual:** Security cameras reflecting in the chrome walls. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.

### The Heart Chamber (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Pulsing Core:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Run:** Wake up in Kahale Village. You must survive your first 'Summit Ambush' using only your bike and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Blood Run Squelch:** Navigate the Skull Run to find the Ghost Rider. You must identify the 'Respiration Seed' (equivalent) before the surge.
3.  **The Speed Demon Infiltration:** Infiltrate the Axel camp to find evidence of their 'Reverse-Rebirth' plan. You must bypass his guards.
4.  **The Heart-Beat Chase:** Lead a fleet of salvaged vehicles (equivalent) through a city-destruction surge. You must cast 'Speed-Spells' while traveling at 100 mph.
5.  **The Brain-Chamber Revelation:** Enter the Summit Vault undercover. You must find your own 'Soul-Memory' without alerting the Inquisitor.
6.  **The Final Reset:** The mountain's central crystal has begun its final 'Reset.' You must defend the Spires while the self-destruct activates.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Titan Dreamer face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?
