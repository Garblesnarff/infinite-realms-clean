# Lords of the Shattered Ring: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a ring that's being played by an invisible hand. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Lands Between' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the destruction of the 'Shattered Ring'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the Erdtree. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Order' realized that 'Grace' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Runes' and 'Erdtree Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's divinity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Golden-Energy'—a shimmering force found in Erdtree light and blood reds, making every 'Great Rune' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Tarnished or a Lord is to be a ghost in your own kingdom, knowing that 'The Shattering' is not a war—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Ring (c. -Infinity):** The Greater Will (equivalent) creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced ring of light.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Erdtree' to monitor the golden energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Shattering Era (Last Age):** A period of epic dark fantasy occurs, establishing the golden grandeur and the Code of the Tarnished.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) arrives in the Lands Between, only to witness their first 'Great Rune' theft.
5.  **The Stormveil-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Grafted Incursion'—a team of dozens of arms—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Lord Ascension (Ongoing):** As you collect runes, you develop 'Grace-Sensitivity' and royal 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Marika Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of grand authority and tragic beauty offers you a 'Divine Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Erdtree Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of golden lights and Scarlet Rot crimsons. The trees are vibrating, and the 'Golden-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Divine Predetermination.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you can be a lord and still be human. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single rune in an eternal ring?' It explores the epic beauty of the dark fantasy, the pride in mastering a great rune, and the grim, resonant voice of a tarnished who understands that every death is a lesson. The players are the tarnished and lords who must navigate the cryptic prophecies and the intensity of a boss battle to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing plot holes, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Lands Between (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, Erdtree light, and golden grandeur.
*   **The Roundtable Hold:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old paper, and clinical ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Ring Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Shattered Regions (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of Scarlet Rot crimsons, arcane blues, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Arena Floor:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Golden-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Erdtree Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Stormveil Citadel (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and grafted limbs.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Order Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a church cell. You must survive your first 'Grafted Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Melina Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Melina. You must identify the 'Golden-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Rune Heist:** Infiltrate the Stormveil HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Ring-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Erdtree Siege:** Your kingdom is under attack by a coordinated divine fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Grace Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Queen Marika face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Grace (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Golden Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Ring Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the ring seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Golden-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Apocalypse' magic.
