# Riders of Alagaesia: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a dragon that's waking up from a long sleep. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Alagaesia' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Dragon Egg'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the bond. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Riders' realized that 'Dragon-Energy' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Language' and 'Brightsteel Swords' to serve as anchors for the world's stability. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Bond-Energy'—a shimmering force found in sapphire scales and glowing Gedwey Ignasias, making every 'Magic Word' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Rider or a Dragon is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Rebellion' is not a war—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Hatch (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Dragon creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced roar through the void.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Rider Sanctuaries' to monitor the bond energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Inheritance Era (Last Age):** A period of epic dragon fantasy occurs, establishing the Michael Whelan beauty (equivalent) and the Code of the Rider.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) finds a blue stone in the forest, only to witness their first 'Dragon Hatching.'
5.  **The Empire-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Ra'zac Incursion'—a team of black-cloaked shadows—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Rider Ascension (Ongoing):** As you bond with your dragon, you develop 'Magic-Sensitivity' and silver palm 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Eragon Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of formal heroic voice and blue dragon offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Galbatorix Citadel. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of vibrant scale colors and oppressive blacks. The wings are vibrating, and the 'Bond-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Imperial Tyranny.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that power is nothing without honor. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single курса in an eternal book?' It explores the epic beauty of the dragon fantasy, the pride in mastering an ancient language, and the formal, resonant voice of a rider who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the riders and dragons who must navigate the cultural diversities and the intensity of a magical duel to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

### The Dragon Riders (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Sacred Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Eragon (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Brightsteel swords, Gedwey Ignasia, and the 'Bond' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Empire Syndicate, The Shade Underground.

**Type:** Elite/High-Society Tyranical Group
**Leader:** Galbatorix (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Black architecture, 'Dominion' magic, and absolute control of the city's dragon-realms.
**Rivals:** The Dragon Riders.

**Type:** Historical/Grounded Dragon Sages
**Leader:** Saphira (Tier 1 NPC - the dragon).

**Type:** Independent/Lost Rebel Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Empire history, 'Resistance-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be ordered.
**Rivals:** The Empire Syndicate.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing magic symbols, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Galbatorix** (Shadow - The Villain) - A man whose voice is Hierarchical, arrogant, and measured. **Voice:** Seductive, intelligent, and controlled charm. **Goal:** To consolidate all global bond into a single 'Global Empire' under his control. **Secret:** He is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the original Silicon surge.
**Eragon** (Order - The Lead) - A Dragon Rider whose voice is formal, heroic, and resonant. **Voice:** Formal, slightly archaic language, young Rider's voice. **Goal:** To protect the 'Humanity' of Alagaesia and guide the new riders. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Bond-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his ancient language glyphs.
**Saphira** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A sapphire dragon whose voice is majestic, wise, and maternal. **Voice:** Majestic, intelligent eyes, elegant movement. **Goal:** To find the 'destination' and stop the empire-shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the dragon noise.
**The Mentor (Ghost)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless operation-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Galbatorix's logic that he's too professional to share.
**The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient student in practical leather armor. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the bond ritual.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Urithiru (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, spires, and glowing dragon-energy.
*   **The Rider Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and dragon-fire ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Bond Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Shattered Plains (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each realm has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Bond-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Empire Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Galbatorix Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and black metal.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a dragon village. You must survive your first 'Ra'zac Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Eragon Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Eragon. You must identify the 'Bond-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Sword Heist:** Infiltrate the Empire HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Brightsteel-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Urithiru Siege:** Your academy is under attack by a coordinated robotic fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Bond Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Galbatorix face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Bond (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Radiant Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Gedwey Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the gedwey seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Bond-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Dominion' magic.

(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.

*   **Order of Play Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the leader's 'Archaic Script.'
