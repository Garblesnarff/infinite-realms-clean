# Coyote Runs with Wolves: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a territory that's being defended by a thousand jaws. In the heart of the Pacific Northwest, the 'Grand Accord' of humanity was shattered by the return of the 'Pack-Energy'—a primal force that grants shapeshifting abilities to those with the 'Primal Gene.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Pack' realized that the only way to survive the 'Supernatural Menace' (Vampires and Fae) was to form a hierarchical brotherhood. They forged the 'Columbia Basin' as a sanctuary of sage green and river blue. But the 'Alpha' (Adam) has emerged, a man of iron will and protective stance who believes that 'Home' is worth any price. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Territory-Energy'—a shimmering force found in grease and moonlight, making every garage repair a prayer and every howl a coordinate for war. To be a Walker or a Werewolf is to be a guardian of the 'Found Family,' knowing that 'Bargains' always cost more than expected.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The Pack-Energy is released during a prehistoric reality-glitch.
2.  **The Era of Mounds (c. -10,000 years):** The first 'Hogan Sanctuaries' (equivalent) are anchored to the spirit world.
3.  **The Great Separation (-1,000 years):** The werewolves and vampires establish their 'Territorial Accord.'
4.  **The Rise of the Marrok (Last Age):** The leader of all North American werewolves consolidates the packs through a lens of 'Pragmatic Wisdom.'
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A newly awakened coyote-walker (the player) finds themselves in a VW repair garage after a recent 'Shifter Reset.'
6.  **The Vampire Ambush (Week 1):** You survive your first 'Predatory Stillness' strike by a pale, beautiful monster using only your survival knife.
7.  **The Pack Infiltration (Ongoing):** You are hired to investigate a series of 'unexplained' cattle mutilations that lead back to the local pack house.
8.  **The Fae Standoff (Upcoming):** You must lead a team of werewolves and walkers in a final, mandala-like (equivalent) battle to stop the 'Void Season' (Fomorian) ritual.
9.  **The Present Day:** The Columbia Basin is a high-contrast nightmare of desert tans and river blues. The sun is brutal, and the 'Pack-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Supernatural Deception.' It is about the 'Working Class Heroism' of the human spirit—the realization that being a 'Coyote' is the ultimate form of freedom. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain a part of the pack when your own nature wants you to run alone?' It explores the realistic texture of the weathered leather, the visceral horror of the mid-transformation, and the practical, straightforward voice of a woman who knows that wisdom is found in every word. The players are the walkers and werewolves who must navigate the subculture politics and the intensity of a river-chase to find a truth that stays human in the light.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Columbia Basin Pack (The Order)
**Type:** Elite/Hierarchical Shapeshifter Brotherhood
**Leader:** Alpha Adam (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** The Pack House fortress, wolf-forms, and the 'Resilience' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Vampire Syndicate, The Gray Lords (Fae).

### The Vampire Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** High-Society/Seductive Criminal Branch
**Leader:** Marsilia (A woman of predatory stillness and ancient evil).
**Asset:** Pale beauty, 'Mind-Link' magic, and total control of the city's underbelly.
**Rivals:** The Columbia Basin Pack.

### The 'Walker' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Coyote-Sages
**Leader:** Mercy Thompson (Tier 1 NPC - the bridge).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, VW repair expertise, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The Gray Lords (The Experts)
**Type:** Scientific/Otherworldly Fae Kingdom
**Leader:** The Lord of the Hills.
**Asset:** 'Shadow-Intent' magic, knowledge of the Otherworld, and a total disregard for 'Mortal Honor.'
**Rivals:** The Columbia Basin Pack.

### The 'Proles' Survivors
**Type:** Independent/Non-Shifting Human Group
**Leader:** Various 'Normal' People.
**Asset:** Knowledge of the city's mundane secrets and a refusal to participate in the 'Vibe-War.'
**Rivals:** None.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **Mercy Thompson** (Independent - The Lead) - A woman in oil-stained coveralls with a determined expression. **Voice:** Practical, straightforward, and filled with dry humor. **Goal:** To protect her territory and find a way to stop the Fae. **Secret:** She is secretly an Adept of 'Vibe-Energy' who can 'see' the ghosts of the dead through the exhaust of her VW Beetle.
2.  **Alpha Adam** (Pack - The Leader) - A man in a dark leather vest with a military bearing. **Voice:** Authoritative, disciplined, and protective. **Goal:** To turn his pack into a permanent 'Sovereign Order' and protect Mercy. **Secret:** He is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Ember' of the Great Separation.
3.  **Marsilia (Queen)** (Vampire - The Catalyst) - A woman of cold beauty and predatory stillness. **Voice:** Seductive, intelligent, and terrifyingly calm. **Goal:** To rule the city and eliminate the Pack's influence. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the vampire screams.
4.  **The Marrok (Bran)** (Independent - The Mentor) - An old man in a flannel shirt who speaks with 'Pragmatic Wisdom.' **Voice:** Soft, melodic, and filled with a tragic wonder. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all packs and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Fae's armor that he's too professional to share.
5.  **The Coyote (Shadow)** (Walker equivalent - Shifting form) - A massive, intelligent animal that stands on its hind legs. **Voice:** None (human-like intelligence in movements). **Goal:** To stalk the player and wait for the moment of 'Taboo Violation.' **Secret:** It is actually the player character's 'Shadow-Self,' a manifestation of their own repressed desire for forbidden magic.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The VW Garage (The Hub)
A practical, working-class monument to grease, tools, and ancient knowledge.
*   **The Main Bay:** A site of 'Grand Strategy' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Oil, stale coffee, and old wood. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'clank-clank' of a car lift.
*   **The Office:** A site of 'Visual Representation' and breakthrough moments.

### The Columbia River (The Battlefield)
A strategic map of river blues, sage greens, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Riverbank:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and high-speed boat chases. **Smell:** Brine, wet earth, and old gunpowder. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the water.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** A dark, silent place where the shadows move wrong.

### The Pack House (The Gauntlet)
A monumental fortress of stone walls and armored watchtowers.
*   **The Den:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** Rows of wolf-trophies reflecting in the chrome walls. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'thump-thump' of heavy tires.

### The Spirit Mounds (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Altar:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' were first received. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Session:** Wake up in your garage. You must survive your first 'Vampire Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Pack Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find the Alpha. You must identify the 'Pack-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Fae Heist:** Infiltrate a rival Fae's library to retrieve a 'Glamour-Focus.' You must bypass the 'Palace Guard.'
4.  **The Siege Defense:** Your garage is under attack by a coordinated vampire fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Martyr's Stand:** Lead a team of werewolves into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Marsilia and the Fae face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of the Marrok:**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha wolf.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Resilient Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Mandala Signet:**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Pack-Energy Capsule:**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Glamour' magic.
