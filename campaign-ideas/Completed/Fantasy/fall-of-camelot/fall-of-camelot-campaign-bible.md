# Fall of Camelot: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a dream that's being played by a king. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Camelot' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Excalibur'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirit of the land. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Round' realized that 'Chivalry' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Code of Honor' and 'Gothic Towers' to serve as anchors for the world's nobility. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Grail-Energy'—a shimmering force found in royal golds and deep blues, making every 'Quest' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Knight or a Chronicler is to be a ghost in your own legend, knowing that 'The Fall' is not a failure—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Sword (c. -Infinity):** The Lady of the Lake creates Excalibur to serve as the anchor for the world's unity.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Round Table' to monitor the chivalric energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Arthurian Era (Last Age):** A period of medieval romance occurs, establishing the impossible nobility and the Code of the Knight.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient squire (the player) arrives at Camelot, only to witness their first tournament match being 'Dishonored.'
5.  **The Grail-Quest Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Morgan-Shadow'—a creature of dark magic and green flame—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Round Table Ascension (Ongoing):** As you join the fellowship, you develop 'Honor-Sensitivity' and heraldic 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Arthur Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of noble grandeur and tragic wisdom offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Camlann Field. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of desaturated grays and blood reds. The towers are vibrating, and the 'Chivalric-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Impossible Ideals.' It is about the 'Heroic Resilience' of the human spirit—the realization that beauty is doomed by its own perfection. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single illumination in an eternal manuscript?' It explores the romantic beauty of the medieval romance, the pride in mastering a code of honor, and the noble, formal voice of a chronicler who understands that every attempt is a gift. The players are the knights and chroniclers who must navigate the tragic grandeur and the intensity of a grail quest to find a truth that stays solid in the fall.

**Leader:** King Arthur (Tier 1 NPC).

**Type:** Independent/Lost Sacred Communities
**Leader:** The Fisher King (equivalent).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Arthur history, 'Sacred-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be forgotten.

**Leader:** Morgan le Fay (The Sorceress).

4.  **Lancelot** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man whose body is a map of countless tournament-scars. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly world-weary. **Goal:** To maintain the peace between all factions and find a 'Safe Moment.' **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in Arthur's sigil that he's too professional to share.
5.  **The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient squire in practical medieval gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the grail ritual.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Camelot (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to Gothic architecture and medieval perfection.
*   **The Round Table Hall:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh chivalric rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and rich tapestries. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Camelot Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### Camlann Field (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of mud, blood, and desaturated despair.
*   **The War Path:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Chivalric-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Neutral Gap:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### Avalon (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to mists and eternal spring.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Chivalry Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up at the Round Table. You must survive your first 'Mordred Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Arthur Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find King Arthur. You must identify the 'Chivalric-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Grail Heist:** Infiltrate the Morgan HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Grail-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Camelot Siege:** Your city is under attack by a coordinated rebel fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Round Table Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Mordred and the Shadow face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.

**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
