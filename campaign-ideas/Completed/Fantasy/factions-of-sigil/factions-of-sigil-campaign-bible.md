# Factions of Sigil: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a torus that's being played by an invisible lady. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Sigil' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Infinite Planes'—nested realities that converge in a single ring-city. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Cant' realized that 'Belief' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Fifteen Factions'—philosophical brotherhoods that regulate the world's mental energy. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Planar-Energy'—a shimmering force found in aged brass and tarnished bronze, making every 'Portal' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Cutter or a Factol is to be a ghost in your own city, knowing that 'The Lady's Shadow' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Ring (c. -Infinity):** The Lady of Pain creates Sigil as a sanctuary for all beings, outside the control of the gods.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Hall of Records' to monitor the philosophical energy of the city.
3.  **The Faction War (-1,000 years):** A periodic reality-glitch occurs, resetting the physical world while the 'Philosophical-Records' persist.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient cutter (the player) wakes up in the Hive, only to witness a beautiful building folding upward impossibly.
5.  **The Portal-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Xaositect Incursion'—a team of mismatched chaos—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Faction Ascension (Ongoing):** As you join a faction, you develop 'Belief-Sensitivity' and planar 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Lady Revelation (Ongoing):** A towering figure in bladed dress offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Sigil Spire. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of tarnished bronzes and aged brasses. The streets are vibrating, and the 'Planar-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Choice.' It is about the 'Philosophical Resilience' of the human spirit—the realization that belief shapes reality. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single rebus in an eternal script?' It explores the surreal beauty of the ring-city, the pride in mastering a planar cant, and the philosophical, resonant voice of a narrator who understands that every thought is a prayer. The players are the cutters and factols who must navigate the impossible geometry and the intensity of a portal shift to find a truth that stays solid in the change.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Harmonium (The Order)
**Type:** Martial/Totalitarian Strategy Brotherhood
**Leader:** Factol Sarin (equivalent).
**Asset:** Red and gold armor, geometric patterns, and the 'Unity' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Xaositects, The Revolutionary League.

### The Xaositects (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/Ancient Shadow Collective
**Leader:** Factol Karan (equivalent).
**Asset:** Mismatched clothing, chaotic magic, and absolute indifference to the Harmonium's order.
**Rivals:** The Harmonium.

### The 'Dustmen' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Death Sages
**Leader:** Factol Skall (Tier 1 NPC - the guide).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, bone-white robes, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Sensate' Underground (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Experience Communities
**Leader:** Factol Erin (equivalent).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Faction history, 'Vibrant-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be silenced.
**Rivals:** The Harmonium.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** The Lady of Pain (The Gatekeeper).
**Asset:** Razor-sharp shadows, reality-tearing portals, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **Factol Skall** (Order - The Lead) - A man whose voice is philosophical, resonant, and calm. **Voice:** Sage wisdom, dark mystery, sardonic humor. **Goal:** To protect the 'Truth of Death' and guide the new cutters. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Planar-Energy' who can 'rewrite' a soul's destiny through his riddles.
2.  **The Lady of Pain** (Shadow - The Villain) - A towering figure in bladed dress with a crown of blades. **Voice:** Absolute silence, punctuated by razor-sharp echoes. **Goal:** To maintain the 'Sigil Balance' and prevent any god from entering the city. **Secret:** She is secretly part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Note' of the original Silicon surge.
3.  **Factol Erin** (Independent - The Catalyst) - A woman whose voice is vibrant, quick, and street-smart. **Voice:** Urban, modern, and witty banter. **Goal:** To experience every reality and stop the worldline shifts. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the planar static.
4.  **The Dabus (Proxy)** (Independent - The Mentor) - A floating humanoid who speaks in visual rebuses. **Voice:** Pictographic warnings, rhythmic humming. **Goal:** To maintain the impossible architecture and guide the architects. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Lady's shadow that he's too professional to share.
5.  **The Rookie (Ghost)** (Independent - The Lead) - A resilient cutter in practical planar gear. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To find his true identity and a place to belong. **Secret:** He was the player character's biological twin (equivalent), the one who first परफॉर्म the portal ritual.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Hive (The Hub)
A crumbling slums monument to architectural chaos and random portals.
*   **The Mortuary:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh funeral rituals. **Smell:** Ash, vanilla, and old paper. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Portal Alley:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### Sigil's Impossible Ring (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where streets curve upward in all directions.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Planar-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Lady's Ward:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Planar Rifts (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and celestial light.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Planar Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in the Hive. You must survive your first 'Xaositect Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Skall Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Factol Skall. You must identify the 'Planar-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Rebus Heist:** Infiltrate the Hall of Records to retrieve a stolen 'Rebus-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Faction Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated rebel fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Sigil Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Lady of Pain face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Blade of Cant (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Planar Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

2.  **The Rebus Signet (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the rebus seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

3.  **The Planar-Energy Capsule (Equivalent):**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Belief' magic.
