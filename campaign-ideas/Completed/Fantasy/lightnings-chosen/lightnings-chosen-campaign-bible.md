# Lightning's Chosen: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a battery that's being charged by a divine bolt. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Storm Citadel' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Thunder Voice'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the planetary electric grid. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Bolt' realized that 'Lightning' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Trials' and 'Lichtenberg Scars' to serve as anchors for the world's divine energy. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Electric-Energy'—a shimmering force found in electric blues and brilliant whites, making every 'Strike' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Chosen or a Rogue is to be a ghost in your own storm, knowing that 'The Void' is not a peace—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Strike (c. -Infinity):** The Thunder Voice creates the world with a single, massive discharge through the void.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Storm Citadel' to monitor the electric energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Electric Era (Last Age):** A period of divine trial occurs, establishing the Lichtenberg beauty and the Code of the Bolt.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Struck' by lightning, only to witness their first 'Void Incursion' revealing the darkness beyond.
5.  **The Arena-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Void-Wraith'—a creature of absolute darkness—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Chosen Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the lightning, you develop 'Electric-Sensitivity' and silver branching 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Marcus Revelation (Ongoing):** A man of zealot's passion and divine certainty offers you a 'Philosophical Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Storm Throne. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of electric blues and brilliant whites. The citadels are vibrating, and the 'Light-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Divine Surveillance.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the darkness to appreciate the flash. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself is watching you through eyes that flash with power?' It explores the beautiful electric divinity of the lightning, the pride in mastering a trial, and the thunder-punctuated, resonant voice of a deity who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the chosen and rogues who must navigate the reality-breaks and the intensity of an electric shift to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Storm Citadel (The Hub)
A grand stone monument to architecture, lightning, and cosmic surveillance.
*   **The Control Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh tactical rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old paper, and clinical ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Bolt Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Arena of Trial (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape of electric blues, brilliant whites, and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Trial Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Electric-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Lightning Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Void Zones (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and absolute darkness.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Light Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in the citadel. You must survive your first 'Void Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Marcus Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find High Conductor Marcus. You must identify the 'Electric-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Bolt Heist:** Infiltrate the Void HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Storm-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Citadel Siege:** Your station is under attack by a coordinated void fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Chosen Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face The Void Speaker face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Bolt (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Electric Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Trial Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the trial seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Electric-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Void' magic.
