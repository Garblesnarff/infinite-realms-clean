# Oath of the Forgotten: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a ruin that's being played by an invisible spirit. In the heart of the 21st-century 'City of the Dead' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Star-Seed'—an ancient, celestial consciousness that exists within the spirits of the forgotten. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Paladin' realized that 'Grace' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Oaths' and 'Sanctuaries of Solitude' to serve as anchors for the world's divinity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Paladin-Energy'—a shimmering force found in eternal twilight and glowing eye sockets, making every 'Bedtime Story' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Squire or a Paladin is to be a ghost in your own family, knowing that 'The Departure' is not a goodbye—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Squelch (c. -Infinity):** The Grand Spirit creates the city with a single, perfectly balanced sigh in the dark.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Bond-Sanctuaries' to monitor the divine energy of the ruins.
3.  **The Paladin Era (Last Age):** A period of melancholic fantasy realism occurs, establishing the character-focused beauty and the Code of the Paladin.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Adopted' by an undead family, only to witness their first 'Divine Grace' reveal.
5.  **The Training-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Undead Incursion'—a team of beautiful but decaying rivals—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Paladin Ascension (Ongoing):** As you develop relationships, you develop 'Divine-Sensitivity' and sacred 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Blood Revelation (Ongoing):** A skeleton knight of deep warm voice and ancient armor offers you a 'Gentle Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Stagnant-Spire (equivalent). You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of golden oranges and soft purples. The ruins are vibrating, and the 'Heart-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Decay.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the love of the family to appreciate the strength of the oath. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single episode in an eternal romance?' It explores the warm beauty of the melancholic fantasy, the pride in mastering a relationship, and the gentle, resonant voice of a mentor who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the squires and paladins who must navigate the awkward domesticities and the intensity of a divine grace to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Martial/Sacred Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Blood (equivalent - the skeleton knight).
**Asset:** Skeleton armor, ghostly glows, and the 'Bond' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Shadow Syndicate, The Stagnant-Spawn.

**Type:** Elite/High-Society Extraction Group
**Leader:** Stagnatio (equivalent).
**Asset:** Crystalline architecture, 'Stagnation' magic, and absolute control of the city's quiet zones.
**Rivals:** The Dead City Family.

**Type:** Historical/Grounded Divine Sages
**Leader:** Mary (Tier 1 NPC - the priestess).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, translucent robes, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

**Type:** Independent/Lost Cultural Communities
**Leader:** Gus (The Scholar).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Dead-City history, 'Memory-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be stagnant.
**Rivals:** The Shadow Syndicate.

### The 'Void' Remnants (The End-Game)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Dark voids, reality-tearing plot holes, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### City of the Dead (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, ruins, and translucent beauty.
*   **The Family Courtyard:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Vanilla, old stone, and woodsmoke. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Paladin Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Ruined Regions (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where ruins are reclaimed by flowering vines and moss.
*   **The Current Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Paladin-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Twilight Ridge:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Stagnant Vault (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and information overload.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Journey Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a ruined city. You must survive your first 'Stagnant Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Blood Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Blood. You must identify the 'Paladin-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Oath Heist:** Infiltrate the Stagnant's HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Oath-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The City Siege:** Your family is under attack by a coordinated stagnant fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Paladin Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Stagnatio face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Oath (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Paladin Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Bond Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the bond seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Paladin-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Stagnation' magic.
