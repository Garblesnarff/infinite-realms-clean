# The Buried Empire: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a floor; it's a pedestal that's being held by an invisible titan. In the heart of the 21st-century 'Archemoor' (reimagined), the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the discovery of the 'Buried Empire'—an ancient, celestial civilization that exists within the spirits of the stone. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Seal' realized that 'Stasis' is the planet's ultimate defense system. They forged the 'Ancient Crystals' and 'Marble Sanctuaries' to serve as anchors for the world's sanity. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Stasis-Energy'—a shimmering force found in pristine whites and golden feathers (equivalent), making every 'Awakening' a focus for reality-warping power. To be a Mortal or a Guide is to be a ghost in your own story, knowing that 'The Weighing of the Heart' (equivalent) is not an ending—it's a fracture in the universe itself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Shine (c. -Infinity):** Ra (equivalent) creates the world with a single, perfectly balanced solar beam.
2.  **The Age of the Seal (c. -10,000 years):** Humanity establishes the 'Twelve Gates' to monitor the ma'at energy of the provinces.
3.  **The Pharaonic Era (Last Age):** A period of ancient grandeur occurs, establishing the hieroglyphic beauty and the Code of the Scale.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient researcher (the player) is 'Reaped' into the Duat (equivalent), only to witness their first 'Weighing of the Heart.'
5.  **The Hall-Duel Ambush (Week 1):** You face your first 'Set-Incursion'—a team of elongated snouts—in a dark, neon-lit alleyway (equivalent).
6.  **The Scale Ascension (Ongoing):** As you master the trials, you develop 'Balance-Sensitivity' and ritual 'Tattoos.'
7.  **The Solaria Revelation (Ongoing):** A woman of pharaonic regalia and absolute authority offers you a 'Sacred Legacy.'
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the center of the Solar Barque (equivalent). You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of terracotta reds and papyrus greens. The scales are vibrating, and the 'Ma'at-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Absolute Order.' It is about the 'Human Resilience' of the spirit—the realization that you need the chaos of the desert to appreciate the order of the Nile. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the universe itself views your life as a single kursi in an eternal book?' It explores the beautiful afterlife fantasy, the pride in mastering a hieroglyphic spell, and the solemn, resonant voice of a priest who understands that every second is a gift. The players are the mortals and guides who must navigate the celestial politics and the intensity of a divine judgment to find a truth that stays solid in the win.

**Type:** Theocratic/Sacred Defense Brotherhood
**Leader:** Anubis (equivalent - the guide).

**Rivals:** The Chaos Syndicate, The Stagnant-Spawn.

**Type:** Elite/High-Society Extraction Group
**Leader:** Set (equivalent - the villain).

**Type:** Historical/Grounded Divine Sages
**Leader:** Empress Solaria (Tier 1 NPC - the judge).

**Type:** Independent/Lost River Communities
**Leader:** The Newsie.
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Duat history, 'Flow-Energy' magic, and a refusal to be weighed.
**Rivals:** The Chaos Syndicate.

**Type:** Independent/Lost Primordial Force
**Leader:** Apep (The Void Serpent).
**Asset:** Reality-tearing plot holes, dark shadows, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Aethermoor (The Hub)
A grand aquatic monument to architecture, judgment, and ritual.
*   **The Scale Room:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh cellular rituals. **Smell:** Incense, old paper, and myrrh. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant siren.
*   **The Stasis Archives:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Duat Regions (The Battlefield)
A high-contrast landscape where each region has its own visual style.
*   **The Shift Zone:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Ma'at-Energy. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Lake of Fire:** Where the 'Reality-Check' was first revealed.

### The Solar Barque (The Gauntlet)
A dark, reality-distorting monument to body horror and solar radiance.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the special grades are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Judgment Tree:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Squelch:** Wake up in a shift zone. You must survive your first 'Set Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Anubis Run:** Navigate the traditional territories to find Anubis. You must identify the 'Ma'at-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Scale Heist:** Infiltrate the Chaos HQ to retrieve a stolen 'Scale-Focus.' You must bypass the guards.
4.  **The Nile Siege:** Your ward is under attack by a coordinated desert fleet. You must defend the walls while the Slayers mount their counter-strike.
5.  **The Judgment Ascension:** Lead a team of champions into the central abyss to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the reality-glitches.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the leader's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Set face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Balance (Equivalent)
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Divine Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Feather Signet (Equivalent)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the feather seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The Stasis-Energy Capsule (Equivalent)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Chaos' magic.
