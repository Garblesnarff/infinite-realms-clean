# Blades of the Holy War: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Land is not a kingdom; it's a prayer made of stone. In the heart of the 12th century, the 'Grand Accord' of the Earth was shattered by the sound of two different names for God echoing through the same valley. The 'Crusaders' arrived from the West, believing they were the bringers of the 'Prime Harmony' and the 'Cross of Reclamation.' But the 'Saracens' already had their own 'Aethel'—the 'Crescent-Energy' of the desert and the 'Gospel of Hospitality.' Jerusalem is the 'Sanctuary' at the center of the world, a city where every stone has witnessed a thousand years of faith and a thousand years of blood. To be a Knight or a Mamluk is to be a ghost in a world of holy sites, knowing that 'All Under Heaven' is a dream that is being burned by the very people who pray for it.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Bloom (c. -1,000 years):** The holy city of Jerusalem is established as the 'Anchor of Faith' for three religions.
2.  **The First Crusade (1099):** The Europeans capture Jerusalem, establishing the Crusader States through a lens of 'Religious Fervor' and atrocity.
3.  **The Rise of Saladin (1174):** A brilliant, honorable leader unifies the Islamic world to reclaim the 'Crescent Territory.'
4.  **The Balian Arrival (Present Day - Day 1):** A young, practical blacksmith (the player) arrives in Jerusalem to find his father's honor.
5.  **The Siege of Kerak (Week 1):** A violent retreat from a desert fortress that results in the first major 'Blood Red' clash between the two world-orders.
6.  **The Leper King Anomaly (Ongoing):** Baldwin IV, the dying king of Jerusalem, maintains a fragile peace through 'Moral Complexity' and iron will.
7.  **The Battle of Hattin (Recent):** The Crusader army is decimated in the desert, losing the 'True Cross' to Saladin's forces.
8.  **The Siege of Jerusalem (Upcoming):** Balian leads a desperate defense of the city's walls against Saladin's massive army.
9.  **The Present Day:** The Holy Land is a high-contrast nightmare of crusader whites and Islamic greens. The air is thick with the scent of incense and old gunpowder, and the 'Cross vs Crescent' pulse is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Religious Conviction' and 'Human Humanity.' It is about the 'Epic Holy War' of the spirit—the realization that 'God wills it' is often a mask for human greed. The 'Why' is the question: 'What is Jerusalem worth?' (Saladin's answer: 'Nothing. Everything.') It explores the desert tans of the landscape, the visceral horror of the religious atrocity, and the tragic beauty of a sophisticated culture meeting its dark mirror. The players are the knights, mamluks, and priests who must navigate the subtext of a peace treaty and the intensity of a wall-breach to find a resolution that honors the sanctity of life.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Kingdom of Jerusalem (The Defenders)
**Type:** Theocratic Feudal Superpower
**Leader:** Baldwin IV (The Leper King - Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Jerusalem stone walls, the 'True Cross,' and a fragile sense of 'Moral Complexity.'
**Rivals:** The Saracen Empire, The Templar Radicals.

### The Saracen Empire (The Reclaimers)
**Type:** Sophisticated/Scientific Islamic Caliphate
**Leader:** Saladin (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Mamluk horsemen, desert Earth tones, and the 'Crescent-Energy' of hospitality.
**Rivals:** The Kingdom of Jerusalem, The Assassin Sect.

### The Templar Radicals (The Zealots)
**Type:** Extremist/Religious Rebel Group
**Leader:** Reynald de Châtillon (A man of blood-red madness).
**Asset:** Heavy chainmail, a refusal to negotiate, and a desire to trigger 'Ragnarok' (Holy War).
**Rivals:** The Saracen Empire, The Moderate Crusaders.

### The 'Hospital' Healers (The Healers)
**Type:** Scientific/Compassionate Order
**Leader:** The Hospitaller (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of 'Wound Biology,' religious neutrality, and a connection to the 'Sanctity' frequency.
**Rivals:** The Templar Radicals (who view them as 'Soft').

### The Assassin Sect (The Unseen)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Shadow Network
**Leader:** The Old Man of the Mountain.
**Asset:** Hidden fortresses, 'Shadow-Intent' magic, and a total disregard for either faith.
**Rivals:** Both Empires.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Balian of Ibelin** (Kingdom - Practical Lead) - A man in a simple white tabard with a lethal blade. **Voice:** Sincere, precise, and emotionally resonant. **Goal:** To protect the people of Jerusalem and find his own place in the story. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Stone-Intent' who can 'persuade' a wall to hold firm against any siege.
**Saladin (Salah ad-Din)** (Saracen - The Sovereign) - A man in flowing silk armor with a deep, authoritative voice. **Voice:** Authoritative, honorable, and multicultural. **Goal:** To reclaim Jerusalem and restore the 'Crescent Harmony.' **Secret:** He secretly respects the Leper King and views their war as a form of divine dialogue.
**Baldwin IV (The Leper King)** (Kingdom - The Witness) - A man in a silver mask whose body is decaying. **Voice:** Whispered, slow, and filled with a tragic authority. **Goal:** To maintain the peace until his last breath and find a worthy successor. **Secret:** He is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the Holy City through the religious dissonance.
**The Hospitaller** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man of Victorian propriety (equivalent) and medical expertise. **Voice:** Soft, melodic, and filled with a world-weary melancholy. **Goal:** To save the souls of the wounded and provide a 'Spiritual Sanctuary.' **Secret:** He is already part-Apocalypse, having witnessed the First Crusade's atrocities.
**Reynald de Châtillon** (Templar - The Villain) - A man in blood-stained chainmail with an increasingly unhinged expression. **Voice:** Booming, theatrical, and filled with a religious rage. **Goal:** To kill every Saracen in the world and trigger the final judgment. **Secret:** He is secretly funded by an 'External Agency' that wants to see both empires destroyed.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Jerusalem (The Hub)
A sophisticated stone-and-marble city floating on a hill.
*   **The Wailing Wall:** A site of 'Saturated Grays' and harsh midday sunlight. **Smell:** Incense, old stone, and charcoal smoke. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant bell.
*   **The Market:** A forest of colorful stalls and religious iconography. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the stone of the first word.

### The Desert of Hattin (The Battlefield)
An ominous orange and murky brown monument to survival and thirst.
*   **The Horns of Hattin:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and white crosses. **Smell:** Decay, wet earth, and old gunpowder. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the desert wind.
*   **The Silent Well:** A place where the 'Gospel of Hospitality' was first broken.

### The Palace of Baldwin (Kingdom HQ)
A silver and gray-stone monument to imperial arrogance and gold trim.
*   **The King's Chamber:** A dark-wood sanctuary with a view of the city's 'Tension Lines.' **Visual:** The silver mask reflecting in the blood of the first word. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.

### The Tent of Saladin (Saracen HQ)
A vibrant green and gold monument to luxury and sophisticated diplomacy.
*   **The Council Room:** Where the 'Peace Treaty' was first spoken. **Visual:** Blue-white light reflecting off the massive gold reserves. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Contact:** Arrive at Jerusalem. You must meet Baldwin IV and successfully tell your 'Red Story' to gain the King's trust.
2.  **The Kerak Rescue:** Retreat from the fortress during a violent uprising. You must use your 'Practical Honor' to survive the Templar ambush.
3.  **The True Cross Mystery:** Someone has stolen the 'True Cross' from the cathedral. Track the thief through the desert villages.
4.  **The Siege of the Spires:** Lead a team of Guardians to the city walls to stop a 'Wall-Breach' ritual. You must use your 'Stone-Intent' intuition to win the battle.
5.  **The Hattin Survival:** You are captured after the battle of Hattin. You must negotiate your way out of Saladin's tent while maintaining your 'Kingdom Honor.'
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the Shadow's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Saladin face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff at the gates of Jerusalem. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Ibelin
**Type:** Legendary Longsword
**Description:** A simple, iron sword that has been touched by the light of the sun.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Stone-Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The silver Mask of Baldwin
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Helmet)
**Description:** A silver mask that hides the wearer's identity and grants absolute confidence.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer advantage on all Charisma (Persuasion and Intimidation) checks and makes them immune to being recognized as a mortal.

### 3. The True Cross (Fragment)
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, wooden fragment that hums with the key of the first Storyteller.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Holy War' magic.

(CR 5). Humanoid. HP 80, AC 16 (Heavy Chainmail).

(CR 2). Humanoid. HP 30, AC 15 (Silk Armor).
**Abilities:**
*   **Crescent Charge:** The horseman has advantage on attacks if they have moved at least 30 feet this turn.
*   **Scimitar Slash:** Melee attack. 1d8+3 damage.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Saladin (Proxy)
(CR 15). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 250, AC 20.
**Abilities:**
*   **Crescent Harmony Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow Saladin's 'Code of Chivalry.'
*   **The Final Choice:** Once per combat, Saladin can force an enemy to choose between saving an ally and achieving the mission objective.
