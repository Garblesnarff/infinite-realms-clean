# When Stars Walk: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Sky didn't just fall; it walked. In the heart of the 21st century, the 'Grand Accord' of the heavens was shattered by the descent of the 'Starborn'—the twelve physical manifestations of the constellations. Long ago, the 'Founders of the Cosmos' realized that the stars were the planet's ultimate custodians. They anchored the constellations to the sky to prevent reality from collapsing. But the anchors broke, creating the 'Starfall.' Humanity is the 'Mortal Champions,' living in a world of impact craters and warped territories. Legend says the first 'Resilient' realized that the Starborn aren't just monsters; they are 'Divine Remnants' discovering mortality for the first time. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Starlight-Energy'—a shimmering force found in star fragments, making every weapon a focus for celestial power. To be a champion is to be a guest in a world of walking gods, knowing that the only way to save the planet is to help them ascend again.

### Timeline
1.  **The Starfall (2024):** The twelve Starborn strike Earth, triggering a global blackout and the first wave of 'Celestial Warping.'
2.  **The Decade of Awe (2024-2034):** Civilization collapses. Humanity retreats into isolated pockets of resistance or service.
3.  **The Rise of the Coalition (2035):** The first 'Mortal-Divine Harmony' is established in the ruins of a major city, built around a fallen Starborn.
4.  **The Crystalline Plague (Ongoing):** A series of 'uncontrolled transformations' among the champions is officially classified as 'Starfall Fever.'
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A resilient champion (the player) arrives at an impact crater, only to witness Leo (the golden king) erupt from the ground.
6.  **The Starborn Incursion (Week 1):** Aries (the flame warrior) begins attacking the coalition's protective shells.
7.  **The Research Facility Heist (Recent):** A team of scavengers infiltrates a hidden, pre-Starfall lab to retrieve a 'Celestial Barrier.'
8.  **The Final Restoration (Upcoming):** The twelve Starborn themes begin to ascend in synchronization, threatening to trigger a second Starfall.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of celestial golds and warped purples. The air is thick with starlight, and the 'Planetary Collapse' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Divine Purpose' and 'Mortal Survival.' It is about the 'Awe and Terror' of standing before a god—the realization that constellations have become flesh. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human when the gods themselves are discovering their own mortality?' It explores the physical magnificence of the 50-foot Starborn, the visceral horror of the warped landscape, and the god-like, inhuman voice of a being that speaks in celestial material. The players are the mortal champions and divine scouts who must navigate the impact craters and cosmic vistas to find a truth that justifies the struggle for redemption.

### The Starborn Coalition (The Heroes)
**Type:** Cooperative/Mortal-Divine Strategic Alliance
**Leader:** Leo (Equivalent - The Radiant King).
**Asset:** Star fragments, divine blessings (third eyes, extra arms), and the 'Resilience' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Starborn Renegades, The Nihilists.

### The Starborn Renegades (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Elite/Martial Arts God Group
**Leader:** Aries (The Flame Warrior) and Scorpio (The Living Shadow).
**Asset:** Celestial material forms, reality-warping 'Taboo' magic, and a refusal to ascend.
**Rivals:** The Starborn Coalition.

### The 'Starlight' Seekers (The Healers)
**Type:** Scientific/Spiritual Research Group
**Leader:** Dr. Star-Sight (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tech (scavenged) goggles, and a desire to stabilize the Starfall.
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Void' Remnants (The Lost)
**Type:** Independent/Lost Pre-Era Force
**Leader:** None.
**Asset:** Ancient ruins, 'AUM-Energy' monoliths, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** All Living Beings.

### The 'Proles' Survivors
**Type:** Independent/Non-Divine Human Group
**Leader:** Various 'Normal' People.
**Asset:** Knowledge of the city's mundane secrets and a refusal to participate in the 'Star-War.'
**Rivals:** None.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Leo (Radiant King)** (Coalition - The Leader) - A 50-foot tall being of radiant golden light and crystal. **Voice:** God-like, powerful, and increasingly vulnerable. **Goal:** To protect the coalition and find a way to ascend his brothers. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Starlight-Energy' who can 'heal' a warped landscape through his presence.
**Aries (Flame Warrior)** (Renegade - The Villain) - A 40-foot tall being of living fire and Segmented stone armor. **Voice:** Harsh, crackling, and filled with ancient rage. **Goal:** To rule the ruins through the control of 'Temperature-Energy.' **Secret:** He was the first Starborn to strike Earth and his 'Fire' is a byproduct of his own lost divinity.
**Dr. Star-Sight (Ghost)** (Independent - Clinical Healer) - A woman with a compassionate bedside manner and high-tech (scavenged) goggles. **Voice:** Clinical but warm, providing a 'Safe Moment' atmosphere. **Goal:** To repair the champions' DNA and find a 'Celestial Cure' for the Starfall. **Secret:** She is secretly working for a shadow-syndicate that wants to harvest the 'Star-Energy.'
**The Champion (Mark)** (Coalition - Elite Warrior) - A human in armor crafted from the starlight fragments of a previous Starfall. **Voice:** Natural, hesitant, and damaged. **Goal:** To protect the coalition's children and find his own place in the story. **Secret:** He is already part-Starborn, having been 'touched' by Virgo years ago.
**The Shadow (Anomaly)** (Shadow - The Mastermind) - The manifest consciousness of the black void. **Voice:** A whispering, distorted chorus of a thousand drips. **Goal:** To reset the universe and return it to a state of absolute silence. **Secret:** It is actually an Architect construct designed to compost the universe.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Impact Crater (The Hub)
A high-contrast landscape of golden light, warped purples, and broken concrete.
*   **The Hub:** A site of 'Grand Strategy' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Ozone, machine oil, and starlight. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of engines and celestial pulses.
*   **The Memory Walls:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Warped Territory (The Battlefield)
A hyper-real landscape of weaponized nature and ancient trees (reimagined).
*   **The Crystal Forest:** A place where reality is shimmering and plants grow through concrete. **Visual:** Vibrant purple bio-luminescence and white-bleached earth. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of mutated creatures.
*   **The Silent Village:** A place where the 'Invisible Apocalypse' has already passed.

### The Celestial Spires (The Gauntlet)
A monumental fortress of crystal light and impossible geometry.
*   **The Throne Room:** Where the 'Final Choice' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

### The Data Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Star-Map Room:** Where the 'Truth-Seeds' are assembly from pieces of the past. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Strike:** Wake up in an impact crater. You must escape an 'Aries Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Coalition Run:** Navigate the warped territory to find Leo. You must identify the 'Respiration Seed' (equivalent) before the flicker.
3.  **The Renegade Infiltration:** Infiltrate the Scorpio camp to find evidence of their 'Reverse-Ascension' plan. You must bypass their 'Watching Eye' security.
4.  **The Heart-Beat Chase:** Lead a fleet of salvaged vehicles through a city-destruction surge. You must cast 'Starlight-Spells' while traveling at 100 mph.
5.  **The Brain-Chamber Revelation:** Enter the Pre-Starfall Lab undercover. You must find your own 'Soul-Memory' without alerting the Starborn.
6.  **The Final Reset:** The coalition's central crystal has begun its final 'Reset.' You must defend the Spires while the self-destruct activates.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face Aries and the Shadow face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff at the top of the spires. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of the Starborn
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the iron shard of the original Alpha Star.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Resilient Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Celestial Barrier
**Type:** Artifact (Focus)
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Storyteller.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Starfall' magic.

### 3. The Star-Seed Compass
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A pair of high-tech goggles that allow the wearer to see the world in pure Starlight-Energy.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user advantage on all Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence (Investigation) checks. Allows the wearer to see the 'True Aspect' of any Shadow.
