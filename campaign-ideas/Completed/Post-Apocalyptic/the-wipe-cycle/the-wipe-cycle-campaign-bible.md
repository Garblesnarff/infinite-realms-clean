# The Wipe Cycle: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is a reset button that's stuck. Long ago, the 'Islanders' were part of a sophisticated, high-tech civilization that attempted to stabilize reality through the 'Grand Accord.' But the experiment failed, creating the 'Wipe Cycle'—a recurring apocalyptic event that erases every human structure and memory every 30 days. The 'Island' is now a beautiful, indifferent proving ground where humanity is forced to start over from zero. Legend says the first 'Survivor' realized that the only things that persist are the 'Blueprints'—metaphysical anchors of knowledge hidden in the irradiated ruins. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Scrap-Energy'—a shimmering force found in rusted metal and debris, making every gear and spring a component for survival. To live in the Wipe Cycle is to be a ghost in a world of ramshackle bases, knowing that everything you build will be ash by the next moon.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. -Infinity):** The first Wipe Cycle is triggered by an ancient reality-glitch.
2.  **The Age of the Rock (Every Month - Day 1):** Every living soul wakes up naked on a rocky beach with nothing but a rock and a torch.
3.  **The Cloth Wrap Era (Every Month - Day 3):** Survivors scavenge hemp and wood to build the first 'Ramshackle Bases.'
4.  **The Metal Age (Every Month - Day 10):** The first 'Sheet Metal' armor and improvised firearms appear as groups consolidate power.
5.  **The Raid on Dome (Every Month - Day 15):** A high-stakes assault on the central irradiated monument to retrieve elite blueprints.
6.  **The Siege of the Spires (Every Month - Day 20):** Large clans use C4 and rockets to dismantle rival bases in a display of 'Brutal PvP.'
7.  **The Decay Anomaly (Ongoing):** Structures begin to crumble if they are not maintained with 'Tool Cupboard' taxes.
8.  **The Final Countdown (Every Month - Day 29):** The sky turns a sickly green as the radiation levels spike globally.
9.  **The Wipe (Every Month - Day 30):** A blinding white flash erases the world. The cycle begins again.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Persistence' in a world of 'Permanent Futility.' It is about the 'Post-Apocalyptic Brutalism' of the human condition—the realization that we are all just a collection of looted gear. The 'Why' is the question: 'Is it worth building something if you know it will be destroyed?' It explores the realistic decay of the rusted metal, the visceral horror of the raided base, and the dark humor of a man who jokes while losing everything. The players are the adventurers and survivors who must navigate the bear traps and landmines to find a truth that survives the flash.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Lone Wolves (The Survivors)
**Type:** Individual/Self-Sufficient Combatants
**Leader:** None (autonomous units).
**Asset:** Stealth, makeshift weapons, and a total disregard for 'Clan Honor.'
**Rivals:** The Zerg Clans, The Law.

### The Zerg Clans (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Large/Coordinated Raider Syndicates
**Leader:** The Warlord (A man of metal armor and cold calculation).
**Asset:** Infinite numbers, armored watchtowers, and the ability to manufacture C4.
**Rivals:** Every other soul on the island.

### The Radtown Guardians (The Immune)
**Type:** Irradiated/Autonomous Security Units
**Leader:** None (programmed responses).
**Asset:** High-tier loot, radiation fields, and a view of the survivors as 'Contaminants.'
**Rivals:** The Blueprints Seekers.

### The 'Scrap' Merchants (The Healers)
**Type:** Neutral/Transactional Safe-Zone Group
**Leader:** The Bandit King (A man who speaks for the gold).
**Asset:** The 'Outpost' sanctuary, vending machines, and a connection to the 'Trade' frequency.
**Rivals:** None (everyone needs their scrap).

### The 'Blueprints' Seekers (The Scholars)
**Type:** Scientific/Obsessive Research Group
**Leader:** The Archivist (A man who remembers the previous wipes).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, high-tech (scavenged) goggles, and a desire to stop the Cycle.
**Rivals:** The Zerg Clans (who view them as 'Loot').

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**The Survivor (Rust)** (Independent - The Lead) - A man in crude leather armor and a metal chest plate. **Voice:** Gruff, weary, and terse. **Goal:** To survive the month and find a way to stop the wipe. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Scrap-Energy' who can 'fix' a weapon using nothing but a rock and a prayer.
**The Warlord** (Zerg - The Villain) - A man in perfectly maintained military gear. **Voice:** Authoritative, disciplined, and increasingly cold. **Goal:** To dominate the island and build an impenetrable fortress. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Wipe Cycle that allows him to keep one item across resets.
**The Archivist** (Independent - The Witness) - A man whose skin is covered in stenciled blueprints. **Voice:** Whispered, slow, and filled with a tragic wonder. **Goal:** To record the history of every wipe and find the 'Seed of Stability.' **Secret:** He was the one who 'accidentally' triggered the original Silicon Surge.
**The Bandit King** (Independent - The Mentor) - A man in a three-piece suit (equivalent) who runs the Outpost. **Voice:** Smooth, transactional, and authoritative. **Goal:** To monetize the Wipe Cycle and maintain the peace... for a price. **Secret:** He is a high-level Adept of 'Trade' who can 'persuade' a vending machine to yield elite loot.
**The Shadow (Anomaly)** (Shadow - The Mastermind) - The manifest consciousness of the Wipe Cycle. **Voice:** A whispering, distorted chorus of a thousand clicks. **Goal:** To reset the universe and return it to a state of absolute silence. **Secret:** It is actually an Architect construct designed to compost the universe.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Outpost (The Hub)
A neutral, transactional sanctuary of vending machines and scrap metal.
*   **The Vending Machine Hall:** A site of 'Saturated Golds' and harsh midday sunlight. **Smell:** Diesel, stale coffee, and ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'clink-clink' of scrap metal.
*   **The Bandit Camp:** A dark, humid labyrinth of makeshift tents and secret deals.

### The Dome (The Battlefield)
A massive, irradiated monument to ancient power and high-tier loot.
*   **The Internal Catwalks:** A site of 'Seismic Tension' and falling hazards. **Visual:** Blue-white light reflecting off the rusted iron. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the radiation.
*   **The Loot Vault:** Where the 'Elite Blueprints' are assembly from pieces of the past.

### The Zerg Base (The Gauntlet)
A monumental fortress of stone walls and armored watchtowers.
*   **The Main Gate:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and C4 explosions. **Smell:** Sulfur, charcoal smoke, and wet concrete. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'thump-thump' of heavy tires.
*   **The TC Room:** Where the 'Tool Cupboard' is hidden behind multiple layers of armor.

### The Rocky Beach (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Spawn Point:** Where the 'Rock and Torch' were first received. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the sand. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Bloom:** Wake up on the beach. You must escape a 'Wolf Ambush' using only your rock and a makeshift spear.
2.  **The Scrap Run:** Navigate the industrial monuments to find the Bandit King. You must identify the 'Blueprint Node' before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Zerg Heist:** Infiltrate a Zerg watchtower to retrieve a stolen 'Code-Lock.' You must bypass their 'Watching Eye' security.
4.  **The Raid Defense:** Your base is under attack by a coordinated C4 squad. You must defend the TC room while your auto-turrets activate.
5.  **The Dome Expedition:** Lead a team of survivors into the central monument to find the 'Seed of Stability.' You must navigate the radiation fields.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the Archivist's secret vault.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at Dawn:** Face the Warlord and the Shadow face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the armor or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of the Wipe
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the iron shard of the original Alpha Rock.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Brutal Takedown' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Master Key Card
**Type:** Artifact (Focus)
**Description:** A small, sapphire card that hums with the key of the first Islander.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Unlock' any magically or physically sealed door on the island. Once per day, the user can 'Silence' a SAM-site for 1 hour.

### 3. The Blueprint of the Stars
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Tome)
**Description:** A thick, clinical book that contains the records of every wipe.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder advantage on all Intelligence (History) and Wisdom (Insight) checks. **Curse:** The user begins to see the world in 'Sickly Greens' and loses all capacity for empathy.

(CR 5). Construct. HP 80, AC 16.
**Abilities:**
*   **Radiation Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet takes 1d10 necrotic damage at the start of their turn.
*   **Neural Prod:** Melee attack. 2d10 damage + Target is paralyzed for 1 round.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Shadow (Proxy)
(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Brutal Futility Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be paralyzed by the weight of the coming flash.
*   **The Final Reset:** Once per combat, the Shadow can force all creatures within 100 feet to re-roll their initiative.
