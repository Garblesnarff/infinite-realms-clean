# The Cordyceps Crown: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Earth didn't fall to a god; it fell to a fungus. In the heart of the 21st century, the 'Grand Recalling' was not a celestial event—it was a biological one. The 'Cordyceps' fungus, having perfected its control over insects, made the jump to humanity. The world we knew didn't die; it was 'Reclaimed' by a nature that viewed our civilization as a host. Humanity is the 'Crown of Thorns,' living in a world of overgrown skyscrapers and sickly yellow-orange fungal plates. Legend says the first 'Immune' realized that the fungus wasn't just a parasite; it was a 'Network of Memory.' The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Spore-Energy'—a shimmering force that flows through the fungal growths, making every breath a gamble with infection and every connection a sanctuary of found family.

### Timeline
1.  **The Outbreak (2013):** The Cordyceps infection sweeps the globe, leading to the collapse of national governments within weeks.
2.  **The Quarantine Era (2013-2033):** FEDRA (equivalent) establishes oppressive concrete zones to contain the remaining human population.
3.  **The Firefly Uprising (Ongoing):** A rebel network begins a campaign of 'Hope and Despair,' seeking a cure through any means necessary.
4.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A world-weary survivor (the player) is hired to smuggle a teenage girl (Ellie) out of a Boston Quarantine Zone.
5.  **The Clicker Encounter (Week 1):** You face your first blind, fungal-headed 'Clicker' in a dark, spore-filled museum.
6.  **The Bill's Town Incursion (Recent):** A team of survivors navigates a town filled with improvised traps and infected Bloaters.
7.  **The University Revelation (Upcoming):** A hidden research facility reveals that the 'Cure' requires the sacrifice of the only person you care about.
8.  **The Final Bloodletting (Upcoming):** A face-to-face standoff at the Firefly hospital. You must choose between saving the world and saving your daughter.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of overgrown greens and rust browns. The air is thick with spores, and the only hope is the desperate love of a dying species.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Purpose' in a world of 'Meaningless Loss.' It is about the 'Oppressive Beauty' of the human spirit—the realization that love is the only thing worth fighting for in a dead world. The 'Why' is the question: 'Would you trade the survival of humanity for the life of one person?' It explores the grounded realism of the weathered leather, the visceral horror of the fungal plate, and the world-weary voice of a man who refuses to lose another person he cares about. The players are the survivors and protectors who must navigate the infected zones and moral darkness to find a truth that stays human in the light.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Survivors (The Guardians)
**Type:** Elite/Survivalist Found Family Units
**Leader:** Joel (Tier 1 NPC - the protector).
**Asset:** Hunting rifles, switchblades, and the 'Found Family' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Infected, The Fireflies.

### The Fireflies (The Seekers)
**Type:** Rebel/Scientific Revolutionary Network
**Leader:** Marlene (A woman of military precision and tragic hope).
**Asset:** Knowledge of Cordyceps biology, high-tech (scavenged) labs, and a desire to find a cure.
**Rivals:** FEDRA, The Survivors.

### The Infected (The Reclaimed)
**Type:** Mutated/Magical Fungal Entities
**Leader:** The Bloater (A massive, armored mountain of spores).
**Asset:** Echolocation (Clickers), reality-warping spore clouds, and absolute indifference to human morality.
**Rivals:** Humanity.

### FEDRA (The Enforcers)
**Type:** Theocratic/Totalitarian Military Agency
**Leader:** Various Officers.
**Asset:** Concrete quarantine zones, ration cards, and the legal authority of the 'Grand Containment.'
**Rivals:** The Fireflies.

### The 'Ghost' Hunters
**Type:** Independent/Lost Human Raiders
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Knowledge of the ruins and a willingness to kill for a backpack or a can of beans.
**Rivals:** Everyone.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Joel** (Survivor - The Protector) - A middle-aged man in a weathered leather jacket with a gray beard. **Voice:** Gruff, world-weary, and protective. **Goal:** To get Ellie to the Fireflies and outrun his own ghosts. **Secret:** He is a high-level Adept of 'Spore-Energy' who can 'feel' an infected's approach through the vibrations of the fungal network.
**Ellie** (Survivor - The Catalyst) - A teenage girl in a red hoodie with a purple backpack and determined eyes. **Voice:** Sincere, sarcastic, and emotionally resonant. **Goal:** To be the 'Cure' and find a place where she belongs. **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the fungal plates.
**Marlene** (Firefly - The Leader) - A woman in military gear with a compassionate but unyielding expression. **Voice:** Precise, authoritative, and filled with a tragic wonder. **Goal:** To save humanity through the sacrifice of the Immune. **Secret:** She was the best friend of Ellie's mother and views her goal as a form of divine dialogue.
**Bill** (Independent - The Expert) - A man whose house is a map of countless traps. **Voice:** Gruff, paranoid, and cynical. **Goal:** To stay alive and outrun his own memories. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the Bloater's armor that he's too professional to share.
**The Clicker (Echo)** (Infected equivalent - Shifting form) - An infected human with a fungal-plate head. **Voice:** None (disturbing clicking sounds). **Goal:** To reclaim the 'Surface Souls' for the fungal network. **Secret:** The Clickers are not hunting for food; they are protecting a 'Reality-Anchor' hidden in the museum.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Boston Quarantine Zone (The Hub)
A high-contrast landscape of concrete grays, barbed wire, and oppressive walls.
*   **The Checkpoint:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh artificial lighting. **Smell:** Bleach, stale sweat, and machine oil. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Ration Line:** A site of 'Dread and Helplessness' and missed clues.

### The Reclaimed Megalopolis (The Battlefield)
A hyper-real city monument to weaponized nature and overgrown skyscrapers.
*   **The Museum:** A site of 'Initial Squelch' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the blood of the first Clicker. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the clicking.
*   **The Spore Cellar:** Where reality is shimmering and walls are covered in orange plates.

### Bill's Town (The Gauntlet)
A small, wooden monument to resourcefulness and improvised traps.
*   **The Church:** A site of 'Spiritual Sanctuary' and warm amber light. **Smell:** Dust, old paper, and a faint hint of 'Spore' ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant bell.
*   **The Trap-Labyrinth:** A chaotic maze of tripwires and secret deals.

### The Firefly Hospital (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Operating Room:** Where the 'Final Choice' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Escape:** Smuggle Ellie out of the Boston QZ. You must survive your first 'Clicker Ambush' while maintaining your 'Silence' level.
2.  **The Museum Heist:** Infiltrate the Cordyceps-filled museum to retrieve a stolen 'Spore-Focus.' You must bypass the blind predators.
3.  **Bill's Town Run:** Navigate the trapped streets to find Bill. You must identify the 'Respiration Seed' (equivalent) before the Bloater arrives.
4.  **The University Revelation:** Infiltrate the hidden research lab to find the truth about the cure. You must navigate the 'Maze of Stories' without getting lost.
5.  **The Hospital Siege:** Lead a charge of Fireflies (or survivors) in a full-scale assault on the operating room. You must protect Ellie while the self-destruct activates.
6.  **The Final Choice:** You are ordered to kill the doctors to save Ellie. You must decide whether to save the world or the individual.
7.  **The Last Lie:** Face Ellie face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of the Immune
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A simple, silver switchblade that seems to hum with the energy of the first Catalyst.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Clicker-Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. Joel's Watch
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A broken watch that never moves and hums with the key of the first Survivor.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Authorize' any action, making it immune to emotional obstacles for 1 hour. Once per day, the user can 'Freeze' a reality-tear.

### 3. The Firefly Pendant
**Type:** Artifact (Focus)
**Description:** A small, silver pendant that contains the name of a fallen rebel.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder advantage on all Charisma (Persuasion and Intimidation) checks inside the ruins. **Curse:** The holder is hunted by FEDRA.

## [TAG: ENEMY_STATBLOCK]
### 1. Infected Runner (Standard)
(CR 2). Humanoid. HP 30, AC 13 (Fungal patches).
**Abilities:**
*   **Primal Rage:** The runner has advantage on attacks if they have moved at least 30 feet this turn.
*   **Vicious Bite:** Melee attack. 1d8+3 damage + Target is infected (DC 13 CON save).

### 2. Fungal Clicker (Elite)
(CR 5). Monstrosity. HP 80, AC 15.
**Abilities:**
*   **Echolocation:** Any creature within 60 feet cannot use the Hide action unless they are stationary.
*   **Clicking Gaze:** Ranged attack (Psychic). 2d10 damage + Target is paralyzed for 1 round.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Bloater (Proxy)
(CR 12). Monstrosity (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Spore Cloud Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the fungus's 'Network Directive.'
*   **The Final Reclaiming:** Once per combat, the Bloater can force an enemy to take an action that serves the stability of the infection.
