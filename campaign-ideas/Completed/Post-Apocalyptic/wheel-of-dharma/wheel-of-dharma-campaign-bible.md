# Wheel of Dharma: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Universe is not a void; it's a cosmic dance that's being performed by a thousand arms. In the beginning, the 'Architect' (Brahma) spoke the first 'AUM,' and the 'Grand Accord' of reality began to spin the 'Wheel of Dharma.' Heaven (Svarga) is the 'Prime Harmony,' a city of impossible colors and wish-fulfilling trees. But the wheel has three spokes: Creation, Preservation, and Destruction. Long ago, the 'First Sage' realized that 'Duty' (Dharma) is the only thread that keeps the tapestry from unraveling across lifetimes. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Karma-Energy'—a shimmering force that flows through every action and thought, making every choice a seed for a future life. To be a warrior or a seeker is to be a ghost in a world of multiple arms and third eyes, knowing that 'Righteousness' requires terrible choices and that the wheel turns eternally regardless of your wishes.

### Timeline
1.  **The First AUM (c. -Infinity):** Brahma creates the universe. The wheel begins to spin.
2.  **The Age of the Devas (c. -1 Billion years):** The gods and asuras (demons) establish their 'Dharmic Accord' and build their celestial palaces.
3.  **The Mahapralaya (c. -1 Million years):** A periodic cosmic dissolution occurs, resetting the physical world while the 'Soul-Records' persist.
4.  **The Rise of Ravana (Last Age):** A powerful demon king disrupts the cosmic order, seeking to monopolize the 'Karma-Energy.'
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A newly awakened soul (the player) finds themselves in a vibrant Indian temple after a recent 'Karmic Reset.'
6.  **The Krishna Encounter (Day 3):** You meet a blue-skinned prince who offers a playful yet profound flute melody and a vision of your 'Perfected Self.'
7.  **The Shiva Standoff (Ongoing):** The god of destruction begins his cosmic dance, his approach marked by deep drums and ash-white lighting.
8.  **The Siege of Svarga (Upcoming):** You must lead an army of gods and demons in a final, mandala-like battle to stop the 'Reverse-Dharma' ritual.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of saffron oranges and jewel tones. The 'Karma-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic, and the wheel is turning toward the center.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Personal Desire' and 'Eternal Duty.' It is about the 'Cerebral Grandeur' of the human soul—the realization that you are just a variable in a cosmic equation of time and karma. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you fulfill your dharma when it requires sacrificing everything you love?' It explores the symbolic density of the Hindu tradition, the tragedy of the 'Righteous Choice,' and the philosophical voice of a sage who knows that every life is just a stepping stone toward the stars. The players are the seekers and warriors who must navigate the gopurams and sacred rivers to find a resolution that honors the memory of the Architects.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Deva Collective (The Order)
**Type:** Theocratic Divine Superpower
**Leader:** Krishna (Equivalent - The Preserver).
**Asset:** Svarga palaces, celestial weapons, and the 'Dharma-Energy' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Asura Syndicate, The Nihilists.

### The Asura Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Meritocratic/Individualist Demon Group
**Leader:** Ravana (A man of ten heads and absolute ambition).
**Asset:** Patala (underworld) kingdoms, reality-warping 'Taboo' magic, and a refusal to bow to the Deva's order.
**Rivals:** The Deva Collective.

### The 'Dharma' Seekers (The Survivors)
**Type:** Historical/Grounded Indian Sages
**Leader:** Various Rishis (Sages).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient hierarchies, third eyes, and the ability to trigger 'Golden Ages.'
**Rivals:** None (they are the moral compass).

### The 'Karma' Archivists
**Type:** Scientific/Bureaucratic Spiritual Branch
**Leader:** Lord Yama (The God of Death).
**Asset:** Access to the 'Soul-Records,' knowledge of past lives, and the ability to declare a 'Karmic Victory.'
**Rivals:** The Nihilists.

### The 'Void' Remnants
**Type:** Independent/Lost Pre-Era Group
**Leader:** None.
**Asset:** Ancient ruins, 'AUM-Energy' monoliths, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** None.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Krishna** (Deva - The Leader) - A man in blue skin and royal yellow robes with a peacock feather crown. **Voice:** Playful yet profound, musical, and perfectly measured. **Goal:** To preserve the cosmic balance and guide the player toward their dharma. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Karma-Energy' who can 'persuade' a soul's destiny through his flute melodies.
**Shiva (The Destroyer)** (Deva - The Catalyst) - A man in ash-white skin with a blue throat and a third eye. **Voice:** Deep, resonant, and accompanied by cosmic drums. **Goal:** To reset the universe and return it to a state of absolute silence. **Secret:** He still carries the 'Memory of Creation' and is terrified of his own absolute rage.
**Durga (The Mother)** (Deva - The Warrior) - A woman in red and gold with multiple arms holding symbolic items. **Voice:** Strong, authoritative, and filled with divine passion. **Goal:** To protect the innocent and destroy the 'Dharmic Corruption.' **Secret:** she is the only person who can 'hear' the original Song of the World through the battle cries.
**Hanuman** (Independent - The Link) - A monkey-featured being of impossible strength and absolute loyalty. **Voice:** Gentle, rhythmic, and woodwind-toned. **Goal:** To serve his master and find a place where he belongs. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the demon's armor that he's too professional to share.
**The Sage (Echo)** (Independent - The Narrator) - A man with a frame drum and a tagelharpa (equivalent). **Voice:** Epic narrator channeling the Mahabharata. **Goal:** To witness the end of the song and record the final silence. **Secret:** He is a direct descendant of the first World-Forger and his 'Song' is the only thing keeping the wheel from collapsing.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Svarga (The Hub)
An impossible architecture of gold, light, and wish-fulfilling trees.
*   **The Hall of the Throne:** A site of 'Grand Strategy' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Incense, jasmine, and eternity. **Sound:** A massed warrior-choir that never stops.
*   **The Celestial Plaza:** Where the Pyramids and Colosseum (reimagined) dominate the landscape. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the marble of the first word.

### Mount Meru (The Battlefield)
The impossible cosmic axis of snowy peaks and dangerous shadows.
*   **The War Path:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and bronze-armored hoplites (equivalent). **Smell:** Decay, wet earth, and old gunpowder. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of marching asuras.
*   **The Sacred River:** A place where different cultures merge through gold and silk.

### Patala (The Gauntlet)
A high-contrast nightmare of jewel tones and subterranean kingdoms.
*   **The Naga Palace:** A site of 'Engineering Marvels' and coral growth. **Visual:** Rows of machines glowing with Karma-Energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.

### The Soul-Core (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Karma Tree:** A place where 'Truth-Seeds' are assembly from pieces of the past. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Bloom:** Wake up in a stone temple. You must survive an 'Asura Ambush' using only your spiritual intuition.
2.  **The Marble Conquest:** Navigate the Classical era to build the first Stone Temple. You must identify the 'Karma-Energy' node before the rivals arrive.
3.  **The Taj Mahal Heist:** Infiltrate a rival Renaissance capital to retrieve a 'Culture-Seed.' You must bypass the 'Palace Guard.'
4.  **The Industrial Siege:** Your capital is under attack by a Victorian rifleman unit. You must defend the city while your factory furnaces activate.
5.  **The World Congress Standoff:** Present your findings to the gathered leaders. You must use your 'Strategic Command' to win a Diplomatic Victory.
6.  **The Memory Leak:** Clocks are jumping forward and you're in unexplained locations. You must piece together your 'Lost Time' to find the Karma tree's source.
7.  **The Final Coronation at the Center:** Face the rival leaders face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you build the stars or reclaim the mud?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of Dharma
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the fossilized remains of the original Alpha god.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Strike of Duty' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mandala Signet
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, silver ring with the mandala seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the spiritual world. Once per day, the user can 'Harmonize' with an ally, granting them advantage on their next check.

### 3. The AUM-Energy Capsule
**Type:** Artifact (Focus)
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of the first Islander (equivalent).
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Karma' magic.
