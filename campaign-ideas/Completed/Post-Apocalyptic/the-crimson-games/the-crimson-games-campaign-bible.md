# The Crimson Games: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a nation; it's a televised execution. In the heart of the opulent 'Capital Vermillion,' the 'Sovereigns' (The Elite) believe that the only way to maintain the 'Grand Accord' of peace is through the annual ritual of 'The Crimson Games.' Long ago, the 'Founders of the Capital' realized that hope is more dangerous than fire, and that the only way to control the 'Districts' is to give them a spectacle of their own destruction. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Fame-Energy'—a shimmering force that turns the crowd's applause into literal resources for the 'Tributes.' To be a Tribute is to be a ghost in a world of luxury, protecting your 'Humanity' while fighting in an 'Arena' designed to turn you into a killer.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon Uprising (c. -75 years):** The Districts rebel against the Capital's excess. The rebellion is crushed, and the 'Grand Accord' is signed.
2.  **The First Crimson Game (c. -74 years):** The annual tradition begins as a reminder of the Districts' failure.
3.  **The Reaping (Every Year - Day 1):** Two children from each of the twelve Districts are selected as Tributes through a lens of 'Dread and Helplessness.'
4.  **The Tribute Parade (Week 1):** The Tributes are presented to the Capital in elaborate, objectifying costumes during a 'Spectacle of Dehumanization.'
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A new tribute (the player) is reaped from District 12, a coal-mining district of 'Muted Grays.'
6.  **The Training Center Scandal (Ongoing):** Seneca Nightweave, the Head Gamemaker, begins using 'Reality-Editing' magic to manipulate the arena's lethality.
7.  **The Bloodbath (Day 8 - 10 AM):** The Games begin. A chaotic, violent clash at the central Cornucopia leaves half the Tributes dead.
8.  **The Act of Defiance (Upcoming):** The remaining Tributes use a 'Forbidden Berry' to force a stalemate, triggering a 'Gamemaker Panic.'
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of imperial crimson and district slate gray. The 'Fame-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic, and the 'Mockingjay' signal is starting to rise.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Performance' and 'Authenticity.' It is about the 'Human Insignificance' of being a tribute—the realization that your life is a toy for the elite. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can you remain human in an arena designed to turn you into a beast?' It explores the decadent baroque of the Capital, the visceral horror of the muttation, and the weary, damaged voice of a survivor who has already seen the end of the world in the arena. The players are the tributes, mentors, and rebels who must navigate the subtext of a sponsor interview and the intensity of a forest fire to find a resolution that honors the memory of the fallen.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Capital Elite (The Sovereigns)
**Type:** Decadent/Totalitarian Urban Superpower
**Leader:** President Bloodworth (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Soaring crystal spires, 'Fame-Energy' manipulation, and total control of the media.
**Rivals:** The District Rebels, The Gamemakers (partially).

### The District Rebels (The Mockingjays)
**Type:** Insurgent/Folk-Based Resistance Network
**Leader:** President Coin (A woman of military precision).
**Asset:** Knowledge of the industrial underbelly, folk songs of resistance, and a growing sense of 'Unity.'
**Rivals:** The Capital Elite.

### The Gamemakers (The Architects)
**Type:** Scientific/Theatrical Research Group
**Leader:** Seneca Nightweave (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Control of the Arena architecture, reality-warping 'Muttations,' and the 'Gamemaker Interface.'
**Rivals:** Both sides (depending on the 'Show').

### The Mentors (The Survivors)
**Type:** Neutral/Broken Professional Group
**Leader:** Brutus Stone (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of previous Games, access to sponsors, and a cynical detachment from the world.
**Rivals:** None (they are the guides).

### The 'Career' Tributes (The Brainwashed)
**Type:** Elite/Martial Arts Combatant Group
**Leader:** Kaelith Swiftarrow (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Lifelong training, superior gear, and a belief in the 'Glory' of the Games.
**Rivals:** The District Tributes.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **President Carmina Bloodworth** (Elite - The Leader) - A woman in extreme haute couture with a deceptively gentle voice. **Voice:** Smooth, refined, and measured. **Goal:** To maintain the Capital's order through the 'spectacle' of the Games. **Secret:** She is a high-level Adept of 'Fame-Energy' who can 'delete' entire families from existence through a single command.
2.  **Maximus Aurelius** (Capital - The Host) - A man in a jeweled suit with a theatrical flair. **Voice:** Rich, theatrical, and building to crescendos. **Goal:** To make every Tribute a 'Star' and keep the ratings high. **Secret:** He secretly funds the District Rebels to keep the 'Conflict' interesting.
3.  **Seneca Nightweave** (Gamemaker - The Expert) - A man in a perfectly tailored business suit with a technical vocabulary. **Voice:** Precise, intellectual, and slightly nasal. **Goal:** To turn the Arena into a masterpiece of 'Technological Horror.' **Secret:** He is already part-Muttation, having experimented on his own DNA to survive the Capital's internal politics.
4.  **Brutus Stone** (Mentor - The Survivor) - A man in ruined clothes who smells of stale whiskey. **Voice:** Gravelly, damaged, and cynical. **Goal:** To get his Tributes home alive, or at least keep them sober. **Secret:** He has won the Games three times, but his soul is still trapped in the arena.
5.  **Kaelith Swiftarrow** (Career - The Warrior) - A woman in polished armor with military precision. **Voice:** Clear, confident, and commanding. **Goal:** To win the Games and bring 'Glory' back to District 1. **Secret:** She was the first Tribute to 'hear' the original Song of the Districts through the Capital's propaganda.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Capital Vermillion (The Hub)
A decadent baroque monument to cyberpunk excess and crystal spires.
*   **The Presidential Balcony:** A site of 'Refined Formality' and harsh artificial lighting. **Smell:** Expensive perfume, bleach, and gold leaf. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Training Center:** A site of 'Magical Enhancements' and objectifying costumes. **Visual:** Living fabrics reflecting in the chrome walls.

### The Arena (The Battlefield)
A hyper-real forest monument to weaponized nature and ancient trees.
*   **The Cornucopia:** A site of 'Initial Bloodbath' and sudden violence. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the blood of the first tribute. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the death cannon.
*   **The Force-Field Edge:** Where reality is shimmering and birds sound mechanical.

### District 12 (The Slums)
An industrial revolution monument to coal dust and muted grays.
*   **The Reaping Square:** A site of 'Dread and Helplessness' and harsh midday sunlight. **Smell:** Coal, stale sweat, and hope. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of a distant bell.
*   **The Hob Market:** A chaotic maze of laundry lines and secret deals.

### The Tribute Center (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Private Suite:** Where the 'Final Strategy' is discussed. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Reaping:** Experience the choice. You must survive your first 'Psychological Break' and find a way to maintain your 'Humanity' level during the parade.
2.  **The Training Heist:** Infiltrate the Gamemaker's Interface to retrieve a shipment of stolen medicines. You must use your 'Fame-Energy' to win sponsor gifts.
3.  **The Bloodbath:** The Games begin. You must escape the central Cornucopia while your own reality is skipping frames.
4.  **The Tracker Jacker Trap:** Someone has triggered a swarm of mechanical wasps. You must find a 'Biological Barrier' before the venom takes effect.
5.  **The Feast Gambit:** The Head Gamemaker has announced a 'Feast' at the Cornucopia. You must decide whether to risk the trap or die of thirst.
6.  **The Act of Defiance:** The final three Tributes are ordered to kill each other. You must lead a 'Berry-Standoff' to force a change in the rules.
7.  **The Final Coronation:** Face President Bloodworth face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the crown or save the soul?

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Mockingjay Pin:**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Jewelry)
**Description:** A simple, gold pin of a bird in flight with an arrow.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer +5 to all Charisma (Persuasion and Performance) checks and allows them to 'Command' any non-royal District citizen.

2.  **Seneca's Interface:**
**Type:** Artifact (Focus)
**Description:** A small, high-tech radio that hums with the key of the Gamemakers.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'See' any communication within 1 mile. Once per day, the user can 'Lock' a target's reality for 1 hour.

3.  **The Bread of District 11:**
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A simple, warm loaf of bread that pulses with the light of the sun.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Heal' an ally's spirit by 50 HP. Once per day, the user can create a 30ft 'Sanctuary Field.'
