# The Devil's Herd: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Earth is not a silent rock; it's a living graveyard of gods that's finally waking up. In the sun-baked, red-dust expanse of the Australian 'Outback,' the 'Grand Accord' of nature was shattered by the return of an ancient earth-spirit: 'The Rainbow Serpent' (The Seed of Nature). Long ago, the 'Founders of the Land' realized that the fauna of Australia was meant to be the planet's ultimate defense system. They granted the 'Terrifying Intelligence' to the emus and kangaroos, creating 'The Devil's Herd.' But the 'Great Emu War' of 1932 was just a skirmish. Now, the full mobilization has begun. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Dreamtime-Energy'—a shimmering force found in the red dust, making every animal heartbeat a tactical coordinate. To be a survivor is to be an intruder in an ecosystem that has perfected the art of 'Total War.'

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon surge (c. 1932):** The Great Emu War occurs. The first emus show signs of coordinated, tactical movement.
2.  **The Rainbow Awakening (2024):** A major seismic event in the Outback releases the Dreamtime-Energy. Australia is immediately quarantined.
3.  **The Mobilization (2025):** The emus and kangaroos consolidate into mobs, establishing 'Territorial Perimeters' around human cities.
4.  **The Road-Warrior Initiative (Ongoing):** Survivors modify their vehicles into 'Outback War Machines' to cross the hostile desert.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A lone survivor (the player) is 'Reaped' into the Outback after their plane crashes in the Red Center.
6.  **The Emu Ambush (Week 1):** You survive your first 'Tactical Strike' by a mob of emus using only your survival knife and a flare gun.
7.  **The Rainbow Signal (Ongoing):** A voice on a salvaged radio (Tier 1 NPC) warns of a massive 'Herd Migration' toward the last safe haven.
8.  **The Kangaroo Siege (Upcoming):** The survivors must defend their convoy from a mob of 'Killer Kangaroos' in a narrow canyon.
9.  **The Present Day:** The Outback is a high-contrast nightmare of red dust and bleached blue sky. The herd is moving, and the 'Dreamtime-Energy' is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Human Resilience' and 'Nature's Retribution.' It is about the 'Visceral Survival' of being an intruder in your own home—the realization that the land you stand on wants you dead. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can humanity find a way to apologize to a planet that has finally lost its patience?' It explores the gritty realism of the weathered metal, the visceral horror of the intelligent predator, and the grizzled, nervous voice of a man who has seen too many neighbors kick-boxed into oblivion. The players are the road warriors and survivalists who must navigate the mob tactics and outback navigation to find a resolution that honors the memory of the land.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Outback Warriors (The Survivors)
**Type:** Elite Frontier Defense/Convoy Units
**Leader:** Bluey (A grizzled rural Australian - Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Outback war machines, knowledge of the Red Center, and the 'Resilience' intuition.
**Rivals:** The Devil's Herd, The Scavenger Clans.

### The Devil's Herd (The Mob)
**Type:** Intelligent/Tactical Fauna Collective
**Leader:** The First Emu (A being of ancient feathers and segmented stone).
**Asset:** Numbers, mob tactics (flanking, decoys), and absolute control of the desert perimeters.
**Rivals:** Humanity.

### The Scavenger Clans (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Opportunistic/Nihilistic Human Raiders
**Leader:** Warlord Rust (A man of patched leather and cold calculation).
**Asset:** Improvised weapons, knowledge of the secret oases, and a total disregard for 'Land Honor.'
**Rivals:** The Outback Warriors.

### The 'Dreamtime' Seekers (The Healers)
**Type:** Scientific/Environmental Preservation Group
**Leader:** Dr. Green (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient Dreamtime-Energy, 'Nature-Seed' medicine, and a connection to the world's original purpose.
**Rivals:** None (they are the lifeline).

### The 'Ghost' Drovers
**Type:** Independent/Lost Human Survivors
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Knowledge of the deepest dunes and a willingness to perform 'Impossible Tasks' for a price.
**Rivals:** None.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Bluey** (Survivor - The Lead) - A man in a dusty akubra with a weathered face. **Voice:** Grizzled, older male, dry and gravelly with a thick rural Australian accent. **Goal:** To get his mob to the 'Green Place' safe haven and outrun his own ghosts. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Dreamtime-Energy' who can 'feel' an emu's approach through the vibrations of the red dust.
**The First Emu (Echo)** (Mob - The Mastermind) - A massive, intelligent bird with glowing eyes. **Voice:** None (subsonic screeching and clicks). **Goal:** To reclaim the Outback for the 'Nature-Children' and witness the final reset. **Secret:** He is already part-Apocalypse, having consumed the 'First Ember' of the 1932 skyrise.
**Dr. Green (Ghost)** (Independent - Clinical Healer) - A woman with a compassionate bedside manner and high-tech (scavenged) goggles. **Voice:** Clinical but warm, providing a 'Safe Moment' atmosphere. **Goal:** To find a way to 'Pacify' the herd and save the people. **Secret:** She is secretly working for a shadow-syndicate that wants to harvest the 'Tactical-Energy.'
**The Drover (Rust)** (Independent - Philosophical Survivor) - A man whose house is a map of countless traps. **Voice:** Gruff, paranoid, and cynical. **Goal:** To stay alive and outrun his own memories. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vulnerability' in the emu's tactical formation that he's too professional to share.
**The Serpent (Anomaly)** (Shadow - The Mastermind) - The manifest consciousness of the Rainbow Serpent. **Voice:** A whispering, distorted chorus of a thousand drips. **Goal:** To reset the universe and return it to a state of absolute silence. **Secret:** It is actually an Architect construct designed to compost the universe.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Red Center (The Hub)
A high-contrast landscape of red dust, rock formations, and bleached blue sky.
*   **The Hub:** A site of 'Grand Strategy' and era-appropriate lighting. **Smell:** Diesel, stale whiskey, and old wood. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of engines and thundering hooves.
*   **The Memory Walls:** Where the 'Sacred Laws' are etched into the stone.

### The Emu Perimeter (The Battlefield)
A dark, humid labyrinth of giant trees and dangerous shadows.
*   **The Ambus Point:** A site of 'Sudden Violence' and tactical emus. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the blood of the first word. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of mutated creatures.
*   **The Silent Village:** A place where the 'Invisible Apocalypse' has already passed.

### The Outback Road (The Gauntlet)
A high-speed arena of high-octane survival and vehicle modification.
*   **The Dust Storm:** A site of 'Seismic Tension' and falling hazards. **Visual:** Blue-white light reflecting off the rusted iron. **Sound:** The thunderous roar of the wind.

### The Green Place (Mental Space)
A clinical white-blue space of absolute knowledge and desaturated reality.
*   **The Safe Haven:** Where the 'Final Story' was first spoken. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Plane Crash:** Wake up in the Red Center. You must escape an 'Emu Ambush' using only your survival knife and your 'Spiritual intuition.'
2.  **The Convoy Run:** Navigate the Outback roads to find Bluey. You must identify the 'Fuel-Energy' node before the herd arrives.
3.  **The Scavenger Infiltration:** Infiltrate the Scavenger HQ to find evidence of the 'Reverse-Rebirth' plan. You must bypass Warlord Rust's guards.
4.  **The Heart-Beat Chase:** Lead a fleet of salvaged vehicles through a city-destruction surge. You must cast 'Dreamtime-Spells' while traveling at 100 mph.
5.  **The Brain-Chamber Revelation:** Enter the Red Center Lab undercover. You must find your own 'Soul-Memory' without alerting the Herd.
6.  **The Final Reset:** The safe haven't central crystal has begun its final 'Reset.' You must defend the Temple while the self-destruct activates.
7.  **The Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the First Emu face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you wear the skin or save the soul?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of the Land
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A massive sword crafted from the iron shard of the original Alpha Emu.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Resilient Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Bluey's Radio
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A professional-grade radio that captures more than just sound.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Hear' a mob's surface thoughts when the radio is played back (DC 18 Wisdom save for the mob).

### 3. The Dreamtime-Energy Capsule
**Type:** Artifact (Focus)
**Description:** A small, sapphire crystal that hums with the key of your own identity.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' a 30ft area of reality, making them immune to the effects of 'Dreamtime' magic.
