# As You Wish: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is not a planet; it's a Storybook that's being read by a Grandfather to a Sick Child. Long ago, the 'First Storyteller' realized that 'True Love' is the most powerful magic in the universe, capable of overcoming even 'Mostly Dead' states. They forged the 'Cliffs of Insanity' and the 'Fire Swamp' as tests for those who would claim the title of 'Hero.' The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Romance-Energy'—a shimmering force that turns saturated colors and theatrical flourishes into reality-warping power. To say 'As You Wish' isn't just to obey; it's a sacred vow that binds two souls across the 'Dread Pirate' seas and the 'Pit of Despair.' But the 'Prince' (Humperdinck) has discovered a different melody—the 'Song of Efficiency,' a cold calculation that views people as variables in a political game.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Chapter (c. -1,000 years):** The First Storyteller begins the narration of Florin and Guilder.
2.  **The Rise of the Dread Pirate (Ongoing):** A series of legendary outlaws claim the name 'Roberts,' each passing it to a worthy successor.
3.  **The Buttercup Vow (5 Years Ago):** A farm boy named Westley says 'As You Wish' to a beautiful girl, establishing the 'True Love' anchor.
4.  **The Dread Pirate Encounter (4 Years Ago):** Westley is believed to have been killed by the Dread Pirate Roberts. Buttercup is 'forced' to marry Prince Humperdinck.
5.  **The Kidnapping (Present Day - Day 1):** Three outlaws—Inigo, Fezzik, and Vizzini—kidnap Buttercup to start a war between Florin and Guilder.
6.  **The Cliffs of Insanity Ascent (Day 2):** A mysterious Man in Black (the player) defeats the three outlaws through 'Logical Deduction' and 'Cerebral Grandeur.'
7.  **The Fire Swamp Survival (Ongoing):** Westley and Buttercup navigate the R.O.U.S.es and the lightning sand using their 'Romantic Idealism.'
8.  **The Pit of Despair (Upcoming):** Westley is tortured by the Prince's 'Machine.' He becomes 'Mostly Dead.'
9.  **The Final Chapter (Upcoming):** The heroes gather at Florin Castle for a final, theatrical standoff against the Prince and Count Rugen.
10. **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of royal blues and ominous oranges. The Grandfather is turning the page, and the 'True Love' pulse is reaching its final harmonic.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'True Love' and 'Political Pragmatism.' It is about the 'Romantic Swashbuckler' of the human heart—the realization that 'Life is Pain, Highness. Anyone who says differently is selling something.' The 'Why' is the question: 'Can a story told with enough love and wit overcome even the most impossible geography and political corruption?' It explores the theatrical composition of the fairy tale, the tragedy of the 'Mostly Dead' hero, and the playful meta-commentary of a wise storyteller. The players are the heroes and outlaws who must navigate the Fire Swamp and the Cliffs of Insanity to find a resolution that honors the Storybook Code.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The 'True Love' Seekers (The Heroes)
**Type:** Mythic/Romantic Task Force
**Leader:** Westley (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** The Man in Black's skills, Inigo's rapier, Fezzik's size, and the 'As You Wish' vow.
**Rivals:** Prince Humperdinck, The Brute Squad.

### The Florin Royal Court (The Pragmatists)
**Type:** High-Society/Bureaucratic Superpower
**Leader:** Prince Humperdinck (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Infinite resources, the 'Machine' of the Pit of Despair, and the legal authority of the State.
**Rivals:** The Kingdom of Guilder, The Heroes.

### The Outlaw Trio (The Specialists)
**Type:** Professional/Theatrical Mercenary Cell
**Leader:** Vizzini (The 'Brains' - initially).
**Asset:** Inigo's 'Inevitable Strike,' Fezzik's 'Unstoppable Fury,' and Vizzini's 'Cerebral Grandeur.'
**Rivals:** The Man in Black (initially).

### The Miracle Workers (The Neutral)
**Type:** Scientific/Whimsical Healers
**Leader:** Miracle Max (A grumpy old man).
**Asset:** Knowledge of 'Mostly Dead' biology, Chocolate-coated pills, and a connection to the 'True Love' frequency.
**Rivals:** Count Rugen (who they view as 'unprofessional').

### The 'R.O.U.S.' Swarm (The Anomaly)
**Type:** Independent/Monstrous Force
**Leader:** None.
**Asset:** Numbers, knowledge of the Fire Swamp, and a total disregard for the Storybook.
**Rivals:** Anyone who enters the swamp.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Westley (Man in Black)** (Hero - The Lead) - A man in a black mask and flowing black clothes. **Voice:** Confident, witty, and deeply romantic. **Goal:** To rescue Buttercup and defeat the Prince. **Secret:** He is a high-level Adept of 'Romance-Energy' who can 'survive' death as long as the story requires it.
**Princess Buttercup** (Hero - The Catalyst) - A woman of storybook beauty and increasing practical confidence. **Voice:** Sincere, passionate, and regal. **Goal:** To be with Westley and find her own strength. **Secret:** She was the one who 'narrated' the location of the Man in Black's first appearance.
**Inigo Montoya** (Outlaw - The Duelist) - A man with flowing dark hair and a rapier at his side. **Voice:** Spanish-accented, theatrical, and honorable. **Goal:** To find the six-fingered man and avenge his father. **Secret:** He has mastered the 'Inevitable Strike'—a move that cannot be blocked if the user's motive is pure.
**Fezzik** (Outlaw - The Gentle Giant) - A man of impossible size and a gentle soul. **Voice:** Soft, rhythmic, and woodwind-toned. **Goal:** To support his friends and find a place where he belongs. **Secret:** He is actually a high-level Adept of 'Unstoppable Fury' who can 'move' buildings if he's protecting an ally.
**Prince Humperdinck** (Order - The Villain) - A man in hunting greens with a perfectly coiffed beard. **Voice:** Measured, authoritative, and cold. **Goal:** To rule Florin through efficiency and the elimination of all variable threats. **Secret:** He is terrified of direct physical combat and views the world as a game he must rig to win.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Cliffs of Insanity
An impossible geography of steep stone and blue-white mist.
*   **The Rope:** A site of 'Dynamic Dueling' and cerebral grandeur. **Visual:** Deep perspectives and the Man in Black's black silhouette. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of the sea below.
*   **The Plateau:** Where the 'Battle of Wits' was fought. **Visual:** Two cups and a bottle of wine in a desaturated landscape.

### The Fire Swamp
An ominous orange and murky brown monument to survival and R.O.U.S.es.
*   **The Lightning Sand:** A place where 'Romantic Idealism' is tested through action. **Smell:** Sulfur, charcoal smoke, and wet earth. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'pop-pop' of fire bursts.
*   **The R.O.U.S. Nest:** A dark, humid labyrinth of broken stone and oversized rats.

### Florin Castle
A royal gold and white marble monument to opulent arrogance and gold trim.
*   **The Pit of Despair:** A stone gray and torch-lit amber chamber beneath the castle. **Visual:** The Machine and the 'Mostly Dead' Westley. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'hum-thump' of the bellows.
*   **The Chapel:** A site of 'Intimate Vows' and theatrical standoffs.

### The Miracle Hut (Mental Space)
A site of 'Whimsical Healing' and desaturated pastels.
*   **The Kitchen:** Where the 'Chocolate-Coated Pill' was forged. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the soul.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Kidnapping:** Buttercup is taken. You must arrive at the scene and meet the Outlaw Trio while maintaining your 'Storybook Hero' disguise.
2.  **The Battle of Wits:** Defeat Vizzini in a high-stakes 'Logical Deduction' challenge. You must choose the right cup without confirming your knowledge of the poison.
3.  **The Inigo Duel:** Face Inigo Montoya at the top of the Cliffs of Insanity. You must match his 'Theatrical Flair' to earn his respect.
4.  **The Fire Swamp Survival:** Navigate the swamp with Buttercup. You must rescue her from the lightning sand and defeat a swarm of R.O.U.S.es.
5.  **The Pit Rescue:** Infiltrate the Pit of Despair to find the 'Mostly Dead' Westley. You must bypass the Albino and his muffled whispers.
6.  **The Miracle Formula:** Convince Miracle Max to create a life-restoring pill. You must use your 'Romantic Idealism' to prove the motive is True Love.
7.  **The Final Standpoint:** A three-way confrontation at Florin Castle between the Heroes, the Prince, and the Count. The truth is revealed through 'Romantic Swashbuckling' and mutual respect as the world is saved.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. Inigo's Rapier
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A simple, elegant rapier made by the master smith Domingo Montoya.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Inevitable Strike' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Dread Pirate's Mask
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Helmet)
**Description:** A simple, black mask that seems to grant the wearer absolute confidence.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer advantage on all Charisma (Deception and Intimidation) checks and makes them immune to being recognized by anyone who doesn't know their story.

### 3. The Miracle Pill
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A large, chocolate-coated pill that hums with the key of the first Storyteller.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Heal' a Mostly Dead creature to full HP. Once per day, the user can 'Silence' a villain's speech for 1 round.

## [TAG: ENEMY_STATBLOCK]
### 1. Brute Squad Guard (Standard)
(CR 2). Humanoid. HP 30, AC 13.
**Abilities:**
*   **Ordered Discipline:** The guard has advantage on attacks if they have an ally within 5 feet.
*   **Club Strike:** Melee attack. 1d8+3 damage.

### 2. R.O.U.S. (Swarm)
(CR 5). Monstrosity. HP 80, AC 14.
**Abilities:**
*   **Swamp Blur:** The swarm has disadvantage on being hit if they are in the Fire Swamp.
*   **Vicious Bite:** Melee attack. 2d10 damage + Target is grappled.

### 3. 'The Strategist' Humperdinck (Proxy)
(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Efficiency Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the Prince's 'Rule of Law.'
*   **The Final Move:** Once per combat, Humperdinck can force an enemy to take an action that serves the stability of Florin.
