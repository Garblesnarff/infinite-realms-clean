# Journey to the Inner World — Style Bake-Off (6 images)

**Purpose:** pick one art style before any real generation. Three subjects, each made in both styles. From `ART-DIRECTION.md` §1.3.

## How to run it

1. **Use one generator for all 6 images** (ImageFX / Imagen). Do not switch tools between styles.
2. **Keep the subject text identical.** Only the style block changes between A and B.
3. **Set the aspect ratio for each image:** 1:1 for images 1 and 2, 4:3 for image 3.
4. **Make 2–4 variations of each** and keep the best one of each.
5. **Judge at real app sizes.** Shrink each keeper to **96px**, and images 1 and 2 also to a **32px circle**. Put the A and B versions side by side.
6. **Scoring:** a style wins a subject if it reads better at small size and fits the mood. **The style that wins 2 of the 3 subjects is locked.** Do not mix styles after the lock.

**What to look for:**

| Subject | Question |
|---|---|
| 1. Captain Eva Rostova | Can you read her face and mood at 96px? Is the silhouette clear at 32px? |
| 2. Grafted Horror | Is it unsettling without being gory? Can you tell it is a creature at 32px? |
| 3. The River of Light | Does the glow feel like the only real light? Is the focal point centred? |

## Style A — Graphic novel "Ink & Glow"

### A1 — Captain Eva Rostova (1:1)

```
Graphic novel illustration. Bold black ink linework with variable line weight. Cross-hatched shadows in the manner of 19th-century engraving. Flat colour fills with 2–3 value steps, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Deep dark base palette. The only saturated colours are glowing light sources, which rim-light the subject. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.

Head-and-shoulders portrait of Captain Eva Rostova, a pragmatic, cynical human expedition leader in her forties. Weathered face, a thin scar through one eyebrow, hard appraising eyes, mouth set in a skeptical half-frown. Short practical hair under a battered leather cap with brass goggles pushed up on it. Canvas-and-leather expedition coat with brass buckles and a coil of rope over one shoulder. Behind her, the soot-grey rock wall of a vast chasm, lit from below by a warm amber lantern that rim-lights her jaw and cheek. Palette: amber, brass, soot grey on an ink-black base.

Square composition. Face in the upper middle of the frame, strong readable silhouette, plain dark background behind the head.

No text, no letters, no captions, no speech bubbles, no panel borders, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no frame.
```

### A2 — Grafted Horror (1:1)

```
Graphic novel illustration. Bold black ink linework with variable line weight. Cross-hatched shadows in the manner of 19th-century engraving. Flat colour fills with 2–3 value steps, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Deep dark base palette. The only saturated colours are glowing light sources, which rim-light the subject. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.

The Grafted Horror, a creature stitched together by bio-sorcerers from mismatched animals: a hulking wolf's head and forequarters, one enormous crab claw in place of a front leg, and a segmented scorpion tail arching over its back. Visible surgical stitches and pale bone plates join the parts. It crouches low, jaws open, tail raised to strike. Unsettling, not gory: no blood, no open wounds. Faint pink bioluminescent glow seeps from the stitch lines and rim-lights the silhouette. Background: the dark, oily shore of an underground sea. Palette: flesh pink and bone on an ink-black and deep teal base.

Square composition. The creature fills the centre 70% of the frame, one clear readable silhouette, head and claw toward the centre.

No text, no letters, no captions, no speech bubbles, no panel borders, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no frame.
```

### A3 — The River of Light (4:3)

```
Graphic novel illustration. Bold black ink linework with variable line weight. Cross-hatched shadows in the manner of 19th-century engraving. Flat colour fills with 2–3 value steps, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Deep dark base palette. The only saturated colours are glowing light sources, which rim-light the subject. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.

The River of Light: a river made not of water but of glowing bioluminescent algae, winding through a giant underground jungle toward the centre of the image. Towering fungus-trees and fern fronds line its banks, lit only by the river's cyan glow and scattered magenta and gold glows from hanging fruit and mushrooms. A tiny expedition party with a single lantern stands on a rock in the foreground for scale. Far above, the cavern roof is lost in darkness. Palette: teal and moss green base, electric cyan river, magenta and sulphur-gold accents.

Wide 4:3 landscape. Focal point: the bend of the glowing river at the centre of the frame. Strong leading lines from the river toward the centre.

No text, no letters, no captions, no speech bubbles, no panel borders, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no frame.
```
