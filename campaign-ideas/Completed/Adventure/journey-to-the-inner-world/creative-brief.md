# Creative Brief: The world is an egg — and it is hatching.

This brief sets the look, sound and feel of every asset for this campaign. It follows the campaign bible. Where they disagree, the bible wins.

## The Campaign in One Paragraph

The world is a colossal egg laid by the Great Wyrm. The surface — the Shell — is ordered, predictable and slowly dying. Beneath it lies the Yolk: a glowing, ever-changing world of primordial life. At its centre burns the Core, the embryo of a new Great Wyrm, and it is about to hatch. The party joins the Delvers' Guild expedition of Baron Alistair Finch and descends through the Great Chasm of Al-Ghor, the Labyrinth of Echoes, the Bioluminescent Jungle and the Sea of Chaos to the Core's Corona. There they decide the fate of both worlds.

## Visual Identity

### Style

- **Stylized illustration, one locked style for all assets: graphic novel "Ink & Glow"** (won the bake-off 2 of 3, locked 2026-09-24). Paste this style block into every prompt, unchanged:
  > Graphic novel illustration. Bold black ink linework with variable line weight. Cross-hatched shadows in the manner of 19th-century engraving. Flat colour fills with 2–3 value steps, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Deep dark base palette. The only saturated colours are glowing light sources, which rim-light the subject. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.
- **Heritage:** 19th-century engraved expedition illustration, modernised. Brass instruments, rope, lanterns and maps against a living, glowing underworld.
- **Readability first.** Most images are shown small: 96px thumbnails and 32px combat circles. Every subject needs a strong silhouette and one clear focal point.

### Palette

- **Base (the dark):** ink-black, midnight blue, deep teal, moss green, bruised purple.
- **Glow (light sources only):** electric cyan, acid green, sulphur yellow, hot magenta.
- **Surface warmth:** brass, copper, lantern amber, coal-smoke grey. Only for surface people and their gear, mostly the Delvers' Guild.

### Colour Script — "the deeper you go, the brighter it gets"

The Core is a sun, so the descent reverses normal cave logic. Each zone has one colour key.

| Zone | Colour key | Light source |
|---|---|---|
| The Great Chasm of Al-Ghor | amber, brass, soot grey | grey daylight from far above, lanterns, furnace glow |
| The Labyrinth of Echoes | cold grey-violet | faint crystal light; sound shown as visible ripple lines |
| The Bioluminescent Jungle | teal and green | cyan, magenta and gold glows from plants, fungi and Sun-Fruit |
| The Sea of Chaos | oily black, iridescent, flesh pink | the glowing primordial sea and the Great Maelstrom |
| The Core's Corona | white-gold, overexposed | the Core itself; black glass throws hard reflections |

### Recurring Motifs

- **Egg and hatching:** cracks, shell fragments, seals.
- **Crystal growth and resonance.**
- **Visible sound** in the Labyrinth of Echoes and on the Echo-Thieves.
- **Patchwork biology with visible stitches** on the Flesh-Weavers and their creations.
- **Everything that glows is alive, or was alive.**

### Characters and Creatures

- **Surface people** — the Delvers' Guild and the Surface Sentinels — wear practical expedition gear: canvas, leather, brass goggles, rope and lanterns. They use the warm surface palette.
- **Inner-World people** look changed by the Yolk: crystal growths on the Geode-Gnostics, grafts on the Flesh-Weavers, mutations on the Yolk-Embracers, glowing fungus on the Sporefolk.
- **Creatures** — Yolk-Spawn, Crystal-Spinner, Echo-Bat, Warden-Scarab, Fungal-Zombie, Grafted Horror, Blind-Worm Digger, Chaos-Mutant, Living Crystal, Core-Spawn — are strange and unsettling, not gory. They are grown, not built, except the Shell-Warden constructs.
- **Shell-Warden constructs** (Warden-Unit 9, the Warden-Scarabs) share one material: grey stone with seams of gold light.
- **Realistic adult proportions and expressive faces.**

### Environments

- **Scale:** tiny figures against vast caverns, chasms and seas.
- **Leading lines:** rope-bridges, glowing rivers and crystal veins pull the eye towards the focal point.
- **Depth:** the Shell is dark and warm-lit; the Yolk glows from within.

### Do Not

- No text, letters, captions, speech bubbles, panel borders, logos, watermarks, signatures or frames in any image.
- Do not name real studios, artists, films or games in prompts. Describe traits only.
- No prehistoric beasts, reptile-people or crystal suns. They belong to an abandoned draft.
- No third-party creatures or characters.

## Voice

- **Narrator:** a warm, measured baritone with a quiet thrill of discovery, like an explorer reading from a field journal by lantern light.
- **Pacing:** unhurried in wonder, quick and clipped in danger.
- **Sound bed:** deep cavern resonance on the Shell. A soft living hum in the Yolk. A single rising tone near the Core — the hatching song.
