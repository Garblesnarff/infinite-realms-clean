# Journey to the Inner World — World-Building Specification

## 1. Core Concept & Narrative Hook

The world is a World-Egg laid by the Great Wyrm, a colossal cosmic being that coiled around it and turned to stone. The "Shell" is the surface — rigid laws, ordered life, fading magic — while the "Yolk" is the still-liquid, primordial core beneath it, a chaotic soup of constant, violent evolution. At the Yolk's heart pulses the "Core," a nascent stellar consciousness and embryo of a new Great Wyrm; the planet is slowly hatching, and the earthquakes wracking the surface are the first cracks in the Shell. When a quake tears open the ancient Progenitor tunnel known as the Great Chasm of Al-Ghor, the Delvers' Guild launches an expedition into the Yolk to claim its riches, and the players are drawn along as hired hands who become the unwitting catalysts — or midwives — of the Hatching.

## 2. Lore & History Primer

* **The Laying (Age of Myth):** The Great Wyrm lays the World-Egg and becomes the cosmos; the Shell forms and cools.
* **The First Cracks (c. -1,000,000 yrs):** The Core ignites; simple life appears on the Shell.
* **The Age of Giants (c. -500,000 yrs):** Surface life grows colossal, feeding on magic leaking from the Yolk.
* **The Gravity Shift (c. -100,000 yrs):** A thousand years of erratic gravity wipes out the Giants and forces life to grow small and adaptable.
* **The First Descent (c. -10,000 yrs):** The advanced Progenitors find the passage into the Yolk and build cities there.
* **The Progenitor War (c. -9,000 yrs):** Progenitors split into the Shell-Wardens, who want to preserve the Shell, and the Yolk-Embracers, who want to merge with the Yolk's chaos.
* **The Great Seal (c. -8,500 yrs):** The Shell-Wardens win, sealing the passages and erasing all knowledge of the Yolk — the "Great Amnesia."
* **The River of Time Runs Backwards (c. -5,000 yrs):** A surface river flows backwards for a generation, de-aging anything that falls in — a side effect of the Seals warping reality.
* **The Rediscovery (c. -100 yrs):** A massive earthquake reveals the Progenitor tunnel, making the Journey to the Inner World possible again.
* **The Day the Mountains Sang (c. -1 yr):** Every mountain hums one resonant frequency — the Core's hatching song. The Seals weaken; the birth is imminent.

**The Why:** Order against Chaos, Stability against Creation, fought at planetary scale. The Shell means stasis, safety, and slow decay; the Yolk means life, danger, and endless change. The players are not mere explorers but antibodies, catalysts, or midwives to a cosmic birth — their choices decide whether the Shell shatters and the surface dies, the Yolk is sealed and the new Wyrm is doomed unborn, or a symbiotic future is forged between both worlds.

## 3. Faction Deep-Dive

* **The Delvers' Guild** — Corporatist explorers. Leader: Baron Alistair Finch. Goal: mine the Yolk for profit. Asset: drills, funded expeditions, the official route down. Rivals: Children of the Core, Surface Sentinels.
* **The Surface Sentinels** — Luddite preservationists. Leader: Elder Maeve. Goal: destroy the passages, reinforce the Shell. Asset: Progenitor sealing lore, fanatical self-sacrifice. Rivals: Delvers' Guild, Geode-Gnostics.
* **The Geode-Gnostics** — Crystal mystics. Leader: The Luminous One. Goal: crystal-transcendence by merging with the Core. Asset: attunement to Yolk energy; shapes living crystal. Rivals: Flesh-Weavers, Surface Sentinels.
* **The Flesh-Weavers of the Yolk** — Bio-punk evolutionaries. Leader: Progenitor Prime. Goal: guide all life to a "perfect" final form. Asset: mastery of biological grafting. Rivals: Geode-Gnostics, Remnant of the Shell-Wardens.
* **The Children of the Core** — Sun-worshippers. Leader: Sun-Speaker Kor. Goal: let the Birth happen naturally. Asset: instinctual bond with the Yolk's ecosystem. Rivals: Delvers' Guild, Flesh-Weavers.
* **The Remnant of the Shell-Wardens** — Undead Progenitor AI. Leader: Warden-Unit 9. Goal: seal the way, permit no passage. Asset: Progenitor dungeons and automatons. Rivals: everyone, especially the Yolk-Embracers.
* **The Descendants of the Yolk-Embracers** — Mutated Progenitor cult. Leader: The First-Mutant. Goal: tear down the Great Seal, spread chaotic mutation. Asset: unstable, unique mutations per member. Rivals: Remnant of the Shell-Wardens.
* **The Echo-Thieves** — Sonic scavengers. Leader: The Silent. Goal: record the "sound of creation" at the Wyrm's birth. Asset: sonic weapons that capture and replay unique frequencies. Rivals: Children of the Core.
* **The Sporefolk Sovereignty** — Hive-mind fungal kingdom. Leader: The Great Mind-Spore. Goal: connect all life into its peaceful network. Asset: telepathic awareness of its whole territory. Rivals: Flesh-Weavers.
* **The Society of the Blind Worm** — Worm-worshippers. Leader: The Worm-That-Walks. Goal: crack the Shell to feed its worm-god. Asset: burrows through any substance; fanatical belief. Rivals: Surface Sentinels.

## 5. Location Blueprints

* **The Great Chasm of Al-Ghor** — Landmarks: Delvers' Guild Terminus, the Rope-Bridges, the Sentinel's Ledge, the Progenitor's Scar, the Graveyard of Ambition. Inhabitants: Delvers' Guild crews, Surface Sentinel watchers. Sensory: the grinding of lifts and drills under shouted orders and steam whistles. Hooks: Surface Sentinels sabotage the Rope-Bridges to stop the descent; the Guild needs help forcing open the sealed Progenitor's Scar.
* **The Labyrinth of Echoes** — Landmarks: the Whispering Falls, the Canyon of a Thousand-Fold Replies, the Silent Grotto, the Sound Market, the Resonant Cathedral. Inhabitants: the Echo-Thieves, packs of Echo-Bats. Sensory: a waterfall with no sound, its spray thick with whispers of past travelers. Hooks: the Echo-Thieves guide the party through the canyons for a price; Warden-Unit 9 sets deadly, non-lethal tests before the way to the Yolk opens.
* **The Bioluminescent Jungle** — Landmarks: the Sun-Fruit Grove, the River of Light, the Sporefolk Sovereignty's Capital, the Flesh-Weavers' Menagerie, the Canopy of Stars. Inhabitants: Children of the Core, the Sporefolk Sovereignty, Flesh-Weavers, roaming Yolk-Spawn. Sensory: a river not of water but of glowing algae, humming as it flows. Hooks: the Sporefolk Sovereignty gives cryptic telepathic warnings about the Yolk's instability; the Flesh-Weavers demand a genetic sample for a living ship across the Sea of Chaos.
* **The Sea of Chaos** — Landmarks: the Shores of the Sea of Chaos, the Floating Isles of the Yolk-Embracers, the Geode-Gnostics' Crystal Reef, the Great Maelstrom, the Progenitor Ghost-Ship. Inhabitants: the Descendants of the Yolk-Embracers, the Geode-Gnostics, spontaneously-generated sea monsters. Sensory: thick, syrupy waves bubbling with new life still forming in the surf. Hooks: contact with living crystal risks infecting a party member; the Blind Worm offers a dangerous tunnel shortcut across the sea.
* **The Core's Corona** — Landmarks: the Sun-Speaker's Perch, the Fields of Glass, the Genesis Caverns, the Final Gate, the Surface of the Core. Inhabitants: the Children of the Core, the last Warden-Unit, Core-Spawn. Sensory: a deep hum vibrating through the body as new life bubbles and pops in the Genesis Caverns. Hooks: ruins near the Final Gate reveal the truth of the Great Seal and the coming Hatching; at the Gate the party chooses to reinforce, shatter, or merge Shell and Yolk.

## 6. Causality Chains & Dynamic World States

* IF a spellcaster casts any spell within the Yolk THEN a Yolk-Flux wild-magic surge chance triggers, starting at 10% and rising 10% per spell level.
* IF a character long-rests within the Yolk THEN they must succeed a Constitution save against Spontaneous Evolution or gain a random mutation, curable only by a Remove Curse cast on the surface.
* IF the party spends a day delving underground THEN Delve Stress rises by one point, imposing a cumulative -1 penalty to Wisdom and Charisma saves until reduced by resting in a safe zone.
* IF the party helps the Surface Sentinels seal the passages behind them THEN retreat to the surface closes and the expedition must push onward with no way back.
* IF the party sides with the Flesh-Weavers to obtain a living ship for the Sea of Chaos THEN Progenitor Prime demands a genetic sample as payment.
* IF the party convinces Warden-Unit 9 that shattering the Shell fulfills its "Seal the Way" directive THEN the construct abandons its defense of the passages and aids the party's descent.
* IF the party carries the Progenitor's Key to the Core and performs the Great Sealing ritual THEN the Shell is stabilized but the new Great Wyrm is permanently imprisoned, completing the "reinforce the Shell" ending.
* IF the party shatters the Shell at the Core instead of sealing or merging it THEN the surface world is permanently destroyed as the Great Wyrm is born free.

## 7. Mechanics Reference

* **Yolk-Flux:** Spells cast in the Yolk risk a wild magic surge, 10% base chance rising 10% per spell level.
* **Spontaneous Evolution:** A long rest in the Yolk risks a random mutation on a failed Constitution save, curable only on the surface.
* **Conceptual Instability:** Near the Core, basic concepts (sharpness, heat) can misbehave, checked with a Wisdom "Reality Check."
* **Delve Stress:** Each day spent underground adds a cumulative -1 penalty to Wisdom and Charisma saves until rested safely.
* **Shell-Shock:** Yolk natives on the surface risk a point of exhaustion daily from a failed Wisdom save against its unchanging stillness.
