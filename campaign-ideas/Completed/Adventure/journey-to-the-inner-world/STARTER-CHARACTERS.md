## Why this party

- Covers the five core roles a solo player might pick: front line (Fighter), healer (Cleric), scout (Rogue), arcane (Wizard), face (Bard).
- Every hook starts from the bible's Session 1 (the Great Quake) and Session 2 (hired by Baron Alistair Finch).
- Each character pulls toward a different faction tension: the Guild (Driller), the Sentinels (Chaplain), the Echo-Thieves (Echo), the lost townsfolk (Lamplighter), the Labyrinth (Surveyor).
