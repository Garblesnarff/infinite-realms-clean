# Mark of the Fox: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Soul is not a face; it's a mask. In the sun-drenched, adobe-white pueblo of Los Angeles, the 'Sovereigns' (Spanish colonial nobles) believe they are the only ones with the right to rule. They have turned the 'Great Accord' of the mission into a system of 'Golden Greed' and peasant despair. But the desert has a sense of justice. It manifested 'The Fox,' a man in iconic black who shouldn't be a threat to anyone. Legend says the Fox's 'Cape' is a woven artifact of pure Shadow, and his 'Rapier' is a focus for the 'Science of Flourish.' He doesn't fight the Governor with power; he fights him with style. The 'Mark of Zorro' isn't just a signature—it's a spiritual brand, the moment where the tyrant's own fear becomes their undoing.

### Timeline
1.  **The Mission Founding (c. 1780):** The pueblo of Los Angeles is established around the mission church. The 'Great Accord' is signed between the Church and the People.
2.  **The Rise of the Governor (1810):** A corrupt official takes control of the pueblo, taxing the peasants into starvation and ignoring the Accord.
3.  **The Return of Diego (1820):** Don Diego de la Vega (the player) returns from Spain, appearing as a harmless, foppish noble.
4.  **The First Mark (Present Day - Day 1):** The Fox appears at the Governor's mansion, carving a 'Z' into the wall and rescuing a peasant leader.
5.  **The Lolita Romance (Week 1):** The Fox meets the beautiful and practical Lolita in a moonlit garden. Chemistry develops alongside the rebellion.
6.  **The Secret Cave Discovery (Ongoing):** The Fox's hidden base behind a waterfall is first used to store recovered peasant gold.
7.  **The Unmasking Trap (Upcoming):** The Governor stages a 'False Trial' to lure the Fox into a public unmasking.
8.  **The Mission Bell Uprising (Upcoming):** The peasants, inspired by the Fox, gather at the mission church for a final standoff.
9.  **The Present Day:** The pueblo is a high-contrast nightmare of adobe whites and dramatic blacks. The sun is setting, and the Fox is waiting on the rooftops... for the Mark.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Noble Responsibility' and 'Systemic Injustice.' It is about the 'Romantic Swashbuckler' of the double-life—the realization that being a hero often requires becoming a ghost. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can one masked man inspire a people to reclaim their own freedom?' It explores the sunset oranges of the California landscape, the dramatic geometry of the duel, and the playful wit of a man who outsmarts his enemies with a smile. The players are the Fox's assistants and the noble allies who must navigate the subtext of a garden party and the intensity of a rooftop escape to find a justice that isn't written in the law.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Fox's Vanguard (The Liberators)
**Type:** Masked Vigilante/Peasant Resistance
**Leader:** The Fox (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Iconic black costume, the Secret Cave base, and the power of the 'Mark.'
**Rivals:** The Governor's Guard, The Inquisitor.

### The Governor's Guard (The Oppressors)
**Type:** Professional/Corrupt Military Branch
**Leader:** Captain Monastario (Equivalent - A man of arrogant discipline).
**Asset:** Infinite resources, the Garrison fortress, and the legal authority of the Crown.
**Rivals:** The Fox.

### The Mission Church (The Neutral)
**Type:** Religious/Charitable Order
**Leader:** Father Felipe.
**Asset:** The Mission bell tower, asylum status, and a connection to the 'Great Accord.'
**Rivals:** The Governor (when he violates the sanctuary).

### The Noble Haciendas (The Complicit)
**Type:** Wealthy Landowner Syndicate
**Leader:** Don Alejandro (Diego's father).
**Asset:** Huge estates, private armies, and a traditionalist belief in their own superiority.
**Rivals:** None (initially).

### The 'Lolita' Seekers
**Type:** Independent/Rebel Intellectual Group
**Leader:** Lolita (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of the pueblo's layout, medical expertise, and a refusal to be bought by the Governor.
**Rivals:** The Governor's Guard.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Don Diego (The Fox)** (Independent - Master Duelist) - A man who appears harmless but is actually a lethal weapon. **Voice:** Foppish disinterest (Public) vs. Confident and heroic (Private). **Goal:** To restore justice to Los Angeles and protect his family. **Secret:** He is a high-level Adept of 'Flourish' who can 'carve' symbols into reality that cause psychological damage.
**Lolita** (Independent - Rebel Heroine) - A beautiful and practical woman in period-appropriate Spanish dresses. **Voice:** Seductive, intelligent, and filled with righteous anger. **Goal:** To support the Fox and find a man she can actually respect. **Secret:** She was the one who 'accidently' left the gate unlocked for the Fox's first raid.
**Captain Monastario** (Governor's Guard - The Villain) - A man of arrogant discipline and dramatic flourishes. **Voice:** Booming, theatrical, and filled with a cold rage. **Goal:** To unmask the Fox and rule the pueblo with an iron fist. **Secret:** He is secretly terrified of the Fox and views their duels as a form of divine punishment.
**Father Felipe** (Church - The Mentor) - A man of humble whites and browns who speaks for the mission. **Voice:** Soft, melodic, and filled with wisdom. **Goal:** To preserve the 'Great Accord' and protect the innocent. **Secret:** He knows Diego's secret and provides him with 'Spiritual Alibis.'
**Bernardo** (Independent - The Silent) - Diego's loyal servant who pretends to be deaf and dumb. **Voice:** None (gestures and whistles). **Goal:** To provide the Fox with real-time intelligence and support. **Secret:** He is a master of 'Silent Navigation' and can move through the pueblo without making a sound.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Pueblo Plaza (Los Angeles)
An adobe-white landscape of family-owned shops and a central fountain.
*   **The Mission Church:** A site of 'Spiritual Sanctuary' and humble whites. **Smell:** Incense, dry earth, and old paper. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'bong-bong' of the mission bell.
*   **The Garrison:** A grim, institutional fortress of bureaucracy and deep reds. **Visual:** Harsh shadows casting patterns of venetian blinds.

### The Secret Cave
A dark, nostalgic sanctuary hidden behind a rock face.
*   **Tornado's Stable:** Where the Fox's horse is kept. **Smell:** Hay, leather, and wet stone. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'whinny' of a loyal companion.
*   **The Training Floor:** A site of 'Dynamic Dueling' and flowing movements.

### The Governor's Mansion
A royal gold and burgundy monument to wealth and arrogance.
*   **The Balcony:** Where the Fox makes his 'Dramatic Escapes.' **Visual:** Sunset oranges and reds over the California landscape. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'clink-clink' of rapiers.

### The Moonlit Garden (Mental Space)
A site of 'Romantic Seduction' and desaturated pastels.
*   **The Bench:** Where the Fox and Lolita first spoke. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Mark:** Arrive in the pueblo as Don Diego. You must carve your first 'Z' into the Governor's wall while maintaining your 'Harmless Fop' disguise.
2.  **The Mission Rescue:** The Governor has taken a peasant leader hostage at the Mission Church. You must rescue him without violating the sanctuary.
3.  **The Secret Cave Heist:** Retrieve a shipment of recovered gold from the Governor's vault. You must use Tornado's speed to navigate the desert road.
4.  **Lolita's Gambit:** Lolita invites you to a garden party undercover. You must navigate the 'Romantic Seduction' without revealing your true face.
5.  **The Unmasking Trap:** The Governor has staged a false trial for Father Felipe. You must lead an escape from the Garrison before the unmasking happens.
6.  **The Mark of the Fox:** Face Captain Monastario in a final, public duel in the Plaza. You must use your 'Science of Flourish' to brand him with the 'Z.'
7.  **The Final Coronation:** The truth is revealed through 'Romantic Swashbuckling' and mutual respect as the Governor is overthrown and justice is restored.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Blade of the Fox
**Type:** Legendary Rapier
**Description:** A simple, black rapier that seems to hum with the energy of the first Fox.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Mark of Zorro' strike (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Mask of Shadow
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Helmet)
**Description:** A simple, black domino mask that seems to meld with the wearer's face.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer advantage on all Charisma (Deception and Intimidation) checks and makes them immune to being recognized by anyone who doesn't already know their secret.

### 3. The Governor's Ledger
**Type:** Artifact (Tome)
**Description:** A thick, clinical book that contains the records of every bribe and tax in the pueblo.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder advantage on all Intelligence (Investigation) and Wisdom (Insight) checks. **Curse:** The holder is hunted by the Governor's Guard.

## [TAG: ENEMY_STATBLOCK]
### 1. Governor's Guard (Standard)
(CR 2). Humanoid. HP 30, AC 13.
**Abilities:**
*   **Ordered Discipline:** The guard has advantage on attacks if they have an ally within 5 feet.
*   **Steel Strike:** Melee attack. 1d8+3 damage.

### 2. Captain Monastario (Elite)
(CR 5). Humanoid. HP 80, AC 15.
**Abilities:**
*   **Arrogant Flourish Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet has disadvantage on Charisma checks.
*   **Vengeful Strike:** Melee attack. 2d10 damage + Target is knocked prone.

### 3. 'The Mastermind' Governor (Proxy)
(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Golden Greed Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be compelled to follow the Governor's 'Order of Progress.'
*   **The Final Move:** Once per combat, the Governor can force an enemy to take an action that serves the glory of the Crown.
