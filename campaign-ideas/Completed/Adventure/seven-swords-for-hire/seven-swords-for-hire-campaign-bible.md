# CAMPAIGN BIBLE: SEVEN SWORDS FOR HIRE

## 1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY
`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`

### The Origin Myth: The Sun's Judgment
In the beginning, the Sun fell in love with the Earth, but the Earth loved the Moon. In a fit of jealous rage, the Sun halted in the sky for a single, agonizing hour—**The High Noon of Hell**. This event scorched the sins into the souls of men and bleached the color from the land. Now, the frontier of **San Perdido** is a place where shadows are heavy, water is scarce, and morality is as dry as the dust. It is said that when a gun is fired here, the echo travels backward in time, killing the shooter's ancestors. Violence is not just an action; it is a currency paid to the jealous Sun.

### The Timeline of Dust and Lead
1.  **-50 Years:** **The High Noon of Hell.** The sun stops moving; rivers boil, and the "Bleached Generation" is born blind.
2.  **-45 Years:** **The Arrival of the Iron Hymnal.** Fanatics begin building a railway track in a perfect circle, believing it will trap the Devil.
3.  **-30 Years:** **The Silver Rain.** A freak storm rains molten silver instead of water. Those who survived became rich; those who didn't became statues.
4.  **-20 Years:** **The War of Five flags.** Five nations claimed San Perdido. All armies vanished overnight in the **Great Haze**.
5.  **-15 Years:** **The Hanging of Judge Gallows.** The only honest judge is hanged by his own gavel. Law officially leaves the territory.
6.  **-10 Years:** **The Birth of Calvera.** A bandit warlord unites the fractured gangs under the banner of "The Red Hand."
7.  **-5 Years:** **The Drought of Silence.** No wind blew for a year. Madness rates spiked.
8.  **-2 Years:** **The Miracle of Santa Roja.** The village of Santa Roja discovers their agave plants bleed red sap (Blood-Agave), a potent narcotic wine.
9.  **-6 Months:** **The First Tithe.** Calvera discovers Santa Roja's wine and demands 90% of the harvest.
10. **-1 Week:** **The Elder's Dream.** Petra, the village elder, dreams of seven black birds fighting a golden snake. She leaves to find help.

### The Metaphysics of Violence
In San Perdido, **"Grit"** is a tangible force. When a man survives a mortal wound, his shadow grows darker. The conflict between the Seven and Calvera is not just tactical; it is a ritual. The land demands a "Balance of Blood." The Seven are not just mercenaries; they are the antibody the land has summoned to cure the infection of Calvera's greed.

## 2. FACTIONS
`[TAG: FACTION_DATA]`

### [The Seven]
**Type:** Ad-hoc Mercenary Unit / Protagonists
**Leader:** The Players (Nominally "Chris")
**Asset:** **The Code.** They possess "Heroic Grit," allowing them to defy physics during 'High Noon' moments.
**Rivals:** Calvera's Legion, The Iron Hymnal.

### [Calvera's Legion]
**Type:** Paramilitary Bandit Army
**Leader:** Calvera (The Golden Warlord)
**Asset:** **The Gatling Wagon.** A stolen military carriage mounted with a rotary cannon.
**Rivals:** The Seven, The Law (defunct).

### [The Villagers of Santa Roja]
**Type:** Agrarian Victims
**Leader:** Petra (Elder)
**Asset:** **Blood-Agave.** A cactus wine that numbs pain and grants temporary courage.
**Rivals:** Starvation, Calvera.

### [The Iron Hymnal]
**Type:** Railway Cultists
**Leader:** Conductor Malachi
**Asset:** **The Spikes.** Railroad spikes made of bone. They nail heretics to the sleepers.
**Rivals:** Anyone interrupting the construction of the "Infinite Loop."

### [The Pale Riders]
**Type:** Albinos / Night-Raiders
**Leader:** "White-Eye" Jack
**Asset:** **Shadow-Cloaks.** They are nearly invisible in moonlight. They never attack during the day.
**Rivals:** The Sun.

### [The Widows of Scorn]
**Type:** Assassin Sisterhood
**Leader:** Madre Dolorosa
**Asset:** **Veiled Poison.** They operate a brothel where the "kiss of death" is literal (cyanide lipstick).
**Rivals:** Cheating husbands, Calvera (he owes them money).

### [The Copper Tongues]
**Type:** Information Brokers / Peddlers
**Leader:** Sidebar Sally
**Asset:** **The Telegraph Web.** They have tapped into the old military lines; they know everything before it happens.
**Rivals:** The Iron Hymnal (who cut their wires).

### [The Bureau of Lost Deeds]
**Type:** Surreal Bureaucracy
**Leader:** The Auditor
**Asset:** **The Ledger.** A magical book that records every crime. They hunt those with "red ink" in their souls.
**Rivals:** Outlaws who don't pay "Sin Tax."

### [The Cactus-Kin]
**Type:** Druidic Survivalists
**Leader:** Old Man Thorn
**Asset:** **Needle-Skin.** They graft cactus spines into their flesh. Unarmed attacks cause bleed.
**Rivals:** Farmers (who chop down cacti).

### [The Society of the Hollow Chamber]
**Type:** Gun-Monks
**Leader:** Abbot Winchester
**Asset:** **The One-Shot Technique.** They meditate for days to fire a single, perfect bullet.
**Rivals:** Anyone who fires "wasteful" automatic weapons.

## 4. LOCATIONS
`[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]`

### Zone 1: Santa Roja (The Village)
*   **The Plaza:** The heart of the defense. A dry fountain sits in the center.
    *   *Sensory:* Smell of roasting peppers and horse manure. Sound of a lone bell tolling off-beat.
*   **The Church of the Bleached Saint:** Adobe walls riddled with old bullet holes.
    *   *Sensory:* Cool air, smell of old wax and stale incense.
*   **The Cantina "El Burro Muerto":** Dark, smoky, fly-infested.
    *   *Sensory:* Sticky floors, taste of cheap tequila, sound of spurs clinking.
*   **The Rooftops:** Flat adobe roofs connected by planks (defensive positions).
    *   *Sensory:* Blistering heat, grit in teeth, vertigo.
*   **The Catacombs:** Beneath the church, storing the wine.
    *   *Sensory:* Smell of fermenting agave (sweet rot), total silence.

### Zone 2: The Badlands
*   **The Devil's Anvil:** A flat mesa where duels happen.
    *   *Sensory:* Heat distortion waves, sound of wind howling through rock flutes.
*   **Calvera’s Camp:** A fortress built into a canyon wall.
    *   *Sensory:* Smell of unwashed men and roasting meat, constant noise of gambling.
*   **The Cactus Forest:** 20-foot tall cacti.
    *   *Sensory:* Buzzing of massive wasps, shadows that look like grasping hands.
*   **The Dry River:** A riverbed of cracked clay.
    *   *Sensory:* Cracking sound underfoot, dust plumes.
*   **The Hanging Tree:** A massive dead oak with 50 nooses.
    *   *Sensory:* Creaking ropes, smell of decay.

## 8. MECHANICS
`[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]`

### The Grit System
Instead of Inspiration, players have **Grit Points** (Max 3).
*   **Gain Grit:** By roleplaying flaws, taking risks, or delivering a "Cool One-Liner."
*   **Spend Grit:**
    *   *Bullet Time:* Force an enemy to reroll an attack.
    *   *Push Through:* Survive at 1 HP instead of 0.
    *   *Fan the Hammer:* Make an extra attack action.

### The Duel Mechanic
When a 1v1 duel initiates:
1.  **Stare Down:** Roll Intimidation vs Insight. Winner gets +5 Initiative.
2.  **The Draw:** Initiative Roll.
3.  **The Shot:** Attack Roll.
4.  **Lethality:** Duels are always critical hits.

### Village Defense Rating
Track the **Defense Score (0-100)**.
*   Start: 0.
*   Training Farmers: +10 per success.
*   Building Walls: +15.
*   Traps: +5 each.
*   **Effect:** In the final battle, every 10 points reduces the number of bandits that spawn per wave by 1.
