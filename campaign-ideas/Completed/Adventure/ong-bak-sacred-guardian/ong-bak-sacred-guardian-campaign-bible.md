# Campaign Bible: Ong Bak - The Sacred Guardian

## **1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY**
`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`
### **Origin Myth: The Tears of the Stone Buddha**
Before the concrete smothered the earth, the spirit of the land, **Phra Ong**, wept for the violence of men. His tears did not turn to water, but to **living stone**. These stones were gathered by the first ascetic monks of Ban Nong Pradu to carve the Ong Bak statue. It is not merely an idol; it is a **spiritual anchor**. The lore states that if Ong Bak’s head is severed from its body, the "Heat of the City" (corruption, greed, heatstroke) will wither the rice paddies of the village, turning the soil into grey ash until the guardian is made whole.

### **Timeline of Volatile History**
1.  **240 BE (Buddhist Era):** The Founding of Ban Nong Pradu on a ley line of "Cool Earth."
2.  **The Year of Broken Tusks:** A Burmese invasion was stopped not by armies, but by a single Muay Boran master who fought for three days straight.
3.  **The Concrete Expansion:** The Capital City paves over the Great Marsh; spirits of the swamp are trapped beneath the asphalt.
4.  **The Rise of Komtuan:** A low-level amulet peddler discovers a cache of Opium Wars gold and buys the police force.
5.  **The Night of Silenced Bells:** **(Rare/Weird)** For one hour, no sound was possible in the Capital. Gunshots were silent. Screams were mute. It is believed a mute spirit passed through the city.
6.  **The Great Fire of District 9:** Burned down the old stadium. Locals say the fire burned green.
7.  **The Banishing of Ting's Father:** Ting's father left the village to fight in the city and never returned, leaving a legacy of shame.
8.  **The Invention of "Dust":** A new street drug made from crushed fluorescent lights and adrenaline hits the streets.
9.  **The Selling of the Head:** Don, a village traitor, saws off Ong Bak’s head during a storm.
10. **The Drought Begins:** The morning after the theft, the village well dries up instantly.

### **The Metaphysical "Why"**
The conflict is between **Satja (Truth/Promise)** and **Maya (Illusion/Materialism)**. The Village represents the physical reality of the earth and muscle. The City represents the illusion of neon, drugs, and wire-transfers. The fight is not just for a statue, but to prove that flesh and bone are stronger than steel and coin.

## **4. LOCATIONS**
`[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]`

### **Zone 1: Ban Nong Pradu (The Village)**
*   **The Ancient Bodhi Tree:** Roots wrapped around stone Buddha heads. **Sensory:** Smell of wet earth and jasmine incense. Sound of wind chimes.
*   **The Dust Bowl:** Red clay training ground. **Sensory:** Taste of iron dust. Sound of shins hitting palm logs.
*   **Elder’s Stilt House:** Teak wood, elevated. **Sensory:** Creaking wood, smell of medicinal herbs.

### **Zone 2: The Sprawl (Capital Outskirts)**
*   **The Tire Mountain:** A massive pile of burning rubber tires used by gangs. **Sensory:** Choking black smoke, heat.
*   **The Floating Market:** Boats jammed together. **Sensory:** Smell of canal water and spicy curry. Shouting vendors.

### **Zone 3: The Neon District (Urban Center)**
*   **Soi 6 (The Red Light):** Flashing neon signs, crowded bars. **Sensory:** Thumping bass, smell of stale beer and cheap perfume.
*   **The Wire Web:** An alleyway completely covered in tangled electrical wires that spark. **Sensory:** Ozone smell, buzzing electricity.

### **Zone 4: The Fight Circuit (Underground)**
*   **The Abattoir:** An old slaughterhouse turned arena. Blood drains in the floor. **Sensory:** Metallic smell of blood, hooks rattling.
*   **The Cage of Bones:** A cage made of rusted rebar suspended over a pit.

### **Zone 5: Komtuan’s High-Rise**
*   **The Penthouse Museum:** Pristine white room filled with stolen artifacts. **Sensory:** Sterile air conditioning, absolute silence.
*   **The Aquarium Walls:** Walls are tanks filled with sharks.

## **8. MECHANICS**
`[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]`

### **The "Bone Crunch" System**
*   **Critical Hits:** Instead of double damage, roll on the **Injury Table** (Broken Rib, Concussion, Shattered Shin).
*   **Stamina:** No "Long Rests" in the city unless you pay for a safe hotel. Sleeping on the street restores only half HP.
*   **Face (Reputation):** Fighting honorably increases "Face" (Villagers help you). Fighting dirty increases "Infamy" (Criminals help you).
*   **Environmental Combat:** Players get +2 to hit if they describe using the environment (smashing a head into a table, throwing sand).
