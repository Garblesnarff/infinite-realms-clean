# Death on the Rails: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Train is not just a machine; it is a moving microcosm of civilization, hurtling through a wilderness of chaos. It was built by the 'Engineers of Order,' who believed that the rhythmic pulse of the wheels on the tracks was the only thing keeping the world from falling apart. The 'Rails' are the veins of the world, and the 'Steam' is its breath. In this closed system, every passenger is a variable in a complex equation of social standing and hidden history. The 'Snowstorm' is the manifestation of the world's indifference—a cold, white wall that traps the variables in a confined space until the equation is solved. Here, a murder is more than a crime; it is a breakdown of the machine itself, a moment where the civilized facade is torn away to reveal the raw, bleeding truth.

### Timeline
1.  **The Golden Spike (c. 1890):** The first trans-continental rails are laid, connecting the disparate empires of the world.
2.  **The Construction of the Express (1925):** The most luxurious train in history is completed, a masterpiece of Art Deco design and engineering.
3.  **The Armstrong Tragedy (10 Years Ago):** A young girl is kidnapped and murdered, a crime that shatters a noble family and leaves a trail of broken lives across two continents.
4.  **The Murderer's Escape (Ongoing):** Cassetti, the man responsible for the Armstrong tragedy, changes his name to Ratchett and disappears into the high-society shadows.
5.  **The Departure (Present Day - Day 1):** The Express leaves Istanbul, carrying an unusual ensemble of passengers, including the legendary detective Hercule Poirot.
6.  **The Snowdrift (Day 2 - Midnight):** A massive snowstorm halts the train in the middle of the Yugoslavian wilderness. The world outside becomes a prison of white.
7.  **The Twelve Wounds (Day 3 - 6 AM):** Ratchett is found dead in his cabin, stabbed twelve times. The 'Civilized Facade' begins to crack.
8.  **The Interviews (Ongoing):** Poirot begins questioning the twelve suspects, each a distinct representative of a different social class and nationality.
9.  **The Final Revelation (Upcoming):** The truth of the Armstrong connection is revealed, forcing Poirot—and the players—to choose between the letter of the law and the spirit of justice.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Vengeance' and 'Justice' in a world that has failed to provide either. It is about the 'Confined Elegance' of the human condition—the realization that even the most sophisticated people are capable of darkness when pushed far enough. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can a crime be justified if the victim was a monster?' It explores the tragedy of the Armstrong family, the intricate geometric designs of the Art Deco era, and the cold, isolating power of the snow. The players are the detectives and observers who must piece together the shattered pieces of twelve lives to find a resolution that satisfies both their 'little gray cells' and their conscience.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Passengers (The Suspects)
**Type:** Diverse Ensemble Cast
**Leader:** Princess Dragomiroff (Socially) and Poirot (Intellectually).
**Asset:** Hidden connections to the Armstrong family, high-society alibis, and distinct class-based perspectives.
**Rivals:** The Truth (officially), Each Other (unofficially).

### The Train Crew (The Facilitators)
**Type:** Neutral Service Personnel
**Leader:** Pierre Michel (The Conductor).
**Asset:** Keys to all compartments, knowledge of the train's layout, and a front-row seat to everyone's movements.
**Rivals:** The Snowstorm (the primary obstacle).

### The Armstrong Remnants (The Vengeful)
**Type:** Secret/Dispersed Network of Grieving Individuals
**Leader:** None formally (initially).
**Asset:** Absolute conviction, shared trauma, and a ten-year plan for retribution.
**Rivals:** Ratchett/Cassetti.

### The Legal Authorities (The Absent)
**Type:** External Law Enforcement
**Leader:** The Yugoslavian Police (Currently unreachable).
**Asset:** The power of the state and the letter of the law.
**Rivals:** The Snowstorm (trapping them outside).

### The 'Gray Cells' Seekers
**Type:** Intellectual/Analytical Investigators
**Leader:** Hercule Poirot (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Logical deduction, an eye for detail, and a refusal to accept the impossible.
**Rivals:** The Deception of the Suspects.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Hercule Poirot** (Independent - Master Detective) - Belgian detective with an immaculate waxed mustache. **Voice:** Belgian-accented, precise, and slightly pompous. **Goal:** To solve the murder using his 'little gray cells.' **Secret:** He is deeply troubled by the moral implications of this specific case and is questioning his own belief in absolute justice.
**Princess Dragomiroff** (Suspect - Aristocratic Hauteur) - An elderly Russian noble with a terrifying presence. **Voice:** Cold, authoritative, and deeply British upper-class. **Goal:** To protect the secret of the Armstrong connection at all costs. **Secret:** She was the godmother of the murdered Armstrong child.
**Colonel Arbuthnot** (Suspect - Military Bearing) - A rigid British officer returning from India. **Voice:** Gruff, disciplined, and strictly by-the-book. **Goal:** To fulfill his duty and protect Mary Debenham. **Secret:** He was a close friend of Colonel Armstrong and views the murder as an execution.
**Mary Debenham** (Suspect - Nervous Gentility) - A governess trying to stay composed. **Voice:** Precise, soft, and perpetually anxious. **Goal:** To escape the train and her past without being noticed. **Secret:** She was the governess in the Armstrong household and is the tactical 'brain' behind the plan.
**Ratchett (Cassetti)** (Criminal - The Victim) - A wealthy American with a dark past. **Voice:** Gruff, paranoid, and practical. **Goal:** To buy his way out of the consequences of his crimes. **Secret:** He knew he was being hunted and tried to hire Poirot for protection.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Express (Art Deco Luxury)
A masterpiece of polished mahogany, brass fixtures, and burgundy velvet.
*   **The Dining Car:** Long tables with white linens and crystal. **Smell:** Coffee, expensive cigars, and snow. **Sound:** The rhythmic thrum of the engine and the clink of silver.
*   **Ratchett's Cabin (Crime Scene):** A cramped, elegant space with a body on the bed. **Visual:** Twelve distinct stab wounds and scattered evidence (a pipe cleaner, a handkerchief).

### The Compartments
Small but elegant private cabins where the suspects hide.
*   **Poirot's Compartment:** A sanctuary of order and perfectly pressed suits. **Visual:** A small mirror showing an immaculate mustache.
*   **The Corridor:** A narrow, dark path where shadows move behind velvet curtains.

### The Yugoslavian Wilderness (Outside)
An endless, isolating expanse of white.
*   **The Snowdrift:** A massive wall of ice that has trapped the train. **Visual:** Blue-white light reflecting off the snow. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure.

### The Armstrong Flashback (Mental Space)
A heartbreaking, desaturated memory of a happy home.
*   **The Nursery:** A room filled with toys and the scent of lavender. **Visual:** A single, broken doll on the floor.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Dead Passenger:** Discover Ratchett's body in his cabin. Secure the scene and find the first three pieces of contradictory evidence.
2.  **The Twelve Interviews:** Question each of the twelve suspects in the Dining Car. Identify the 'Class-Based lie' in each of their testimonies.
3.  **The Snowdrift Mystery:** Someone was seen in the corridor during the night. Track the footprints in the snow outside before they are covered.
4.  **The Armstrong Connection:** Link each suspect to the Armstrong tragedy using the evidence from their luggage.
5.  **The Maestro's Deduction:** You must present your findings to the gathered suspects. Can you explain how twelve people could commit a single murder?
6.  **The Moral Choice:** Poirot offers two solutions: one legal, one 'Just.' You must decide which story to tell the police when the snow clears.
7.  **The Final Whistle:** The train begins to move again. Deal with the emotional fallout of the resolution as the Express reaches its destination.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. Poirot's Pocket Watch
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A perfectly balanced gold watch that never loses a second.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Rewind' a single 6-second moment once per day to observe a detail they missed.

### 2. The Armstrong Handkerchief
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A fine, cambric handkerchief with an embroidered 'H.'
**Mechanics:** When held, the user can 'feel' the strongest emotion currently in the room (Grief, Fear, or Vengeance).

### 3. The Master Key of the Express
**Type:** Artifact (Gatestone)
**Description:** A heavy, brass key that opens every door on the train.
**Mechanics:** Allows the user to enter any compartment without making a check and grants advantage on all Intelligence (Investigation) checks inside the train.

## [TAG: ENEMY_STATBLOCK]
### 1. The Shadow in the Corridor
(CR 2). Humanoid (Suspect). HP 30, AC 14.
**Abilities:**
*   **Distraction:** The shadow can use a reaction to impose disadvantage on a Perception check.
*   **Quiet Footsteps:** The shadow has advantage on Stealth checks in the train corridor.

### 2. The Snowstorm (Environmental)
(CR 5). Hazard.
**Abilities:**
*   **Isolating Cold:** Any creature outside the train for more than 1 minute takes 1d10 cold damage per round.
*   **Whiteout:** Perception checks based on sight have disadvantage beyond 5 feet.

### 3. 'The Executioner' (Collective)
(CR 8). Humanoid (Twelve Suspects). HP 150, AC 16.
**Abilities:**
*   **Shared Burden:** The executioner is immune to being charmed or frightened as long as all twelve members are present.
*   **Twelve Strikes:** Melee attack. 12d4 piercing damage (only if the target is paralyzed or restrained).
