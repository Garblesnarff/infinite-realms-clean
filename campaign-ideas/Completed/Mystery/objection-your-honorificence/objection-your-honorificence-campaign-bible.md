# Objection, Your Honorificence!: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Law is not a human invention; it is a primal enchantment woven into the foundations of reality. In the beginning, the 'Architects of Order' realized that chaos could only be contained by the 'Magical Contract.' They forged the first 'Aetheneum of Law,' a cosmic library where every truth is recorded in glowing script. The 'High Courts' were established not just to judge men, but to maintain the stability of the universe. In this world, an 'Objection' is more than a word—it is a 'Counter-Spell' that can shatter a false argument or reveal a hidden truth. The Judges are not just officials; they are often ancient constructs or spectral entities bound by the 'Oath of Impartiality.' To practice law here is to be a combat-mage of the mind, using evidence as your focus and logic as your blade.

### Timeline
1.  **The Age of the Contract (c. -5,000 years):** The first magical legal codes are etched into the 'Stone of Truth.'
2.  **The Founding of the High Courts (-2,000 years):** The judicial system is centralized in the grand city of Lexis. The first Golem Judges are activated.
3.  **The Great Perjury War (-500 years):** A dark sorcerer attempts to rewrite the laws of physics through legal loopholes. He is defeated by a legendary defense attorney.
4.  **The Rise of the Arcane Prosecution (100 years ago):** A new branch of the court is formed, specializing in crimes involving high-tier magic and reality distortion.
5.  **The Trial of the Century (50 years ago):** A prince is acquitted of regicide through a brilliant use of 'Spectral Testimony.'
6.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A passionate and determined young lawyer (the player) wins their first case in the High Court of Lexis.
7.  **The Stone Golem's Glitch (Recent):** One of the comically stern Golem Judges begins to show signs of 'Emotional Bias.'
8.  **The False Evidence Conspiracy (Upcoming):** A mysterious organization begins using 'Truth-Illusions' to frame high-ranking officials.
9.  **The Present Day:** The legal system is at a breaking point.flamboyant prosecutors and shifty witnesses are clashing in the grandest courtrooms the universe has ever seen.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the battle for 'Objective Truth' in a world of magical deception. It is about the 'Dramatic Flair' of the intellectual battle—the thrill of the breakthrough and the wit of the counter-argument. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can logic prevail in a world where reality itself can be enchanted?' It explores the exaggerated expressions of humanity, the weight of a divine impartial judgment, and the comedy found in the absurdities of the law. The players are the rookie lawyers and veteran prosecutors who must use their wits, evidence, and a touch of magical flair to ensure that justice is not just done, but seen to be done.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Defense Guild (The Truth-Seekers)
**Type:** Association of Independent Defense Attorneys
**Leader:** Phoenix Wright (Equivalent - The Mentor).
**Asset:** Logical deduction, empathy, and the ability to find the 'one contradiction' that shatters a case.
**Rivals:** The Arcane Prosecution, The False Evidence Syndicate.

### The Arcane Prosecution (The Enforcers)
**Type:** Elite/High-Society Legal Prosecutors
**Leader:** Prosecutor Von Karma (Equivalent - A man of perfection).
**Asset:** Overwhelming evidence, 'Truth-Spells,' and a theatrical command of the courtroom.
**Rivals:** The Defense Guild.

### The High Judicial Council
**Type:** Governing Body of Judges
**Leader:** The Golem Prime (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** The 'Gavel of Silence,' magical impartiality, and control of the court's physical architecture.
**Rivals:** None (officially neutral).

### The Aetheneum Librarians
**Type:** Scientific/Spectral Legal Researchers
**Leader:** The Spectral Librarian (An ancient ghost).
**Asset:** Access to every legal document ever written and the ability to summon 'Precedent Echoes.'
**Rivals:** The False Evidence Syndicate.

### The False Evidence Syndicate (The Manipulators)
**Type:** Secret Criminal Organization
**Leader:** Unknown (The 'Phantom' of Lexis).
**Asset:** Truth-Illusions, 'Memory-Wipe' mist, and a network of shifty witnesses.
**Rivals:** The Defense Guild, The Arcane Prosecution.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**The Protagonist (Apollo)** (Defense - Passionate Rookie) - A lawyer with wide, determined eyes and a pointing finger. **Voice:** Energetic, sharp, and prone to 'Objection!' shouts. **Goal:** To save his clients and find the ultimate truth. **Secret:** He can 'perceive' a witness's nervous habits as visual distortions.
**Prosecutor Miles** (Prosecution - Flamboyant Rival) - A man in a deep red suit with a sharp, arrogant smile. **Voice:** Witty, confident, and patronizingly correct. **Goal:** To achieve a 'Perfect Verdict' and uphold the dignity of the court. **Secret:** He secretly respects the Protagonist and views their battles as a form of intellectual art.
**The Golem Judge** (Judiciary - Imposing Construct) - A massive stone construct with glowing blue eyes. **Voice:** Booming, monotone, and comically stern. **Goal:** To maintain order and deliver a fair verdict. **Secret:** He secretly enjoys the Protagonist's 'Dramatic Flair' and scores their arguments in his head.
**The Shifty Witness (Larry)** (Independent - Clumsy Grifter) - A man who is perpetually nervous and prone to comically panicked squeaks. **Voice:** High-pitched, fast-paced, and unreliable. **Goal:** To stay out of trouble and tell a 'good enough' story. **Secret:** He is almost always an accidental witness to the most important events.
**The Spectral Librarian** (Grail Seeker equivalent - Expert/AI) - An ancient ghost who guards the Aetheneum. **Voice:** Wise, glitchy, and multi-tonal. **Goal:** To ensure the records of Lexis remain untainted. **Secret:** He was the first defense attorney to ever lose a case, and he's still bitter about it.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The High Court of Lexis
A grand courtroom with towering pillars and glowing magical runes.
*   **The Defense Bench:** A sleek, wooden stand with a glowing interface for evidence. **Smell:** Old paper and ozone. **Sound:** The rhythmic tapping of a determined lawyer.
*   **The Judge's Bench:** A massive, stone altar where the Golem Judge sits impassively. **Visual:** Venetian blind shadows casting patterns of light and shadow.

### The Aetheneum of Law
An ancient, cosmic library filled with every truth ever recorded.
*   **The Hall of Precedents:** A cavernous space where holographic books float in the air. **Visual:** Cosmic dust catching the light of a spectral lamp.
*   **The Forbidden Vault:** A magically sealed room containing 'Truths that could break the world.'

### The Lexis Police Department
A detailed, urban investigation hub with a comic-book feel.
*   **The Evidence Room:** A cluttered space filled with glowing bags and magically sealed murder weapons. **Smell:** Ink and sterile green cleaner.
*   **Gumshoe's Desk:** A monument to incompetence and dedication.

### The Underworld of Lexis
A grittier, mysterious sector where the shifty witnesses hide.
*   **The Blind Alley:** A place where 'Truth-Illusions' are sold. **Visual:** Flickering neon signs and the shadows of ghosts.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Objection:** Win your first trial in Lexis. Discover a contradiction in a witness's testimony by using your 'Perception' ability.
2.  **The Golem's Dilemma:** Defend a client against a comically stern Golem Judge who has been enchanted by a 'Bias Spell.'
3.  **The Aetheneum Heist:** Someone has stolen the 'Stone of Truth' from the archives. You must track the thief through the Underworld.
4.  **The Flamboyant Rival:** Face Prosecutor Miles in a 'Trial by Performance.' You must match his theatrical flair to keep the jury engaged.
5.  **The Spectral Witness:** Channel the spirit of a dead victim to find the location of a 'Lost Evidence' piece.
6.  **The Phantom's Frame:** You are accused of tampering with evidence. You must prove your own innocence in a trial presided over by the Golem Prime.
7.  **The Final Verdict:** A three-way legal standoff at the center of Lexis. Break the 'False Evidence Syndicate' and restore the Magical Contract.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Gavel of Silence
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A massive, golden gavel that hums with the power of the Judicial Council.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Silence' a witness for 1 round (DC 18 Wisdom save). If successful, the witness must reveal their most guarded secret.

### 2. The Attorney's Badge
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A simple, gold pin that glows when near a contradiction.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer advantage on all Wisdom (Insight) and Intelligence (Investigation) checks. Once per day, the wearer can shout 'OBJECTION!' to negate an enemy's action.

### 3. The Magatama of Truth
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A glowing green jewel that reveals the 'Locks' on a person's heart.
**Mechanics:** Allows the user to see the 'Psych-Locks' protecting a secret. Each lock can be broken by presenting the correct piece of evidence.
