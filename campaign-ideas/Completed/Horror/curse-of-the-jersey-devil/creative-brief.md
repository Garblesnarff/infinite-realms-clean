# Creative Brief: Curse of the Jersey Devil

This document outlines the core creative direction for the assets of this campaign, ensuring a consistent and fitting tone across all visuals, audio, and more.

## Art Style: Colonial Woodcut (locked)

The style was chosen by a bake-off on 2026-09-24. "Colonial Woodcut" won all 3 subjects against a painted gothic style. It reads clearly at 96px and in 32px circles. Paste this block unchanged into every image prompt:

> Dark folk-horror woodcut illustration. Bold carved black lines and gouged white highlights like an 18th-century woodblock print, with rough hand-carved texture. Limited palette of ink black, bone white and aged-paper sepia, plus one accent colour only: ember red, used for eyes, fire and lantern light. Flat shapes and strong silhouettes, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.

**Negative line (every prompt):** No text, no letters, no captions, no speech bubbles, no panel borders, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no frame. No blood, no gore.

### Palette

*   **Base:** ink black, bone white, aged-paper sepia.
*   **Accent (one only):** ember red. Use it for the devil's eyes, fire, lanterns, furnace glow and lit windows. Never for blood.
*   **Rule:** every image has one ember-red focal point. It holds the silhouette at small sizes.

### Mood and Subjects

*   **Keywords:** cursed, colonial, hereditary, wild, isolated, ancestral sin, folk horror.
*   **Feeling:** trapped by history and bloodline. The pines feel hostile. The past is never dead.
*   **Environments:** stunted pitch pines, cedar swamps with tea-dark water, cranberry bogs, bog-iron furnace ruins and slag heaps, colonial graveyards, Leeds Point, the ruined Leeds Homestead.
*   **People:** working-class colonial and rural folk in practical clothes (homespun, wool, bonnets, oilcloth). Leeds descendants carry subtle signs of the curse: unusual eyes, a too-sharp tooth.
*   **The Jersey Devil:** a chimera about 8–10 feet long. The muscular body of a large goat or horse, bat wings spanning 12–15 feet, a long neck ending in a horse-like head with glowing ember-red eyes and sharp teeth, clawed forearms, hooved hind legs, a long whip-like forked tail. Frightening, never gory.
*   **Composition:** small figures in a vast hostile forest. Trees form tunnels and frames. Show the devil from below.

### Do Not

*   No real Indigenous people or nations, and no "tribe" imagery.
*   No text in images. No gore.
*   No third-party creatures or characters. No real living people.

## Voice

A weathered, female voice with a slight Southern/Mid-Atlantic regional accent, speaking in a tone of warning and sorrow. The pacing is measured and careful, as if sharing dangerous knowledge. The emotion is one of deep sadness mixed with fear—someone who has lost family to the curse and knows its terrible power. Audio quality is warm but slightly hollow, as if recorded in a wooden room. Role: A Leeds family descendant who escaped the curse but lost everything to it, now warning others.
