# Curse of the Jersey Devil — Style Bake-Off (6 images)

**Purpose:** pick one art style before the full art run. Three subjects, each made in both styles. Same protocol as Journey.

## How to run it

1. **Use one generator for all 6 images.**
2. **Keep the subject text identical.** Only the style block changes between A and B.
3. **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 for images 1 and 2, 4:3 for image 3.
4. **Make 2–4 variations of each.** Keep the best one of each.
5. **Judge at app sizes:** shrink each keeper to 96px, and images 1 and 2 also to a 32px circle. Put A and B side by side.
6. **The style that wins 2 of the 3 subjects is locked.** Do not mix styles after the lock.

| Subject | Question |
|---|---|
| 1. Father Michael | Can you read his face and guilt at 96px? Is the silhouette clear at 32px? |
| 2. The Jersey Devil | Is it frightening and clearly a winged creature at 32px? Does it avoid gore? |
| 3. The Leeds Homestead | Does the scene read at 96px, or does it turn to mud? Is the lit window the clear focal point? |

Keep the keepers. The winning Father Michael and Jersey Devil images may be reused in the final set.

## Style A — "Colonial Woodcut"

### A1 — Father Michael (1:1)

```
Dark folk-horror woodcut illustration. Bold carved black lines and gouged white highlights like an 18th-century woodblock print, with rough hand-carved texture. Limited palette of ink black, bone white and aged-paper sepia, plus one accent colour only: ember red, used for eyes, fire and lantern light. Flat shapes and strong silhouettes, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.

Head-and-shoulders portrait of Father Michael, a gaunt human former priest in his fifties, tormented by his own sins. Hollow cheeks, pleading guilt-ridden eyes, unshaven jaw. A frayed black cassock with a loosened clerical collar, a small iron bell on a cord around his neck, rosary wrapped around one hand held near his chest. A single lantern below lights his face; twisted pine trunks in darkness behind him.

Square composition. Face in the upper middle of the frame, strong readable silhouette, plain dark background behind the head.

No text, no letters, no captions, no speech bubbles, no panel borders, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no frame. No blood, no gore.
```
