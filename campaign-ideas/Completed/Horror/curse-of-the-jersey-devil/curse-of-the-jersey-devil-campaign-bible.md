# Curse of the Jersey Devil: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
In 1687, Daniel Leeds arrived in the West Jersey colony with a printer's press, a surveyor's chain, and a hunger no almanac could satisfy. Within twenty years he owned thousands of acres of the sandy, unwanted pine country nobody else wanted — the Barrens — and the freeholders whispered that Leeds Point had prospered too fast, too cleanly, for honest work. The truth was worse than gossip. On a black solstice night at the edge of his own bog, Daniel Leeds knelt in the cedar water and struck a bargain with a devil-lord named Astaroth: wealth, land, and standing for his line, in exchange for a due bill Astaroth would collect Himself, in His own time, in His own coin. The coin was children. Every thirteenth child born into the Leeds bloodline belongs to Astaroth by right of the pact, and on the night of its birth the debt comes due in flesh. The family's own legend blurred the devil-lord into the mother who bore the price: Mother Leeds, wife of a later Leeds son, gave birth to her own thirteenth child on a howling winter night in 1735, and the thing that came out of her writhed, sprouted leather wings, and took the roof off the birthing room on its way into the pines. The Barrens have never been rid of it since. Folk call it the Leeds Devil, or the Jersey Devil, and tell their children it is a monster from nowhere. It is not from nowhere. It is the first installment on a three-hundred-year-old debt, and the ledger is still open.

### Timeline
1.  **The Founding (1687-1704):** Daniel Leeds surveys and claims the sandy interior of West Jersey that no one else will farm, building the family's first holdings at what becomes Leeds Point.
2.  **The Pact at the Cedar Water (1704):** Daniel Leeds, deep in debt and desperate to secure his family's fortune, makes his bargain with Astaroth. Leeds prosperity triples within five years; no one asks why.
3.  **The Furnace Boom (1710-1740):** Leeds capital seeds the region's bog-iron furnaces. The furnace towns run on the labor of indentured debtors and convict-bond workers who are worked, fed, and buried without ceremony — a second, quieter sin the family profits from and never accounts for.
4.  **The Thirteenth Child (Winter, 1735):** Mother Leeds, married into the family, bears her thirteenth child. It transforms within the hour of its birth — hooved, winged, horse-headed — and destroys the birthing room before fleeing into the pines. The original Jersey Devil is born.
5.  **The First Sightings (1735-1790):** Livestock vanish, travelers go missing, and the winged shape is seen over the bogs on moonless nights. The Leeds family denies everything and quietly begins burying its own dead close to home, away from consecrated ground.
6.  **The Founding of the Devil Worshippers (1790s):** A splinter branch of Leeds cousins and furnace-town believers, unable to explain their family's luck any other way, begins to venerate the thing in the pines rather than fear it. They found a hidden sanctuary and pass their theology down in secret for generations.
7.  **The Scattering (1800s-1900s):** As furnace towns fail and the iron industry moves west, Leeds descendants scatter across the region, many losing track of their own lineage. The pact does not care whether they know their own name — only whether they are the thirteenth.
8.  **Reverend Crane's Ruin (1970s):** A young minister named Josiah Crane, newly arrived at Leeds Point, learns the family history from a dying parishioner and attempts an unauthorized ritual to sever the pact using an incomplete set of components. The ritual fails catastrophically; his own young family is lost to the Barrens that same year. He has spent every year since guarding what he learned and refusing to touch it again.
9.  **The Present Day — Barrens Blood:** Livestock deaths and a missing child in the Leeds Point district draw outside attention. The pattern is unmistakable to anyone who knows the family's true history: the attacks are following Leeds-owned land.
10. **The Present Day — Emma's Reckoning:** Emma Leeds, a Leeds descendant unaware of her heritage's terms, is nine months pregnant with her family's thirteenth child of this generation. Silas Mortimer's cult and the original Jersey Devil both know it before she does.

### The "Why"
This is a story about inherited sin and the myth that ambition is ever free. Daniel Leeds did not curse his family out of malice — he mortgaged them, the way an ambitious man mortgages a farm, believing he would be the one who found a way to pay it off before the note came due. Three hundred years later, no one has. The central tension of the campaign is not whether the Jersey Devil can be killed — it can be fought, wounded, even driven off — but whether a debt made in someone else's name, generations ago, can ever really be settled, or whether the Leeds line is obligated forever to keep paying for a bargain none of the living ever agreed to. Every faction in this campaign has its own answer: pay the debt gladly (the Devil Worshippers), refuse to pay and run (the unaware Bloodline), find a loophole in the contract (Reverend Crane, the Antiquarian Society), or absorb the cost onto someone else's shoulders one more time (Silas Mortimer, who wants the curse for himself). The players are not myth-busters here. They are the first people in three hundred years with enough distance from the Leeds name to actually read the fine print.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Leeds Bloodline
**Type:** Scattered Family Descendants
**Leader:** No formal leader; Emma Leeds is its most visible and most endangered living member.
**Goal:** To survive, protect their children, and go on believing — for as long as possible — that the family curse is just an old, ugly story.
**Agenda:** Publicly, they are ordinary Barrens families with an unlucky surname. Privately, the branches that remember the old stories quietly avoid having a thirteenth child, move away from Leeds Point, or change their names.
**Asset:** Scattered family Bibles, land deeds, and burial records that, pieced together, trace the true bloodline the cult and the Devil are both hunting.
**Rivals:** The Devil Worshippers, who hunt them; distrustful of the Antiquarian Society, which treats their tragedy as an exhibit.

### The Devil Worshippers
**Type:** Hereditary Doomsday Cult
**Leader:** Silas Mortimer, a charismatic distant Leeds cousin who has spent his adult life trying to engineer his own transformation.
**Goal:** To venerate the Jersey Devil as the true, undiluted heir of the pact and to multiply its kind until the Barrens belong to it outright.
**Agenda:** Publicly, they are landowners, furnace-town descendants, and respectable Barrens residents. Privately, they hunt Leeds descendants for sacrifice, manipulate the Curse Progression toward transformation, and protect the original Jersey Devil as a living god.
**Asset:** A hidden sanctuary deep in the Barrens, three generations of ritual knowledge, and informants scattered through Leeds Point, the County Watch, and the furnace ruins.
**Rivals:** The Leeds Bloodline, Saint Michael's Watch, the County Watch.

### Saint Michael's Watch
**Type:** Rural Congregation and Lay Historians
**Leader:** Reverend Josiah Crane, an elderly minister who has spent fifty years documenting the curse and refusing to act on what he knows.
**Goal:** To shelter the faithful, keep watch over the one true ritual component still in mortal hands, and prevent another family from being destroyed the way Crane's own was.
**Agenda:** Publicly, a small, aging congregation offering charity and comfort to a hard-luck district. Privately, Crane's inner circle holds the Saint's Relic needed to break the pact and has sworn never to use it again after what it cost him.
**Asset:** A fortified chapel, decades of research into the Leeds pact, and a network of parishioners who report strange happenings to Crane before anyone else hears of them.
**Rivals:** The Devil Worshippers; wary of the Antiquarian Society's interest in the Relic.

### The Barrens Folk
**Type:** Independent Rural Households
**Leader:** Hosea Pruitt, an elder storyteller who speaks for the district's oldest families at the general store and the crossroads.
**Goal:** To keep to the old ways, keep the Devil at arm's length, and be left alone by outsiders, cultists, and curious academics alike.
**Agenda:** Publicly, "we mind our business and respect the old ways." Privately, many households leave offerings of milk and salt at their property lines on the new moon, and some know a great deal more about the curse than they will say to a stranger.
**Asset:** Generations of survival knowledge, hidden trails, and folk countermeasures against the Devil — charms, wardings, and old warnings passed mother to daughter.
**Rivals:** Suspicious of the County Watch's authority; quietly afraid of the Devil Worshippers, whom some believe walk among them unrecognized.

### The County Watch
**Type:** Rural Law Enforcement
**Leader:** Sheriff Wyatt Corbin, a pragmatic lawman who has spent a career explaining away things he cannot explain.
**Goal:** To keep the peace, close the livestock-killing case with a rational explanation, and keep panic from spreading through the district.
**Agenda:** Publicly, the attacks are the work of poachers, feral dogs, or a bear. Privately, Corbin has seen enough over the years to know something worse is out there, and he is running out of ordinary explanations.
**Asset:** Legal authority, a small but loyal deputy roster, county resources, and jurisdiction over the roads in and out of the Barrens.
**Rivals:** The Devil Worshippers, who have at least one informant inside the department; frustrated by the Barrens Folk's silence.

### The Antiquarian Society of Leeds Point
**Type:** Colonial Records Society
**Leader:** Dr. Prudence Ashgrove, a historian who believes the Leeds pact document would make her career, whatever it costs the family that owns it.
**Goal:** To locate, authenticate, and publish the original Leeds pact — the "Original Pact" ritual component — regardless of who gets hurt in the process.
**Agenda:** Publicly, a respectable heritage society preserving colonial-era records and touring the old Leeds Manor ruins for paying visitors. Privately, Ashgrove has been quietly buying up Leeds family documents from descendants who don't know what they're worth.
**Asset:** Access to colonial archives, legal claims on several Leeds-era properties, and a reputation that opens doors the players may need opened.
**Rivals:** Saint Michael's Watch, which refuses her access to Crane's research; the Leeds Bloodline, who see her as a vulture.

### The Ironmasters' Remnant
**Type:** Ghost-Town Descendants
**Leader:** Abel Sconce, the last caretaker of his family's furnace ledgers, living alone among the ruins his ancestors built.
**Goal:** To keep the true, ugly history of the bog-iron furnace towns — built on debt-bonded and convict labor, bankrolled by Leeds pact money — from being forgotten or, worse, sanitized.
**Agenda:** Publicly, Sconce is a recluse squatting in a ghost town. Privately, his family's ledgers name every laborer worked to death at the furnace and every shilling of "Leeds luck" that paid for it — proof the pact's first debt was paid in more than one currency.
**Asset:** The Ironworks' original account books, unmatched knowledge of the ghost town's ruins, and a bitter grudge against the Leeds name that makes him a dangerous but valuable ally.
**Rivals:** The Antiquarian Society, who want his ledgers for their own glory; the Devil Worshippers, who have tried to buy his silence.

### The Cedar Water Circle
**Type:** Folk Protection Circle
**Leader:** Widow Patience Yoder, a hex-woman whose bloodline has warded Barrens households against the Devil for six generations.
**Goal:** To protect Barrens families — and especially pregnant Leeds women — from the curse with old, informal magic that predates and quietly resists Daniel Leeds's bargain.
**Agenda:** Publicly, a loose circle of midwives, herbalists, and "wise women" who help with births and hard winters. Privately, the Circle keeps its own pact-breaking lore, distinct from and distrustful of Reverend Crane's church version.
**Asset:** Protective folk rituals, deep knowledge of the cedar swamps, and the trust of Barrens mothers that no outsider institution has ever earned.
**Rivals:** Skeptical of Saint Michael's Watch; actively hunted by the Devil Worshippers, who see the Circle's wards as a threat to the pact.

### The Newspaper Row Skeptics
**Type:** Local Press and Cryptid Debunkers
**Leader:** Cora Vance, editor of the region's last surviving weekly paper, who built her reputation debunking "Jersey Devil" sightings.
**Goal:** To get the true story of the livestock killings into print before the County Watch's cover story or the cult's rumor mill does — and to prove, once and for all, that the Devil is a hoax.
**Agenda:** Publicly, rational, skeptical, a little smug about it. Privately, Vance is beginning to find evidence she cannot explain away, and it terrifies her more than she will admit in her own paper.
**Asset:** A wide network of tipsters, back issues going back a century that quietly document every prior "Devil" scare, and a printing press that can spread the truth fast once she believes it.
**Rivals:** The County Watch, who resent her digging; targeted by the Devil Worshippers as a threat to their secrecy.

### The Marked Kin
**Type:** Secret Support Network of the Devil-Touched
**Leader:** Ezra Leeds, hiding in plain sight while quietly gathering others like himself.
**Goal:** To find and protect Leeds descendants who already show minor devil traits before the cult, or their own families, discover what they are.
**Agenda:** Publicly, they don't exist — most of the Marked Kin pass as ordinary Barrens residents. Privately, they trade warnings, hiding places, and old family remedies to disguise their traits from everyone, including each other's kin.
**Asset:** A hidden network of safehouses and disguises, and members scattered quietly through every other faction's ranks, giving them eyes everywhere.
**Rivals:** Hunted by the Devil Worshippers, who want to "awaken" them; wary of the Leeds Bloodline's unaware majority, who might panic if they learned the truth.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **Emma Leeds** (Human Fighter) - Nine months pregnant, sharp-tongued, and used to solving her own problems with a shotgun and stubbornness. **Voice:** Clipped, dry, quick to cut off pity with a joke. **Goal:** To have a healthy baby and keep her family safe, curse or no curse. **Secret:** She has started to believe the stories, and in her worst moments has considered handing her child over to Silas Mortimer's cult just to make the fear stop.
2.  **Reverend Josiah Crane** (Human Cleric) - Elderly, soft-spoken, and carrying fifty years of guilt like a stone in his coat pocket. **Voice:** Slow, careful, the voice of a man who has learned that every word can cost something. **Goal:** To keep the Saint's Relic out of the wrong hands without ever having to touch the ritual himself again. **Secret:** His failed ritual attempt in the 1970s didn't just kill his family — it may have been what first drew Silas Mortimer's cult to Leeds Point.
3.  **Silas Mortimer** (Human Warlock) - Magnetic, patient, and utterly convinced that becoming a devil is a promotion, not a punishment. **Voice:** Warm and reasonable, like a man selling you something that's actually good for you. **Goal:** To engineer circumstances so that he — not Emma's child — becomes his generation's thirteenth transformation. **Secret:** He is a distant Leeds descendant himself, several times removed, and has spent twenty years quietly falsifying genealogies to make his own claim look stronger than Emma's.
4.  **Father Michael** (Human Cleric) - A guilt-ridden former priest who left the Church after a ritual he performed thirty years ago failed a different cursed child in a different county. **Voice:** Hoarse, apologetic, prone to trailing off mid-sentence when memory catches up with him. **Goal:** To atone by making sure this thirteenth child does not suffer the same fate as the one he couldn't save. **Secret:** He still carries a fragment of that earlier child's christening cloth, convinced — perhaps wrongly — that it holds some protective power he has never dared to test again.
5.  **Old Man Harker** (Human Ranger) - A wiry hermit who has lived alone in the deep Barrens for forty years and claims to have seen the Jersey Devil more times than anyone living. **Voice:** Gravelly, matter-of-fact, unbothered by things that would send other men running. **Goal:** To keep the secret paths through the Barrens out of the cult's hands. **Secret:** He was there the night Reverend Crane's ritual failed in the 1970s, and has never forgiven himself for not stopping it in time.
6.  **Sheriff Wyatt Corbin** (Human Fighter) - A tired, practical lawman who has spent twenty years writing "animal attack" on reports he doesn't believe. **Voice:** Flat, procedural, occasionally cracking under real strain. **Goal:** To close this case without anyone else's name going on a headstone. **Secret:** One of his own deputies is feeding information to the Devil Worshippers, and he doesn't yet know which one.
7.  **Cora Vance** (Human Bard) - The editor of the Leeds Point Ledger, sharp-eyed and famous locally for debunking Devil sightings in print. **Voice:** Fast, wry, performative even when she's scared. **Goal:** To break the true story before the cult or the County Watch can bury it. **Secret:** Her own great-grandmother was a Leeds descendant who changed her name to escape the family's luck; Cora doesn't know it yet.
8.  **Dr. Prudence Ashgrove** (Human Wizard) - A brisk, ambitious historian who sees the Leeds pact as the find of her career and the family's suffering as regrettable but secondary. **Voice:** Precise, academic, impatient with anyone who calls her cold. **Goal:** To locate and authenticate the Original Pact document before Reverend Crane or the cult destroys it. **Secret:** She has already found one page of the pact and has been sitting on it for a year, afraid that publishing it will point the cult straight at Emma Leeds.
9.  **Abel Sconce** (Human Rogue) - The last resident of the Ironworks ghost town, thin and watchful, living among ledgers no one else wants to read. **Voice:** Terse, bitter, occasionally lit up with dark humor about the ruins around him. **Goal:** To make sure someone, someday, knows what the furnace towns really cost. **Secret:** His own ancestor helped Daniel Leeds hide the furnace's worst debts, and Sconce has spent his life trying to make up for it.
10.  **Widow Patience Yoder** (Human Warlock) - A weathered hex-woman whose pact was made generations ago with something far smaller and stranger than Astaroth, purely to protect her own line. **Voice:** Low, sing-song, every sentence sounding faintly like a charm. **Goal:** To ward Emma Leeds and her child before the cult or the Devil can reach them. **Secret:** Her protective pact requires a toll of its own — a small, private grief she pays every few years and has never told anyone about.
11.  **Jedediah Sawney** (Human Ranger) - A moonshiner and trapper who knows every sunken causeway and hidden still in the cedar swamps. **Voice:** Slow, sly, always sounding like he's about to ask for a favor. **Goal:** To stay out of everyone's business and keep his stills running. **Secret:** He has sold information to the Devil Worshippers before, more than once, and is deeply afraid the party will find out.
12.  **Brother Amos Tull** (Human Barbarian) - The Devil Worshippers' enforcer, a massive former furnace laborer who took the cult's oath after losing everything to the mills. **Voice:** Booming, blunt, oddly gentle when not on cult business. **Goal:** To protect Silas Mortimer and the sanctuary at any cost. **Secret:** He privately doubts the cult's theology but has nowhere else left to belong.
13.  **Sister Ruth Calloway** (Human Sorcerer) - The cult's ritualist, a distant Leeds cousin whose blood carries faint devil-taint that flares when she casts. **Voice:** Breathless, intense, prone to finishing sentences other people started thinking. **Goal:** To perfect the ritual that will let Silas claim the thirteenth transformation instead of Emma's child. **Secret:** Her own magic is slowly consuming her from the inside, and she has quietly begun to hope the ritual fails before it kills her.
14.  **Zebulon Leeds** (Human Paladin) - Emma's estranged grandfather, once sworn to protect the family's secrets, now half-mad with the weight of decades of silence. **Voice:** Booming when lucid, muttering and fractured when not. **Goal:** To keep his oath to guard the family's darkest records, even from his own granddaughter. **Secret:** He knows exactly where Daniel Leeds's original pact is buried — and has been too afraid to go near it in thirty years.
15.  **Caleb Leeds** (Human Rogue) - Emma's younger brother, restless and resentful of a family name that has only ever cost him. **Voice:** Fast, defensive, quick to turn a conversation into an argument. **Goal:** To get out of Leeds Point for good, by any means necessary. **Secret:** Silas Mortimer has quietly promised him wealth and standing if he helps deliver Emma's child to the cult.
16.  **Agatha Winter** (Human Druid) - The district's only midwife, brisk and kind, who has delivered half of Leeds Point. **Voice:** Warm, no-nonsense, the voice of someone who has talked people through worse nights than this. **Goal:** To get Emma and her child through the birth alive, curse or no curse. **Secret:** She quietly reports every detail of Emma's pregnancy to Widow Patience Yoder, believing it is the only thing keeping the child safe.
17.  **Dr. Marcus Whitfield** (Human Wizard) - A university naturalist chasing the "Jersey Devil" as an undiscovered species, utterly certain a rational explanation exists. **Voice:** Excitable, lecturing, prone to citing his own unpublished papers. **Goal:** To capture physical proof of the creature and publish first. **Secret:** He has already seen the Devil up close once, and has been quietly unraveling ever since without telling his university colleagues why.
18.  **Ezra Leeds** (Tiefling Rogue) - A hidden Leeds descendant born with faint horn-buds and a forked shadow, who has spent his whole life hiding what he is. **Voice:** Guarded, flat, warming only when he trusts you. **Goal:** To disappear before either the cult or his own family finds out what his blood marks him for. **Secret:** He is genuinely unsure whether he fears becoming a devil or, on his worst nights, wants to.
19.  **Deputy Nathaniel Cobb** (Human Fighter) - A friendly, well-liked young deputy on Sheriff Corbin's roster. **Voice:** Easygoing, helpful, disarmingly ordinary. **Goal:** To feed the Devil Worshippers everything the County Watch learns, in exchange for a promised place among the faithful. **Secret:** He is Sheriff Corbin's informant leak, and has not yet worked out how far he is willing to go for the cult.
20.  **Hosea Pruitt** (Human Bard) - An elderly storyteller who speaks for the Barrens Folk at the crossroads store, keeper of a hundred half-true legends. **Voice:** Warm, theatrical, always circling the real point three stories before he gets to it. **Goal:** To keep the old protective customs alive in a generation that has stopped believing in them. **Secret:** One of his "half-true" legends is entirely true, and he has never told anyone which one.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Leeds Point
The colonial-era village where the Leeds family first put down roots, now a half-abandoned crossroads clinging to its own bad name.
*   **The Old Leeds Manor:** The collapsed shell of the family's original in-town house, its gardens long overgrown, still legally owned by three feuding Leeds cousins. **Smell:** Mildew, cold hearth ash, wet plaster. **Sound:** Loose shutters knocking in the wind, settling timbers.
*   **Saint Michael's Chapel:** A small, weathered stone church where Reverend Crane has preached for fifty years to a shrinking congregation. **Smell:** Beeswax candles, old hymnals, damp stone. **Sound:** A cracked bell, Crane's low, careful sermons.
*   **The Sawyer's Inn:** The district's only working lodging house, its taproom the real center of Leeds Point gossip. **Smell:** Woodsmoke, spilled ale, roasting venison. **Sound:** Clinking glasses, low arguments about livestock counts.
*   **Town Hall Archive:** A cramped records office holding two centuries of deeds, births, and burials — including whatever the Leeds family couldn't quite manage to hide. **Smell:** Dust, old paper, mouse droppings. **Sound:** Creaking floorboards, the scratch of Clerk Fenwick's pen.
*   **The Colonial Graveyard:** Rows of tilting headstones behind the chapel, with an unmarked, deliberately separate plot for Leeds family dead. **Smell:** Wet earth, cut grass, faint rot. **Sound:** Crows, wind through leaning stones.

### The Pine Barrens Wilderness
A vast, stunted forest of pine and oak, laced with bogs and hidden trails known only to those who never leave.
*   **The Twisted Oak:** A massive, centuries-old tree at the forest's heart, its roots said to mark the edge of the Devil's true hunting ground. **Smell:** Pine sap, wet bark, ozone before a storm. **Sound:** Groaning branches, an unnatural absence of birdsong.
*   **The Bog of Souls:** A treacherous wetland where the ground gives way without warning and old bones surface after heavy rain. **Smell:** Sulfur, stagnant water, decay. **Sound:** Bubbling mud, the suck of boots pulling free.
*   **Harker's Cabin:** A cluttered one-room hermitage strung with wind chimes made of bone and bottle glass. **Smell:** Woodsmoke, tallow candles, gun oil. **Sound:** Wind chimes, Harker muttering to himself over a whittling knife.
*   **The Boundary Stones:** A line of colonial-era survey markers used for generations to navigate the trackless interior. **Smell:** Cold granite, moss, pine needles. **Sound:** Wind funneling between the stones, distant wingbeats.
*   **The Ranger's Watchtower:** A County Watch fire-lookout tower repurposed to watch for something worse than fire. **Smell:** Rust, pine resin, coffee gone cold. **Sound:** Creaking metal stairs, a crackling radio.

### The Cedar Swamps
A lower, wetter stretch of the Barrens where cedar and cranberry bog crowd out the pines, and the old protective customs run deepest.
*   **The Cranberry Bogs:** Flooded harvest fields that turn a startling red each autumn, worked by families who leave the fields the moment the sun sets. **Smell:** Wet peat, ripe cranberries, standing water. **Sound:** Sloshing waders, distant harvest chatter cut short.
*   **Sawney Hollow:** A hidden clearing of moonshine stills and trapper shacks, reachable only by paths Jedediah Sawney refuses to draw on any map. **Smell:** Fermenting mash, woodsmoke, wet fur. **Sound:** Copper stills hissing, low laughter, a dog's warning bark.
*   **The Widow's Hearth:** A low cedar longhouse where the Cedar Water Circle gathers to ward births and bury their own dead in secret. **Smell:** Dried herbs, candle tallow, cedar smoke. **Sound:** Murmured chants, the creak of a rocking chair.
*   **The Sunken Causeway:** A half-drowned colonial roadbed that floods at high water, forcing travelers to time their crossings carefully. **Smell:** Brackish water, rotting timber. **Sound:** Lapping water, the groan of the ferry rope.
*   **The Drowned Chapel:** The flooded ruin of an even older church than Saint Michael's, its bell said to ring itself once a year. **Smell:** Stagnant water, wet stone, mildewed cloth. **Sound:** Dripping water, a single distant bell-note with no bell-ringer.

### The Ironworks Ghost Town
The abandoned remains of a bog-iron furnace town whose fortune, and whose forgotten dead, both trace back to Leeds money.
*   **The Furnace Ruins:** The collapsed brick stack of the old furnace itself, cold for over a century but never quite cold enough. **Smell:** Cold ash, rust, scorched earth. **Sound:** Wind moaning through the flue, settling brick.
*   **The Sconce Ledger House:** Abel Sconce's cluttered home, its walls lined floor to ceiling with his family's furnace account books. **Smell:** Old paper, lamp oil, dust. **Sound:** Turning pages, Sconce's low muttering as he cross-references names.
*   **The Slag Heaps:** Black glassy mounds of furnace waste that glint strangely by moonlight and are said to hum on the coldest nights. **Smell:** Mineral dust, faint sulfur. **Sound:** A high, faint ringing that isn't quite wind.
*   **The Collier's Row:** A line of collapsed workers' cottages, home to nothing now but weeds and the occasional squatter. **Smell:** Rotten wood, mildew, cold hearths. **Sound:** Creaking doors on broken hinges, settling roofs.
*   **The Company Store:** The only structure in the ghost town still standing whole, kept swept and stocked by Mercy Talbot out of pure stubbornness. **Smell:** Dry goods, kerosene, dust. **Sound:** A brass bell over the door, floorboards underfoot.

### The Devil's Reach
The deepest, most remote heart of the Barrens, where the original pact was struck and where the Jersey Devil still returns to lair.
*   **The Leeds Homestead:** The ruined shell of the original Leeds family house, the birthing room's collapsed roof still open to the sky after three centuries — the Devil's lair. **Smell:** Sulfur, old blood, wet char. **Sound:** Leathery wingbeats, a low, rhythmic growl that never quite stops.
*   **The Birth Room:** The one chamber of the Homestead still partly intact, where Mother Leeds gave birth and where the Original Pact document is said to be hidden beneath the floor. **Smell:** Iron, dust, faint sulfur. **Sound:** A whisper of wind that sounds, if you listen wrong, like a woman screaming.
*   **The Cult's Sanctuary:** A hidden chapel built from salvaged furnace brick and pine timber, where Silas Mortimer's congregation conducts its rites. **Smell:** Incense, tallow smoke, old blood. **Sound:** Low chanting, the crackle of ritual fire.
*   **The Blood Altar Clearing:** A ring of standing stones outside the Sanctuary where the cult performs its offerings under the new moon. **Smell:** Wet stone, ash, copper. **Sound:** Wind through the standing stones, a distant, answering howl.
*   **The Whispering Hollow:** A low depression in the pines where sound carries strangely and cult initiates claim to hear the Devil's voice offering bargains of its own. **Smell:** Damp pine needles, ozone, decay. **Sound:** Overlapping whispers with no clear source.

## Mechanics
[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]
### The Thirteenth Child: Curse Progression
The pact's due bill does not wait patiently. The DM tracks **Curse Progression** on a 10-point scale, starting at 0 when the party learns of Emma Leeds's pregnancy.
*   Progression rises by 1 for every session in which the party fails to protect Emma, fails to slow the cult, or allows a plot deadline to lapse; it rises by 2 after any direct cult attack on Emma that the party did not prevent.
*   At 5 points, Emma's unborn child begins visibly manifesting minor devil traits, detectable by magic or careful examination.
*   At 10 points, the child is born fully transformed unless the party has completed the curse-breaking ritual first. A second Jersey Devil now exists, and every faction's plans change overnight.
*   Progression can be reduced by 1 (minimum 0) whenever the party gathers a ritual component, secures a safehouse for Emma, or wins the trust of a new faction ally.

### Dread: The Barrens' Weight
The Pine Barrens themselves are hostile to outsiders, and the longer the party stays, the more it shows.
*   Each in-game day spent within the Barrens (outside Leeds Point proper) without a full rest in a warded location adds 1 point of **Dread**, tracked per character, to a maximum of 5.
*   At 2 Dread, a character has disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made at night. At 4 Dread, they have disadvantage on saving throws against being frightened. At 5 Dread, they must succeed on a Wisdom save at the start of each day or gain one level of exhaustion.
*   Dread is reduced by 2 for a full night's rest at a warded location (the Sawyer's Inn, Saint Michael's Chapel, or the Widow's Hearth) and reset to 0 entirely upon leaving the Barrens for a full week.

### Barrens Navigation
The Pine Barrens are disorienting by design, and getting lost here is rarely just an inconvenience.
*   The DM calls for a Wisdom (Survival) check whenever the party travels off a known trail. Failure doesn't simply mean lost time — it can mean stumbling into cult territory, into the Jersey Devil's hunting range, or onto something better left undiscovered.
*   Barrens Folk and Cedar Water Circle guides can be hired to reduce the DC of these checks significantly, but not every guide who offers can be trusted.
