## Portrait subjects (style-neutral — the locked style block is added in the Muse doc)

- **The Tracker** (`the-tracker`): Lean, weathered human woodsman in a patched oilcloth coat and wide-brimmed hat, longbow over one shoulder, a sprig of cedar tucked in the hatband, sharp wary eyes scanning the trees, crouched beside a split hoofprint in the sand, lantern light from below, twisted pitch pines behind
- **The Circuit Preacher** (`the-circuit-preacher`): Broad-shouldered human preacher in a dusty black frock coat over scale mail, a heavy hand bell hanging from the belt, holy symbol on a leather cord, kind tired eyes under a flat-crowned hat, mace in one hand, a lantern glowing at his side, a small white clapboard chapel in the dark pines behind him
- **The Almanac-Keeper** (`the-almanac-keeper`): Slight human scholar in a worn wool waistcoat with ink-stained cuffs, round spectacles, a thick leather almanac held open in one hand, the other hand raised with a faint ember glow of a spell at the fingertips, star charts and printer's type on a shelf behind, candlelight from below
- **The Moonshiner** (`the-moonshiner`): Stocky halfling bootlegger in a patched canvas jacket and battered felt hat, a clay jug on a cord over one shoulder, hand crossbow at the hip, sly grin and sharp eyes, standing on a mossy log in a dark cedar swamp, a small ember-red lantern glowing beside the boots
- **The Furnace-Born** (`the-furnace-born`): Towering half-orc ironworker in scarred chain mail under a leather smith's apron, battleaxe resting on one shoulder, shield slung on the back, soot on the forehead, a lump of black slag hanging from a cord at the throat, steady determined eyes, the ember-red glow of a ruined furnace behind

## Why this party

- Five core roles: scout (Ranger), healer (Cleric), arcane (Wizard), sneak (Rogue), front line (Fighter).
- Every hook starts from the bible's Session 1 (Barrens Blood: livestock killings on Leeds land) and Session 2 (The Missing Child).
- Each character pulls toward a different thread: the Barrens Folk (Tracker, Moonshiner), Saint Michael's Watch and Emma Leeds (Circuit Preacher), the Leeds Bloodline and the Antiquarian Society (Almanac-Keeper), the Ironmasters' Remnant (Furnace-Born).
