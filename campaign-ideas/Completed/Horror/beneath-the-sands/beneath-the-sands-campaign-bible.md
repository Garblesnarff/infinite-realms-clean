# Beneath the Sands: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The Earth is not a floor; it's a mouth. In the sun-baked, dust-covered valley of Ashenvale (reimagined as a desert), the 'Sovereigns' (frontier settlers) realized that the 'Great Accord' of the mission was a lie. They were not invited to the land; they were being farmed. These 'Sandwyrms' are not animals; they are an evolved predatory immune system, segmented serpentine monsters that have lived beneath the dunes since the first star fell. For eons, they slept, their bodies becoming the tectonic plates of the desert. But the 'Silver Strike'—a mining operation that dug too deep—has awakened them. The wyrms don't just hunt for meat; they hunt for 'Vibration.' Every step is a heartbeat, and every heartbeat is a dinner bell. To survive in Dusthaven is to be a ghost on the ground, knowing that the only safe place is the sky.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silver Strike (c. 1880):** A massive silver vein is discovered in the valley. The first 'Mining Shafts' are sunk into the dunes.
2.  **The First Ripple (1885):** A series of 'unexplained disappearances' among the miners is officially classified as 'Desert Fever.'
3.  **The Construction of Dusthaven (1890):** The town is established, built on wooden walkways and elevated platforms to avoid the 'Bad Ground.'
4.  **The Nest Awakening (10 Years Ago):** A mining blast triggers a massive seismic event. The first 'Sandwyrm Siege' occurs, leaving half the town in ruins.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A new prospector (the player) arrives in Dusthaven, only to witness a Sandwyrm erupt from the main street.
6.  **The Evolving Predators (Ongoing):** Slayers identify new wyrm types: 'Stalkers' (with legs), 'Spitters' (acid sacs), and 'Behemoths' (armored hide).
7.  **The Water Tower Standpoint (Recent):** Sheriff Kord leads a desperate defense of the town's water supply against a multi-wyrm assault.
8.  **The Descent into the Mine (Upcoming):** Doc Mira identifies the location of the 'Master Nest.' The Guild of the Hunt leads an expedition into the narrow tunnels.
9.  **The Present Day:** The valley is a high-contrast nightmare of sandy yellows and blood red. Every step is a gamble, and the only prayer is for a strong wind to cover your tracks.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Human Ingenuity' and 'Nature's Ruthlessness.' It is about the 'Primal Fear' of the unseen predator—the realization that the ground you stand on is your greatest enemy. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can humanity find a way to coexist with a land that wants to swallow them whole?' It explores the gritty realism of the weathered wood, the visceral horror of the tooth-filled maw, and the wry humor of a grizzled survivor who has seen too many neighbors vanish into the sand. The players are the Dusthaven residents and Guild specialists who must use improvised weapons, makeshift defenses, and clever tactics to ensure that the town survives the night.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Dusthaven Guardians (The Slayers)
**Type:** Elite Frontier Defense Force
**Leader:** Sheriff Kord (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** shotgun badges, knowledge of vibration patterns, and the 'Strike the Earth' (inverted) intuition.
**Rivals:** The Sandwyrms, The Mining Syndicate.

### The Sandwyrms (The Predators)
**Type:** Evolving/Serpentine Super-Entities
**Leader:** The Master Wyrm (A city-sized mountain of teeth).
**Asset:** Natural camouflage, seismic senses, and the ability to evolve legs or armor.
**Rivals:** Humanity.

### The Mining Syndicate (The Disruptors)
**Type:** High-Tech/Greed-Based Corporate Agency
**Leader:** Director Vane (A man who views the wyrms as 'Biological Assets').
**Asset:** Heavy machinery, silver mining rights, and a total disregard for the town's safety.
**Rivals:** The Dusthaven Guardians.

### The 'Mira' Medical Unit (The Healers)
**Type:** Scientific/Compassionate Research Group
**Leader:** Doc Mira (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of wyrm biology, improvised medicine, and a connection to the 'Vibration' frequency.
**Rivals:** None (they are the lifeline).

### The 'Ghost' Prospectors
**Type:** Independent/Lost Human Survivors
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Knowledge of the deepest dunes and a willingness to live in total silence.
**Rivals:** The Sandwyrms.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Sheriff Kord** (Guardian - Grizzled Leader) - A dwarven sheriff in dusty leather gear. **Voice:** Gravelly, weathered, and filled with a Western drawl. **Goal:** To protect Dusthaven and kill every wyrm in the valley. **Secret:** He is secretly an Adept of 'Seismic Sensing' who can 'feel' a ripple in the sand from a mile away.
**Doc Mira** (Independent - Elven Healer) - A woman in practical robes with a medical satchel. **Voice:** Soft, melodic, and filled with gentle determination. **Goal:** To find a way to 'Pacify' the wyrms and save the town. **Secret:** she is the only person who has entered a wyrm nest and returned with her soul intact.
**Director Vane (Syndicate)** (Syndicate - Corporate Villain) - A man in a perfectly pressed suit who views the desert as an engineering problem. **Voice:** Measured, cultured, and supremely confident. **Goal:** To harvest wyrm eggs for military research and secure the silver vein. **Secret:** He is the one who triggered the 'Silicon Surge' that awakened the wyrms.
**The Prospector (Rust)** (Independent - Philosophical Survivor) - A man who has lived in the dunes for twenty years. **Voice:** Wry, cynical, and philosophical. **Goal:** To witness the end of the world with dignity. **Secret:** He has discovered a 'Vibration-Free' material that makes a person invisible to the wyrms.
**The Master Wyrm (Echo)** (Predator - Mythic Leader) - A being of ancient teeth and segmented stone. **Voice:** None (subsonic rumbles). **Goal:** To reclaim the valley from the 'Vibrating Parasites' (humans). **Secret:** The wyrms are not hunting for food; they are protecting a 'Reality-Anchor' hidden in the mine.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Dusthaven (Frontier Town)
A small, wooden monument to resourcefulness and elevated planks.
*   **The Main Street:** A site of 'Seismic Tension' and harsh midday sunlight. **Smell:** Dust, stale whiskey, and old wood. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'creak-creak' of elevated walkways.
*   **The Water Tower:** Where the final standoff occurred. **Visual:** A single, rust-colored landmark against an endless sky.

### The Silver Mine (The Nest)
A dark, narrow labyrinth of wooden support beams and organic chambers.
*   **The Master Chamber:** A humid, chemical-smelling room where the wyrm eggs are kept. **Visual:** Rows of segmented monsters glowing with internal energy. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of generators.
*   **The Gravity Well:** A portal of shifting light where the party 'falls up.' **Feeling:** A sickening sense of weightlessness.

### The Endless Dunes (The Battlefield)
A site of 'Heat Shimmer' and primal fear.
*   **The Salt Flats:** A flat, white-bleached expanse where there is nowhere to hide. **Visual:** Blue-white light reflecting off the cracked earth. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of an approaching wyrm.

### The Mission Church (Sanctuary)
A humble white and brown monument to spiritual silence and asylum status.
*   **The Bell Tower:** Where the 'Oracle' sits to watch the sand. **Visual:** A single harsh spotlight in the center of the dark.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Ripple:** Arrive in Dusthaven as a new prospector. You must survive your first wyrm attack while maintaining your 'Silence' level.
2.  **The Water Tower Defense:** The wyrms are attacking the town's water supply. You must defend the tower using improvised explosives and your 'Seismic Sensing.'
3.  **The Syndicate Infiltration:** Infiltrate the Mining Syndicate lab to find evidence of the wyrm egg harvesting. You must bypass Director Vane's guards.
4.  **The Evolving Trap:** A new wyrm type (Stalker) has been spotted. You must capture a 'Live Specimen' for Doc Mira without being eaten.
5.  **The Descent into the Mine:** Lead an expedition into the deepest part of the silver mine to destroy the Master Nest. You must navigate the narrow tunnels without making a sound.
6.  **The Cultist's Gamble:** The 'Ghost' prospectors have been sabotaging the town's defenses. You must find their leader and convince them to join the Hunt.
7.  **The Final Bloodletting at the Source:** Face the Master Wyrm face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you strike the earth or reclaim the sand?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Sheriff's Shotgun
**Type:** Legendary Weapon
**Description:** A custom-made shotgun with a badge engraved on the handle.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and allows them to perform the 'Seismic Blast' (once per combat, deals 10d10 radiant damage).

### 2. The Wyrm-Scale Vest
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Armor)
**Description:** A leather vest reinforced with the segmented chitin of a behemoth wyrm.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer +5 to AC and resistance to all non-magical weapon damage. The vest provides advantage on Stealth checks in the sand.

### 3. The Vibration-Free Boots
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A pair of sturdy boots made from a specialized material found in the mine.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to move across any surface without making a sound or a ripple. The user can 'Freeze' a seismic sensor for 1 round.

## [TAG: ENEMY_STATBLOCK]
### 1. Sandwyrm (Serpentine)
(CR 3). Monstrosity. HP 45, AC 14.
**Abilities:**
*   **Seismic Tracking:** The wyrm has advantage on attacks if the target moved at least 10 feet this turn.
*   **Eruption Strike:** Melee attack. 2d8+4 damage + Target is grappled (DC 14 DEX save).

### 2. Wyrm Stalker (Evolved)
(CR 5). Monstrosity. HP 80, AC 15.
**Abilities:**
*   **Natural Camouflage:** Any enemy within 30 feet has disadvantage on Perception checks to see the stalker.
*   **Acid Spit:** Ranged attack. 2d10 acid damage in a 15ft cone.

### 3. 'The Master' Behemoth (Proxy)
(CR 12). Monstrosity (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Primal Fear Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be paralyzed by the sound of the wyrm's rumble.
*   **The Final Ripple:** Once per combat, the behemoth can force all creatures within 100 feet to re-roll their initiative.
