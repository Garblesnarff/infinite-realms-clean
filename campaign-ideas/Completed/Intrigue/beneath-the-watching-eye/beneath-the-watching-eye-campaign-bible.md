# Beneath the Watching Eye: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is a machine that has forgotten its purpose. In the beginning, there was the 'Grand Plan,' a vision of absolute social harmony and industrial perfection. The 'Architects of the State' realized that individuality was the only flaw in the equation. They forged the 'Watching Eye'—a global network of telescreens and cameras designed to eliminate privacy and standardize the soul. Legend says the 'First Leader' (Big Brother) realized that if you control the present, you control the past, and if you control the past, you control the future. Aethelgard isn't just a city; it's a monumental concrete altar to the deity of Conformity. Here, love is a thoughtcrime, truth is a shifting broadcast, and the only prayer is the daily 'Two Minutes Hate.'

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon Purge (c. 1950s):** The Great Revolution replaces national governments with the State. The first brutalist cities are constructed.
2.  **The Construction of Aethelgard (1960):** The capital city is completed, a masterpiece of monumental concrete and industrial uniformity.
3.  **The Erasure of Memory (1975):** The State completes the 'Ministry of Truth' and begins the systematic rewriting of history.
4.  **The Silicon Shift (1984):** The telescreen network becomes mandatory in all homes. The concept of 'Privacy' is officially deleted from the language.
5.  **The Arrival of the Functionary (Present Day - Day 1):** An Outer Party member (the player) begins their day at the Ministry of Truth, carefully censoring their own thoughts.
6.  **The Proles Anomaly (Day 3):** A visit to the Proles district reveals a piece of forbidden pre-Party art—a flower made of silk.
7.  **The Forbidden Relationship (Ongoing):** The functionary begins a secret affair with a fellow Party member, creating a 'Shadow-Memory' of their own life.
8.  **The Thought Police Raid (Upcoming):** The 'Watching Eye' detects a deviation in the functionary's biometrics. The hunt begins.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of concrete grays and propaganda reds. Big Brother is watching, and every thought is a crime.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Authenticity' in a world of 'Gray Conformity.' It is about the 'Psychological Horror' of having every thought monitored—the realization that the only freedom left is inside your own mind. The 'Why' is the question: 'Is it better to live in a comfortable lie or die for a dangerous truth?' It explores the oppressive scale of brutalist architecture, the sickly green glow of the telescreen, and the bravery of maintaining a forbidden hope in a hopeless world. The players are the Party functionaries and Prole rebels who must navigate the monotone landscape to find a truth that hasn't been rewritten yet.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Party (The Inner Circle)
**Type:** Totalitarian Theocratic Superpower
**Leader:** Big Brother (Tier 1 NPC - figurehead).
**Asset:** The Watching Eye (surveillance), the Thought Police, and total control of the narrative.
**Rivals:** The Brotherhood (the resistance), The Anomaly Species (Proles).

### The Brotherhood (The Resistance)
**Type:** Secret/Underground Rebel Network
**Leader:** Emmanuel Goldstein (A man of whispered truth).
**Asset:** Knowledge of pre-Party history, illegal printing presses, and a network of 'Mind-Vaults.'
**Rivals:** The Thought Police.

### The Outer Party (The Drones)
**Type:** Middle-Class/Monitored Workers
**Leader:** Various Department Heads.
**Asset:** Technical skills, access to state data (for rewriting), and a carefully neutral expression.
**Rivals:** None (they are the primary victims).

### The Proles (The Ignored)
**Type:** Working-Class/Underclass Survivors
**Leader:** None formally.
**Asset:** Numbers, relative freedom from constant surveillance, and a residual sense of humanity.
**Rivals:** None (they are seen as 'Animals' by the Party).

### The Architects of Truth
**Type:** Scientific/Bureaucratic Propaganda Branch
**Leader:** Minister of Truth (O'Brien - Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** The ability to delete people and events from existence, both physically and historically.
**Rivals:** The Memory Seekers.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Winston** (Outer Party - The Functionary) - A man in worn blue overalls with a gaunt face. **Voice:** Monotone, precise, and carefully neutral. **Goal:** To find a piece of truth that cannot be rewritten. **Secret:** He has started a secret diary where he records his 'unapproved' memories.
**O'Brien** (Inner Party - The Inquisitor) - A powerful man in a black uniform with a practiced warmth. **Voice:** Authoritative, cultured, and terrifyingly reasonable. **Goal:** To identify and 're-educate' all thought-criminals. **Secret:** He is already part of the Brotherhood, or at least he wants you to believe that.
**Julia** (Outer Party - The Rebel) - A bold woman who works in the Fiction Department. **Voice:** Sarcastic, energetic, and filled with a hidden joy. **Goal:** To experience pleasure and rebellion in a world of gray conformity. **Secret:** She doesn't care about the truth; she only cares about the moment.
**Big Brother** (The Eye - Mythic Leader) - The face on every telescreen and propaganda poster. **Voice:** A monotone, perfect broadcast. **Goal:** Absolute control of the human soul. **Secret:** He doesn't exist as a human being; he is a symbolic construct of the State.
**The Prole Woman** (Prole - The Witness) - A woman who sings pre-Party work songs in the slums. **Voice:** Strong, organic, and filled with a forbidden beauty. **Goal:** To live her life and remember the songs. **Secret:** She is the only person in Aethelgard who truly understands the meaning of the word 'Love.'

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Ministry of Truth (Aethelgard)
A massive, brutalist concrete pyramid that looms over the city.
*   **The Records Room:** A labyrinth of gray cubicles and pneumatic tubes. **Smell:** Ink, stale air, and machine oil. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'whoosh' of documents being deleted.
*   **The Interrogation Wing (Room 101):** A clinical white space where 'Truth' is adjusted through pain. **Visual:** A single harsh spotlight in the center of the dark.

### The Proles District (The Dregs)
A chaotic, crumbling maze of laundry lines and improvised repairs.
*   **The Antique Shop:** A dark, nostalgic sanctuary where pre-Party items are hidden. **Smell:** Dust, old paper, and a faint hint of lavender. **Sound:** The rhythmic ticking of a clock that still has a second hand.
*   **The Courtyard:** Where the Proles sing and the Party's influence is thinnest.

### The Victory Mansions
A forest of identical brutalist apartment blocks under an overcast sky.
*   **The Living Room:** A place where the telescreen is omnipresent. **Visual:** Sickly green light reflecting in the character's carefully neutral eyes. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the Party's broadcast.

### The Chestnut Tree Café
A place of 'Desaturated Joy' where the broken functionaries gather.
*   **The Booth:** A site of 'False Confessions' and clinical whites. **Visual:** A single, broken mirror on the wall.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Censorship Assignment:** Arrive at the Ministry of Truth. You must 'Adjust' a series of historical records to fit the Party's latest proclamation without leaving a trace.
2.  **The Proles Incursion:** Visit the slums to investigate a report of 'Forbidden Songs.' You must find the source of the music without alerting the Thought Police.
3.  **The Secret Diary:** Someone has left an empty notebook in your cubicle. You must find a way to take it home and write your first 'unapproved' thought.
4.  **The Forbidden Affair:** Meet Julia in a secret room. You must navigate the 'Shadow-Memory' of your relationship while your biometrics are being monitored by the telescreen.
5.  **The Brotherhood Signal:** Receive a message from Emmanuel Goldstein. You must retrieve a 'Truth-Seed' from a hidden memory-vault in the Ministry.
6.  **The Siege of the Senses:** You are captured by O'Brien. You must survive the 'Re-education' process in Room 101 while maintaining your own internal melody.
7.  **The Final Betrayal:** Face Big Brother face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you love him or die for the individual?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Glass Paperweight
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A heavy, glass dome containing a piece of coral from the pre-Party era.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Stabilize' their own memories, making them immune to the effects of the Ministry of Truth or any mental rewriting for 1 hour.

### 2. The Brotherhood Encryption Code
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, paper-based code that hums with the key of the resistance.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'See' the truth behind any state broadcast. Once per day, the user can 'Silence' a nearby telescreen for 1 hour.

### 3. Big Brother's Eye
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Cursed)
**Description:** A small, silver lens that mimics the gaze of the state.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user advantage on all Intelligence (History) and Wisdom (Insight) checks. **Curse:** The user begins to see the world in 'Concrete Grays' and loses the ability to feel any emotion other than 'Party Loyalty.'
