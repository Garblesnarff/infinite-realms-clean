**The Impossible Vault**

## 1. Core Concept & Narrative Hook
*   **Directive:** This is the foundational context. All generated content must align with this core premise.
*   **Content:** Darian Ashcombe recruits the Eleventh to help rob the Grand Celestial Casino's Vault of Stars during the Tournament of Eclipses. The crew believes the job is gold. The true prize is the Anchor Key, needed to reverse Lord Midas Goldweaver's Eclipse Draw and free four hundred people he banked into chips during the Silence of the Slots — including Lyra Nightingale's little sister, Posy. Both Midas and Darian are dying of the same luck from opposite ends of the same theft, and neither has said so aloud. As the eclipse nears, the Eleventh must decide whether to cash out the banked souls, keep the Key, or find some dimmer, honester third way for Glimmerfall.

## 2. Lore & History Primer
*   **Directive:** The Lore Generation Agent must create detailed entries for each of the following prompts. These entries will form the historical and cultural bedrock of the world.
*   **Prompts:**
    *   Detail the Probability Anchor: the scar left where the Goddess of Fortune and the God of Entropy dueled, and how their mingled blood became Chance-Matter, the liquid luck that powers Glimmerfall.
    *   Write the Goldweaver Ascension — how the Goldweaver family learned to refine raw Chance-Matter into stable fuel, turning a mining camp into a city of streetlights that never flicker.
    *   Describe the Ashcombe Reckoning — how reformer Edric Ashcombe was framed for "metaphysical embezzlement" by a tip from Quillon Marsh, and how his son Darian grew up being told his father was a thief.
    *   Detail the Silence of the Slots — the hour ten years ago when every machine in the city stopped and four hundred people vanished, and the truth known to perhaps three people alive: Lord Midas Goldweaver banked them as living chips in the Vault of Stars.
    *   Explain the Weeping Ace — a card, mid-tear, that has never stopped appearing since the Silence, understood by those who study it as four hundred people trying to knock on a door they no longer have hands for.
    *   Write about the Tournament Decree — how Lord Goldweaver's Tournament of Eclipses is, in truth, a very expensive way of keeping four hundred thousand eyes on the arena and none of them on the vault beneath it.

## 3. Faction Deep-Dive
*   **Directive:** The Faction Generation Agent must create a detailed profile for each faction listed below. Each profile must contain the specified fields.
*   **Factions Roster:**
    *   **The Casino Security Consortium** (Major)
        *   **Goals:** Protect the Casino's assets and maintain its reputation as impenetrable.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Corporate paramilitary order under Commander Vex.
        *   **Public Agenda:** We keep every patron and every asset in this house safe.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** Vex has quietly doubled patrols on a sub-basement corridor nobody assigned him, chasing a truth about the Silence of the Slots he was never told.
        *   **Assets:** The Panopticon Engine, a crystal eye that sees five seconds into the future.
        *   **Relationships:** Rivals with the Underworld Alliance and the Society of the Folded Hand.
    *   **The Underworld Alliance** (Major)
        *   **Goals:** Control Glimmerfall's criminal enterprises without starting a war it can't win.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Criminal syndicate and merchant guild under Madame Fortuna.
        *   **Public Agenda:** We provide what the house won't.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** Fortuna has loved Commander Vex for eleven years and has told him precisely never, for both their sakes.
        *   **Assets:** The Sewers of Whispers, a tunnel network that carries every secret spoken near a grate.
        *   **Relationships:** Rivals with the Casino Security Consortium and the Accountants of Zero.
    *   **The Specialists' Guild** (Minor)
        *   **Goals:** Keep freelance underworld talent employed and the Guild profitable.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Unionized freelance underworld under broker Quillon Marsh.
        *   **Public Agenda:** We are independent professionals for hire, at a fee that always feels fair until the job is finished.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** Quillon sold out Edric Ashcombe twenty years ago for less than he'd charge for a lockpick today, and has never told a soul.
        *   **Assets:** A ledger that can locate any freelance talent in the city within the hour.
        *   **Relationships:** Undercut by unlicensed amateurs; neutral toward most other factions.
    *   **The Accountants of Zero** (Minor)
        *   **Goals:** Balance every unearned fortune in Glimmerfall back to zero.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Mathematical zealots under Auditor Null.
        *   **Public Agenda:** Every jackpot is a theft from someone who will never know they were owed the difference.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** Null keeps one lucky coin in his own robes that he has never turned in to his own ledgers.
        *   **Assets:** The Void-Ledgers, which erase a name's fortune from the world's ledger of luck.
        *   **Relationships:** Opposed to everyone who has ever profited from chance.
    *   **The Neon Saints** (Minor)
        *   **Goals:** Protect and control the city's bioluminescent light grid.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Aesthetic paladin order under Lumen Halcyra.
        *   **Public Agenda:** We keep the city's light honest.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** Lumen took her lighting oath the year the Slots went silent and has always privately suspected the two events are one event.
        *   **Assets:** Control of the light grid, able to dim Glimmerfall to a heartbeat of glow or burn it blinding.
        *   **Relationships:** Rivals with the Velvet Cord.
    *   **The Velvet Cord** (Minor)
        *   **Goals:** Preserve absolute social immunity for its members inside the Casino.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Elitist secret society under the masked Corwin Ashcombe.
        *   **Public Agenda:** A gathering of the city's most refined company.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** Corwin is Darian's estranged older brother, sided with Midas the year their father was framed, and still believes he chose the winning side.
        *   **Assets:** House guards bound by old favors not to see a Cord member commit so much as a faux pas.
        *   **Relationships:** Preyed on by the Specialists' Guild, who make a living stealing Cord invitations.
    *   **The Second-Second** (Minor)
        *   **Goals:** Keep chronomancer time-cheats running for those who can afford them.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Chronomancer cheaters' circle under Hourglass Harry.
        *   **Public Agenda:** A discreet edge for discreet clients.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** Harry has rewound the same two seconds of his life four hundred times and never once used it to fix what he actually regrets.
        *   **Assets:** Watches that rewind the wearer exactly two seconds.
        *   **Relationships:** Hated by every pit boss in the city.
    *   **The Society of the Folded Hand** (Minor)
        *   **Goals:** Give shape and grief to the city's losses.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Rogue philosophers of chance, a standing council of three masked members who never interrupt.
        *   **Public Agenda:** Chance deserves philosophy, not just odds.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** They fold a Weeping Ace into the chest of every golem they build and won't say why, except that it "feels correct."
        *   **Assets:** Paper golems built from losing lottery tickets.
        *   **Relationships:** Considered a public nuisance by the Casino Security Consortium.
    *   **The Chip Widows** (Minor, Potential Allies)
        *   **Goals:** Keep the memory of the four hundred alive.
        *   **Hierarchy:** No formal leader; the loudest griever in a given week tends to be listened to.
        *   **Public Agenda:** We remember every name, in order, whether anyone is listening or not.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** They leave a folded Weeping Ace at every Casino door each anniversary, and have never once missed a year.
        *   **Assets:** The largest living memory of the Silence of the Slots left in Glimmerfall.
        *   **Relationships:** Opposed to the Eclipse Committee.
    *   **The Eclipse Committee** (Major, Midas's Creatures)
        *   **Goals:** Keep the entire city's attention fixed on the Tournament arena.
        *   **Hierarchy:** Unnamed and plural; no two people agree they've met the same member twice.
        *   **Public Agenda:** We run the fairest tournament in Glimmerfall's history.
        *   **Secret Agenda:** Every match is rigged, and the tournament exists to keep every eye off the vault humming beneath it.
        *   **Assets:** The Tournament of Eclipses itself.
        *   **Relationships:** Hostile to anyone who asks, even once, who actually owns the betting lines.

## 5. Location Blueprints
*   **Directive:** The Environment Generation Agent must create a detailed blueprint for each location below, populating all specified sub-sections.
*   **Location Roster:**
    *   **The Grand Celestial Casino:** The target of the heist, built over the Probability Anchor.
        *   **Key Landmarks:** The Gilded Lobby, the Gaming Floor, the VIP Floating Isles, the Tournament Arena, the Vault of Stars.
        *   **Primary Inhabitants:** Wealthy patrons, casino staff, security personnel, tournament competitors, the Eclipse Committee.
        *   **Available Goods & Services:** Gambling, luxury accommodations, fine dining, fortune-telling, chip cashing.
        *   **Potential Random Encounters (x5):** A pit boss eyes the crew for card counting; the Panopticon's crystal eye sweeps the section; a Weeping Ace turns up in someone's hand; security escorts a banned cheat off the floor; a jackpot alarm draws a crowd and two guards.
        *   **Embedded Plot Hooks & Rumors (x3):** "The Vault has never been breached." "Old Ivory rigs every hand so he never has to lose one." "Midas hasn't been seen without his gloves in years."
        *   **Sensory Details:** Sight (crystal chandeliers, gold-bright fountains, neon-algae glow), Sound (chips clicking like a countdown, a thousand small bells never quite in tune), Smell (ozone, orange blossom, cigar smoke).
    *   **The Vault of Stars:** The legendary vault beneath the Casino, ending in the Core.
        *   **Key Landmarks:** The Airlock, the Hall of Mirrors, the Gravity Trap, the Ward Lattice, the Core.
        *   **Primary Inhabitants:** Vault Corridor Guards, automated wards, four hundred banked chips.
        *   **Available Goods & Services:** None — strictly forbidden access.
        *   **Potential Random Encounters (x5):** A pressure door cycles unexpectedly; the Ward Lattice chimes a warning; a trick of gravity drops loose gear; a reflection in the Hall of Mirrors moves wrong; a whispered lullaby drifts from the Core.
        *   **Embedded Plot Hooks & Rumors (x3):** "One of the four hundred is still, faintly, aware." "The Vault was designed by someone who now works for the house." "The Core hums a different lullaby for each chip."
        *   **Sensory Details:** Sight (a spiral of warm gold chips), Sound (four hundred voices, none louder than a breath), Smell (warm gold and ozone).
    *   **The Lantern Warehouse:** The crew's hideout in Glimmerfall Streets.
        *   **Key Landmarks:** The planning room, the practice vault mockup, the equipment cache.
        *   **Primary Inhabitants:** The crew.
        *   **Available Goods & Services:** Planning resources, training space, Luna's carriage in the yard.
        *   **Potential Random Encounters (x5):** A specialist reveals a hidden talent; a rehearsal reveals a flaw in the plan; someone's past catches up to them; a disagreement threatens crew cohesion; Luna's carriage engine mutters to itself for no reason anyone can explain.
        *   **Embedded Plot Hooks & Rumors (x3):** "This warehouse was chosen because nobody looks twice at a dark building in a city built on light." "Someone in the crew is being watched." "Zara has never once explained why she won't touch open flame."
        *   **Sensory Details:** Sight (blueprint-covered walls), Sound (creaking rafters), Smell (old rope and lantern oil).

## 6. Causality Chains & Dynamic World States
*   **Directive:** The World Simulation Agent must implement the following trigger-based state changes. For each "IF" condition, the agent must pre-generate the narrative and environmental consequences for the "THEN" outcome.
*   **Triggers:**

* IF Heat reaches 10 THEN the Casino enters full lockdown, the exits seal, and Commander Vex responds to the vault corridor in person.
* IF Lyra's Loyalty reaches 5 THEN she sides fully with the crew at the Double-Cross and reveals Midas's countdown to the Eclipse Draw.
* IF Lyra's Loyalty falls to 0 THEN she stalls the crew at the Double-Cross to protect Posy's chip, even at the cost of the plan.
* IF the Eclipse Clock fills to 12 segments THEN the Eclipse Draw begins at the Tournament Arena, whether or not the crew is ready.
* IF a PC receives the Weeping Ace THEN they gain a clue toward the Core, and the Casino's suspicion, Heat, rises by 1.
* IF the crew cashes out all four hundred banked chips THEN the Vault's Chance-Matter reserves collapse, the Casino's lights and the city's luck go dark, and Glimmerfall must learn to live without the house's fortune.
