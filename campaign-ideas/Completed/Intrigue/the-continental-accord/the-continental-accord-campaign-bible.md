# The Continental Accord: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is a machine that runs on 'Consequence.' In the shadows of every major city, there is a hidden society of professional killers who operate by a code as ancient as the blade itself. Legend says the first 'Manager' realized that chaos is bad for business, and that even murderers need a place to call home. They forged the 'Continental Accord'—a treaty that established neutral sanctuaries where no blood can be spilled. The 'High Table' was formed to govern the underworld through a lens of 'Noir Elegance' and 'Sophisticated Power.' In this world, a gold coin is more than currency; it's a social contract. To be an assassin is to be an artist of the 'Deadly Ballet,' knowing that every action you take is a debt that will eventually be paid in full.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon Treaty (c. -500 years):** The High Table is established to regulate the disparate assassin guilds of the world.
2.  **The Founding of the New York Continental (1920):** The first modern sanctuary is opened, a masterpiece of art deco elegance and marble.
3.  **The Great Excommunicado (1975):** A legendary assassin breaks the Accord by killing a High Table member on Continental grounds. The world witnesses the first 'Underworld Purge.'
4.  **The Rise of the Baba Yaga (10 Years Ago):** John Wick (Equivalent - The Ghost) completes an 'Impossible Task' to buy his way out of the life.
5.  **The Arrival of the Rookie (Present Day - Day 1):** A newly minted assassin (the player) checks into the Continental Hotel for their first official 'Job.'
6.  **The Seduction at the Gala (Day 3):** An undercover operation to retrieve a 'Marker' from a rival boss. You meet the enigmatic Concierge.
7.  **The Shadow Breach (Ongoing):** Evidence surfaces that a High Table member is selling 'Excommunicado' lists to the highest bidder.
8.  **The Continental Siege (Upcoming):** The High Table declares the New York Continental 'Deconsecrated.' The sanctuary is no longer safe.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of deep blacks and rich golds. The rules are being broken, and every coin is a countdown to the final confrontation.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Professional Honor' and 'Survival.' It is about the 'Deadly Grace' of being an elite operative—the realization that your reputation is your only true shield. The 'Why' is the question: 'What is the value of a code in a world that rewards betrayal?' It explores the opulent environments of the luxury hotel, the gritty reality of the training hall, and the balletic violence of the professional kill. The players are the assassins who must navigate the subtext of a polite conversation and the intensity of a hall of mirrors ambush to find a truth that doesn't belong to the High Table.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Continental Network (The Sanctuaries)
**Type:** International Neutral Sanctuary System
**Leader:** Winston (Equivalent - The Manager).
**Asset:** The Continental hotels, gold coins (currency), and the 'Code of Neutrality.'
**Rivals:** T.H.R.U.S.H. (Equivalent - The Anarchists), The Law.

### The High Table (The Sovereigns)
**Type:** Global/Totalitarian Underworld Governing Body
**Leader:** The Elder (A man of ancient, desert-dwelling authority).
**Asset:** Infinite resources, the 'Adjudicators,' and the power to declare anyone 'Excommunicado.'
**Rivals:** The Continental (when interests clash).

### The Bowery (The Information Brokers)
**Type:** Street-Level/Gritty Network
**Leader:** The Bowery King (A man of pigeons and street-smart truth).
**Asset:** Knowledge of every secret passage in the city, an army of 'Invisibles,' and a total disregard for the High Table.
**Rivals:** The High Table.

### The Marker Seekers (The Obsessive)
**Type:** Elite/Debt-Based Assassin Guild
**Leader:** Various.
**Asset:** Blood Markers (unbreakable debts) and a commitment to the 'Deadly Ballet.'
**Rivals:** Each Other.

### The Adjudicators (The Inquisitors)
**Type:** High-Tier/Emotionless Enforcers
**Leader:** The High Adjudicator.
**Asset:** Absolute authority from the High Table and a refusal to be swayed by reputation or gold.
**Rivals:** Anyone who breaks the rules.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**The Concierge (Charon)** (Continental - Professional Calm) - A man in a perfectly tailored black suit with a neutral accent. **Voice:** Smooth, controlled, and precisely measured. **Goal:** To provide the agents with the 'Perfect Sanctuary' and maintain the order of the house. **Secret:** He is a high-level Adept of 'Deadly Grace' who can 'disarm' an opponent with a single look.
**The Manager (Winston)** (Continental - The Strategist) - A man of art deco elegance and dark-wood sanctuaries. **Voice:** Authoritative, sophisticated, and British. **Goal:** To protect the Continental from the High Table's overreach. **Secret:** He has been building a secret 'Mirror Hall' beneath the hotel to survive the next purge.
**The Bowery King** (Independent - Street Leader) - A man of pigeons, rags, and absolute truth. **Voice:** Gruff, theatrical, and filled with street-smart wisdom. **Goal:** To overthrow the High Table and give the 'Invisibles' their due. **Secret:** He was once a High Table candidate who was 'erased' from history.
**The Adjudicator** (High Table - Cold Inquisitor) - A woman in a black uniform with a reflective face shield. **Voice:** Sterile, precise, and emotionless. **Goal:** To adjust the underworld to fit the High Table's narrative. **Secret:** She is secretly working for the Bowery King to expose the Table's internal rot.
**The Rival (Cassian)** (Independent - Star Assassin) - A man of honor and balletic violence. **Voice:** Determined, professional, and respectful. **Goal:** To fulfill his Blood Marker and retire with dignity. **Secret:** He views the player character as his only true peer and brother.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Continental Hotel (Sanctuary)
An art deco masterpiece of black marble floors, gold columns, and warm chandelier light.
*   **The Lobby:** A site of 'Polite Formality' and high-society business. **Smell:** Bourbon, expensive tobacco, and old stone. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Weapon Hangar (The Sommelier's):** A site of 'Tactical Elegance' and organized chaos. **Visual:** Rows of gold-plated firearms and silk armor.

### The High Table Vault (Rome)
A royal gold and deep crimson monument to ancient power and sophisticated menace.
*   **The Hall of Markers:** A room filled with blood-stained medals and golden maps. **Visual:** Prismatic light reflecting in the blood of the first debt. **Sound:** The deep, rhythmic thrum of the High Table's broadcast.

### The Bowery (The Underworld)
A chaotic, crumbling maze of tunnels, rooftop pigeon lofts, and secret passages.
*   **The Pigeon Loft:** Where the Bowery King sits to watch his city. **Smell:** Feathers, dry wood, and rain. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'coo-coo' of 1,000 birds.

### The Mirror Hall (Ambush Site)
A site of 'Visual Paranoia' and reality-bending combat.
*   **The Glass Maze:** A place where reflections show different dimensions and anyone could be an enemy. **Visual:** Neon blues and pinks reflecting in infinite mirrors.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **Checking In:** Arrive at the Continental for the first time. You must check in with the Concierge and receive your first gold coin.
2.  **The Marker Retrieval:** Infiltrate a High Table gala to retrieve a Blood Marker stolen by a rival boss. You must navigate the 'Hall of Mirrors' ambush.
3.  **The Sommelier's Tasting:** Meet the Sommelier to select your mission equipment. You must complete a 'Safety Demonstration' that involves disarming three guards.
4.  **The Bowery Signal:** Receive a message from the Bowery King. You must retrieve a 'Truth-Seed' from a hidden vault in the High Table's Rome HQ.
5.  **Excommunicado:** You are declared 'Excommunicado' by the High Table. You must escape the city while being hunted by every assassin in the Accord.
6.  **The Siege of the Continental:** Lead a defense of the hotel against a High Table 'Adjustment' squad. You must protect Winston while the self-destruct activates.
7.  **The Final Consequence:** Face the Elder face-to-face in a final, reality-bending standoff. The truth is revealed—will you serve the Table or die for the Code?

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The High Table Gold Coin
**Type:** Artifact (Currency)
**Description:** A heavy, gold coin with the High Table seal.
**Mechanics:** Grants the holder absolute authority in the underworld. Once per day, the user can 'Buy' a 1-hour truce from any assassin.

### 2. The Continental Suit (3-Piece)
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Armor)
**Description:** An immaculate, black suit with subtle armor plating worked into the design.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer +5 to AC and resistance to all non-magical weapon damage. The suit never gets wrinkled or dirty.

### 3. The Blood Marker
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Contract)
**Description:** A circular, gold medal that holds a single, dried drop of blood.
**Mechanics:** When 'Opened,' the user can force an NPC to succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be compelled to perform an 'Impossible Task.'

## [TAG: ENEMY_STATBLOCK]
### 1. Professional Assassin (Standard)
(CR 3). Humanoid. HP 45, AC 15.
**Abilities:**
*   **Balletic Violence:** The assassin has advantage on attacks if they have moved at least 30 feet this turn.
*   **Silenced Precision:** Ranged attack. 2d10+4 damage.

### 2. High Table Adjudicator (Elite)
(CR 8). Humanoid. HP 120, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Sovereign Authority Aura:** Any enemy within 30 feet has disadvantage on Charisma checks.
*   **Rule of Law:** Once per combat, the adjudicator can force an enemy to take a reaction that reveals their own darkest secret.

### 3. 'The Ghost' (Elite Leader)
(CR 15). Humanoid (Baba Yaga). HP 250, AC 20.
**Abilities:**
*   **Impossible Task Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be paralyzed by the Ghost's reputation.
*   **Deadly Grace:** The Ghost has three reactions per round and can never have disadvantage on an attack.
