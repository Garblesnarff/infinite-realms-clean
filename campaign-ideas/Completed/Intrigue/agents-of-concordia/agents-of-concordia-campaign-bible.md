# Agents of Concordia: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World is a dance floor where the music is about to stop. In the heat of the Cold War, the 'Grand Accord' realized that traditional warfare would only lead to silence. They forged 'Concordia'—a secret, international agency where the finest minds from both sides of the Iron Curtain work together to prevent global annihilation. But Concordia isn't just about diplomacy; it's about 'Style-Fi.' Legend says the first 'Agent' realized that a perfectly tailored suit is more effective than a tank, and that a well-timed quip can disarm a bomb. The 'Aethel' of this world is the 'Glamour'—a metaphysical force that turns 1960s European elegance into a tactical advantage. To be an Agent of Concordia is to be a ghost in a world of luxury, protecting the peace while looking incredibly cool.

### Timeline
1.  **The Silicon Accord (1955):** The US and USSR secretly agree to form Concordia to manage 'Existential Threats' without public knowledge.
2.  **The Rome Convergence (1960):** The first major operation where American and Russian agents were forced to cooperate against a common enemy.
3.  **The Berlin Wall Construction (1961):** Concordia establishes a 'Neutral Zone' within the wall to facilitate secret exchanges.
4.  **The Monte Carlo Incident (1963):** A luxury cruise ship is 'accidentally' saved from a nuclear hijack by a pair of bickering agents.
5.  **The Forced Team-Up (Present Day - Day 1):** Napoleon Solo (American) and Illya Kuryakin (Russian) are both after the same target in Rome. They are ambushed and forced to cooperate.
6.  **The Fake Marriage (Week 1):** Solo and Kuryakin must pose as a married couple to infiltrate a high-society gala in Berlin.
7.  **The Gaby Discovery (Ongoing):** Gaby, a German mechanic with warmth and efficiency, is revealed as the key to a 'Retro-Futuristic' magic-tech device.
8.  **The Final Choice (Upcoming):** The partners must choose between their respective nations and each other as a global threat is revealed.
9.  **The Present Day:** The world is a high-contrast nightmare of Mediterranean golds and Cold War grays. Concordia is under siege, and the partners must decide when 'Fake' becomes 'Real.'

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'National Loyalty' and 'Human Connection.' It is about the 'Stylish Cold War' of the human heart—the realization that the person you were told to hate is the only one who can save you. The 'Why' is the question: 'Can two enemies become a partnership that saves the world?' It explores the bright colors of 1960s European glamour, the intellectual precision of the Russian mind, and the charming smoothness of the American ego. The players are the Agents of Concordia who must navigate the bickering and the banter to find a truth that transcends borders.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### Concordia (The Accord)
**Type:** International Secret Intelligence Agency
**Leader:** Waverly (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** The Hub headquarters, retro-futuristic magic-tech, and the power of the 'Style-Fi' Glamour.
**Rivals:** T.H.R.U.S.H. (Equivalent - The Anarchists), The Hardline Nationalists.

### T.H.R.U.S.H. (The Disruptors)
**Type:** Global/Villainous Anarchist Syndicate
**Leader:** Victoria (The Italian Temptress).
**Asset:** Infinite wealth, stolen magic-tech, and a network of high-society infiltrators.
**Rivals:** Concordia.

### The Berlin Propaganda Unit
**Type:** Cold War/Psychological Warfare Branch
**Leader:** Various.
**Asset:** Control of the city's narrative and the ability to turn citizens into 'Sleepers' using bright propaganda colors.
**Rivals:** The Berlin Underground.

### The Monte Carlo Merchant Guild
**Type:** Neutral/Luxury Trade Syndicate
**Leader:** The Casino Master.
**Asset:** Control of the world's most exclusive gambling tables and a network of information brokers.
**Rivals:** T.H.R.U.S.H. (who they view as 'unreliable').

### The 'Gaby' Mechanics (The Specialists)
**Type:** Scientific/Efficiency Research Group
**Leader:** Gaby (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of the latest magic-tech and the ability to fix anything with a wrench and a smile.
**Rivals:** The T.H.R.U.S.H. Engineers.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Napoleon Solo** (Concordia - Charming Lead) - A smooth-talking American agent in a perfectly tailored suit. **Voice:** Charming, smooth, and perfectly paced. **Goal:** To solve the mission and look good doing it. **Secret:** He is a high-level Adept of 'Glamour' who can 'persuade' inanimate objects to function.
**Illya Kuryakin** (Concordia - Intellectual Lead) - A Russian agent with controlled intensity and a turtleneck. **Voice:** Russian-accented, precise, and intellectual. **Goal:** To achieve the mission objective with absolute efficiency. **Secret:** He suffers from sudden bouts of 'Red Rage' when his intellectual limits are pushed.
**Waverly** (Concordia - British Authority) - The dry-witted leader of Concordia. **Voice:** Authoritative, dry, and British. **Goal:** To keep the Americans and Russians from killing each other long enough to save the world. **Secret:** He was the original 'Agent of Concordia' and knows the location of the first magic-tech device.
**Gaby** (Independent - German Efficiency) - A warm, efficient mechanic caught in the crossfire. **Voice:** German-accented, warm, and direct. **Goal:** To find her father and use her technical skills for peace. **Secret:** She is the only person who can 'sync' with the latest Concordia magic-tech.
**Victoria** (T.H.R.U.S.H. - Italian Temptress) - A dangerous, elegant woman in high-society golds. **Voice:** Seductive, intelligent, and world-weary. **Goal:** To destroy Concordia and rule the world through T.H.R.U.S.H. **Secret:** she is Napoleon Solo's former rival and the only person who can out-charming him.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Hub (Concordia HQ)
A sleek, retro-futuristic fortress of art deco elegance and magic-tech.
*   **Waverly's Office:** A dark-wood sanctuary with a view of the world's 'Tension Lines.' **Smell:** Pipe tobacco and old maps. **Sound:** The rhythmic scratching of a silver quill.
*   **The Gadget Workshop:** A site of 'Retro-Futuristic' magic-tech and organized chaos. **Visual:** Neon news tickers and professional smiles.

### Rome (The Convergence)
A warm, Mediterranean landscape of terracotta, azure, and gold.
*   **The Piazza:** A site of 'Forced Team-Ups' and high-speed car chases. **Smell:** Espresso and expensive perfume. **Sound:** Upbeat jazz and the roar of a 1960s engine.
*   **The Rooftop Standoff:** A place where Solo and Kuryakin first realize they're on the same side.

### Berlin (The Divided City)
A cold, industrial nightmare of grays divided by bright propaganda colors.
*   **The Checkpoint:** A site of 'Fake Marriages' and psychological warfare. **Visual:** Flickering neon signs and the shadows of ghosts. **Feeling:** A heavy, silent pressure on the mind.
*   **The Underground Lab:** Where Gaby's father is being held.

### Monte Carlo Casino
A royal gold and casino red monument to luxury and champagne bubbles.
*   **The VIP Tables:** Where the final game of 'Style-Fi' is played. **Sound:** The rhythmic clinking of chips and the husky laugh of Victoria.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Rome Ambush:** Solo and Kuryakin are both after the same target. You must force them to cooperate to survive a T.H.R.U.S.H. hit squad.
2.  **The Berlin Gala:** Solo and Kuryakin must pose as a married couple to infiltrate a high-society party. You must navigate the 'Fake Marriage' chemistry without blowing your cover.
3.  **The Gaby Rescue:** Retrieve Gaby from her mechanic shop before T.H.R.U.S.H. kidnaps her. You must use your 'Style-Fi' Glamour to win her trust.
4.  **The Car Chase:** Lead a fleet of T.H.R.U.S.H. vehicles on a high-speed chase through the streets of Rome. You must cast 'Style-Fi' spells while driving at 100 mph.
5.  **The Monte Carlo Infiltration:** Board a luxury cruise ship undercover. You must identify Victoria's connection to the nuclear threat.
6.  **The Final Choice:** Solo and Kuryakin are ordered to kill each other by their respective nations. You must decide whether to follow orders or join them in a 'Third Path.'
7.  **The Kiss at Dawn:** Reach the T.H.R.U.S.H. volcano fortress. The truth is revealed through 'Polite Destruction' and mutual respect as the world is saved.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. Napoleon Solo's Cufflinks
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Jewelry)
**Description:** A pair of silver cufflinks with a subtle draconic pattern.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer +5 to all Charisma (Persuasion and Performance) checks and allows them to 'Charm' any NPC of lower rank.

### 2. Illya Kuryakin's Radio
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A small, high-tech radio that hums with the key of the Accord.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'See' any communication within 1 mile. Once per day, the user can 'Lock' a target's magic-tech for 1 hour.

### 3. Gaby's Wrench of Efficiency
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A simple, worn wrench that seems to hum with the power of harmony.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Fix' any mechanical or magic-tech device instantly. Once per day, the user can create a 30ft 'Harmony Field' that calms all enemies.

(CR 12). Humanoid (Mastermind). HP 180, AC 18.

*   **Style-Fi Majesty Aura:** Any creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be paralyzed by the weight of Victoria's arrogance.
*   **The Final Choice:** Once per combat, Victoria can force an enemy to choose between saving an ally and achieving the mission objective.
