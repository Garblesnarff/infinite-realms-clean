# Wildspace Corsairs: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The stars aren't cold, distant balls of gas; they are the beacons of the gods, lighting the paths between worlds. Long ago, the first 'Spelljammers'—powerful wizards who could harmonize their will with the cosmic flow—realized that the 'Airless Void' was actually a medium that could be sailed. They built wooden ships that could hold their own atmosphere and gravity, allowing them to travel between the 'Crystal Spheres' that enclose each solar system. Beyond the spheres lies the 'Phlogiston'—a swirling, rainbow-colored sea of pure potential where time has no meaning. Wildspace is the frontier, the border between the known and the infinite. To sail it is to claim the ultimate freedom; to get lost in it is to become a star yourself.

### Timeline
1.  **The First Voyage (-2,000 years):** A nameless wizard on a primitive wooden raft becomes the first to cross the atmosphere and enter Wildspace.
2.  **The Age of Discovery (-1,500 years):** The Great Schools of Magic fund the construction of the first 'Galleons' and 'Hammerships.' The first Crystal Spheres are mapped.
3.  **The Neogi Incursions (-800 years):** Evil eel-creatures from a dark sphere begin a campaign of slave-trading across Wildspace, forcing the first naval alliances.
4.  **The Founding of the Rock of Bral (-500 years):** A massive asteroid is carved into a city-state, becoming the neutral hub for all Wildspace travel.
5.  **The Great Armada War (-200 years):** A conflict between the Elven Imperial Navy and the Scourge Pirates that nearly shattered three Crystal Spheres.
6.  **The Discovery of the Spelljammer (50 years ago):** Rumors of a city-sized ship shaped like a manta ray surface, drawing every corsair and explorer to the outer reaches.
7.  **The Giff Mercenary Boom (20 years ago):** Hippo-people from a gunpowder-rich sphere arrive in force, forever changing the nature of space combat.
8.  **The Phlogiston Surge (1 year ago):** The rainbow sea begins to flow faster, opening new routes to previously unreachable spheres.
9.  **The Present Day:** The Wildspace frontier is more crowded and dangerous than ever. New worlds are being discovered daily, and the corsairs are leading the charge.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle for 'Freedom' in a cosmos that is being increasingly regulated by ancient empires and evil syndicates. It is about the romance of exploration—the thrill of seeing a new planet on the horizon and the camaraderie of a crew that has survived the airless void together. The 'Why' is the question: 'In a universe of infinite variety, what is worth fighting for?' It explores the contrast between the warmth of a wooden deck and the cold majesty of the stars. The players are the ones who refuse to be bound by gravity or law, seeking their fortune in the infinite frontier.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Elven Imperial Navy (The Regulators)
**Type:** Feudal Cosmic Military
**Leader:** Admiral Sun-Sail.
**Asset:** Elegant 'Butterfly' ships, superior magical navigation, and a sense of absolute racial superiority.
**Rivals:** Neogi Slavers, The Scourge Pirates.

### The Rock of Bral Merchant Guild
**Type:** Neutral Trade Syndicate
**Leader:** Prince Andru (The 'Shadow Mayor' of the asteroid).
**Asset:** Control of the most important port in Wildspace, infinite gold, and a network of information brokers.
**Rivals:** The Elven Navy (who want to tax them).

### The Neogi Slavers (The Monsters)
**Type:** Evil Alien Slaver Group
**Leader:** The Great Old Master.
**Asset:** 'Nautiloid' ships (organic shells), mind-control abilities, and an army of Umber Hulk servants.
**Rivals:** Everyone (but especially the Giff).

### The Scourge Pirates (The Outlaws)
**Type:** Loose Confederation of Raiders
**Leader:** Captain Black-Sails (A human with a mechanical eye).
**Asset:** Heavily modified 'Squid' ships, improvised weapons, and a total disregard for the Crystal Spheres.
**Rivals:** The Elven Navy, Merchant Guild.

### The Giff Mercenary Corps
**Type:** Professional Military-for-Hire
**Leader:** General Boomboom (A Giff obsessed with gunpowder).
**Asset:** Massive muskets, explosive experts, and 'Hammerships' designed for pure ramming power.
**Rivals:** Neogi Slavers (who they view as 'unsporting').

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Captain Valiant** (Human - Corsair/Leader) - An enthusiastic naval captain with a mechanical arm. **Voice:** Energetic, booming, and filled with wonder. **Goal:** To find the legendary 'Spelljammer' manta-ship. **Secret:** He was once a high-ranking officer in the Elven Navy before he 'saw too much.'
**General Boomboom** (Giff - Mercenary/Heavy) - A hippo-person in a red Victorian military jacket. **Voice:** Gruff, raspy, and obsessed with ballistics. **Goal:** To have the largest explosion in the history of Wildspace named after him. **Secret:** He is a master of delicate clockwork repairs, which he does in secret.
**Sylas** (Plasmoid - Navigator/Expert) - A translucent blue ooze in a sailor's coat. **Voice:** Multitonal, bubbling, and calm. **Goal:** To map the 'Void Spots' where no star shines. **Secret:** He can 'consume' memories from anyone he touches, making him the perfect spy.
**Zorp** (Hadozee - Rigger/Acrobat) - A flying squirrel-person who hangs from the rigging. **Voice:** High-pitched, frantic, and street-smart. **Goal:** To buy a planet for his family. **Secret:** He is a direct descendant of the first Spelljammer pilot.
**The Merchant Prince (Andru)** (Independent - Political Boss) - The ruler of the Rock of Bral. **Voice:** Smooth, manipulative, and patronizingly kind. **Goal:** To keep the Rock neutral and profitable. **Secret:** He is secretly funding the pirates to keep the Elven Navy occupied.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Rock of Bral
A massive, gothic city carved into a hollowed-out asteroid.
*   **The High Docks:** A forest of wooden masts and glowing sails floating in zero gravity. **Smell:** Tar, sea salt, and exotic spices. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'creak-groan' of ships.
*   **The Low Market:** A chaotic maze of shops carved into the rock. **Visual:** Neon lanterns and alien creatures from 100 spheres.

### The Elven Sun-Palace
A beautiful, crystalline fortress floating near a star.
*   **The Hall of Sails:** A room made of living wood and golden energy. **Visual:** Giant butterfly-ships being 'grown' from star-seeds.
*   **The Observatory:** A transparent dome with a view of the entire solar system.

### The Neogi Home-Sphere
A dark, industrial nightmare enclosed in an impenetrable shell.
*   **The Slave Pits:** A humid, clicking labyrinth where thousands are kept in chains. **Sound:** The constant 'click-hiss' of neogi communication.
*   **The Great Shell:** The interior of the crystal sphere, covered in spider-web cities.

### The Phlogiston Sea
The swirling, rainbow-colored void between the spheres.
*   **The Rainbow River:** A high-speed current of pure potential. **Visual:** Swirling colors that change based on your mood. **Danger:** The Phlogiston is highly flammable.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Launch:** Secure a Spelljammer helm and install it into a standard wooden galleon. Survive your first ascent through the atmosphere.
2.  **The Rock of Bral Heist:** Retrieve a 'Sphere Map' from the Merchant Prince's private vault without alerting the Elven Navy patrols.
3.  **The Neogi Trap:** You are captured by a neogi slaver ship. You must organize a prison break among the Giff and Hadozee prisoners.
4.  **The Battle of the Void:** Defend a merchant convoy from a massive fleet of Scourge Pirates in the airless void between planets.
5.  **The Star-Seed Mystery:** Travel to a fire-world to retrieve a 'Star-Seed'—the only item that can repair a damaged crystal sphere.
6.  **The Hunt for the Spelljammer:** Track the legendary manta-ship through the Phlogiston. Discover the secret of its origin.
7.  **The Cosmic Standoff:** A three-way battle at the center of the universe between the Elven Navy, the Neogi, and the Pirates for control of the Spelljammer.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Crown of the First Voyager
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Helmet)
**Description:** A simple, wooden crown that glows with a faint, blue light.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer the ability to 'feel' the location of the nearest Crystal Sphere and provides resistance to cold damage.

### 2. General Boomboom's Big One
**Type:** Artifact (Explosive)
**Description:** A massive, brass-bound bomb that hums with gunpowder energy.
**Mechanics:** When detonated, it deals 20d10 fire/force damage in a 100ft radius and creates a temporary 'thin spot' in reality.

### 3. The Sun-Sail Cloak
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A cloak made of translucent, golden energy that pulses like a star.
**Mechanics:** The wearer can use an action to gain a fly speed of 60ft for 1 hour. While flying, they project a 10ft aura of sunlight.
