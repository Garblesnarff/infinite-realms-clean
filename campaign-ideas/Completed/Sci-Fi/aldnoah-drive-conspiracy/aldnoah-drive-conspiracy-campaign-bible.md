# Aldnoah Drive Conspiracy: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The moon didn't just break; it shattered the human ego. In 1972, the Apollo 17 mission discovered a hypergate on the lunar surface leading to Mars. The explorers found the 'Aldnoah'—an ancient, god-like energy source left behind by a long-dead Martian civilization. Those who stayed on Mars claimed the Aldnoah as their birthright, forming the Vers Empire and declaring themselves superior to the 'Terrans' of Earth. The Aldnoah isn't just power; it's a social contract. It only activates for those with the 'Royal Gene,' creating a feudal system where technology is the ultimate divider of class. The war isn't just for territory; it's a battle to prove whether mass-produced human ingenuity can defeat divinely-granted superweapons.

### Timeline
1.  **The Discovery (1972):** The hypergate is found on the moon. The Vers Empire is established shortly after on Mars.
2.  **Heaven's Fall (1999):** A war between Earth and Mars results in the destruction of the hypergate. The moon shatters, raining debris onto Earth and killing billions.
3.  **The Fifteen-Year Truce (1999-2014):** A period of desperate rebuilding for Earth and isolation for Mars. Earth develops mass-produced Kataphrakts (KG-7s).
4.  **The Princess's Arrival (2014):** Princess Asseylum of Vers travels to Earth on a peace mission. She is 'assassinated' by a Martian conspiracy to restart the war.
5.  **The Second Invasion (Present Day):** The Martian Orbital Knights launch a full-scale assault, using their unique Aldnoah-powered Kataphrakts to crush Earth's defenses.
6.  **The Battle of Shinawara (Recent):** Inaho Kaizuka, a high school cadet, uses basic physics and tactical analysis to defeat a 'superior' Martian Kataphrakt.
7.  **The Rise of the Bat (Recent):** Slaine Troyard, an Earth-born servant to the Martians, begins his descent into obsession and power-seeking.
8.  **The Aldnoah Drive Conspiracy (Upcoming):** Rumors of a 'Master Drive' that can deactivate all Aldnoah tech surface, drawing the attention of both sides.
9.  **The Present Day:** Earth is a patchwork of rubble and resistance. Mars is internally divided. The war has become a stalemate of tactical precision.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the clash between 'Divine Right' and 'Human Necessity.' It explores the dehumanizing nature of war and the weight of being a 'Genius' in a world of attrition. The 'Why' is the question: 'Does power come from the tools you are given, or the way you use them?' It examines the class divide—the pristine, golden arrogance of Mars versus the dirty, desperate functionality of Earth. The players are the ones who must navigate the tactical chessboard, knowing that one wrong move means being crushed by a superweapon they can barely comprehend.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The United Earth Forces (UEF)
**Type:** Mass-Produced Military Resistance
**Leader:** Captain Magbaredge (A woman of cold command).
**Asset:** Numbers, mass-produced KG-7 Kataphrakts, and tactical ingenuity.
**Rivals:** The Vers Empire, The Martian Orbital Knights.

### The Vers Empire (The Royalists)
**Type:** Feudal Martian Superstate
**Leader:** Empress Asseylum (Tier 1 NPC - figurehead).
**Asset:** The Aldnoah Drive, ornate Kataphrakts, and absolute technological superiority.
**Rivals:** The United Earth Forces, The Rebellious Knights.

### The Martian Orbital Knights
**Type:** Independent Warlords/Feudal Lords
**Leader:** Count Cruhteo and Count Saazbaum.
**Asset:** Unique, one-of-a-kind Kataphrakts with god-like abilities (e.g., molecular shields, dimensional cutting).
**Rivals:** Each other, The United Earth Forces.

### The Terran Resistance (The Slum Army)
**Type:** Insurgent Human Survivors
**Leader:** Unknown.
**Asset:** Improvised weapons, knowledge of the rubble, and a complete lack of fear.
**Rivals:** Anyone with Aldnoah tech.

### The Aldnoah Seekers
**Type:** Secret Research/Conspiracy Group
**Leader:** Slaine Troyard (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Knowledge of ancient Martian tech and a willingness to betray both worlds for their goal.
**Rivals:** The Vers Royal Family.

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Inaho Kaizuka** (UEF - Tactical Genius) - An Earth cadet with dead, emotionless eyes. **Voice:** Deadpan, analytical, and clipped. **Goal:** To calculate the most efficient path to victory. **Secret:** He sees the world as a series of physics equations and has almost no capacity for empathy.
**Princess Asseylum** (Vers - Royal Idealist) - The platinum blonde heir to the Martian throne. **Voice:** Kind, elegant, and filled with a tragic hope. **Goal:** To bring peace between Earth and Mars. **Secret:** She is the primary key to the Aldnoah Drive and her death would deactivate all Martian tech.
**Slaine Troyard** (Vers/Independent - Obsessive Survivor) - An Earth-born servant who has risen to power in the Martian military. **Voice:** Frantic, desperate, and increasingly cold. **Goal:** To protect Asseylum, even if he has to destroy the world to do it. **Secret:** He was the one who 'rescued' the Princess after her assassination attempt.
**Count Saazbaum** (Vers - Vengeful Knight) - A Martian lord who hates both Earth and the Martian royalty. **Voice:** Booming, arrogant, and filled with a deep-seated rage. **Goal:** To destroy the Vers royal family and Earth simultaneously. **Secret:** He blames the royal family for his father's death during Heaven's Fall.
**Rayet Areash** (UEF - Vengeful Survivor) - A Martian citizen living on Earth who hates Martian knights. **Voice:** Intense, bitter, and prone to sudden violence. **Goal:** To kill every Martian knight in existence. **Secret:** Her father was one of the conspirators who tried to kill Asseylum.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Shinawara District
A gray, rubble-strewn urban battlefield on Earth.
*   **The School Bunker:** A makeshift military base in an old high school. **Smell:** Dust, stale air, and machine oil. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'clank-clank' of KG-7s patrolling.
*   **The Rubble Fields:** A labyrinth of collapsed skyscrapers and rusted cars. **Visual:** Ash falling like snow.

### The Vers Landing Castle
An ornate, golden fortress that descended from orbit.
*   **The Throne Room:** White marble, gold filigree, and a view of the conquered Earth below. **Visual:** The golden glow of the Aldnoah Drive at the center.
*   **The Duel Arena:** A pristine courtyard for Kataphrakt combat. **Sound:** The elegant hum of Martian engines.

### The Deucalion
A massive UEF flying carrier powered by a 'stolen' Aldnoah Drive.
*   **The Bridge:** A high-tech, clinical room where tactical chess is played. **Sound:** Constant electronic pings and clipped military reports.
*   **The Hangar:** A dark, industrial space filled with boxy KG-7s. **Smell:** Jet fuel and sweat.

### The Shattered Moon
A gothic field of floating debris and ancient Martian ruins.
*   **The Hypergate Remnants:** A silent, alien monument to human hubris. **Visual:** Blue-white Earth visible through the cracks.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Calculation:** Use a standard KG-7 Kataphrakt to defeat a Martian 'superweapon' by exploiting a specific physics flaw (e.g., surface tension, light refraction).
2.  **The Princess's Shadow:** Escort a 'civilian' girl (Asseylum in disguise) through a Martian-occupied city without being detected by Orbital Knight scryers.
3.  **The Deucalion Heist:** Infiltrate a Martian Landing Castle to 're-activate' the Deucalion's Aldnoah Drive.
4.  **Count Saazbaum's Gambit:** Defend the UEF carrier from a multi-wave assault by three different Orbital Knights, each with a unique Kataphrakt ability.
5.  **Slaine's Dilemma:** You are captured by Slaine Troyard. He offers you a choice: help him 'save' Asseylum or be executed as a Terran spy.
6.  **The Battle of the Moon:** Lead a desperate boarding action against the Martian flagship in the lunar debris field.
7.  **The Aldnoah Reset:** Travel to the center of the Martian capital to trigger the 'Master Drive' and end the technological divide forever.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. Inaho's Cybernetic Eye
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A high-tech implant that replaces a lost eye, linked to a combat AI.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to Initiative and allows them to ignore all forms of concealment or camouflage.

### 2. The Aldnoah Activation Key
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A handheld device that allows a non-royal to activate Aldnoah tech for 1 hour.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to pilot a Martian Kataphrakt and use its unique 'Blood Ability.'

### 3. Asseylum's Royal Pendant
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A simple, elegant pendant that contains a sliver of the original Aldnoah source.
**Mechanics:** The wearer can use an action to project a 30ft 'Null-Field' that deactivates all electronic devices (including Kataphrakts) for 1 round.
