# Aethelgard: The Once and Future King - Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The stars are not silent; they are singing the names of kings. In the beginning, there was the 'Aethel,' a primal energy that flows through the seams of the universe. The first King, Arthur, was not a man of Earth, but the first 'Armiger Pilot'—a being who could harmonize his soul with the Aethel to control the 'Caliburn,' a city-sized mech of living light. Arthur forged the first 'Round Table' of systems, bringing order to the cosmic chaos. But the 'Morgana Anomaly'—a parasitic shadow energy—corrupted the Grail, the source of the Aethel, and the King fell. Now, the prophecy of the 'Once and Future King' is coming true. The Aethel is surging again, and the ancient spirits of the knights are awakening in new pilots across the stars. Camelot Prime is rising from the gas giant's clouds, and the war for the cosmic throne has begun.

### Timeline
1.  **The Age of Arthur (c. -10,000 years):** The first Unification of the Aethelgard systems. The construction of Camelot Prime and the forging of the first Armigers.
2.  **The morgana Betrayal (c. -8,000 years):** The corruption of the Grail and the fall of the Round Table. The 'Long Dark' begins.
3.  **The Age of Feudalism (c. -8,000 to -100 years):** The systems of Aethelgard fragment into warring duchies. Technology becomes weathered and mythic.
4.  **The First Awakening (100 years ago):** The spirit of Sir Lancelot is reborn in a young pilot on a forest moon. The first 'modern' Armiger is activated.
5.  **The Rise of the Duchy of Avalon (50 years ago):** A faction claiming descent from Arthur begins to consolidate power using recovered ancient tech.
6.  **The Grail Signal (1 year ago):** A massive burst of Aethel energy emanates from the center of the galaxy. Every Armiger in the universe begins to glow.
7.  **The Arrival of the Black Knight (Recent):** A mysterious, Morgana-corrupted Armiger begins destroying Avalon outposts.
8.  **The Rebirth of Camelot (Present Day):** Camelot Prime, the neo-gothic space fortress, appears in the orbit of the capital gas giant.
9.  **The Present Day:** The Round Table is being reformed. New knights are being called to pilot the walking cathedrals of technology and myth to stop the Morgana Anomaly.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the struggle between 'Honor' and 'Corruption.' It is about the 'Mythic Sci-Fi Realism' of being a hero in a world of epic grandeur. The 'Why' is the question: 'Is a legend defined by the power of the machine or the soul of the pilot?' It explores the fusion of medieval heraldry and futuristic materials, the weight of a divine destiny, and the awe of vast cosmic landscapes. The players are the newly awakened Knights of the Round Table, the pilots who must prove their worthiness to wield the power of the Aethel and restore the cosmic balance.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Round Table (The Restorers)
**Type:** Feudal Cosmic Order
**Leader:** The Reborn King (Tier 1 NPC - unknown).
**Asset:** Camelot Prime, the 'Excalibur' Armiger, and the loyalty of the systems.
**Rivals:** The Morgana Shadow, The Warring Duchies.

### The Morgana Shadow (The Corruptors)
**Type:** Parasitic Energy/Infection
**Leader:** Morgana (A shadow-spirit manifesting in the Aethel).
**Asset:** The 'Black Knight' Armiger, shadow-corrupted tech, and the ability to turn a pilot's own fear against them.
**Rivals:** The Round Table.

### The Duchy of Avalon (The Traditionalists)
**Type:** Neo-Feudal Superpower
**Leader:** Duchess Guinevere (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** The largest fleet of 'Galahad' class Armigers and total control of the Aethel-mining moons.
**Rivals:** The Round Table (who they view as 'usurpers').

### The Grail Seekers (The Scholars)
**Type:** Scientific/Religious Order
**Leader:** Merlin (An ancient AI/Human hybrid).
**Asset:** The Stellar Archives, knowledge of ancient Armiger designs, and the 'Starlight Map.'
**Rivals:** None formally (everyone wants their knowledge).

### The Ronin Knights (The Freelancers)
**Type:** Independent Mercenary Pilots
**Leader:** Various.
**Asset:** Heavily modified, one-of-a-kind Armigers and a complete lack of political ties.
**Rivals:** The Duchy of Avalon.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **The Narrator/The Reborn King** (Unknown - Leader/Voice) - A deep, resonant voice that echoes through the Aethel. **Voice:** Regal, theatrical, and measured. **Goal:** To guide the new knights and restore the Round Table. **Secret:** He is not a physical being yet, but an Aethel-echo of Arthur himself.
2.  **Duchess Guinevere** (Avalon - Political Boss) - The ruler of the most powerful duchy. **Voice:** Authoritative, sophisticated, and cold. **Goal:** To rule Aethelgard through Avalon's strength and tradition. **Secret:** She was once a pilot but lost her Armiger to the Morgana Anomaly.
3.  **Merlin** (Grail Seeker - Expert/AI) - An ancient intelligence that has lived for eons. **Voice:** Wise, glitchy, and multi-tonal. **Goal:** To find the Grail and stabilize the Aethel for the new King. **Secret:** He is actually the original designer of the Armigers and feels responsible for Arthur's fall.
4.  **Sir Lancelot (Reborn)** (Round Table - Star Pilot) - The first of the new knights to awaken. **Voice:** Determined, resolute, and filled with a tragic grace. **Goal:** To protect the King and destroy the Morgana Shadow. **Secret:** He suffers from constant visions of his past life's betrayal.
5.  **Morgana (Anomaly)** (Morgana - Antagonist/Spirit) - The manifest shadow within the Aethel. **Voice:** A whispering, distorted chorus of a thousand voices. **Goal:** To consume the Grail and turn the universe into a silent, lightless void. **Secret:** She was once the 'High Priestess' of the Aethel before the Grail's corruption.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### Camelot Prime
A colossal, neo-gothic space fortress orbiting a purple gas giant.
*   **The Hall of the Round Table:** A vast, circular chamber with a holographic table showing the status of the entire galaxy. **Visual:** Golden light reflecting off white marble. **Sound:** The deep, resonant echo of ancient prophecies.
*   **The Armiger Docks:** A forest of towering mechs, each tethered to a repair spire. **Smell:** Ozone, incense, and machine oil.

### The Duchy of Avalon (Mining Moons)
A series of bio-luminescent forest moons with gleaming, high-tech cities.
*   **The Aethel Mines:** Glowing green caverns where the primal energy is harvested. **Visual:** Prismatic crystals and energy fields. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'hum-thump' of mining equipment.
*   **The Palace of Avalon:** An ornate, silver structure surrounded by a fleet of starships.

### The Morgana Anomaly (The Shadow Realm)
A dark, reality-distorting sector where the Aethel has turned black.
*   **The Void Fortress:** A derelict, ancient space station consumed by shadow. **Visual:** Flickering red alarms and impossible geometry. **Feeling:** A heavy, suffocating pressure on the soul.

### The Stellar Archives (Ancient Ruin)
A derelict, ancient library drifting in an asteroid field.
*   **The Chamber of Records:** A clinical, white space filled with holographic books. **Visual:** Cosmic dust particles catching the light of a distant star.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The First Awakening:** Your spirit awakens in an ancient, derelict Armiger. You must defend your home moon from a Morgana-corrupted raid.
2.  **The Journey to Camelot:** Travel to the legendary space fortress to prove your worth to Merlin and the newly formed Round Table.
3.  **The Avalon Standoff:** Infiltrate an Avalon mining moon to retrieve a Grail Fragment. You must navigate the political tensions between the Duchess and the King.
4.  **The Black Knight's Trail:** Track the corrupted Armiger through a dark nebula. Discover the pilot's connection to your own past life.
5.  **The Siege of the Void:** Lead a fleet of knights in a full-scale assault on the Morgana Void Fortress to stop the spread of the anomaly.
6.  **The Grail Vision:** You enter the center of the galaxy to find the source of the Aethel. You must choose between your own legend and the needs of the universe.
7.  **The Final Coronation:** The final confrontation with Morgana at the heart of the Grail. Restore the Aethel and take your place at the Round Table.

## Item Database
[TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
1.  **The Excalibur Armiger:**
**Type:** Legendary Mech
**Description:** A city-sized Armiger made of living, golden light and ancient white marble.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user +5 to all attack rolls and makes them immune to the 'Morgana Rot.' Its primary weapon, the 'Light of Arthur,' deals 20d10 radiant damage in a 100ft line.

2.  **The Grail Fragment:**
**Type:** Artifact Focus
**Description:** A small, glowing emerald that contains a sliver of the original Grail.
**Mechanics:** Grants the user the ability to 'Heal' an Armiger's systems by 50 HP as a bonus action.

3.  **The Round Table Signet:**
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)
**Description:** A simple, gold ring with the crest of the Round Table.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer advantage on all Charisma (Persuasion and Intimidation) checks with any resident of Aethelgard.
