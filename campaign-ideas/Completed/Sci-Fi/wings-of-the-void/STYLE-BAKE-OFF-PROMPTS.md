# Wings of the Void — Style Bake-Off (6 images)

**Purpose:** pick one art style before the full art run. Three subjects, each made in both styles. Same protocol as Journey and Jersey Devil.

## How to run it

1. **Use one generator for all 6 images.**
2. **Keep the subject text identical.** Only the style block changes between A and B.
3. **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 for images 1 and 2, 4:3 for image 3.
4. **Make 2–4 variations of each.** Keep the best one of each.
5. **Judge at app sizes:** shrink each keeper to 96px, and images 1 and 2 also to a 32px circle. Put A and B side by side.
6. **The style that wins 2 of the 3 subjects is locked.** Do not mix styles after the lock.

| Subject | Question |
|---|---|
| 1. Skydancer Kaia | Can you read her age, calm and regret at 96px? Is the silhouette clear at 32px? |
| 2. Void Glider | Is it a clear, strange creature at 32px? Does the half-there shimmer read? |
| 3. The Launchpad | Do you feel the drop at 96px? Does the colour shift from sky to void read? |

Keep the keepers. The winning Skydancer Kaia, Void Glider and Launchpad images may be reused in the final set (they are real entity names).

## Style A — "Altitude Screenprint"

### A1 — Skydancer Kaia (1:1)

```
Modern screen-print poster illustration. Bold flat colour shapes with clean, crisp edges, a limited palette of 5 to 6 colours, subtle halftone dots and paper grain, smooth gradient skies. Strong readable silhouettes, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.

Head-and-shoulders portrait of Skydancer Kaia, a very old human woman, the founder of the Fallers. Deeply weathered face, calm regretful eyes, long silver hair whipped sideways by strong wind, a faded patched wingsuit collar with frayed membrane fabric, a single wind-chime charm at her throat. She stands at the edge of a cliff; behind her, a glowing violet rift in the sky fades into star-flecked black.

Square composition. Face in the upper middle of the frame, strong readable silhouette, plain background behind the head.

No text, no letters, no captions, no speech bubbles, no panel borders, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no frame. No blood, no gore.
```

### A2 — Void Glider (1:1)

```
Modern screen-print poster illustration. Bold flat colour shapes with clean, crisp edges, a limited palette of 5 to 6 colours, subtle halftone dots and paper grain, smooth gradient skies. Strong readable silhouettes, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.

A Void Glider in flight: a large manta-ray-like sky creature with wide translucent wing membranes, its body edges flickering in and out of solid form like a mirage, faint geometric patterns glowing along the wing veins. It dives toward the viewer out of a twilight sky of purple and orange clouds.

Square composition. The creature fills the centre 70% of the frame, one clear readable silhouette, wings spread wide.

No text, no letters, no captions, no speech bubbles, no panel borders, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no frame. No blood, no gore.

### A3 — The Launchpad (4:3)

```
Modern screen-print poster illustration. Bold flat colour shapes with clean, crisp edges, a limited palette of 5 to 6 colours, subtle halftone dots and paper grain, smooth gradient skies. Strong readable silhouettes, no painterly blending, no photoreal texture. Realistic adult proportions, expressive faces.
