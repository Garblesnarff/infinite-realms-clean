## Portrait subjects (style-neutral — the locked style block is added in the Muse doc)

- **The Rimrunner** (`the-rimrunner`): Wiry young human in a patched, many-times-mended wingsuit with the arm membranes folded, goggles pushed up into wind-tangled hair, a coin on a cord at the throat, crouched on the edge of a rusted gantry above a bottomless glowing rift, grinning, strong wind pulling at the fabric
- **The Rigger** (`the-rigger`): Stocky mountain dwarf with a braided grey-streaked beard, a heavy leather work apron over chain mail, a coil of cable thread and a steel needle roll on the chest strap, holding a half-finished wingsuit wing across both arms, goggles on the forehead, workshop lamps glowing behind
- **The Cartographer** (`the-cartographer`): Small bright-eyed rock gnome in a fitted flight jacket covered in pockets and pencils, a brass altimeter strapped to one wrist, a rolled chart under one arm, round goggles with a cracked lens, a faint violet arcane glow at the fingertips, clouds and a glowing rift far below
- **The Windspeaker** (`the-windspeaker`): Calm half-elf priest in a flowing layered flight robe tied close for the jump, a holy symbol of a stylised updraft on a cord, small wind chimes braided into long hair, eyes closed as if listening, one hand raised into the wind, soft light rising from the glowing rift below
- **The Deserter** (`the-deserter`): Broad-shouldered half-orc in a scuffed military wingsuit with the Sky Council insignia torn off one shoulder, a longbow slung across the back, a scar across one tusk, steady watchful eyes, standing on a wind-battered ledge with a stormy sky and the glowing rift behind

## Why this party

- Five core roles: scout and face (Rogue), front line (Fighter), arcane (Wizard), healer (Cleric), ranged tracker (Ranger).
- Every hook starts from the bible's Session 1 (The Jump: initiation at the Nest, meeting Skydancer Kaia) and Session 2 (The First Drift).
- Each character pulls toward a different faction: Beggar's Ledge and the Rim Cities (Rimrunner), the Rusty Gantry riggers (Rigger), the Vertical Cartographers Guild (Cartographer), the Anchorites (Windspeaker), the Sky Council (Deserter).
