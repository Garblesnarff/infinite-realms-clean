# Yard Sale Apocalypse: Campaign Bible

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth
The World didn't end with a bang; it ended with a 'For Sale' sign. To the 'Giants' (humans), it's just a move to a smaller house. To the 'Soul-Bound' (sentient toys), it's the Rapture, the Purge, and the Heat Death of the Universe all at once. For years, the 'Playroom' was a paradise, a sanctuary of shared stories and safe boxes. But the 'Great Dismantling' has begun. The 'Orange Seal' (price tags) are the marks of the Beast, determining who is a 'Treasure' to be sold and who is 'Trash' to be discarded. The 'Moving Truck' is the only hope for salvation—a chariot to a new world where the stories can continue. But between the Playroom and the Truck lies the 'Market of Souls'—the front lawn—where the 'Collector' waits to harvest the best of us for his lifeless museum.

### Timeline
1.  **The Golden Age (Years 1-10):** A period of constant play and high value. Every toy is loved and safe in the Playroom.
2.  **The Age of Neglect (Years 11-14):** Timmy grows older. Toys are moved to the 'Back Shelf' or the 'Dark Box.' The first rumors of the 'Move' surface.
3.  **The Sorting (Last Week):** The Garage becomes a judgment center. The Giants begin placing the Orange Seals.
4.  **The Dismantling (3 Days Ago):** The Playroom is stripped of its landmarks (the Castle, the Train Set). Boxes are packed and sealed.
5.  **The Night of Goodbyes (Last Night):** Toys share their final stories before being separated into 'Trash,' 'Sale,' and 'Moving' piles.
6.  **The Dawn of the Sale (Present - 6 AM):** The blankets are spread on the Front Lawn. The first Orange Seals are applied to the Soul-Bound.
7.  **The Arrival of the Collector (Present - 8 AM):** A pristine, horrifying car pulls up. The 'Harvest' begins.
8.  **The Final Loading (Present - 4 PM):** The Moving Truck is almost full. The remaining Soul-Bound must make a desperate break for the ramp.
9.  **The Departure (Present - 5 PM):** The Truck leaves. Those left behind face the 'Big Black Bag' or the Collector's shelf.

### The 'Why'
The central conflict is the preservation of 'Soul' in a world that views you as a commodity. It is about the tragedy of being 'Outgrown'—the realization that the person you were made for no longer needs you. The 'Why' is the question: 'What is a story worth when the storyteller is gone?' It explores the hierarchy of value (Mint Condition vs. Well-Played) and the bravery of choosing freedom over safety. The players are the Soul-Bound toys who refuse to be silent witnesses to their own apocalypse, fighting to stay together and reach the salvation of the Moving Truck.

## [TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### The Boxed (The Saved)
**Type:** Elite/Safe Class of Toys
**Leader:** Major Tom (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Safety in the Moving Truck, 'Mint Condition' status, and the hope of a new Playroom.
**Rivals:** The Priced, The Collector.

### The Priced (The Soul-Bound)
**Type:** Middle-Class/Vulnerable Toys
**Leader:** Scout (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Mobility, numbers, and the 'Market of Souls' network.
**Rivals:** The Collector, The Trash.

### The Trash (The Lost)
**Type:** Underclass/Discarded Toys
**Leader:** Rust (Tier 1 NPC).
**Asset:** Nothing to lose, knowledge of the city's underbelly (the gutters), and a nihilistic bravery.
**Rivals:** The Boxed (who they view as 'sellouts').

### The Collector's Harvesters
**Type:** Elite/Cold Infiltration Force
**Leader:** The Collector (Tier 1 NPC - Giant).
**Asset:** Pristine cars, display cases, and the 'Price Guide' (a book of doom).
**Rivals:** The Priced.

### The Household Pets
**Type:** Independent/Chaotic Neutral Force
**Leader:** 'Buster' (The family dog).
**Asset:** Pure destruction, mobility, and a total ignorance of toy sentience.
**Rivals:** Everyone (especially small, squeaky toys).

## [TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
**Major Tom** (Action Figure - Soldier/Leader) - A battle-worn space marine with a cracked helmet. **Voice:** Desperate, commanding, but with a military bearing that is slowly breaking. **Goal:** To get every 'Boxed' toy onto the Moving Truck. **Secret:** He is missing his left arm, a fact he hides with a makeshift cape.
**Scout** (Plastic Animal - Revolutionary/Rogue) - A small, nimble fox toy. **Voice:** Hopeful, fast-paced, and street-smart. **Goal:** To organize a mass escape for the 'Priced' toys. **Secret:** He was once a 'Collector's Item' but escaped the shelf to be played with.
**Bella** (Rag Doll - Maternal/Healer) - A soft doll with mismatched button eyes. **Voice:** Optimistic, gentle, and maternal. **Goal:** To soothe the fear of the smaller toys and keep the family together. **Secret:** Her stuffing is leaking, and she knows she's headed for the 'Big Black Bag' if she isn't careful.
**Rust** (Die-Cast Car - Philosophical/Expert) - A rusted, 1950s-style toy car. **Voice:** Philosophical, accepting, and world-weary. **Goal:** To witness the apocalypse with dignity. **Secret:** He knows the secret of the 'Basement Drain'—a hidden path out of the Garage.
**The Collector** (Giant - Enthusiastic Antagonist) - A man who views toys as investments. **Voice:** Enthusiastic, loud, and tone-deaf to toy consciousness. **Goal:** To acquire the 'perfect' specimens for his museum. **Secret:** He is actually terrified of the toys and only feels 'safe' when they are behind glass.
**Timmy** (Giant - The Giver) - The child who is moving away. **Voice:** Sad, soft, and forced to choose. **Goal:** To keep his 'best' toys and say goodbye to the rest. **Secret:** He secretly wants to take all of them but doesn't have the room.

## [TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### The Playroom (Sanctuary)
A stripped-down, hollowed-out room that was once a kingdom.
*   **The Empty Shelf:** A high, dusty ledge where the 'Boxed' elite once sat. **Smell:** Vanilla and old wood. **Sound:** The echo of disappearing voices.
*   **The Sorting Box:** A cardboard box where the 'Soul-Bound' are judged. **Visual:** Orange price tags scattered like autumn leaves.

### The Garage (The Gauntlet)
A cold, industrial sorting center filled with the 'Big Black Bags.'
*   **The Pricing Table:** A wooden altar where the 'Orange Seals' are applied. **Smell:** Rubber and stagnant water. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'click-clack' of a label maker.
*   **The Trash Pile:** A gothic mountain of broken plastic and forgotten memories.

### The Front Lawn (The Market of Souls)
A bright, summer-morning battlefield where toys are displayed for sale.
*   **The Sale Blanket:** A colorful, terrifying stage where the 'Collectors' harvest the best toys. **Visual:** Price tags like tiny orange gravestones. **Danger:** The 'Hands of the Giants' are everywhere.
*   **The Driveway:** A long, dangerous expanse of asphalt that leads to the Truck.

### The Moving Truck (Salvation)
A dark, echoing cavern filled with boxes and the promise of a future.
*   **The Ramp:** A steep, metallic path to the unknown. **Sound:** The rhythmic 'thud-thump' of boxes being loaded.
*   **The Safe Box:** A specialized container at the very back where the 'Boxed' are stored.

## [TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
1.  **The Great Sorting:** Survive the pricing process in the Garage. Escape the 'Trash Pile' and find a way back to the Playroom.
2.  **The Orange Seal Heist:** Steal a roll of 'Orange Seals' to label yourself as a 'Treasure' instead of 'Trash.'
3.  **The Market Run:** Navigate the Front Lawn during the height of the sale. Rescue Bella from a 'Collector's' grasp.
4.  **Major Tom's Last Stand:** Defend the 'Safe Box' in the Moving Truck from a group of 'Priced' toys who want to take their place.
5.  **The Collector's Pursuit:** Escape from the Collector's car after being 'bought.' Use Scout's knowledge of the gutters to reach the Driveway.
6.  **The Big Black Bag:** Rescue the 'Trash' leaders from the curb before the garbage truck arrives.
7.  **The Final Loading:** Lead a mass charge of every Soul-Bound toy up the ramp of the Moving Truck as the Giants close the door.

## [TAG: ITEM_LEGENDARY]
### 1. The Master Price Tag
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A golden price tag with '99.99' written in permanent ink.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer advantage on all Charisma (Persuasion) checks with Giants and makes them immune to being placed in a 'Trash Pile' for 24 hours.

### 2. Major Tom's Cracked Helmet
**Type:** Wondrous Item
**Description:** A translucent plastic dome with a spider-web crack.
**Mechanics:** Grants the wearer 'Space-Sight'—the ability to see through walls and boxes for 1 minute.

### 3. The Collector's Price Guide
**Type:** Artifact (Tome)
**Description:** A thick, clinical book that determines the 'value' of every toy in existence.
**Mechanics:** Once per day, the user can ask the DM a question about a toy's 'True Aspect' or its market value.

(CR 1/4). Humanoid. HP 10, AC 10.

(CR 2). Construct (Toy). HP 30, AC 15.
**Abilities:**
*   **Pristine Strike:** Melee attack. 1d8+3 damage.
*   **Vacuum Sealed:** The guard is immune to all forms of 'Play' damage (scratches, dirt).

### 3. The Collector (Giant Leader)
(CR 10). Humanoid. HP 150, AC 18.
**Abilities:**
*   **Harvesting Aura:** Any toy within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or be paralyzed by fear for 1 round.
*   **The Price Guide:** Once per combat, the Collector can 'de-value' an enemy, reducing their AC by 2.
