# Campaign Bible: ARKS - Defenders of Eternity

**Genre:** Sci-Fi Anime / Space Opera / Cosmic Horror
**System:** Custom (Photon Arts System)
**Campaign Length:** 15+ Sessions (Level 1-17)

---

## 1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY
`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`

### Origin Myth: The Equation of Light and Void
In the era before stars, the **Photoners** sought to solve the "Entropy Problem"—the universe's inevitable drift toward heat death. They engineered **Photons**, particles of solidified will and light that could defy physics. However, the universe demands balance. To counter the infinite creation of Photons, the cosmos manifested the **Profound Darkness**—a sentient, hunger-driven universal constant of negation. The Photoners did not "shatter" a god; they accidentally woke the universe's immune system, which viewed their creation as a cancer to be excised. Humanity was synthesized in test tubes not as children, but as biological containment units (Vessels) to trap this darkness, a cruel purpose forgotten by history but recorded in the DNA of every ARKS operative.

### Timeline of the Oracle
1.  **The Photoner Apex:** The Photoner civilization achieves post-scarcity via Photon manipulation. They build Xion, the omniscient oceanic intelligence.
2.  **The Great Rejection:** The Profound Darkness manifests for the first time, wiping out 60% of the Photoner galaxy in three days.
3.  **Project Vessel:** Photoners create the first humans. They attempt to seal the Darkness into human hosts.
4.  **The Fall of Luther:** Photoner scientist Luther attempts to control a sealed Dark Falz, resulting in the corruption of the Photoner leadership.
5.  **The Oracle Launch:** The surviving Photoners and their human creations board the Oracle Fleet, abandoning their home dimension to flee the Darkness.
6.  **The Void-Tide:** The Oracle Fleet jumps into a nebula composed entirely of "dead" timelines. They salvage the technology to build the Dimension Gates.
7.  **Founding of ARKS:** Humanity overthrows the remaining corrupt Photoners. The Artificial Relict to Keep Species (ARKS) is formed.
8.  **The First Dive:** ARKS operatives successfully defend a primitive Parallel Earth (Code: Alpha) from Darkers, proving humanity can fight back.
9.  **The Ten-Year Silence:** Matoi (The Second Clarion) disappears into a dimensional rift. Photon energy readings across the fleet drop by 40%.
10. **The Awakening:** Present Day. Deep Space Sensors detect the resurgence of Dark Falz [Elder]. The Oracle Fleet moves to intercept.

### The Thematic Conflict: Will vs. Determinism
*   **The Light (ARKS/Photon):** Represents **Evolution through Will**. The belief that destiny can be rewritten, physics can be ignored, and the future is forged by those with the courage to dream it.
*   **The Shadow (Darkers/Profound Darkness):** Represents **Peace through Negation**. The belief that existence is inherently painful and chaotic, and the only true order is the silence of non-existence.
*   *Campaign Thesis:* Is the pain of living worth the struggle, or is oblivion a valid mercy?

## 2. FACTIONS
`[TAG: FACTION_DATA]`

### [ARKS (Artificial Relict to Keep Species)]
*   **Type:** Interdimensional Military Organization
*   **Leader:** Regius (Human, Council of Six)
*   **Asset:** The Oracle Fleet (Massive generation-ships) and Photon weaponry.
*   **Rivals:** The Darkers, The Void Institute.

### [The Darker Hive]
*   **Type:** Cosmic Horror Collective
*   **Leader:** The Profound Darkness / Dark Falz
*   **Asset:** **Corruption Spores.** Can turn biological matter and machinery into hostile monstrosities.
*   **Rivals:** All sentient life.

### [The Void Institute]
*   **Type:** Rogue Science Division
*   **Leader:** Director Luther (The Fallen Photoner)
*   **Asset:** **Cloning Vats.** They seek to manufacture "Perfected Humans" to control Dark Falz energy.
*   **Rivals:** ARKS Main Command, Matoi.

### [Planetary Defense Force (Earth-Beta)]
*   **Type:** Local Military (Parallel Earth)
*   **Leader:** General Yasaka
*   **Asset:** **Aether Technology.** A weaker, native variant of Photon energy derived from Earth's ley lines.
*   **Rivals:** Darker Invasions.

### [The Castaways]
*   **Type:** Scavenger Fleet
*   **Leader:** Captain Zig
*   **Asset:** **Retro-fitting.** They specialize in recovering tech from timelines that were destroyed by Darkers.
*   **Rivals:** The Void Institute (who want their tech).

### [Cult of the Monolith]
*   **Type:** Religious Extremists
*   **Leader:** High Priestess Elmir
*   **Asset:** **Sacrificial Rites.** They believe feeding people to Darkers appeases the "Great Silencer."
*   **Rivals:** ARKS, Common Sense.

### [Star-Singers (Quna's Entourage)]
*   **Type:** Cultural/Propaganda Unit
*   **Leader:** Quna (The Idol/Assassin)
*   **Asset:** **Sonic Manipulation.** They use music to boost ARKS morale and disrupt Darker coordination.
*   **Rivals:** None openly (Secretly: The Council).

### [Code: Abyss]
*   **Type:** Black Ops / Assassins
*   **Leader:** Unknown (Secretly Maria)
*   **Asset:** **Stealth Tech.** They eliminate ARKS operatives who succumb to corruption before they turn.
*   **Rivals:** Corrupted Operatives.

### [The Xion Collective]
*   **Type:** Artificial Intelligence Network
*   **Leader:** Xion (The Ocean of Data)
*   **Asset:** **Timeline Prediction.** They can calculate the probability of mission success.
*   **Rivals:** The Profound Darkness (Logical Rivalry).

### [The Photon-Eaters] (Weird/Rare)
*   **Type:** Transhumanist Mutants
*   **Leader:** "Gulp"
*   **Asset:** **Anti-Photon Biology.** They are immune to Photon Arts but starve without consuming light energy. They eat weapons.
*   **Rivals:** Logistics Division.

## 3. NPC ROSTER
`[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]`

**1. Matoi** (Human/Living Weapon) - *The Amnesiac Key.* Innocent, soft-spoken, but wields terrifying power when cornered.
*   **Voice:** High pitch, breathy, hesitant. Speed up when anxious.
*   **Goal:** Remember who she was before the darkness took her.
*   **Secret:** She is the vessel for the "Clarity," the only force capable of purifying the Profound Darkness, not just destroying it.

**2. Xion** (AI Entity) - *The Tragic Mother.* Holographic, composed, speaks in riddles and probabilities.
*   **Voice:** Monotone, echoing, mid-range. No breathing sounds.
*   **Goal:** Guide the players to a timeline where they survive.
*   **Secret:** She has simulated this war 4 million times; this is the first time the players have lived past Session 5.

**3. Gettemhult** (Human Hunter) - *The Berserker.* Aggressive, loud, obsessed with strength.
*   **Voice:** Gravelly, shouting, aggressive. Emphasis on verbs.
*   **Goal:** Find a fight that actually hurts him.
*   **Secret:** He is already partially corrupted; he fights to burn out the infection with adrenaline.

**4. Luther** (Photoner/Deuman) - *The Manipulator.* Elegant, arrogant, treats people like lab rats.
*   **Voice:** Smooth, sneering, articulate. Mid-Atlantic accent.
*   **Goal:** Gain omniscience by dissecting Xion.
*   **Secret:** He is technically dead; his body is held together by pure Photon calculation.

**5. Hitsugi Yasaka** (Earth Human) - *The Otaku Hero.* High school girl thrust into war. Uses a katana made of "cool vibes."
*   **Voice:** Energetic, teenage slang, varies between scared and hyped.
*   **Goal:** Protect her brother and her friends.
*   **Secret:** Her "Aether" power can override the physics of the Oracle Fleet because she operates on "Anime Logic" (willpower = reality).

**6. Dark Falz [Elder]** (Cosmic Entity) - *Blind Destruction.* A muscle-bound giant of purple chitin.
*   **Voice:** Multiple voices layered: a deep roar mixed with a child's scream.
*   **Goal:** Break things. Specifically, break the Oracle Fleet.
*   **Secret:** He retains the memories of the human he possessed; he hesitates when attacking his former daughter.

**7. Quna** (Newman Assassin/Idol) - *The Invisible Star.* Pop star by day, invisible killer by night.
*   **Voice:** **Idol Mode:** Cheerful, high energy. **Assassin Mode:** Cold, flat, whispery.
*   **Goal:** Eliminate threats to the fleet without causing panic.
*   **Secret:** She is invisible to electronic surveillance naturally; her "tech" is just a cover.

**8. Zeno** (Human Hunter) - *The Mentor.* Laid back, uses a gunblade, treats war like a 9-to-5.
*   **Voice:** Relaxed, warm, chuckles often. "Cool uncle" vibes.
*   **Goal:** Keep the rookies (the players) alive.
*   **Secret:** He lost his entire previous team to [Elder] and refuses to lead again.

**9. Echo** (Newman Force) - *The Tsundere Mage.* Short-tempered, powerful magic, hates Zeno's laziness.
*   **Voice:** Sharp, rapid-fire, annoyed. Snaps ends of sentences.
*   **Goal:** Prove she doesn't need protection.
*   **Secret:** She is tailoring a wedding dress in secret.

**10. Zig** (Cast/Android Smith) - *The Weapons Grandpa.* Huge, bulky robot, obsessed with craft.
*   **Voice:** Deep, metallic, slow. Like grinding gears.
*   **Goal:** Forge the "Star-Shatterer" weapon.
*   **Secret:** He puts small auditory hallucinations into weapons to keep wielders "alert."

## 4. LOCATIONS
`[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]`

### Zone 1: The Oracle Fleet (Mothership)
*   **The Gate Area:** A massive crystalline dome where dimensional gates flare.
    *   *Sensory:* Hum of teleportation, announcement chimes, smell of ozone and antiseptic.
*   **The Shopping Plaza:** Neon-drenched multi-level mall for off-duty operatives.
    *   *Sensory:* J-Pop music playing softly, smell of crepes and soldering iron.
*   **Bridge of the Ur:** Command center, dark with glowing holographic star charts.
    *   *Sensory:* Cold air, low thrum of engines, hushed tactical chatter.
*   **Matoi’s Garden:** A secret hydroponic bay with flowers from dead worlds.
    *   *Sensory:* Smell of damp earth (rare in space), absolute silence.
*   **The Grind Lab:** Where Dudu works.
    *   *Sensory:* Sounds of grinding metal and occasional explosions.

### Zone 2: Naberius (Forest Planet)
*   **The Verdant Run:** Giant trees with bioluminescent veins.
    *   *Sensory:* Smell of pine and rot, sound of screeching monkeys.
*   **Ruins of the First:** White stone pillars reclaimed by moss.
    *   *Sensory:* Texture of cold smooth stone, unnatural stillness.
*   **Tundra Peak:** Frozen jagged mountains.
    *   *Sensory:* Biting wind, sound of crunching snow, blinding white glare.

### Zone 3: Lilipa (Desert Planet)
*   **The Rust Dunes:** Sand made of oxidized iron dust.
    *   *Sensory:* Metallic taste in mouth, heat haze, clanking of underground machines.
*   **Automata Graveyard:** Miles of destroyed giant mechs.
    *   *Sensory:* Groaning metal in the wind, leaking oil smell.

### Zone 4: Amduskia (Volcanic Caverns)
*   **Dragon Altar:** Floating islands over magma.
    *   *Sensory:* Sulfur smell, heat distortion, roaring of dragons below.
*   **Crystal Caverns:** Geodes large enough to fly ships through.
    *   *Sensory:* Singing resonance when struck, prismatic light.

### Zone 5: The Darker Nest (Dimension of Negation)
*   **The Twisted Spire:** A tower made of corrupted flesh and geometry.
    *   *Sensory:* Visuals glitching (screen tearing effect), sound of whispering.
*   **The Red Sea:** A liquid ocean of blood-like data.
    *   *Sensory:* Smell of copper, feeling of vertigo.

## 8. MECHANICS
`[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]`

### Photon Arts (PA) System
Instead of standard spell slots, players use **Photon Points (PP)**.
*   **Regen:** PP regenerates only when landing "Normal Attacks" or taking the "Charge" action.
*   **Arts:** Special moves (PAs) cost PP.
    *   *Rising Edge (Hunter):* Costs 20 PP. Launches enemy into air.
    *   *Divine Launcher (Ranger):* Costs 30 PP. Massive AOE blast.
    *   *Foie (Force):* Costs 15 PP. Fireball that leaves burning trail.

### The Mag
A floating robotic pet.
*   **Auto-Action:** Attacks every round independently (low damage).
*   **Photon Blast:** When player takes X damage, Mag transforms into a spectral avatar (Unicorn, Hydra, etc.) for a massive 1-turn nuke.
*   **Feeding:** Players feed items to the Mag to stat-boost. Feeding it "Ranged Weapons" increases user DEX.

### Emergency Code
At the start of any combat, roll d6. On a 6, an **Emergency Code** triggers.
*   **Code: Attack:** Enemies double in number.
*   **Code: Avoid:** An invulnerable gunship chases the party for 3 rounds.
*   **Code: Rescue:** An NPC crashes into the battlefield; protect them.
