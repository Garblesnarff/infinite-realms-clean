# Campaign Bible: Bang of the Syndicate

## 1. DEEP LORE & HISTORY
`[TAG: LORE_CORE]`

**Origin Myth: The Red Asphalt**
The Red Dragon Syndicate did not begin as a crime family, but as the "First Descent Labor Union"—the initial wave of terraformers on Mars who were abandoned by Earth corporations when the budget collapsed. To survive the toxic dust and starvation, they developed "Red Eye," a combat-stimulant derived from Martian fungi that suppresses fear and empathy. This drug allowed them to slaughter the corporate enforcers sent to liquidate them. Over a century, the Union calcified into the Syndicate. They no longer fight for survival, but for control of the "Breath of Mars"—the ventilation and water reclamation grids. They are not just criminals; they are the landlords of the atmosphere.

**Timeline of Events**
1.  **2071, The Corporate Abandonment:** Earth megacorps pull funding for Mars terraforming, leaving 50,000 workers to suffocate.
2.  **2072, The Red Eye Rebellion:** The First Descent Union synthesizes Red Eye and storms the atmospheric processors.
3.  **2080, The Treaty of Ash:** Earth recognizes Mars' independence in exchange for regular Helium-3 tithes. The Union rebrands as the Red Dragon Syndicate.
4.  **2095, The Gateway Accident:** An experimental warp gate explosion shatters the moon, creating the "Debris Ring" around Earth and making space travel hazardous.
5.  **2100, The Rise of the Elders:** Five families monopolize the five necessities: Air, Water, Fuel, Drugs, and Weapons.
6.  **2115, The Titan War:** A failed expansion into Saturn’s moons. The Syndicate relies on "The Vets" (including the Party and Vicious) to hold the line against corporate private militaries.
7.  **2118, The Julia Incident:** Vicious discovers his partner (a PC) sleeping with Julia. The brotherhood fractures. The PC leaves the Syndicate.
8.  **2119, The Night of Long Knives:** Vicious, feeling the Elders have grown soft, slaughters all five in a single night using only a katana to save ammunition costs.
9.  **2120, The Purge:** Vicious issues the "Traitor's Decree." Any associate of the former Elders is marked for death.
10. **Current Day:** The Solar System is in a fragile state of "Cold Peace," managed by bounty hunters (Cowboys) because police jurisdiction is too fractured to function.

**The "Why": Iron vs. Flow**
The central conflict is **Stagnation vs. Entropy**.
*   **Vicious (Iron):** Believes the only way to survive the chaos of space is absolute, rigid control. He wants to freeze the hierarchy, removing all "weakness" (emotion).
*   **The Party (Flow):** Represents the jazz philosophy—improvisation, freedom, and accepting that things fall apart. They fight not to rule, but to drift freely.

## 2. FACTIONS
`[TAG: FACTION_DATA]`

### [Red Dragon Syndicate]
*   **Type:** Crime Empire / Shadow Government
*   **Leader:** Vicious (The Usurper)
*   **Asset:** Control of the "Red Eye" drug trade and Martian Atmospheric Processors.
*   **Rivals:** The White Tigers, The ISSP.

### [Inter-Solar System Police (ISSP)]
*   **Type:** Corrupt Law Enforcement
*   **Leader:** Chief "Ironhead" Bunta
*   **Asset:** Orbital Cruisers and endless bureaucratic immunity.
*   **Rivals:** Bounty Hunters (who steal their thunder/money), Red Dragon.

### [The White Tigers]
*   **Type:** Traditionalist Triad
*   **Leader:** Wang Long
*   **Asset:** Smuggling routes through the Earth Debris Ring.
*   **Rivals:** Red Dragon (Blood Feud).

### [The Frequency 99]
*   **Type:** Information Brokers / Pirate Radio
*   **Leader:** "The DJ" (Identity unknown)
*   **Asset:** An encrypted sub-layer of the solar-net hidden inside jazz broadcasts. They sell enemy coordinates as song requests.
*   **Rivals:** ISSP Censors.

### [The Organ-Grinders]
*   **Type:** Medical Repo-Men
*   **Leader:** Doc "Stitch"
*   **Asset:** Highly advanced cybernetics that they "reclaim" from those who miss payments. They operate illegal chop-shops.
*   **Rivals:** Red Dragon (who tax them heavily).

### [The Dust-Lung Union]
*   **Type:** Radical Labor Movement
*   **Leader:** Mama K
*   **Asset:** Control of the subterranean transport tunnels on Mars. They can shut down transit across the planet.
*   **Rivals:** Corporate Interests.

### [The Neon Monks]
*   **Type:** Techno-Religious Cult
*   **Leader:** Father Filament
*   **Asset:** They worship the electrical grid. They can cause localized blackouts or overload security systems through prayer (hacking).
*   **Rivals:** Anyone who disrespects the "Divine Current."

### [The Second Hand]
*   **Type:** Scavengers
*   **Leader:** Old Man Rickets
*   **Asset:** A fleet of salvage ships that operate in the dangerous Earth Debris Ring. They find things others think are lost.
*   **Rivals:** Orbital Customs.

### [Blue Sun Corporation]
*   **Type:** Megacorp
*   **Leader:** Director Halloway
*   **Asset:** Private military contractors and legal ownership of the Warp Gates.
*   **Rivals:** Red Dragon Syndicate.

### [The Mourning Star]
*   **Type:** Assassins Guild
*   **Leader:** The Widow
*   **Asset:** A neutral ground hotel where no violence is permitted. They broker high-value contracts.
*   **Rivals:** Amateur bounty hunters.

## 3. NPC ROSTER
`[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]`

**Major NPCs**

1.  **Vicious** (Human Syndicate Boss) - Cold, nihilistic, obsessed with power. Wields a katana. **Voice:** Monotone, whispering, zero inflection, slow pace. **Goal:** Absolute control of Mars. **Secret:** He is dying of "Red Eye" withdrawal/poisoning and wants to burn the world before he goes.
2.  **Julia** (Human Femme Fatale) - Mysterious, melancholic, wears trench coats. **Voice:** Soft, breathy, slight French accent, sounds constantly on the verge of tears. **Goal:** To escape Vicious with the Party. **Secret:** She has the encryption key to the Syndicate's bank accounts in a locket.
3.  **Jet Black** (Cyborg Ex-Cop) - Gruff, fatherly, loves bonsai trees. **Voice:** Deep, gravelly, throat-clearing, sounds like a tired bear. **Goal:** Keep the "family" (party) alive. **Secret:** He still sends money to the daughter of a man he killed.
4.  **Faye** (Human Con-Artist) - Vain, brash, secretly terrified of abandonment. **Voice:** High-pitched, fast, sharp, sarcastic, rapid changes in volume. **Goal:** Pay off her massive debt. **Secret:** She has no memories before 3 years ago (cryo-amnesia).
5.  **Ed** (Human Hacker) - Androgynous, chaotic, speaks in third person. **Voice:** Singsong, high energy, erratic rhythm, makes sound effects with mouth. **Goal:** Find the "Ghost in the Machine." **Secret:** Her father is the cartographer of Earth's surface.
6.  **Ein** (Corgi Data-Dog) - A dog with human intelligence. **Voice:** Barks, whines, but types on keyboards. **Goal:** Snacks. **Secret:** Contains the algorithm for the Warp Gate physics.
7.  **Mao Yenrai** (Human Elder - Deceased/Flashback) - Traditional, honorable. **Voice:** Wheezing, slow, grandfatherly. **Goal:** Maintain balance. **Secret:** He knew Vicious would betray him but accepted it as karma.
8.  **Shin** (Human Enforcer) - Quiet, professional, wears sunglasses. **Voice:** Clipped, military precision, no contractions. **Goal:** Protect Vicious (Publicly) / Aid the Party (Privately). **Secret:** He is Vicious's younger brother.
9.  **Lin** (Human Enforcer) - Loyal to a fault, nervous. **Voice:** Stuttering, higher pitch, apologetic tone. **Goal:** Prove his worth to the Syndicate. **Secret:** He is in love with Shin.
10. **The Van** (Cyborg Brute) - Vicious's bodyguard. Mostly metal. **Voice:** Metallic filter, heavy breathing, sentences are 3 words max. **Goal:** Crush. **Secret:** He feels no pain but feels phantom itching in missing limbs.
11. **Judy** (TV Host) - Host of "Big Shot" bounty show. **Voice:** Over-the-top enthusiastic, fake Texas accent, loud. **Goal:** High ratings. **Secret:** She hates bounty hunters.
12. **Punch** (TV Co-Host) - Judy's partner. **Voice:** Deep "announcer" voice, very smooth. **Goal:** Get through the show. **Secret:** He is actually a retired assassin.
13. **Gren** (War Veteran) - Saxophone player, tragic figure. **Voice:** Melodic, soft, pauses frequently as if listening to music. **Goal:** Find closure for the Titan War. **Secret:** He was a test subject for experimental drugs.
14. **Laughing Bull** (Shaman) - Lives in a tent on Mars. **Voice:** Ancient, creaky, speaks in riddles/metaphors. **Goal:** Guide lost souls. **Secret:** He is the wealthiest man on Mars but chooses poverty.
15. **Antonio** (Mobster) - Old friend of the party. **Voice:** Stereotypical mobster, "Ay, fuhgeddaboudit", raspy laughter. **Goal:** Survive Vicious's purge. **Secret:** He sold out the Elders to Vicious.
16. **Rocco** (Small-time crook) - Young, naive, wants to be like the party. **Voice:** Cracking teenage voice, eager, fast. **Goal:** Learn how to fight. **Secret:** Stole a rare plant worth millions.
17. **Dr. Londe** (Mad Scientist) - Creator of "Red Eye". **Voice:** Manic, giggly, high pitched, nasally. **Goal:** Perfect the drug. **Secret:** He is addicted to his own supply.
18. **Anastasia** (Information Broker) - Runs a convenience store. **Voice:** Bored, monotone, chewing gum noises. **Goal:** retire to Venus. **Secret:** She is a former ISSP comms officer.
19. **Coffee** (Bounty Head) - A woman dressed as a man. **Voice:** Husky, assertive, confident. **Goal:** Revenge on her ex-husband. **Secret:** She is carrying a grenade launcher in her guitar case.
20. **Tongpu** (The Mad Pierrot) - Engineered super-killer. Child mind. **Voice:** High pitched giggling, baby-talk, then sudden screaming. **Goal:** Play "games" (killing). **Secret:** Terrified of cats.

## 8. MECHANICS
`[TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]`

**1. The Weight (Karma System)**
*   Every time a PC kills an innocent, accepts a dirty bribe, or fails to save a friend, they gain 1 point of **Weight**.
*   **Mechanic:** When making a Death Saving Throw, the DC is 10 + Current Weight.
*   **Reduction:** Weight can only be reduced by selfless acts that yield no monetary reward.

**2. The "Bang" Mechanic**
*   **Trigger:** When a PC is reduced to 0 HP.
*   **Action:** The player can shout "Bang."
*   **Effect:** They immediately take a full turn before falling unconscious. Attacks made during this turn are critical hits. After the turn, they suffer 2 failed Death Saves automatically.

**3. Noir Investigation (Information Gathering)**
*   Instead of rolling one check, investigation is a 3-step process:
    1.  **The Word on the Street (Charisma):** Finding the right person.
    2.  **The Grease (Gold):** Paying the bribe.
    3.  **The Squeeze (Intimidation/Persuasion):** Getting the truth.

**4. Space Dogfighting**
*   Simplified vehicle combat.
*   **Roles:** Pilot (Evasion), Gunner (Attacks), Engineer (Shields/Repairs).
*   **Momentum:** Successful maneuvers grant "Momentum" dice (d6) that can be added to damage rolls.
