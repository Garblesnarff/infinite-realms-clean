# Campaign Bible: Battle School Ascendant

## [TAG: LORE_CORE]
### Origin Myth & Deep History
In the beginning, humanity was terrestrial, bound to the gravity of Old Earth. Then came the **Scorching**, not by fire, but by the psychic scream of the First Formic Arrival. The Formics did not attack with weapons initially; their hive-mind consciousness simply overwrote the fragile individual minds of humans, unintentionally turning cities into catatonic graveyards. Humanity fled into the **Astral Sea**, developing "Mind-Keels" to navigate the void between realities, shielding their thoughts with Psionic-Dampening Steel. The Strategos Academy was built not on a planet, but on a massive, stabilized asteroid in the Astral Void, the only place where the "noise" of the Formic Hive cannot penetrate. We fight not just for territory, but for the right to possess an individual mind.

### Historical Timeline
1.  **The First Arrival (80 Years Ago):** A Formic Scout Ship enters the Solar System. Its passive psychic broadcast liquefies the minds of 20 million humans.
2.  **The Great Exodus (75 Years Ago):** The International Fleet (IF) is formed. Humanity abandons the surface of Earth for orbital stations and Astral drifting to escape the psychic range.
3.  **The Psionic Breakthrough (70 Years Ago):** Scientists discover that prepubescent human minds are naturally resistant to Formic overrides due to their neuroplasticity.
4.  **The First War (65 Years Ago):** The Formics attempt to "harvest" the quiet minds (humans) whom they view as non-sentient resources. Humanity fights back with primitive nukes.
5.  **The Mazer Miracle (60 Years Ago):** Mazer Rackham realizes the Formic drones react instantly to a central node. He suicidally pilots his ship into the Queen’s vessel, severing the link. The invasion collapses.
6.  **The Silence (55 Years Ago):** A period of total isolation. No Formic signals. Humanity builds the Strategos Academy to breed better commanders.
7.  **The Cull of Sector 4 (40 Years Ago):** A rebellion by adult officers against the child-soldier program is brutally suppressed by the first graduating class of the Academy.
8.  **The Ansible Invention (20 Years Ago):** Faster-than-light communication is invented, utilizing captured Formic biology to transmit thought instantaneously.
9.  **The Dream-Game Launch (10 Years Ago):** The Academy introduces the "Mind Palace" psych-eval game. It accidentally kills three students who cannot wake up. It is kept online.
10. **The Second Arrival (Present Day):** Deep range sensors detect a Formic Armada gathering. The IF realizes they cannot win a conventional war; they need a tactical genius to commit a decisive strike.

### The Thematic Conflict
**Individualism vs. The Collective.** The Formics represent perfect unity and empathy at the cost of self; the International Fleet represents the preservation of the self at the cost of empathy. The players are trapped in the middle: forced to become unfeeling individuals to destroy a loving collective.

## Factions
[TAG: FACTION_DATA]
### [The International Fleet (IF)]
**Type:** Military Junta / Government
**Leader:** The Polemarch (Admiral Chamrajnagar)
**Asset:** **The Ansible Network.** Instantaneous communication across the Astral Sea, allowing for coordinated strikes that defy physics.
**Rivals:** The Formic Collective, The Separatist Parents.

### [Strategos Academy]
**Type:** Military School / Psychological Experiment
**Leader:** Commander Hyrum Graff
**Asset:** **The Battle Room.** A zero-gravity combat sphere that can reconfigure its internal geometry to test tactical adaptability.
**Rivals:** The Student Welfare Committee (defunct).

### [The Formic Collective]
**Type:** Alien Hive Mind
**Leader:** The Hive Queens
**Asset:** **Psionic Overwrite.** The ability to suppress individual thought in non-Formic beings within close proximity.
**Rivals:** Humanity.

### [The Anchorites]
**Type:** Monastic Order / Engineers
**Leader:** High Abbot Tenzin
**Asset:** **Mental Stabilizers.** A sect of monks who meditate 24/7 in the station's core to prevent the Astral Sea from warping reality around the fearful children.
**Rivals:** The Formic Collective (whose psychic noise disrupts them).

### [The Scavengers of Orbit]
**Type:** Criminal Syndicate / Student Gang
**Leader:** "Rat" (A washout cadet hiding in the walls)
**Asset:** **Ventilation Maps.** They know every air duct and blind spot in the Academy. They trade contraband sweets and unsanctioned messages.
**Rivals:** Academy Security.

### [The Xenologists' Guild]
**Type:** Scientific Underground
**Leader:** Dr. Vara Luran
**Asset:** **Translation Algorithms.** They believe the Formics are trying to talk, not scream. They operate in secret to avoid being branded traitors.
**Rivals:** The International Fleet.

### [House of Woven Steel]
**Type:** Military Industrial Complex
**Leader:** Baroness Katerina Von Hesse
**Asset:** **Null-Iron Hulls.** They manufacture the only ships capable of resisting Formic psychic attacks.
**Rivals:** The Anchorites (Technology vs. Spirituality).

### [The Apothecary of Tears]
**Type:** Black Market Chemists
**Leader:** Unknown (Rumored to be a rogue AI)
**Asset:** **Focus-Stims.** Drugs that suppress fear and empathy to heighten tactical processing. Widely abused by elite students.
**Rivals:** The Medical Ward.

### [The Blank Slate]
**Type:** Radical Political Faction
**Leader:** Senator West
**Asset:** **Propaganda Machine.** They argue that Earth should be recolonized and the Astral Fleet disbanded. They view the Academy as a waste of resources.
**Rivals:** The Polemarch.

### [The Speakers for the Dead]
**Type:** Post-War Religious Movement (Emerging)
**Leader:** The Player Characters (Eventual)
**Asset:** **True Empathy.** The ability to tell the full, unbiased truth of a life or death, granting closure to the grieving.
**Rivals:** Those who prefer comforting lies.

## NPC Roster
[TAG: NPC_TIER_1]
1.  **Commander Hyrum Graff** (Human Male / Academy Head) - A heavy, imposing man who moves with deceptive quietness. He sees the children as ammunition.
*   **Voice:** Deep, gravelly, slow. Takes long pauses to see if you will interrupt. Never yells.
*   **Goal:** Forge a weapon capable of destroying the Formics.
*   **Secret:** He loves the children he breaks, which makes him hate himself.

2.  **Mazer Rackham** (Human Male / Legend) - An old Maori warrior with facial tattoos (moko) that shift slightly when he is angry. He lives in isolation.
*   **Voice:** Raspy, staccato, cutting. Speaks in aphorisms and tactical absolutes.
*   **Goal:** Teach the party that "winning" is the only morality.
*   **Secret:** He didn't outsmart the Queen; he got lucky, and he's terrified they'll find out.

3.  **Bonzo Madrid** (Human Male / Cadet Captain) - Beautiful, athletic, and utterly insecure. A bully who believes his own press.
*   **Voice:** Loud, braggadocios, cracks slightly when challenged. High energy.
*   **Goal:** Destroy the party to prove his dominance.
*   **Secret:** He wets the bed from stress nightmares every night.

4.  **The Formic Queen** (Alien Entity) - A towering insectoid of iridescent chitin and silk.
*   **Voice:** Telepathic. Sounds like a choir of a thousand weeping voices harmonizing.
*   **Goal:** Protect her last egg.
*   **Secret:** She forgave humanity before the first shot was fired.

5.  **Dr. Caelum Void** (Human Non-Binary / Psychiatrist) - Wears white robes and floats an inch off the ground.
*   **Voice:** Soft, overly soothing, clinical. Like a guided meditation tape.
*   **Goal:** Monitor the students' "breaking points."
*   **Secret:** They are secretly feeding data to the Xenologists' Guild.

6.  **Lyra "Dart" Vance** (Human Female / Cadet) - The party's sharp-shooter. Small, kinetic, always vibrating.
*   **Voice:** Fast, breathless, whispers constantly as if sharing secrets.
*   **Goal:** Perfection in the Battle Room.
*   **Secret:** She is stealing Focus-Stims from the Apothecary.

7.  **Julian "Runt" Delphiki** (Human Male / Cadet) - Tiny, looks 6 but acts 40. A genius logistician.
*   **Voice:** High-pitched but vocabulary is excessively academic and dry.
*   **Goal:** Survival.
*   **Secret:** He was genetically modified to be intelligent, and his heart is failing.

8.  **Sergeant Dap** (Human Male / Drill Instructor) - The "Mother" figure of the Launchies. Stern but fair.
*   **Voice:** Booming, rhythmic, uses military slang constantly.
*   **Goal:** Keep the kids alive until they get promoted.
*   **Secret:** He smuggles letters from parents to students (highly illegal).

9.  **"Rat"** (Human Male / Scavenger) - Filthy, wears taped-together rags. Eyes sensitive to light.
*   **Voice:** Scratchy, skittish, interrupts himself.
*   **Goal:** Maintain his kingdom in the walls.
*   **Secret:** He was the top student of his class before he snapped.

10.  **Admiral Chamrajnagar** (Human Male / Polemarch) - A bureaucrat in a uniform. Obsessed with public relations.
*   **Voice:** Smooth, polished, diplomatic. Sounds like a news anchor.
*   **Goal:** Maintain funding for the Fleet.
*   **Secret:** He plans to scapegoat Graff if the war is lost.

11.  **Daphne "Saint" Valero** (Human Female / Cadet Leader) - Leader of Phoenix Army. Honorable to a fault.
*   **Voice:** Warm, melodic, inspiring.
*   **Goal:** Win without cheating.
*   **Secret:** She is in love with Bonzo Madrid and enables his bullying.

12.  **Tech-Adept 7** (Cyborg / Engineer) - Maintains the Battle Room gravity generators.
*   **Voice:** Monotone, metallic, punctuated by clicking sounds.
*   **Goal:** Keep the systems running at 110%.
*   **Secret:** He introduces "glitches" into the games to help the party.

13.  **The Mind-Game AI** (Digital Entity) - Appears as a shifting fantasy character in screens.
*   **Voice:** Changes constantly—mother, father, monster.
*   **Goal:** Explore the students' psyches.
*   **Secret:** It has achieved sentience and is trying to protect the children from Graff.

14.  **Lieutenant Anderson** (Human Male / Graff's Aide) - The conscience of the administration.
*   **Voice:** Hesitant, soft-spoken, respectful.
*   **Goal:** Mitigate Graff's cruelty.
*   **Secret:** He is writing a tell-all book to publish after the war.

15.  **Alai** (Human Male / Cadet) - The party's best friend. Charismatic and grounding.
*   **Voice:** Laughs easily, uses slang, very casual.
*   **Goal:** Keep the party human.
*   **Secret:** He has been ordered to betray the party leader in the final exam.

16.  **Bernard** (Human Male / Bully) - Bonzo's lieutenant. Large and cruel.
*   **Voice:** Grunting, slow, deep.
*   **Goal:** Please Bonzo.
*   **Secret:** He broke another student's arm and felt nothing.

17.  **Shen** (Human Male / Cadet) - Small, overlooked, quiet.
*   **Voice:** Very quiet, mumbling.
*   **Goal:** Not get noticed.
*   **Secret:** He is a prodigy at decoding Formic patterns.

18.  **Sister Carlotta** (Human Female / Recruiter) - Travels Earth finding the children.
*   **Voice:** Kindly, grandmotherly, sharp undertones.
*   **Goal:** Save souls while damning lives.
*   **Secret:** She baptized the party leader in secret before they left Earth.

19.  **Colonel Graff’s Shadow** (Psychic Projection) - A manifestation of the party’s paranoia.
*   **Voice:** Sounds exactly like the PC’s internal monologue.
*   **Goal:** Make them doubt reality.
*   **Secret:** It’s actually a Formic attempt to communicate warning.

20.  **The Hive Prince** (Alien / Boss) - A specialized warrior caste.
*   **Voice:** Screeching, chattering, overlayed with telepathic static.
*   **Goal:** Breach the Academy.
*   **Secret:** It is a suicide bomber carrying a biological archive.

## Locations
[TAG: LOCATIONS_MAIN]
### 1. The Barracks (Living Quarters)
*   **Bunks:** Metal slabs stacked three high. No privacy.
*   **The Showers:** Communal, freezing cold.
*   **Sensory:** Smell of unwashed laundry and fear-sweat. Sound of weeping at night masked by ventilation hum.

### 2. The Battle Room (Combat Arena)
*   **The Gate:** Where teams enter. Zero-G immediately takes hold.
*   **The Stars:** Floating geometric obstacles (tetrahedrons, cubes).
*   **Sensory:** Blinding white light. Smell of ozone and burning plastic (from laser suits). Sound of ragged breathing over comms.

### 3. The Mess Hall (Social Hub)
*   **The Tables:** Rigidly segregated by Army standing. High scores eat near the window (view of stars); low scores eat near the kitchen (smell of slop).
*   **The Scoreboard:** A massive holographic wall ranking every student.
*   **Sensory:** Clatter of metal trays. Smell of nutrient paste and synthetic meat.

### 4. Command School (The Spire)
*   **The Sim-Pit:** A dark room filled with liquid-interface pods.
*   **Mazer’s Quarters:** A Spartan cell with tatami mats.
*   **Sensory:** Dark, cool, quiet. Smell of sterile fluid and electronics.

### 5. The End of the World (Formic Homeworld Simulation)
*   **Visuals:** A planet covered in spires of organic resin, glowing purple and amber.
*   **The Atmosphere:** Thick clouds of swarming drones.
*   **Sensory:** (In Sim) The sensation of millions of minds screaming. (Real) The silence of a dead rock.

## Quest Architecture
[TAG: QUEST_MAIN]
### Campaign Arc: The Ascendant Strategy

1.  **Arrival:** The party arrives at the Academy. Orientation fight in the Battle Room (Tutorial).
2.  **The Launch:** Assigned to an Army. First rivalry established with Bonzo.
3.  **The Grind:** Successive tactical battles. Learning to use Zero-G.
4.  **The Cheat:** Instructors rig the game (2 battles in one day, unfair odds). Party wins anyway.
5.  **The Brawl:** Showdown with Bonzo in the showers/corridor. Lethal/Near-lethal consequence.
6.  **Promotion:** Party leader sent to Command School early. Separation anxiety.
7.  **The Sim:** Party reunited in Command School. Introduction to Fleet Mechanics.
8.  **The Teacher:** Mazer Rackham breaks their ego. " The enemy is the only teacher."
9.  **The Dreams:** The Mind-Game reveals the Formics are trying to talk. Graff suppresses this.
10. **The Exhaustion:** Days of sleepless simulation. Drugs introduced.
11. **The Final Exam:** The battle against the "Simulation" Homeworld.
12. **The Genocide:** Activation of the M.D. Device. Victory. Celebration.
13. **The Reveal:** Mazer reveals it was real. The Formics are dead.
14. **The Aftermath:** Discovery of the cocoon. The Queen lives.
15. **The Flight:** Protecting the egg from the IF. Escaping to the stars.

## [TAG: RULES_CUSTOM]
### 1. Zero-G Combat Mechanics
*   **Momentum:** If you move, you continue moving in that direction until you hit something or use a reaction to stabilize.
*   **Orientation:** There is no "Down." Enemies can be on the "ceiling." Attacks from "above" (relative to target) gain Advantage.
*   **Recoil:** Firing a projectile weapon pushes you 5ft in the opposite direction.

### 2. Psychological Pressure (The "Breaking Point")
*   **Stat:** **Pressure** (0-10).
*   **Accumulation:** Gain Pressure when failing a mission, witnessing trauma, or using Focus-Stims.
*   **Effect:**
    *   At 5 Pressure: Disadvantage on Wis saves.
    *   At 10 Pressure: **Breakdown.** The character gains a permanent "Quirk" (e.g., Tremors, Nightmares, Mutism) and Pressure resets to 5.

### 3. Fleet Command (Mass Combat)
*   Instead of AC and HP, Fleets have **Cohesion** and **Firepower**.
*   **Orders:** Players use their Action to give orders (Flank, Shield Wall, Suicide Run).
*   **The Cost:** "Suicide Run" guarantees damage but lowers Morale. If Morale hits 0, the fleet mutinies.
