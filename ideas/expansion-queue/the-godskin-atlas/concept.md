# The Godskin Atlas

## Logline

A new continent is discovered to be the sleeping, city-sized body of a forgotten deity. The party is hired to rescue an expedition that sent a single message: “It's waking up.”

## Concept frame

- Category: The Veiled
- Tone: Awe-inspiring, ominous
- Genre: Exploration, cosmic horror
- Core concept: The world is a living god
- Locus: The slumbering god's body
- Sensory anchor: Rhythmic pulse in the ground and golden ichor
- Player verbs: Explore, rescue, placate

## Expansion hook

Other, smaller god-creatures begin to stir in the world's oceans, drawn by the awakening of their progenitor.

This is an expansion-queue stub, not a production-ready campaign package.
