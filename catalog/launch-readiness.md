# Launch Readiness

## Recommended 3/3/3 slate

| Length | Campaign | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Short | The Impossible Vault | Social/heist; complete 8–10-session package |
| Short | St. Mercy's Magical Menagerie | Light/cozy episodic medical fantasy |
| Short | The Vanishing Village | Horror investigation with a clear escalation |
| Medium | Jazz Noir City | Urban mystery and social play |
| Medium | Ascension Protocol | Adventure/combat progression |
| Medium | Atomic Arcana | Science-fantasy intrigue |
| Long | The Eternal Feast | Social-fantasy flagship; 35 sessions and existing assets |
| Long | The Porcelain Court | Dark social horror with deep faction play |
| Long | Wings of the Void | Science-fiction exploration |

Alternates: **Combat Healer Chronicles**, **Murder on the Astral Express**, **The Infinite Stacks**.

Post-launch flagships: **The Godskin Atlas**, **The Infinite Stacks**, **Combat Healer Chronicles**.

IP transformation required: **Colossus Ascent** (*Shadow of the Colossus* names/beats), **Camp Half-Blood Chronicles**, **Xenomorph Protocol**.

High-potential expansion queue: **The Godskin Atlas**, **The Cacophony of Stillness**, **The Grammar of Creation**.

## Named-campaign findings

- The Eternal Feast is genuinely long-form and receives readiness credit for 14 Drive assets.
- The Godskin Atlas is a strong sheet-only concept with no GitHub package; its present readiness is concept-level but its flagship potential is high.
- The Porcelain Court is among the strongest dark/social packages.
- The Impossible Vault is normalized as Short despite its author-assigned “medium” label.
- The Infinite Stacks is promising but lacks a campaign bible.
- Colossus Ascent and Combat Healer Chronicles are richer outside `Completed` than their copies inside it.
- St. Mercy's and The Vanishing Village are strong short candidates but still need bibles/assets/safety passes.
